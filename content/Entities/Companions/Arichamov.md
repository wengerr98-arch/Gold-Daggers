# Arichamov

## Known Information

Arichamov is a small draconic familiar belonging to [[Sadam Salim]], known affectionately as MovMov. The creature communicates telepathically through Sadam and serves primarily as a scout, using its Blindsight to reconnoitre areas the party cannot safely approach directly.

## Session Appearances

- Session 063: Flies around the obelisk in the Goliath quarter of Skjall, scouting using Blindsight. Communicates through Sadam via telepathy.
- Session 064: Mirrored or duplicated versions appear inside the uncanny house/tavern manifestation during the Nightmare Overseer encounter; it is unclear whether this is Arichamov itself or a copy generated by the dream-house entity.

## Known Facts

- Small draconic familiar; owner: [[Sadam Salim]].
- Nickname: MovMov (also written Mov Mov).
- Communicates telepathically via Sadam Salim.
- Capable of Blindsight; used for scouting.
