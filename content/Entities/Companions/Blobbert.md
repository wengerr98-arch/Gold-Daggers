# Blobbert

## Known Information

Blobbert is an Imp familiar belonging to [[Eloryn]], known to the Gold Daggers mostly as a grumpy goldfish. He served extensively as a forward scout and assassination delivery vector before being fought and killed by Eloryn herself during the raid on the crab structure beneath Helikonos. After his death he returned to the Waterplane and was rebound to Eloryn's pocket dimension in a transformed form, with his familiar bond taking on new properties.

## Known Facts

- Race: Imp; role: Familiar; owner: [[Eloryn]].
- The Gold Daggers knew him mostly as a grumpy goldfish.
- Can fly; used extensively as a forward scout.
- Session 024: Transformed by Eloryn's ritual into a small cephalopod-like imp form with the ability to hold objects and shape-change.
- When Eloryn loses Shen as a patron, Blobbert also disappears.
- Killed by Eloryn during the crab structure raid (Session 052); dropped a golden ring; returned to the Waterplane.
- After Session 052, familiar bond transformed — Blobbert became bound to Eloryn's pocket dimension/shell.
- R'lyeh, Shen, and Blobbert are linked through the familiar history described in the Book of Azatoth (Session 052).
- Eventually fought and defeated by the party in his true squid/octopus form at Helykonos.
