## Known Information

Dave is a Kobold fighter who joined the Gold Daggers as a companion, played by guest player Luc in Session 013. His full noble name is Gabriel Comte Esteban de Morena. He met the party at the [[Fightclub Untergrund]] alongside [[Gladis Gladigator]] and led them to the kobold village north of [[Dalior]] where he grew up. Dave gave [[Snix]] a tribe cloak — a black cloak with red symbols that every member of his tribe receives — and his tribe's stolen eggs may be connected to Snix's identity.

## Session Appearances

- Session 013: Full noble name revealed; gave [[Snix]] the tribe cloak (Kobold Stamm Umhang); accompanied party to the spa and notice board.
- Session 029: Referenced in the kobold village context — the burial ritual was for Dave's kobold kin and community members who had perished.
- Session 030: Warned the party that people were heading toward the kobold village and urged them to move on; acted as protective lookout for the settlement.
