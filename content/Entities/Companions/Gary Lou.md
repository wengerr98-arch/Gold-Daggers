## Known Information

Gary Lou is a baby blue-black dragon (or large lizard — species is ambiguous; described as "en chliine blue dragon (the normal animal)" with possible "blue dragon fish" references) who hatched from a large egg discovered during a wild magic event in Session 074. He was immediately adopted by Snix and Eloryn as their "child." The wild magic context of his hatching (roll: 94 → 77) may suggest he is more than a mundane animal. Also called "Drache schnägg" (dragon snail) and referred to as a companion of [[Fred]] in Session 077.

## Session Appearances

- Session 074: Hatched from a wild magic egg in Skjall during the Wild Hunt crisis. "De Snix und d Elo send etz d eltere vom Gary Lou." Adopted on the spot by Snix and Eloryn.
- Session 077: Arrives at Volk camp with [[Fred]] and Natok. Referred to as "de schnägg" (the snail).
- Session 078: Listed as "Dragon Snail Lu"; present during party preparations for teleportation and departure from the Volk settlement toward Aarum and Ophris.

## Known Facts

- Species: ambiguous — described as a small blue-black dragon or dragon-like lizard ("blue dragon fish"); exact nature unconfirmed.
- Hatched from a large egg during a wild magic event (Session 074).
- Adopted by Snix and [[Eloryn]]; they are considered his "parents."
- Nicknames: Gary-Lue, Dragon Snail, de schnägg.
- Arrived at Volk camp alongside [[Fred]] and Natok (Session 077).
