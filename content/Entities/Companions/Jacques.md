## Known Information

Jacques is a severed zombie cooking hand taken by the party from Dr. Mason's kitchen during Session 008. The Gold Daggers named him, adopted him as an informal companion, and have carried him through multiple sessions — initially in a bag, later on a leash. He is associated with the party's brewery operation in Aarum, appearing in that context alongside Goldie Bräu.

## Session Appearances

- Session 008: Zombie cooking hand encountered in Dr. Mason's kitchen. Named "Jacques" by the party and kept in a bag.
- Session 021: Shown to an unspecified contact by the party on the day of the prison break; provokes no reaction from the person shown.
- Session 040: Walked openly on a leash through Aarum's streets, attracting public notice; stealth implications flagged as a concern.
- Session 043: Present at the brewery among the party's assets in Aarum; party checks on the brewery during date preparations.
- Session 078: Associated with the party's brewery operation (Goldie Bräu) in Aarum during a stop at the city.
