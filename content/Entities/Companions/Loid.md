## Known Information

Loid is the nickname the Gold Daggers gave to their sentient Nautiloid ship, first boarded at the ocean floor in Session 051. The bond between the ship and the party was cemented when [[Eloryn]] cast invisibility on it and formed a personal emotional bond with it, after which the group began calling it "Loid." The Nautiloid has served as the party's primary vessel, mobile home base, and staging point throughout the campaign; it should not be confused with [[Calamitas]], which is the astral ship.

## Session Appearances

- Session 051: Party boarded the Nautiloid at the ocean floor; [[Eloryn]] cast invisibility on the ship and formed an emotional bond with it, naming it "Loid." The ship traveled through a portal into the Waterplane, fought whale ships, and BB closed the portal using the portal stick.
- Session 052: The party remodeled the Nautiloid into separate living spaces — Barzan built a secret prayer room, BB planned an atelier and library, Koda planned a workbench and animal-carving area, Eloryn added a plant-processing station; the ship served as home base sailing toward Helikonos.
- Session 053: The Nautiloid served as the party's base of operations during the approach to the Revolutionary Army stronghold and the gorge; Lorilla transformed into a Plesiosaurus to lead the escape through water after the portal chamber collapsed.
- Session 054: Party recovers aboard Loid after their fight; Barzan prays for divine intervention aboard ship. Used as base for downtime activities including long rest, breakfast, study, and communications before teleporting away. [[Eloryn]] finds a +1 shield aboard.
- Session 056: Referenced as "boat / Loid"; the group discusses future travel options and the ship's forge as a potential resource for Treth and his family to become a strong crew addition.
- Session 058: The party parks Loid outside Skjall's massive steel wall on arrival; subsequently finds a section of the wall that can be opened to accommodate Loid inside the wall structure, allowing goods to be unloaded in the freezing weather.
- Session 060: Loid serves as the party's base and refuge during the Skjall crisis; Lorilla invites dwarves from the [[Kirche der Herbstgöttin]] aboard under her protection. A doppelgänger discovered among the guests is killed by Snix.
- Session 065: The party uses Loid as their base in Limbo during Skjall operations; observations of the snowstorm and a snow/ice/crystal creature stamping out cold shockwaves were made from Loid's position.
- Session 076: Referenced as the party's base and staging point during the Skjall throne-hall assault; the Gold Daggers mount their push from Loid's position for the wall-breach and final fight sequence.
- Session 101: Referenced as "Hole de Loid" — the party calls for the Nautiloid to be retrieved or summoned; Loid remains the Gold Daggers' primary vessel as the group moves into the Astral Sea phase.
