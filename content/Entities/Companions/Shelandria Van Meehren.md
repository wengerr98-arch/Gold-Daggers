## Known Information

Shelandria Van Meehren, known as Shelly, is a slender Half-Elf who escaped a captive camp and guided the Gold Daggers to the rescue site in Dalior. She is acrobatic in movement, wears close-fitting clothing with a hooded cloak, and carries an epic bow. Her full name was revealed in Session 014; she was played by Sam during the period when Leumas was kidnapped in Dalior.
