# Trafalgar

## Known Information

Trafalgar (also called Trafi) is a Salamander freed by [[Leumas]] through a telepathic bargain following a rodeo event inside [[Skjall]]. After the bargain was struck, the creature effectively joined the Gold Daggers as a party companion.

## Session Appearances

- Session 058: Named and freed via [[Leumas]]'s telepathic bargain after the rodeo inside [[Skjall]]; joined the party.

## Known Facts
