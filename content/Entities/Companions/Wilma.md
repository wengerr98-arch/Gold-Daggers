# Wilma

## Known Information

Wilma is a rogue and lockpicker introduced by [[Cheers]] as "D'Wilma" for the Zentralbank heist in Session 049. Her defining skill is her ability to unlock any lock, and she proved essential in solving the vault's star-constellation style mechanical puzzle. She was part of what the party internally nicknamed the "Scoobie Doo crew."

## Session Appearances

- Session 049: Heist crew member — introduced by [[Cheers]] as D'Wilma, unlocked vault mechanisms, and participated in solving the star-constellation mechanical puzzle inside the [[Zentralbank]].

## Known Facts

- Class/Role: Rogue / Lockpicker.
- Recruited by [[Cheers]] for the Session 049 Zentralbank heist.
- Known as "D'Wilma" in session text.
- Capable of unlocking all locks; solved the star-constellation style vault puzzle.
- Part of the "Scoobie Doo crew" (in-world nickname for the heist team).
