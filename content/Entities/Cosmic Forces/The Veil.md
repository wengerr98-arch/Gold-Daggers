# The Veil

## Known Information

The Veil is the campaign-specific term for the liminal border between the Material Plane and the afterlife — the space between life and death. Related terms used in-session include **Limbo** ("Es scheint ein Zwischenraum zu sein zwischen Leben und Tod. Limbo.") and **afterlife** ("welcome to the afterlife"), treated as synonymous with The Veil in campaign context. The session title for Session 063 is "The Veil — der Vorhang zwischen Leben und Tod," making it the primary setting of that session's afterlife sequence.

## Session Appearances

- Session 063: Primary setting of the session's afterlife sequence. Party crosses The Veil via the Obelisk activation in the Goliath quarter of [[Skjall]], encounters lost souls, [[Karoth]], and the child-entity [[Ophris]]; the [[Wild Hunt]] is glimpsed through The Veil.

## Known Facts

- The border between the Material Plane and the afterlife; not a physical place but a liminal threshold.
- Synonymous in-campaign with "Limbo" and "afterlife."
- The Gold Daggers crossed The Veil via the Obelisk in the Goliath quarter of [[Skjall]] (Session 063).
- The [[Wild Hunt]] can be glimpsed through The Veil.
- [[Karoth]] is found in The Veil, helping lost souls complete unfinished business.
- The [[Wintergöttin]] has influence over The Veil but not full control.
- Some souls have been held in The Veil for eternity — something is preventing them from moving on.
- The Veil contains an afterlife throne room where the armor of all Torr kings of Skjall is stored.
