# Ambulan

## Known Information

The Ambulan family is a wealthy household based in [[Lervik]], identified by their name on a mailbox at a large estate. Their home was the target of a heist carried out by the Gold Daggers on behalf of the [[Storm-Phönix]] guild. The family's political connections and broader standing have not been fully established.

## Session Appearances

- Session 001: The Ambulan family estate in Lervik was the target of a guild heist; stolen items included a statue of the kletternden Hand, an ever-expanding goldfish, and a cloak bearing the [[Erdbrecher]] symbol.

## Known Facts

- Type: wealthy family/household faction
- Base: large estate in [[Lervik]]
- A second family name, Malon, also appeared on a mailbox at the same estate
- Targeted by the Gold Daggers in a theft mission for the [[Storm-Phönix]] guild (Session 001)
