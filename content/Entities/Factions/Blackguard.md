# Blackguard

## Known Information

The Blackguard is a military unit of [[Nolia]], functioning as an elite or specialised force with apparent religious ties to [[Siaroddin]]. A Blackguard operative encountered in Session 017 presented as a priest/paladin type bearing the Dornenkrone (thorn crown) symbol and attempted to breach the Entertainment District during a lockdown, before being killed by [[Silk]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 017: A Blackguard agent attempted to enter the Entertainment District during the district sealing operation; confronted the party and district defenders, then defeated and killed by [[Silk]].

## Known Facts

- Type: military unit / faction of [[Nolia]]
- Religious affiliation: associated with [[Siaroddin]] and the Dornenkrone (thorn crown) symbol
- Known operative type: priest/paladin archetype
- Items carried by Session 017 operative: large axe (looted), chain with two large creature amulets, Halbplatten-Rüstung (half-plate armour), Dornenkrone religious symbol
- Related military units: [[Rosengarde]], [[Blackguard]] (within the Nolia military structure)
