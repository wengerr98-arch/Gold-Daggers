## Known Information

The Blackguard is a military unit of [[Nolia]], functioning as an elite or specialised force with apparent religious ties to [[Siaroddin]]. A Blackguard operative encountered in Session 017 presented as a priest/paladin type bearing the Dornenkrone (thorn crown) symbol and attempted to breach the [[Entertainment District (Dalior)|Entertainment District]] during a lockdown, before being killed by [[Silk]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 017: A Blackguard agent attempted to enter the [[Entertainment District (Dalior)|Entertainment District]] during the district sealing operation; confronted the party and district defenders, then defeated and killed by [[Silk]].

- Religious affiliation: associated with [[Siaroddin]] and the Dornenkrone (thorn crown) symbol
