# Chrom-Brigade

## Known Information

The Chrom-Brigade is one of [[Nolia]]'s primary offensive military units. [[Mogak]] served as its Kommandant before being imprisoned for killing a superior officer who issued unjust orders, suggesting the unit has a command hierarchy with accountability — at least nominally.

## Session Appearances

- Session 022: Named by [[Mogak]] in his backstory as the unit he commanded; described as a primary offensive force of the Nolia military.

## Known Facts

- Type: military unit / faction of [[Nolia]]
- Role: primary offensive attack force
- Former Kommandant: [[Mogak]] (imprisoned for killing superior officer over unjust orders)
- Related Nolia military units: [[Rosengarde]], [[Blackguard]]
