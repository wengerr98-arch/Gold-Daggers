# Cousins

## Known Information

The Cousins are an informal group of NPCs defined by a shared naming pattern — most members carry a "Bro-," "Brah-," or similar pun-variant in their name. Most of the Cousins are based at the [[Shipyard (Ophris Coast)]], and they have been associated with supplying provisions and with community-building activity near a shipyard. Their precise organisational purpose (workforce, community group, or something more) remains unclear.

## Session Appearances

- Session 055: Referenced as "Cousins" bringing provisions and beer; [[Bromir]] (one of the Cousins) encountered at the Helikonos shipyard.
- Session 060: [[Bromir]] and [[Bronils]] mentioned by [[Bruhnik]] (aboard Loid in Skjall) as co-founders of a village near a shipyard; indirect reference confirming these Cousins members are active and associated with [[Bruhnik]].

## Known Facts

- Type: informal group / faction
- Base: [[Shipyard (Ophris Coast)]]
- Known or likely members: [[Bromir]], [[Bromar]], [[Bramir]], [[Brovin]], [[Brovir]], [[Bradir]], [[Bronils]]
- Naming convention: "Bro-" / "Bra-" prefix variants
- Associated with supplying provisions and beer (Session 055)
- [[Bromir]] and [[Bronils]] co-founded a village near a shipyard (per [[Bruhnik]], Session 060)
