# Das Mehr

## Known Information

Das Mehr (also called "The Mehr") is a revolutionary movement based in [[Dalior]], led by [[Aubonne]]. The faction is connected to the underground network operating through the [[Fightclub Untergrund]] in the [[Untergrund (Dalior)|Untergrund]], and serves as a key link between the Gold Daggers and the plot surrounding the [[Oubliette]] prison.

## Session Appearances

- Session 013: [[HErvir]], who runs the [[Fightclub Untergrund]] and is a member of Das Mehr, tells the party "Ich bin Teil des Mehrs" and promises to inform them when the timing of the prison raid is known.

## Known Facts

- Type: revolutionary movement / faction
- Base: [[Dalior]]
- Leader: [[Aubonne]]
- Known member: [[HErvir]] (runs the [[Fightclub Untergrund]] in the [[Untergrund (Dalior)|Untergrund]])
- Role in campaign: faction connecting the party to the [[Oubliette]] raid plot (Session 013)
- Old vault description: *"Revolutionsbewegung in Dalior unter der Leitung von Aubonne."*
