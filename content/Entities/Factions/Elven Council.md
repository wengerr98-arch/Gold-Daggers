# Elven Council

## Known Information

The Elven Council is the governing body of the [[Red-Walled Elven City]], composed of librarians and councillors. When the gods departed the city, the Council enacted a contingency plan to maintain their hold on power, effectively continuing to rule in the gods' absence as though nothing had changed. They are the faction most directly responsible for the city's post-divine self-governance.

## Session Appearances

- Session 099: Referenced in party's research about the Elven city and the Forgotten War. The Council is identified as the body that took over governance after the gods disappeared, having prepared a "backup plan" in advance.

## Known Facts

- Governing body of the [[Red-Walled Elven City]].
- Composed of librarians and councillors.
- Had a pre-prepared contingency plan for ruling after the gods' departure.
- Continued to rule the city after the gods left, maintaining the appearance of normal governance.
