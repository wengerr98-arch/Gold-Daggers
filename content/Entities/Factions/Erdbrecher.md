# Erdbrecher

## Known Information

The Erdbrecher (also known as the Earthbreakers) are a faction or cult with connections to cosmic-level threats including the titans, the Silence, and planar corruption. Their symbols were found on items in the party's possession as far back as Session 007, and their relevance was confirmed at the highest levels when Levistus explicitly named them during his briefing in Session 100. A split structure within the cult has been suggested, with Treth potentially having a separate connection to the faction.

## Session Appearances

- Session 007: Erdbrecher (Earthbreaker) symbols found on items in the party's possession; the nature and meaning of the symbols flagged as an open investigation thread.
- Session 092: BB and Barzan confront a cultist-like figure tied to Erdbrecher imagery and rune tattoos; the party notes the possibility that the Erdbrecher cult has a split structure and that Treth may have a separate connection.
- Session 100 / [[Session 100 - Illumination]]: Explicitly named by Levistus in his briefing to the party in the context of titans, the Silence, and the wider planar corruption threat.
