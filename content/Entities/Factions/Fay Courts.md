# Fay Courts

## Known Information

The Fay Courts are a homebrew cosmological faction system governing the Feywild, divided into two courts: the Red Court (Court of Control) and the Black Court (Court of Creation). Each court is led by a Queen, a Lady, and a Mother, and each has an elected Knight role. The courts represent structural roles rather than specific individuals. Leumas's parent is a member of the Black Court, making the Fay Courts directly relevant to his character arc — as a Half-Fairy (Changeling), he will one day face a choice between his Human and Fay heritage.

## Session Appearances

- Session 044: Detailed Fay Courts lore revealed via a lore book that Eloryn reads. The two-court structure, the queen/lady/mother/knight hierarchy, and Leumas's Black Court parent connection are all established.
- Session 056: Leumas's vision of Felix features "hands with red fingernails pulling at story-threads" and his own arms showing "Marionette strings" — the Red Court appears as a metaphysical actor. Confirmed as one of the Fae Courts (not a separate faction).
