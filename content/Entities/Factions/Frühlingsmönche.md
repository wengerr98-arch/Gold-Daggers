# Frühlingsmönche

## Known Information

The Frühlingsmönche (Spring Monks) are a religious order observed by the party in [[Ophris]] during Session 036. Their theology centres on life, death, and the afterlife, with a distinctively celebratory view of death — they see it as a natural and beautiful transition, "wie eine Blüte die kaputt geht" (like a flower that falls apart), rather than something to mourn. They were seen praying on the Vierte Platte (Fourth Plate/Level) of the city.

## Session Appearances

- Session 036: Observed praying on the Vierte Platte in [[Ophris]]. [[Barzan]] deciphers their prayer and shares its philosophical content with the party.

## Known Facts

- Religious order active in [[Ophris]], observed on the Vierte Platte.
- Theology centres on life, death, and the afterlife.
- View death as bittersweet and natural rather than tragic — "like a flower that falls apart."
- Also known as "Spring Monks" (English alias).
