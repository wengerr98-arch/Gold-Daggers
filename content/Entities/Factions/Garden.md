# Garden

## Known Information

The Garden (also referred to as "Wächter") is the city guard of [[Dalior]], operating as a unit under the Nolia Military. They are active in the Entertainment District and surrounding areas. In Session 020 they were mobilised in response to a fireworks incident caused by the party.

## Session Appearances

- Session 004: Guards of the Garden observed removing barricades in Dalior; broader role and context in this session unclear.
- Session 020: The Garden/Wächter begin searching for the party after Eloryn shoots a light-plant bloom with fireworks, triggering a chain reaction that closes approximately 100 light-plant blooms across the district.

## Known Facts
