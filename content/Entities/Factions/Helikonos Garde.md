# Helikonos Garde

## Known Information

The Helikonos Garde is the military and guard force of Helikonos, a city-state that operates as its own independent nation, allied with but distinct from Nolia. In Session 047, the Garde was observed enforcing a citywide lockdown during the Gold Daggers' escape and infiltration, pushing citizens indoors and restricting movement.

## Session Appearances

- Session 047: City guard forces push people indoors and lock down Helikonos during the party's escape and infiltration.

## Known Facts

- Military/guard force of Helikonos
- Helikonos is an independent nation, not part of Nolia
- Helikonos is allied with [[Nolia Faction|Nolia]]
- Distinct from the Nolia military
