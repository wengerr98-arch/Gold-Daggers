# Kirche der Herbstgöttin

## Known Information

The Kirche der Herbstgöttin (Church of the Autumn Goddess) is the religious organization devoted to [[Herbstgöttin Frelka]], with its primary seat at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]. [[Don Hor]] serves as Abbot (Abt) and [[Udir]] is his second-in-command. The church's domain covers autumn, harvest, and seasonal change, though Udir has described Frelka as "only a rough guide" and noted that "autumn is a broad field."

## Session Appearances

- Session 031: The harvest feast (Herbstbeginn Neue Ernte) is conducted under the church's auspices. Don Hor and Udir preside. Barzan and Leumas both pray to Frelka before the monastery trial.
- Session 060: A group of Herbst Kirche dwarves is present in Skjall during the crisis; Lorilla invites them aboard Loid for protection. A doppelgänger is discovered to have infiltrated and murdered one of their clerics, impersonating the body.

## Known Facts

- Deity: [[Herbstgöttin Frelka]]
- Primary location: [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]
- Known leaders: [[Don Hor]] (Abt / Abbot), [[Udir]] (second-in-command)
- Domain: autumn, harvest, seasonal change
- Has dwarf clergy members (witnessed in Skjall, Session 060)
