# Kletternde Kirche

## Known Information

The Kletternde Kirche (Climbing Church) is a named church organization referenced in a letter Lorilla read during Session 049's Rosenpalast infiltration. A designated priest — referred to as "Dr Priester der kletternden Kirche" — appears in correspondence addressed to "M." (possibly Mason) related to the wedding; a spy servant describes this priest as "a very difficult person." The church's possible ties to [[Dr. Mason]] and the infernal events at the wedding remain unresolved.

## Session Appearances

- Session 048: Named in a letter Lorilla reads ("Dr Priester der kletternden Kirche"); the letter is addressed to "M." (possibly Mason). A spy servant describes the priest as "a very difficult person."
- Session 049: Referenced as an active faction; the group discusses Kletternde Kirche connections in the context of Mason and the broader political situation following the heist.

## Known Facts

- Also known as the "Climbing Church"
- Has a designated priest title: "Dr Priester der kletternden Kirche"
- Mentioned in correspondence connected to the [[Rosenpalast]] wedding and addressed to "M."
- Possible connection to [[Dr. Mason]]
