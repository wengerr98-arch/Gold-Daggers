# Northern Tribes

## Known Information

The Northern Tribes are a faction or group of peoples referenced in connection with the Kasta desert region. Their existence was raised in the context of Genasi origins, with the question of whether Genasi are originally from north of [[Kasta]] and associated with these northern tribes.

## Session Appearances

- Session 084: Referenced in connection with the Kasta desert region and possible Genasi origins. The session poses the question: "Are Genasi originally from north of Kasta? Are they associated with the northern tribes?"

## Known Facts

- Referenced in Session 084
- Associated with the region north of [[Kasta]]
- Possible connection to Genasi / Genazi peoples
