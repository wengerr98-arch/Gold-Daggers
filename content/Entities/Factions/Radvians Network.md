# Radvians Network

## Known Information

Radvians Network is an armed vigilante group led by [[Radvian]], operating in Shicla / [[Ophris]] territory. The group captures and questions suspicious individuals — the party was detained by them in Session 035. [[Radvian]] grew up with the unnamed local king of Shicla / Ophris, suggesting the network may have political backing or personal motivation behind its activities.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 035]]: The group captures and questions the party. [[Radvian]] is their leader; she grew up with the local king.
