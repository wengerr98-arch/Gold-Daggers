# Revolutionaries (Helikonos)

## Known Information

The Revolutionaries are a faction active in [[Helikonos]] who oppose [[Shen]] and the existing power structure of the city. They refer to themselves simply as "revolutionaries" and have no confirmed canonical faction name beyond this. By Session 055 they were actively coordinating with the Gold Daggers party during a relief operation, with their guards — including [[Udrin]] — visibly exhausted from the effort following [[Arthur Ruger]]'s death.

## Session Appearances

- Session 055: Actively coordinating with the party during the Helikonos relief operation; their guards (including [[Udrin]]) participated in the operation.

## Known Facts

- Operate in [[Helikonos]]
- Opposed to [[Shen]] and the existing Helikonos power structure
- Opposed to the Helikonos army (per Session 050 correction note)
- Coordinated with the Gold Daggers as of Session 055
- Known member/guard: [[Udrin]]
- No confirmed canonical faction name beyond "revolutionaries"
