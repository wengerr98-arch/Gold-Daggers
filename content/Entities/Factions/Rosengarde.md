# Rosengarde

## Known Information

The Rosengarde is a special paladin unit within the Nolia Military, confirmed to serve as an elite escort force. In Session 008 they escorted the Gold Daggers to Mason Mansion on the second tier of [[Dalior]] (the [[Zweite Platte]]). Their command structure, oath, and relationship to other Nolia Military units remain undocumented.

## Session Appearances

- Session 008: Escorted the Gold Daggers to Mason Mansion on the Zweite Platte of [[Dalior]].

## Known Facts

- Part of the [[Nolia Faction|Nolia Military]]
- Classified as an elite paladin unit / escort force
- Active in [[Dalior]] (Zweite Platte) as of Session 008
