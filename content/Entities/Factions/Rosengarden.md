# Rosengarden

## Known Information

Rosengarden is a named group encountered by the party at the entrance to the [[Entertainment District (Dalior)]] in Session 015. The party fought them at the district entrance, and Lorilla acquired medium armour as a result of the encounter. Whether they are a guard faction, mercenary group, or criminal organisation is not yet confirmed. Note: "Rosengarden" (here) is likely distinct from the "Rosengarten Hotel" in [[Aarum]] — different spelling, different location; do not merge without DM confirmation.
