# Sarrukh

## Known Information

The Sarrukh are described by [[Snix]] (Session 088) as the first humanoid species, whose descendants are the [[Yuan-Ti]]. A small figure resembling a Sarrukh — described as "like a homunculus" — was observed in [[Gilgamesh Workshop|Gilgamesh's Workshop]] during the party's 6000-year time jump. The extent of campaign-specific homebrew lore around this species is not yet confirmed by the DM.

## Session Appearances

- Session 088: Referenced by [[Snix]] during encounter in [[Gilgamesh Workshop|Gilgamesh's Workshop]] (6000-year past timeline); a figure resembling a Sarrukh was present.

## Known Facts

- Described as the first humanoid species
- Ancestral to the [[Yuan-Ti]]
- A Sarrukh-like figure was observed in [[Gilgamesh Workshop|Gilgamesh's Workshop]] 6000 years in the past
- Source of identification: [[Snix]] (Session 088)
