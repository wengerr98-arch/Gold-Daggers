# Schwarzes Gänseblümchen

## Known Information

Schwarzes Gänseblümchen ("Black Daisy") is an internal codename given by King [[Azan Aeducan]] for a newly established special guard unit. The party (the Gold Daggers) were formally inducted as its members during a private meeting at [[Bar Tonne]] in Session 037. It functions as a covert and special-operations arm of the king's apparatus.

## Session Appearances

- Session 037: King Azan formally names and establishes the unit during the private meeting at [[Bar Tonne]]. Party members are inducted as members and receive gold medallion credentials.

## Known Facts

- Internal codename meaning "Black Daisy"
- Established and named by King [[Azan Aeducan]]
- The Gold Daggers are members of this unit
- Members receive seven gold medallions as formal credentials (Gauntlet insignia)
- Functions as a covert/special-operations arm of the king's apparatus
