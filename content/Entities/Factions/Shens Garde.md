# Shens Garde

## Known Information

Shens Garde (Shen's Guard) is the personal guard and military unit of [[Shen]], operating as part of the [[Helykonos]] Military (formerly known as Shi Cheng). The unit is under the direct command of Shen within that military hierarchy. Party member [[Eloryn]] has prior history with Shens Garde, having escaped from them in Shi Cheng/Helykonos.

## Session Appearances

- Session 036: Referenced by [[Dhedry]], who asks [[Eloryn]] how she escaped from Shens Garde in [[Shi Cheng]] (Helykonos).

## Known Facts

- Personal guard and military unit of [[Shen]]
- Part of the [[Helykonos]] Military hierarchy
- Formerly based in Shi Cheng (now Helykonos)
- [[Eloryn]] has a history of escaping from this unit
