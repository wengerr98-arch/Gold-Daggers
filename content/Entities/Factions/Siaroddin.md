# Siaroddin

## Known Information

Siaroddin is a religious order often described as extremist and fanatical. Its symbol is the Dornenkrone (thorn crown). In Session 017, a Siaroddin priest — described as noble in strategy, vain, and proud — attempted to enter the sealed Entertainment District during a lockdown, and may be connected to a Blackguard operative killed later in the same session.

## Session Appearances

- Session 017: A Siaroddin priest attempts to enter the Entertainment District during the lockdown; possibly the same individual as or connected to the [[Blackguard]] operative killed later in the session.
