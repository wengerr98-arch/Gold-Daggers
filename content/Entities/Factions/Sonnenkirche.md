# Sonnenkirche

## Known Information

The Sonnenkirche (Sun Church) was a religious institution formerly active in [[Aarum]] that ran an orphanage on the island approximately 12 years before the current session timeline. The orphanage burned down, the head priest died in the fire, and another priest fled, leaving the Sonnenkirche with no active presence on the island. The church is distinct from both [[Sommerkirche]] and [[Winterkirche]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 041: Referenced as the former operator of Aarum's orphanage; its burned building and history discussed. Its former book collection is noted as purchased by [[Randrus Torren]], and the [[Sonnenbibel]] was acquired from that collection by [[Barzan Aeducan]].

## Known Facts

- Former religious institution in [[Aarum]], now dissolved
- Ran an orphanage on the island ~12 years before current timeline
- Orphanage burned down; head priest died in fire; another priest fled
- No longer has an active presence in [[Aarum]]
- Had a book collection, later purchased by [[Randrus Torren]]
- The [[Sonnenbibel]] came from the Sonnenkirche's collection
- Distinct from [[Sommerkirche]] and [[Winterkirche]]
- The [[Whiterose]] faction is implied to have burned the old church
