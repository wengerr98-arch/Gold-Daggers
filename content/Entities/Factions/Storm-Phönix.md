# Storm-Phönix

## Known Information

Storm-Phönix is a thieves' guild (Diebesguilde) to which all of the Gold Daggers originally belonged. The guild is based in [[Lervik]], where it maintained a tavern with a cellar featuring a large table, two statues, and seven empty seats. The Gold Daggers were members when they first met in Lervik and carried out their initial mission at the [[Ambulan]] family estate.

## Session Appearances

- Session 001: Named as the founding guild of the party ("Unsere Guilde: Storm-Phönix"); the Gold Daggers were members when they first met in [[Lervik]] and carried out a mission at the [[Ambulan]] family estate.
