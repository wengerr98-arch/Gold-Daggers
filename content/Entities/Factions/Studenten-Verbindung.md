# Studenten-Verbindung

## Known Information

The Studenten-Verbindung ("Student Fraternity/Corps") is a student group identified as the culprits behind the headless copper statues quest in Session 006. Six members were arrested and imprisoned following their exposure by Leumas. The group claimed to be acting on behalf of [[Dr. Mason]], though whether they were hired, coerced, or acting willingly remains unclear.

## Session Appearances

- Session 006: Identified by Leumas as the culprits behind the headless copper statues. Six members sent to prison. Connected to [[Dr. Mason]].

## Known Facts

- Six members arrested and imprisoned after Session 006.
- Claimed to act "für Dr. Mason!" — connected to [[Dr. Mason]].
- Responsible for the headless copper statues incident.
