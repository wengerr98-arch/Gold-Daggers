# Sturm-Phönix

## Known Information

Sturm-Phönix is the thieves' guild (colloquially called the Diebesgilde) to which all original Gold Daggers party members belonged when they first met in Lervik. The guild operates out of a tavern cellar in Lervik and is divided into three swarms (Schwärme) under separate leaders: Yorni (gnome, guild chief / high society), Thavyra (Dragonborn, shadow operations), and [[Askot]] (fat human, offensive/Gangwars branch). The guild name derives from two individuals — Sturm, an Earth Genasi, and her twin sister Phönix — whose statues stand in the Lervik cellar.

## Session Appearances

- Session 001: Named as the guild (Storm-Phönix) to which the Gold Daggers belonged when they first met in Lervik; the party met in the guild's tavern cellar and carried out a heist at the Ambulan family estate.
- Session 002: Referenced as "Diebesgilde" / "Guilde hat drei Schwärme" — the organisation with three swarms, whose leaders are described in the session shorthand.
- Session 007: Referenced as "Diebesgilde" — the party uses the guild's job-board (Schwarzes Brett) at the [[Rakshasa Tempel]] as their main source of work orders in Dalior.
- Session 022: Lorilla recognises the Earth Genasi prisoner Sturm from a statue in the Lervik guild cellar; Sturm reveals she is a co-namesake of the guild and that her twin sister is named Phönix — confirming the guild name derives from these two individuals.

## Known Facts

- True name: Sturm-Phönix; common in-world nickname: Diebesgilde (also spelled Diebesguilde in older sources).
- All original Gold Daggers party members were guild members when they first met in Lervik.
- Operates out of a tavern in Lervik; the cellar features a large table, two statues, and seven empty chairs.
- Three swarms (Schwärme) each under a separate leader: Yorni (gnome, guild chief / Ballraumpolitik), Thavyra (Dragonborn, shadows), [[Askot]] (fat human, offensive branch / Gangwars).
- Job-board (Schwarzes Brett) at the [[Rakshasa Tempel]] in Dalior used by the party for work orders.
- Guild name derives from Sturm (Earth Genasi) and her twin sister Phönix; their statues stand in the Lervik cellar.
