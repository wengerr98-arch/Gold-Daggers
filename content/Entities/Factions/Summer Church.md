# Summer Church

## Known Information

The Summer Church (Sommerkirche) is a religious faction associated with the summer deity, possibly the [[Sommergöttin]]. It was identified in Session 030 as Barzan's religious affiliation, making him the only confirmed member; the group may be a small or secretive order rather than a large institution. Its relationship to the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]] and the autumn deity's tradition has not yet been established.

## Session Appearances

- Session 030: Listed in session metadata as a faction; noted as Barzan's religious affiliation. Not referenced in the main session transcript.

## Known Facts

- Associated with the summer deity (possibly [[Sommergöttin]]).
- Barzan is the only confirmed member.
