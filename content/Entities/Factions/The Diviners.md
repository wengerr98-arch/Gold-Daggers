# The Diviners

## Known Information

The Diviners are a Stygian organisation that conducts background checks for attendees of the MidWinter-Ball (see [[Stygian Gala Invitation]]). They employ divination magic focused not just on past history but also on future aspects of individuals, and charge an admission fee of up to 1,000 gold for ball registration. Whether they are a formal guild, a noble house, or an informal group is unknown.

## Session Appearances

- Session 098: Mentioned as conducting background checks and future divinations for MidWinter-Ball attendees; charge up to 1,000 gold for registration.

## Known Facts

- Operate in [[Stygia]], tied to the MidWinter-Ball / [[Stygian Gala Invitation]].
- Use future-focused divination magic (not solely past history).
- Charge up to 1,000 gold for background verification and registration.
