# The Tower

## Known Information

The Tower (also referred to as the Turm Gang) is a faction based in [[Aarum]], operating from a church-like structure with guarded entry, winding stairs, and graffiti-covered walls. The building is located at the end of Aarum's Marmorstrasse (the marble/prostitution street) and is distinguished as the only place in [[Aarum]] with living plants. It is named after the Tarot Card "The Tower." The faction is co-led by Nasri and her brother Dakun (who has a mechanical arm), with Nasri as the sole possessor of the recipe and production capacity for the faction's munitions, including a revolver rifle design intended for mass production. The Tower's primary stated goal is to blast open the cave ceiling and expand their territory, and they are in active conflict with the [[Whiterose]] faction over district space. The party used the Tower as a base during their time in [[Aarum]].

## Known Facts

- Physical headquarters: church-like tower building at the end of Aarum's Marmorstrasse.
- Named after the Tarot Card "The Tower."
- Distinguishing feature: only place in [[Aarum]] with living plants; people regularly enter and do not re-emerge.
- Co-led by Nasri and her brother Dakun, who has a mechanical arm.
- Goal: blast open the cave ceiling and expand territory into the wider region.
- Maintains a book collection including a [[Sonnenbibel]] and priest sermons.
- Nasri is the sole possessor of the recipe and production capacity for the Tower's munitions.
- The Tower was involved in both causing and rebuilding damage to a local shop.
- In active territorial conflict with [[Whiterose]], which wants to claim more space in the district.
- Agreed to take responsibility for the former sex workers from Madame Dimdelle's Pocket Dimension as part of the new district governance arrangement (Session 042).
- Nasri revealed weapon production plans including a revolver rifle design intended for mass production (Session 044).
- Used by the party as a base during their time in [[Aarum]]; Nasri operated a weapon-design partnership from the location.
- Associated with [[Whiterose]] as a known Aarum power structure.
