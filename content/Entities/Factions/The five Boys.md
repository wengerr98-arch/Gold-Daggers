# The five Boys

## Known Information

The five Boys (also known as the College-Boys) are a group of five young men from the university in [[Dalior]] who ended up imprisoned in the [[Oubliette]] because of an entity known as Big Brush. They are associated with the decapitation of a headless Mason Statue (a bronze statue found kopflos) and were identified as perpetrators in that incident.

## Session Appearances

- Session 005: Referred to as "College-Boys" (as perpetrators, "als Täter") in connection with the headless Mason Statue (bronze statue found decapitated). Confirmed that "College-Boys" = "The five Boys" — same group.

## Known Facts

- Five young men from the university in [[Dalior]].
- Alias: College-Boys.
- Imprisoned in the [[Oubliette]] because of Big Brush.
- Connected to the decapitation of a Mason Statue (bronze, found headless).
