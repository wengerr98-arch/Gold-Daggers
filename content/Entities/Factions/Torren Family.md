# Torren Family

## Known Information

The Torren Family is one of Aarum's major criminal-political power factions. Led by [[Randrus Torren]] ("Daddy"), the family controls the [[Torrenviertel]] district of [[Aarum]] — a commercially significant area bearing the Torren wappen (coat of arms) on every building. Their operations span smithing/foundry work, materials trade, enchantment, espionage, and island-wide political brokerage.

The family maintains a working alliance with the Gold Daggers from Session 042 onward, formalised through a shared governance arrangement negotiated at the Pocket Dimension dinner.

## Operations

### The Giesserei (Foundry)

The Giesserei (lit. "Foundry/Forge") is the Torren Family's front operation in [[Aarum]], functioning as a legitimate craft business while serving as a cover for their wider operations. Party members [[Barzan Aeducan]] and [[Leumas]] visited in Session 041 to collect custom-commissioned items (armour and BB's monocle). Access was gained by mentioning [[Grandir]]'s name, indicating Grandir serves as a referral contact or affiliated figure.

An anonymous Giesserei material supplier reportedly hired [[Volkam und Partner]] to pursue the Gold Daggers — as warned by Randrus Torren in Session 040.

**Known Giesserei facts:**
- Front operation in Aarum; functions as a legitimate smithing/foundry business
- Provides custom item commissions (armour, monocle, etc.)
- [[Grandir]]'s name serves as a referral or access credential
- Connected to an anonymous materials supplier who engaged [[Volkam und Partner]] against the Gold Daggers (Session 040)

### Torrenviertel

The [[Torrenviertel]] is a named market district in Aarum entirely under Torren control. The Torren wappen appears on every building. Contains the Torren Marktgasse (main commercial street) and the Giesserei. Functions as the family's commercial stronghold within the city.

### Nonna's Restaurant

[[Nonna]] is Torren's grandmother; her restaurant in Aarum serves as a private meeting venue. The party was hosted there in Session 057 for a dinner covering the disrupted wedding, trade, magical hotspots, and the wider political situation.
