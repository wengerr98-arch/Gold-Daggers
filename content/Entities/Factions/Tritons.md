# Tritons

## Known Information

The Tritons are an underwater civilisation based in [[Helykonos]] (Helikonos), a submerged city. Their leader is [[Shen]], though the party has learned that the Triton teachers and refugee population have been controlled via [[Dominate Person Stones]] for two or more years. The entire campaign arc spanning Sessions 044–046+ concerns the party infiltrating the Triton city, and the civilisation's internal political structure and conflicts remain ongoing plot elements.

## Session Appearances

- Session 045: Major faction context established. [[Shen]] confirmed as Triton leader; city confirmed as Helikonos. Party plans infiltration. [[Ronross]] is a Triton refugee teacher offering shelter. [[Dominate Person Stones]] have controlled their teachers. Robot/sand automaton guards patrol the trenches leading to the city. [[Eloryn]] has a compromised history with them — killed the last rebel leader approximately 1.5 years ago and betrayed [[Shen]].

## Known Facts

- Underwater civilisation based in [[Helykonos]] (Helikonos), a submerged city.
- Led by [[Shen]].
- Teachers and refugee population controlled via [[Dominate Person Stones]] for two or more years.
- Sand automaton/robot guards patrol the trenches leading to the city.
- [[Ronross]] is a Triton refugee teacher who offered shelter to the party.
- [[Eloryn]] killed the last Triton rebel leader ~1.5 years ago and betrayed [[Shen]].
