# Volkam & Partner

## Known Information

Volkam & Partner is an aggressive credit and debt-collection firm based in [[Aarum]], feared by market traders as enforcers who physically coerce debtors. The firm is owned by [[Fellbrün Volkam]] and represents a significant economic power in [[Aarum]]'s underworld and merchant scene. Note: "Volkram & Partner" (appearing in the Session 039 transcript) is a transcription error — the canonical spelling is Volkam.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 039]]: Referenced by market traders as aggressive creditors operating in Aarum. Feared and disliked. Owner identified as [[Fellbrün Volkam]].

## Known Facts

- Type: credit and debt-collection firm operating in [[Aarum]].
- Owner: [[Fellbrün Volkam]].
- Methods: physical enforcement ("verklopfen andere" — beat others) to collect debts.
- Reputation: feared and disliked by market traders in [[Aarum]].
