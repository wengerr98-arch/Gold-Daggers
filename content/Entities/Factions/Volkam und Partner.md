# Volkam und Partner

## Known Information

Volkam und Partner (also "Volkam & Partner") was a criminal organisation controlling a district of [[Aarum]], operating out of a brewery headquarters in the western quarter of the city — now known as [[Goldie Bräu]]. The organisation was headed by Felbrim Volkam (see [[Fellbrün Volkam]]), who was killed by the Gold Daggers during Session 040. An anonymous [[Torren Family|Giesserei]] material supplier reportedly hired Volkam & Partner to pursue the Gold Daggers, per a warning from Randrus Torren ("Daddy").

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 040 - Deals like Daggers|Session 040]]: Antagonist organisation; the party stormed their brewery headquarters and killed Felbrim Volkam. The golem ("Goldie") and brewery were seized by the party.

## Known Facts

- Type: criminal organisation controlling a district of [[Aarum]].
- Former headquarters: brewery in the western quarter of [[Aarum]], now [[Goldie Bräu]].
- Former leader: Felbrim Volkam ([[Fellbrün Volkam]]) — killed by the Gold Daggers in Session 040.
- Hired against the Gold Daggers by an anonymous [[Torren Family|Giesserei]] material supplier (per Randrus Torren's warning).
- Assets seized by the Gold Daggers: the golem ("Goldie") and the brewery.
