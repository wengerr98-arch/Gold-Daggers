# Whiterose

## Known Information

Whiterose (also "White Rose") is a criminal faction operating in [[Aarum]], run by or closely associated with [[Madame Dimdelle]]. It is one of the known power players in Aarum alongside [[The Tower]], engaged in active territorial conflict to claim more district space. Following [[Madame Dimdelle]]'s death (confirmed after Session 041) and the dismantling of her Pocket Dimension facility by the Gold Daggers in Session 042, the faction's leadership and territorial future are unresolved.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 039]]: Mentioned briefly as a known Aarum entity disliked by an unnamed contact ("Dudette doesn't like Whiterose, Tower whatever"). Linked to [[Madame Dimdelle]] per DM correction note.
- [[Session 041 - Deals like Daggers|Session 041]]: Referenced by Torren as a rival faction — White Rose wants to claim more district space, creating territorial conflict with the Tower. Torren describes younger, more aggressive thieves who steal from him as possibly associated with White Rose.
- [[Session 042]]: [[Madame Dimdelle]]'s Pocket Dimension facility was dismantled by the Gold Daggers; the future of Whiterose leadership and district territory became an open question following the faction dinner.

## Known Facts

- Run by or closely associated with [[Madame Dimdelle]] (per DM correction note, Session 039).
- In active territorial conflict with the Turm Gang ([[The Tower]]), wanting to claim more district space (Session 041).
- Has younger, more aggressive members or associates who steal from Torren's faction (Session 041).
- Leadership is now vacant following [[Madame Dimdelle]]'s death (Session 041/042).
