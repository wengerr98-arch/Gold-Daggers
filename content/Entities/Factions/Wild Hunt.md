# Wild Hunt

## Known Information

The Wild Hunt is a major supernatural faction based in or strongly associated with [[Skjall]], operating under the motto "Join, hide or die." It is intimately connected to Skjall's burial traditions — the corpse-boiling practice derives from older beliefs that the soul enters the Wild Hunt after death — and the [[Skjall]] monument must be fed with sacrifices (people or monsters) to keep it at bay. With approximately 90,000 members and still growing while all other Skjall populations shrink, the Wild Hunt exerts overwhelming pressure on [[Skjall]]'s survival. The faction has directly captured named party-adjacent characters: [[Sadam Salim]] was pulled through a wall opening in Session 064, and [[Eloryn's Pact with Chetzi|Eloryn]] flew into the Wild Hunt in Session 065 and was confirmed inside it via Barzan's Sending in Session 066.

## Known Facts

- Motto: "Join, hide or die" (Session 049 documents).
- Approximately 90,000 members and growing, while all other Skjall populations are shrinking (Session 049 documents).
- Campaign-specific [[Skjall]] institution — not a generic D&D reference.
- Connected to Skjall burial traditions — the corpse-boiling practice derives from older beliefs that the soul enters the Wild Hunt after death (Session 059).
- The [[Skjall]] monument must be fed with sacrifices (people or monsters) to keep the Wild Hunt at bay; publicly confirmed by [[Maradak Dreamkiller]] to all clan leaders in Session 065.
- [[Brentan]] is the only known survivor to have entered the Wild Hunt and returned (Session 061 archival texts).
- [[Sadam Salim]] was physically pulled into the Wild Hunt through a wall opening at the end of Session 064 — the first directly witnessed capture of a named party-adjacent character.
- Eloryn underwent a visible transformation (white-burning eyes, wings) and flew toward the Wild Hunt at the end of Session 065; confirmed inside by Barzan's Sending in Session 066.
- An Odin-like force in the far north sends the best warriors away in never-return rituals, apparently feeding or empowering the Wild Hunt.
- The [[Key to the Door in Skjall]] reportedly grants access to the door in [[Skjall]] that leads to or interacts with the Wild Hunt; held by the party since Session 049.
