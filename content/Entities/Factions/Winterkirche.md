# Winterkirche

## Known Information

The Winterkirche (Winter Church) is a religious organisation whose emblem was found on a recovered boat in [[Aarum]] and identified by party member [[BB]] in Session 041. The emblem is now held by the party as a story-significant item, creating a direct link between the organisation and the player characters. The Winterkirche is distinct from [[Sonnenkirche]] (Sun Church) and [[Sommerkirche]] (Summer Church); its beliefs, structure, and current regional presence are otherwise unknown.

## Session Appearances

- Session 041: [[BB]]'s emblem (recovered from a boat) is identified as belonging to the Winterkirche. The emblem is held by the party as a story-significant item.

## Known Facts

- Type: religious organisation.
- Symbol/emblem: recovered from a boat in [[Aarum]]; now held by the party.
- Identified by [[BB]] in Session 041.
- Distinct from [[Sonnenkirche]] and [[Sommerkirche]].
