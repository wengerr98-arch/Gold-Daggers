# Yuan-Ti

## Known Information

The Yuan-Ti are the descendants of the [[Sarrukh]] — the first humanoid species — and were named as such by Snix during the party's 6000-year time jump to [[Gilgamesh Workshop|Gilgamesh's Workshop]]. They represent a lore-significant species group in the campaign's deep history, comparable to how other ancient lineages such as the [[Tritons]] are tracked in the vault. Their connection to the Sarrukh positions them as an ancient bloodline with potential campaign relevance beyond standard lore.

## Session Appearances

- Session 088: Named by Snix as the descendants of the [[Sarrukh]] during the 6000-year past timeline encounter at Gilgamesh's Workshop.
