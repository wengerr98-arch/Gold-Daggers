## Known Information

Anubis is an ancient god whose divine tomb exists near [[Kasta]], reachable via \"divine gates\" — a site so lethal that no party has ever returned from attempting it. He predates the current calendar era and is tied to the Forgotten War and a vast pre-history that includes pyramid complexes, desert religious sites, and an ancient civilisation visible through a mountain gate. By Session 097, the party encountered Anubis directly at the infernal gate on the [[River Styx]] border: he is imprisoned by unknown forces and sought the party's help, naming three suspects — \"der willlose König\" (the willless king), \"der Soldat\" (the soldier), and \"der Rebell\" (the rebel).

## Session Appearances

- Session 079: Named by [[Sujo]] as the god whose divine tomb exists near [[Kasta]]; flagged as a legendary dungeon challenge reachable via "divine gates."
- Session 080: Central to the party's Legend Lore vision — the vision involves Anubis, titans, and the destruction or sealing of an ancient god; [[Barzan]] continues investigating via Kasta titan lore and [[Mahdi]]'s star chart.
- Session 081: The party discusses Anubis, his grave, and titan-related lore at the [[The Eladrin Library]]; Anubis-related knowledge is described as incomplete and fragmented; the pyramid lead may link to a second or third ruin.
- Session 082: The party follows Mahdi's desert route "toward Anubis"; they fall into a buried chamber (likely part of the Anubis pyramid complex) containing a golem and homunculi, and retrieve a scroll with four encoded phrase fragments referencing Ziz and a sealed chamber below.
- Session 083: The party follows the star-map scroll to [[Ha'roshon Mesugo]] (ancient desert ruin), performs a burning-and-washing ritual tied to Anubis-era religion, and defeats two guardians; the site is confirmed as an ancient religious complex.
- Session 086: Anubis-related lore appears in library books recovered from the Kasta eladrin library alongside archfeys, ley-lines, and Abraham threads.
- Session 087: The party's desert gate encounter — pyramid effects that alter age and form, a stone gate to a hidden mountain passage, and an ancient green civilisation beyond — is linked to the Anubis-era pre-calendar civilisation.
- Session 097: Encountered directly at the infernal gate ([[River Styx]] border). Anubis is imprisoned and seeks the party's help; [[Cerberus]] was his "last tether." Uses the Waage (scale) to judge the moral weight of Leumas and Barzan's hearts. Connection: Ephraim ("only sealed" Anubis).
