# Aria

## Known Information

Aria is the Goddess of Time and one of the original primordial deities. At some point she fractured into four aspects — an event connected to the Forgotten War and the unknown territories. She is a parent of [[Ophris]] alongside [[Zima]] (God of Justice). Her fractured state appears to be actively disrupting the cosmic cycle: the moon crisis in Session 067, the planar breach in Session 068, and Barzan's legend lore vision in Session 070 all point to Aria's ongoing fragmentation as a central cosmological thread.

## Session Appearances

- Session 042: Revealed via legend lore — Aria is the Goddess of Time who split into four aspects; named as parent of [[Ophris]] alongside [[Zima]].
- Session 051: Referenced under alias "Arya" in connection with entities encountered in the Waterplane; classification as entity vs. lore concept flagged for review.
- Session 063: Referenced as "Aira" by Barzan in discussion about the winter/summer goddess split and a disconnect in the planes; party's passage through the Veil raises new questions about Aria's role in planar structure.
- Session 067: Barzan's divine contact with [[Nyx]] during the moon crisis suggests both day and night powers are tied to Aria; overwhelming divine experience (stretched sensation, stars forming eyes, sense of being pulled apart) implies Aria's fractured aspects are active in the current cosmic crisis.
- Session 068: Referenced in the context of the planar breach at the Dreamhouse in [[Skjall]] — [[Eloryn]]'s vessel turns milky and unstable, associated with Aria's fragmentary state; day/night instability from Session 067 continues as backdrop.
- Session 070: Barzan's legend lore vision on the space elves includes a future scene of Arya being expelled, situating Aria's fate within the ancient elven cosmology and the red-walled elven city arc.
- Session 071: The [[Wintergöttin]] reacted to Barzan's appeal invoking Aria — her response (pinned with nails, apparently recognising the approach) suggests a possible memory or identity link between the Wintergöttin and Aria.
- Session 077: Referenced in the plane-collapse and Wildhunt/Titan/ancient-war backstory revisited by the party; Barzan's connection to the [[Wintergöttin]] through Felix's finger echoes the Aria–Wintergöttin identity question.
- Session 099: Referenced in the party's library research in [[Stygia]] on forgotten peoples, old gods, and planar history.
- Session 100: Referenced in [[Levistus]]'s briefing on planar corruption — Aria's fractured aspects and the corrupting presence affecting multiple planes remain an active background concern.
