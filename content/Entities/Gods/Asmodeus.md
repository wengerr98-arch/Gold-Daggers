# Asmodeus

## Known Information

Asmodeus is the King of Hell ("König vo de Höll") and ruler of [[Stygia]], the 5th Circle of Hell. He imprisoned [[Ysra]], a planar cartographer, for an unknown duration prior to Session 094, with her captivity apparently tied to some forgotten conflict or war. The party entered his domain in Session 098, observing that residents of Stygia bear branded sigils as markers of his ongoing control over the plane.

## Session Appearances

- Session 094: Referenced as the entity who imprisoned [[Ysra]] in the 5th Circle of Hell ([[Stygia]]) before her rescue by the party; party learns Asmodeus is the King of Hell.
- Session 098: The party travels through [[Stygia]], Asmodeus's domain; they navigate the city's rules and bureaucracy, observing residents with branded sigils marking his control.
- Session 102: Attended the Planar Ball in a poorly concealed disguise — described as very active but transparently disguised, and noted to have never been defeated. Approached directly by [[Odyss Trion]] (who shapeshifted into [[Leumas]] to challenge him to a duel). After [[Barzan]] used a Subtle Spell to revert Odyss's disguise, Asmodeus offered *Die Wette des Hadeses* — a board game wagering HP pools for souls. The Gold Daggers staked 650 HP and demanded all "Nothing"-infected persons be banished to the 5th plane; Asmodeus demanded immunity from being touched. After a dramatic three-round combat encounter (involving confusion effects, wild magic, a Jabberwalker, and a Dimension Door escape), the party won. Six infected persons vanished. Asmodeus dismissed the party with a "Nöchschti!" and invited them to play again some time.
