# Azatoth

## Known Information

Azatoth is the "blind idiot god," a cosmic deity who rules the Waterplane and the deep seas. He is the power source behind the Waterplane's chain of rule: [[R'lyeh|R'Lhey]] was Azatoth's first warlock and conquered the seas through this pact, with [[Shen]] implied as a successor in that chain. Azatoth's lore was revealed through the [[Book of Azatoth]] recovered in Session 052, confirming him as a genuine campaign entity rather than a generic Lovecraftian reference.

## Session Appearances

- Session 052: Named in the [[Book of Azatoth]] recovered from the crab sarcophagus; central deity of the Waterplane lore revealed this session.

## Known Facts

- Domain: the Waterplane and the deep seas.
- Described as "the blind idiot god" in the [[Book of Azatoth]].
- [[Yogsototh]] is referenced as a counterpart to Azatoth in the [[Book of Azatoth]].
- [[R'lyeh|R'Lhey]] was Azatoth's first warlock and conquered the seas through this pact.
- Warlock successor chain: R'Lhey → [[Shen]] (implied in the [[Book of Azatoth]]).
