## Known Information

Cithrul is the 11th primordial god — unhatched, representing space and gravity — whose existence was revealed to the party through [[Dr. Mason]] (Drak) casting Legend Lore in Session 042. Described as an egg that is growing stronger, the cracks in its ancient seal are slowly re-forming. [[Felix]] and nine companions sealed the Cithrul crack approximately 50 years ago, but Felix is now described as "tired" and "not playing," suggesting the seal may be weakening. [[Madame Dimdelle]] was a worshipper of Cithrul, and her recovered bible contained FC-marked notes potentially annotated by Dr. Mason using a DD/DM cipher.
