# Ereshkigal

## Known Information

Ereshkigal is an ancient deity encountered in campaign lore, paired with [[Nergal]] as his consort ("sini Frou"). She is associated with a shrine situated beside a lake near the same mountain site as Nergal's shrine, at an ancient settlement complex that predates the campaign calendar. Both she and Nergal appear to be pre-calendar deities tied to the desert gate complex and ancient Genasi/ley-line history.

## Session Appearances

- Session 086: Mentioned in library books found at Kasta; part of layered lore the party reads during the forced reading challenge, alongside Nergal and ancient Genasi/ley-line history.
- Session 087: Named in Barzan's legend-lore vision — shrine by a lake near the mountain site. Described as Nergal's consort. Both shrines situated at an ancient settlement predating the campaign calendar.

## Known Facts

- Consort/wife of [[Nergal]]
- Has a shrine beside a lake near the ancient mountain settlement
- Both her shrine and Nergal's shrine are located at a pre-calendar desert gate complex
- Associated with ancient Genasi/ley-line history
- Referenced in books in the Kasta library
