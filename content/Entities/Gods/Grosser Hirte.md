# Grosser Hirte

## Known Information

The Grosser Hirte (Great Shepherd) is an ascended deity ("aufgestiegene Gottheit") associated with forests, woodland creatures, and the [[Deep Wilds]]. He presides over a sheltered, idyllic grove domain and is linked to Lorilla's fate — she saw herself connected to the forest and the Great Shepherd in a future vision. The party located him in Session 091, and he revealed that a portal is split between a [[Demogorgon]] side and a [[Grave King]] side, though he appeared visibly stressed by the situation. In Session 092, Barzan connected the Great Hirt / Faun figure to [[Kaldera]] and old-war history, suggesting a potential role in Barzan's family origins.

## Session Appearances

- Session 008: Referenced via a painting in Mason Mansion. Described as an "aufgestiegene Gottheit" (ascended deity).
- Session 032: Named in the entity list; referenced in the monastery context (alias form "Grosser Hirt" used).
- Session 044: Referenced under the alias "Faun"; appears in the context of Leumas's research into changelings and Fay identity in the library, territory adjacent to the Great Shepherd's domain.
- Session 089: Appears as "the great shepherd" in Lorilla's future vision — presiding over a forest gathering scene with woodland creatures including [[Jack the Rabbit]]; Lorilla is shown linked to the forest, the great shepherd, and the demon-prince conflict.
- Session 090: Referenced as "Hirt" — Lorilla reveals transformation abilities and the group continues searching for the Hirt, who remains unfound at session end.
- Session 091: Found in a sheltered, idyllic grove. Reveals the portal is split between the [[Demogorgon]] side and the [[Grave King]] side. Visibly stressed. Also encountered alongside Windnoise (an Air Genasi seeking her heart/flower).
- Session 092: Barzan connects the Great Hirt / Faun figure to [[Kaldera]] and old-war history; discussed as a potentially key figure in Barzan's family origins and the broader cosmic backstory.
