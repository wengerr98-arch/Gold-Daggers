# Herbstgöttin Frelka

## Known Information

Herbstgöttin Frelka (Autumn Goddess Frelka) is a deity whose monastery, [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]], is located in the mountains near Ophris. [[Leumas]] holds a devotional relationship with her as a divine devotee, and [[Barzan Aeducan]] also worships her, wearing her religious emblem. She is capable of active divine intervention — she responded to Barzan's Divine Intervention in Session 042 — but gave no sign when Leumas meditated for her in Session 041, leaving the nature and terms of her relationship with Leumas ambiguous. She appeared in Barzan's spirit-realm trial in Session 045 alongside [[Sommergöttin]] and [[Succubus]], and was reportedly unhappy when Barzan chose the Sommergöttin as patron.

## Session Appearances

- Session 028: Indirectly referenced — the party is staying at her monastery (Kloster der Herbstgöttin) during this session.
- Session 029: Leumas's divine sense detects the Autumn Goddess present during his storytelling moment at the kobold village burial. The party's stated destination is the Kloster der Herbstgöttin (monastery associated with her).
- Session 031: Referenced in an open thread — Leumas is expected to receive guidance from Frelka; her monastery is the site of the harvest feast and the trial that the party undergoes this session.
- Session 033: Barzan defends wearing her religious emblem during interrogation by Don Hor; Leumas argues that Barzan needs the emblem to continue spreading the goddess's word; the emblem becomes an explicit point of tension during the monastery proceedings.
- Session 041: Leumas meditates and tries to receive a sign from Frelka (Frejlka) but receives none; her silence becomes a source of unresolved tension as Barzan separately contacts the Summer Goddess instead.
- Session 042: Responds to Barzan's Divine Intervention and fixes the problem with the ceiling and shafts in the Pocket Dimension; her active intervention confirms that she remains reachable by Barzan in Session 042 after her silence in Session 041.
- Session 045: Appears in Barzan's spirit-realm trial alongside Sommergöttin and Succubus, presenting Barzan with a choice of divine patron. Barzan chooses the Sommergöttin; Frelka is reportedly unhappy with this choice.

## Known Facts

- Title: Herbstgöttin (Autumn Goddess); confirmed identical to the deity referred to as "the Autumn Goddess" in earlier sessions (same aliases: Frelka, Frejlka, Autumn Goddess).
- Associated location: [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]] — a monastery in the mountains near Ophris; site of the harvest feast and monastery trial (Session 033).
- [[Leumas]] is her divine devotee (paladin/cleric connection implied by his divine sense).
- [[Barzan Aeducan]] wears her religious emblem; the emblem's legitimacy was debated by Leumas and Don Hor in Session 033.
- Responded to Barzan's Divine Intervention in Session 042, fixing a structural problem with the Pocket Dimension's ceiling and shafts.
- Did not respond to Leumas's meditation in Session 041.
- Appeared in Barzan's spirit-realm trial in Session 045 alongside Sommergöttin and Succubus; was not chosen as Barzan's patron and is reportedly unhappy with Barzan's choice.
