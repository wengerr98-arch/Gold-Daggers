## Known Information

Kaldera is an early earth goddess (Göttin der Erdbeben — goddess of earthquakes) and mother of natural catastrophes. According to lore shared by [[Mahdi]] the market bookseller, she may have been the first Earth Genasi ancestral figure and is connected to the Forgotten War cosmology. In Session 092, Barzan drew a connection between Kaldera and the [[Grosser Hirte]] / Faun figure, suggesting she plays a role in Barzan's family origins and the broader cosmic backstory.
