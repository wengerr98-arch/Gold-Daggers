## Known Information

Kurthul Mak is a classical kobold deity confirmed in Session 091 via a religion check (result 20: "esch en klassischi Kobold Gottheite. To Be Safed!"). He is worshipped by Snigli (Snix's sibling, trapped in Pandemonium) and is known by the epithets "horn sorcerer" and "gnome smasher." According to campaign lore, Kurthul Mak was unjustly banished to Avernus.
