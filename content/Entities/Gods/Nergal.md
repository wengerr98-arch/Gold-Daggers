## Known Information

Nergal is an ancient deity whose shrine ("Dankesstätte Nergal") was located near a mountain lake settlement that predates the campaign calendar system — placing him among the oldest known divine figures in the world. He was paired with [[Ereshkigal]], described in legend-lore as "sini Frou" (his wife), suggesting a divine partnership between the two. His name surfaced first in lore texts found in the Kasta library and was then confirmed via Barzan's legend-lore vision in Session 087.

## Session Appearances

- Session 086: Mentioned in the Kasta library books as part of the layered lore the party reads during the forced reading challenge, alongside [[Ereshkigal]] and ancient Genasi/ley-line history.
- Session 087: Named in Barzan's legend-lore vision — shrine at a mountain site associated with an ancient settlement, pre-dating the calendar. Paired with [[Ereshkigal]].

## Known Facts

- His shrine ("Dankesstätte Nergal") is located at or near a mountain gate site.
- The settlement near his shrine predates the first world-breaking and the campaign calendar.
- Paired with [[Ereshkigal]], described as "sini Frou" (his wife) in the legend-lore vision.
