## Known Information

Nyx is one of the original ten primordial deities, named in Barzan's Legend Lore cosmological vision in Session 042. She was found imprisoned in a dedicated vault room in the Zentralbank (Hades sub-level), encountered as a shadowy, star-like goddess figure with a connection to the Ophris network. The party freed Nyx from her containment, shifting her to freed/allied status. In Session 067, Barzan's message to Nyx during the moon crisis in Skjall produced a divine response connecting the day and night powers to [[Arya]], suggesting Nyx and Arya are both involved in the moon bleeding and the escalating crisis.

## Session Appearances

- Session 042: Named as one of the original ten primordial deities in Barzan's Legend Lore vision (cosmological revelation about the ten primordials and the 11th unhatched god Cithrul).
- Session 049: Found imprisoned in a dedicated vault room in the Zentralbank (Hades sub-level); encountered by the party as a shadowy, star-like goddess figure connected to the Ophris network. The party freed Nyx from her containment; she shifts to freed/allied status after the session.
- Session 067: Barzan sends a message to Nyx during the moon crisis in Skjall; the divine response connects the day and night powers to Arya, suggesting Nyx and Arya are both involved in the moon bleeding and the escalating crisis.
