## Known Information

Ophris is a god who sacrificed himself, and the country, city, and mountain range of [[Ophris]] are all named in his honour. He is the child of [[Aria]] (goddess of time) and [[Zima]] (god of justice), born of their divine union in the primordial cosmological structure. In his current state he manifests as a child-like figure connected by hundreds of threads to the Material Plane, disconnected from both the living and the dead worlds — a consequence of his sacrifice. Barzan encountered him directly in the Astral Sea city in Session 090.

**Note:** Do not confuse with the city of Ophris (see [[Places/Ophris]]) or the Ophris Faction (see [[Entities/Factions/Ophris Faction]]) — both are named after this deity.

## Session Appearances

- Session 042: Named in Barzan's Legend Lore vision as the child of Aria (goddess of time) and Zima (god of justice); the Cithrul bible deepens the understanding of Ophris's divine lineage and role in the primordial structure.
- Session 063: Appears as a child-like figure in the afterlife throne room, connected by hundreds of threads to the Material Plane. Barzan calls out "Zima!" when confronting the Ophris-entity. DM confirmation: Ophris was a god who sacrificed himself.
- Session 068: Referenced in the context of the Skjall planar breach crisis; the divine fractured-state of the Ophris entity parallels the destabilisation events.
- Session 090: Barzan meets Ophris directly in the Astral Sea city; the nature of Ophris's constraints and the Astral Sea / Material Plane mechanics become more explicit through direct confrontation alongside [[Zima]].
