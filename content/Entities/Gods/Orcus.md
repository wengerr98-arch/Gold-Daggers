# Orcus

## Known Information

Orcus, the Daemon Prince of Undeath, is a campaign-relevant deity who appears in the backstory of [[Milly]] as her former patron. BB discovered notes about Orcus while investigating Silk's room in Session 077, establishing Orcus as a meaningful element of Milly's character arc and history within the campaign.

## Session Appearances

- Session 077: Referenced in Milly's notes found by BB in Silk's room — Orcus is identified as Milly's ex-patron.

## Known Facts

- Title: Daemon Prince of Undeath.
- Former patron of [[Milly]].
- Discovered via Milly's notes found by BB in Silk's room (Session 077).
