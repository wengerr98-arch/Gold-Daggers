## Known Information

The Sommergöttin (Summer Goddess) is a divine patron figure who chose Barzan as her worshipper during a near-death spiritual trial in Session 045, when he selected her over [[Herbstgöttin Frelka]] and the Succubus. She has responded to Barzan's prayers and divine interventions multiple times with tangible, powerful effects — including granting a full party long rest mid-session and expanding a shared magical refuge room. Her church is an established presence inside [[Skjall]], and she is associated with a Garden of Eden where apples connected to the cosmic force Silence were discovered by BB in Session 092.

## Session Appearances

- Session 041: Barzan sends a message to the Summer Goddess; she appears as a giant drunken face and responds casually rather than reverently.
- Session 045: Central figure in Barzan's near-death spiritual trial. Barzan chose the Sommergöttin over [[Herbstgöttin Frelka]] and the Succubus as his divine patron. She appears in his dreams alongside [[Lady Ty]]. She stated she does not remember "Arya" when asked by Barzan — a sensitive topic noted by [[Lady Ty]]. Barzan temporarily cannot cast spells following the trial; [[Herbstgöttin Frelka]] is reportedly unhappy with Barzan's choice.
- Session 054: Barzan prays for divine intervention aboard the Naughty-Boy; the Sommergöttin appears, expands the magical room linking it to Barzan's origin and combining stone/cold energy with fiery summer energy. Grants Barzan Word of Recall and Cure Wounds at long range.
- Session 058: Her church is present as an established sub-location inside [[Skjall]], alongside the Winterkirche.
- Session 062: Barzan calls on Divine Intervention; the Sommergöttin grants the entire party a full long rest immediately after the fight against The Mother.
- Session 063: Referenced in Barzan's theological discussion connecting her domain to Aira and the winter/summer faith split; Barzan reflects on the broader absence or disconnect in the planes during the afterlife meditation sequence.
- Session 067: Referenced in the deity list during the moon crisis and earthquake events in Skjall; linked to Barzan's experience of overwhelming divine contact as he receives Nyx's response connecting day and night powers to Arya.
- Session 077: Referenced alongside the [[Wintergöttin]] as opposing seasonal-divine influences shaping the planar crisis.
- Session 092: BB visits the Garden of Eden (Garten Eden) and meets the Sommergöttin there; BB retrieves apples from the garden. Silence is subsequently found hidden inside the apples.

## Known Facts

- Barzan's divine patron as of Session 045 (chosen over [[Herbstgöttin Frelka]] and the Succubus).
- Responded casually to Barzan's message in Session 041, appearing as a giant drunken face — not a solemn divine presence.
- Granted Barzan Word of Recall and Cure Wounds at long range (Session 054); also expanded the party's shared magical refuge room.
- Granted the entire party a full long rest via Divine Intervention after the fight against The Mother (Session 062).
- Her church is an established location within [[Skjall]], alongside the Winterkirche.
- Does not remember "Arya" — a topic [[Lady Ty]] signalled Barzan to avoid.
- Associated with the Garden of Eden (Garten Eden); Silence was found hidden inside apples retrieved there (Session 092).
- Appears alongside [[Wintergöttin]] as opposing seasonal-divine forces in the planar crisis narrative.
