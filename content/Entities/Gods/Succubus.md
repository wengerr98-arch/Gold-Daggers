## Known Information

The Succubus is a dark-red female divine figure classified by the DM as a patron and a god — not a generic NPC. She was observed by Barzan during his scrying of [[Yorni]] in Session 036, whispering in Yorni's ear in his bureau, suggesting she serves as his patron or divine adviser. She was also one of the divine patrons Barzan was offered during his near-death spiritual trial in Session 045, before he chose the [[Sommergöttin]] instead. In Session 089, she is referenced in connection with [[Snix]] being socially influenced during the fight against Enoch at the Gilgamesh Workshop.

## Session Appearances

- Session 036: Seen by Barzan during his scrying of [[Yorni]] — a dark-red female succubus figure whispering in Yorni's ear in his bureau. Likely his patron or adviser.
- Session 045: Referenced as one of the divine patron options presented to Barzan during his near-death spiritual trial; Barzan chose the [[Sommergöttin]] instead.
- Session 089: Referenced as "Sukkubus"; [[Snix]] is affected by the succubus/Yazi thread during the fight against Enoch in the [[Gilgamesh Workshop]], becoming socially influenced during combat.
