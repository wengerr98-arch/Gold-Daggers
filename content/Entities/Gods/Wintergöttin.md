## Known Information

The Wintergöttin (also called Wintergott; aliases: SnowQueen) is a deity of winter worshipped in and around [[Skjall]], with a dedicated church — the [[Winterkirche]] — located inside the city. She is a powerful and dangerous divine presence: by Session 065 an unnatural snowstorm had descended on Skjall, and the party traveled into Limbo to attempt appeasement with a gulasch offering, though combat still broke out. As of Session 071 she was found pinned with nails through her arms and hands in the shattered Minotaur quarter, and Barzan's appeal to Aria provoked a visible reaction from her, suggesting a possible memory or identity link between the Wintergöttin and Aria.

## Known Facts

- Has a dedicated church, the [[Winterkirche]], inside [[Skjall]], connected via a secret passage to the crypts (Session 058).
- Worshipped by at least [[Sarmulin]], a dwarf from Skjall (Session 039).
- Has a named minion/avatar: SnowPeter, encountered in combat and banished by Barzan in Limbo (Session 066).
- As of Session 068, confirmed by the king to be hostile, not approachable, and already wounded; located in the minotaur quarter of Skjall.
- As of Session 071, found pinned with nails through her arms and hands in the shattered Minotaur quarter; Barzan's appeal to Aria provoked a reaction suggesting a possible memory or identity link with Aria.
- As of Session 077, Barzan is connected to the Wintergöttin through contact with Felix's finger; nature and implications unresolved.
- Canonical name form (feminine "Wintergöttin" vs masculine "Wintergott") unconfirmed — both recorded as aliases pending DM review.
