# Yog Sototh

## Known Information

Yog Sototh is a cosmic/divine entity named in Barzan's Legend Lore result in Session 051, connected to the fish/Titan balance conflict in the Waterplane. The balance mechanic implied is that an unnamed fish serves as the counterweight to the Titan in the Waterplane, and releasing that fish could restore balance — similar to how "Arya" might be restored — with Yog Sototh named in this cosmological context. Whether this is a campaign-original entity or a campaign-specific use of the Lovecraftian name Yog-Sothoth has not been confirmed by the DM.

## Session Appearances

- Session 051: Referenced in Barzan's Legend Lore result in the context of the Waterplane cosmological balance and the Titan conflict.

## Known Facts

- Named in Barzan's Legend Lore result (Session 051) in connection with the Waterplane, the Titan, and a fish-based balance mechanic.
- Aliases: Yog-Sothoth (standard D&D/Lovecraftian spelling).
