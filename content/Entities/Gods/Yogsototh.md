# Yogsototh

## Known Information

Yogsototh is a campaign deity referenced in the [[Book of Azatoth]] as a counterpart to [[Azatoth]], forming part of the cosmological lore of the Waterplane. The campaign uses "Yogsototh" as the primary spelling, distinct from the standard D&D/Lovecraftian "Yog-Sothoth." A separate stub ([[Yog Sototh]]) was generated from Session 051; these may be the same entity and should be confirmed by the DM before either stub is promoted to canon.
