# Zima

## Known Information

Zima is the God of Justice and one of the original primordial deities, parent of [[Ophris]] (the divine child) alongside [[Aria]] (Goddess of Time). The party confirmed his name in Session 042's Legend Lore vision; "Seema" is a misread of his name per Correction Memory. Zima is an active cosmological force: he made a deal with three Arch-Feys to secure safe passage for Medicine and Ophris, he caused Wanda's staff to vanish as a direct act of judgment, and the party met him personally in the Astral Sea city in Session 090. His role as God of Justice is increasingly entangled with the "expanding nothingness" thread threatening the campaign world.

- Session 042: Named multiple times in Legend Lore vision and cosmological discussion; Ophris described as "Kind von Aria und Zima."
- Session 054: Referenced indirectly — the party recalls that Zima made a deal with three Arch-Feys to gain a safe pass for Medicine and Ophris, situating Zima as an active force in the broader cosmological negotiations.

- Session 074: Named in the sidecar during the Skjall exploration session (BB/Leumas focus); referenced in the ongoing cosmological background of the Skjall crisis.
- Session 077: Wanda (Queen of Wands) explicitly names Zima as responsible for making her staff vanish; reveals a direct conflict or act of judgment between Zima (God of Justice) and Wanda. Recovering the staff becomes an active party side-quest.

- Session 099: Referenced in the context of the party's library research in Stygia on forgotten peoples, old gods, and planar history — Zima's role as God of Justice and parent of Ophris places him within the research threads the party pursues in the Stygian library.
