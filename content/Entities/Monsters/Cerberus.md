## Known Information

Cerberus is a three-headed dog guardian bound to the infernal gate near the [[River Styx]], standing at the border between the material plane and the afterlife realm. The party encountered Cerberus as the central boss encounter in Session 097, and by defeating the creature they severed what was described as [[Anubis]]'s "last tether."

## Session Appearances

- Session 097: Central encounter — guards the infernal gate (das Tor); legendary actions boss fight; party severs Anubis's last tether via the Cerberus combat.
- Session 098: Referenced in aftermath — the party carries a holy horn and hair token from Cerberus as material components following their S097 victory; items noted in inventory as they travel through [[Stygia]].

## Known Facts
