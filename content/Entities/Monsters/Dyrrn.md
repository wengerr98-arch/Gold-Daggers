# Dyrrn

## Known Information

Dyrrn is a monster encountered at [[The Monument (Seelenschredder)|The Monument]] during a major battle in Session 069. Visually perceived as a monkey-shaped figure ("Affefiguuuuur") within the undead army, Dyrrn acted as the named combat antagonist across all 8 rounds of the fight and could not be permanently destroyed — banished repeatedly but never killed outright.

## Session Appearances

- Session 069: Named combat antagonist at The Monument (Seelenschredder). Present across all 8 rounds; banished repeatedly but not destroyed. Uses legendary actions, grapple, and gravestones as weapons. Spotted by Snix as a monkey-shaped figure within the undead army.

## Known Facts

- Monster classed as non-NPC (DM correction, Session 069).
- Visual appearance: monkey-shaped figure seen within an undead army at The Monument.
- Uses legendary actions, grapple attacks, and gravestones as weapons.
- Was banished multiple times during the Session 069 fight but not destroyed.
