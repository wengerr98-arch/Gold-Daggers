# Ed

## Known Information

Ed is a beholder-type guardian monster stationed at the top of the Library tower in [[Kasta]], behind a DC 30 steel door. He acts as a paired gatekeeper alongside [[Og]], and the two confronted [[Snix]] when her bluff was exposed at the end of Session 085, escalating into full combat the following session.

## Session Appearances

- Session 085: Named guardian at top of Library tower, Kasta. Paired with [[Og]]. Confronted [[Snix]] who was bluffing; session ends with bluff exposed.
- Session 086: Escalated into full combat against the party in the Kasta library tower; defeated alongside [[Og]] during the fight.

## Known Facts

- Beholder-type guardian monster (DM confirmed); specific variant unknown.
- Stationed at the top of the Kasta library tower behind a DC 30 steel door.
- Paired with [[Og]]; acts in concert with Og as library gatekeepers.
- Defeated by the party in combat after [[Snix]]'s bluff was exposed (Session 086).
