# Golden Lady

## Known Information

The Golden Lady is a golden construct (golem-type monster) encountered in the [[Gilgamesh Workshop]] during the fight against [[Enoch]] in Session 089. She intervened in Round 3 to seize the [[Bands of Imprisonment]] ("Statue: Hot gold lady takes the armbänders") and was killed by [[BB]] in Round 4.

## Session Appearances

- Session 089: Encountered in the Gilgamesh Workshop fight against [[Enoch]]. Seized the [[Bands of Imprisonment]] in Round 3, then killed by [[BB]] in Round 4.
