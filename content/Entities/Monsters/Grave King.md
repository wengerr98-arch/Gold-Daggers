# Grave King

## Known Information

The Grave King — also known as Skelettmann — is a powerful and deeply unsettling entity first revealed in Session 060 at the Wildhunt gate. His appearance is profoundly wrong: a body assembled from different ethnicities with the wrong number of heads and limbs, rolling rather than walking, wearing a gladiator mask and a red cloak. He radiates an aura that distorts the world around him and is capable of forcing Wisdom Saving Throws on observers. He is distinct from "der König" (Maradak Dreamkiller / the Torr), who fought at the Thronsaal battle.

## Session Appearances

- Session 060: Revealed at the Wildhunt gate; dragged a corpse toward the gate, then closed the northern gate; forced a Wisdom Saving Throw on Barzan.
