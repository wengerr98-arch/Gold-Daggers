# Luchs

## Known Information

Luchs (German for lynx) is a giant lynx-like creature encountered and fought by the party in Session 068. It is no ordinary animal: it possesses legendary actions and resistances, and has fingers in place of claws, marking it as a powerful mythological or cosmic entity. According to the DM, Luchs is the eternal opponent of [[Yum Ka'ax]] (the Fox), suggesting the two represent opposing mythological forces — Fox versus Lynx.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 068 - Breach|Session 068]]: Encountered as a giant lynx with legendary resistances and finger-like claws; described as "horrible." Fought by the party and apparently defeated.

## Known Facts
