# Moloch

## Known Information

Moloch is an Archdevil encountered in Nessus, the ninth and deepest layer of the Nine Hells. The party was pulled through a portal to Nessus in Session 093 and faced him in combat, defeating him with the assistance of four lesser demons and Leumas's force cage. His signature ability is Breath of Despair, and he commands lesser demons including a Pit Fiend.

## Session Appearances
