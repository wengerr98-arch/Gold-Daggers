# Og

## Known Information

Og is a beholder-type monster that served as a guardian at the top of the Library tower in Kasta, paired with [[Ed]]. The two were stationed behind a steel door requiring a DC 30 lock to open. In Session 085, they confronted [[Snix]] and exposed his bluff; in Session 086 the standoff escalated into full combat and both were defeated by the party. See also: [[Ed]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 085: Named guardian at the top of the Kasta library tower, behind a DC 30 steel door; paired with [[Ed]]; confronted [[Snix]] and exposed his bluff.
- Session 086: Escalated into full combat against the party; defeated alongside [[Ed]].
