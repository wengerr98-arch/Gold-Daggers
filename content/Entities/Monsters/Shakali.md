## Known Information

Shakali are a creature type or category of guardian encountered at the pyramid complex near the desert gate. Multiple Shakali appear during combat, emerging from the pyramids themselves, suggesting they are either spawned by or native to those structures. They engage the party with "stab'n'shoot" tactics and specifically target [[Lorilla]], appearing alongside or preceding the multi-eyed Sphinx guardian at the site.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 087]]: Combat encounter at the pyramid complex near the desert gate. Multiple Shakali emerge from the pyramids and attack the party, particularly [[Lorilla]].

## Known Facts
