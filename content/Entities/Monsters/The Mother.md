## Known Information

The Mother is a corrupted flesh-creature and major boss encountered in the [[Thronsaal|Skjall throne room]], described as a large entity with spider legs, tentacles, and the ability to launch facehugger-like parasites that infest party members. BB polymorphed her into a turtle and contained her in an obsidian tube (Obsidian Röhre), after which she was presented to the [[Königin|König]] (Thautok) on a cart. The King reacted strongly upon seeing The Mother and hearing her name spoken, suggesting a deep personal or familial connection. Immediately after the fight, a red-haired Goliath woman appeared in tears.
