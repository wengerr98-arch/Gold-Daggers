## Known Information

The Nightmare Overseer is the named boss entity of Session 064, which took its name as the session title. It is a controlling intelligence associated with the [[The Silent Dance Tap Room|Silent Dance Tap Room]] dream-house construct, manipulating illusion and dream space and sending phantom arms to grab victims. Upon defeat its head explodes. A worm-like entity subsequently attempts to re-enter [[Snix]], suggesting a parasite or corrupting spirit rather than a standalone creature. The relationship with the barkeeper-like statue nicknamed "Lottie" is ambiguous — the Overseer may use the statue as an anchor/vessel, or they may be distinct; the statue crumbles at session's end and "Applaus ganz fiin" is heard, suggesting a benevolent element was freed by the Overseer's defeat.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 064 - Nightmare Overseer|Session 064]]: Session-title-level boss fight at the [[The Silent Dance Tap Room|Silent Dance Tap Room]]. Defeated in combat ("De Kopf explodiert"). Worm-like entity attempts to enter [[Snix]] after defeat. Both [[Eloryn]] and [[Snix]] emerge with corrupted eyes.
