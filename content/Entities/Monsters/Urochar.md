# Urochar

## Known Information

The Urochar is a named combat creature encountered during the party's time-jump to the 6,000-year-past setting at [[Gilgamesh Workshop]]. It uses grapple and swallow mechanics, and a natural 20 triggers a restraint effect. Notably, it spat out BB mid-combat, which revealed her to be a changeling.

## Session Appearances

- Session 088: Combat encounter in [[Gilgamesh Workshop]] during the past-timeline sequence; grappled and swallowed BB, whose changeling nature was revealed when the Urochar spat her out.

## Known Facts

- Named combat creature; encountered in the 6,000-year-past timeline.
