# Aaramit

## Known Information

Aaramit is a local baker in [[Tulip]] who invented "Silser Brötli," a local bread product. [[Barzan Aeducan]] and [[Leumas]] each invested 100 GP in his venture during Session 038, establishing an ongoing financial relationship between Aaramit and the party.

## Session Appearances

- Session 038: Named baker in Tulip; invents Silser Brötli; receives investment of 100 GP each from Barzan and Leumas.

## Known Facts

- Occupation: Baker in [[Tulip]].
- Creator of "Silser Brötli" (a local bread product).
- Received 100 GP investment from [[Barzan Aeducan]] and 100 GP from [[Leumas]] in Session 038.
