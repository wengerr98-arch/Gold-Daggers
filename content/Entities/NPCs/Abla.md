# Abla

![[Abla.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Abla is a small kobold girl living in [[Kobolddorf]], notable as the only magic user in the village — a witch and sorcerer with divine-soul gifts. She has a connection to [[BB's Tarot Cards|The Cards]], BB's tarot set, and BB left her a pouch of magic components so she could grow stronger. In Session 054, Abla blessed BB and revealed a Wheel of Fortune card from the deck, which granted BB the Remove Curse effect when used.

## Session Appearances

- Session 028: Present in the kobold settlement during the party's stay; listed among entities encountered around the kobold village and the Kloster area.
- Session 029: Present during the party's extended stay at the kobold settlement; consulted by BB and Snix after the cave events, and present at the burial ritual for over 25 kobolds and slain wild animals.
- Session 054: Speaks with BB and blesses her; reveals a Wheel of Fortune card. BB later uses the card and receives Remove Curse from it. Abla demonstrates her divine-soul-sorcerer gifts in this interaction.
