# Abrahim (Ephraim)

## Known Information

Ephraim — also known as Abrahim, Saul, Abraham, Isaak, Isaac, and various spelling variants — is one of [[The Eladrin Library|The Seven Wizards of Kasta]], specialising in Abjuration magic, and is believed to be a founder-figure of Kasta who has operated under multiple false identities over at least 400 years. He holds the Gnolia Vault Book (also called the Nolia Vault Book), a volume from the Nolia/Gnolia historical archive. His Zentralbank vault room (labelled "Effraim") contained philosophical notes, scrolls about the forgotten war, and academic material about Kasta. His Sending reply under the "Isaac/Abraham" identity surfaced the phrase "Ha'roshon Mesugo," suggesting he holds hidden knowledge about the desert obelisk site and the cycle of ritual, settlement, and decline. He is connected to the ancient ruins of [[Ha'roshon Mesugo]] and to the layered lore surrounding ley-lines, wizard soul-transfer, and Kasta's hidden past. Whether he is aligned with the party or with the god of death ([[Anubis]]) remains an open and pressing question.
