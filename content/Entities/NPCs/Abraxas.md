## Known Information

Abraxas (also known as Abrax) is a mage operating out of [[The Tower]] quarter in [[Aarum]], locally known as "the crazy wizard in Aarum." He possesses detailed knowledge of moon phases and their connection to the periodic opening of Hell portals, and confirmed to the party that the golden apples have magical side effects on Lorilla. He has served as a recurring knowledge contact for mana and portal-related questions.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 093 - Lured|Session 093]]: Speaks with authority about moon phases and the Nine Hells drawing near, explaining why portals open periodically.
- [[Session 094 - Eine Frage der Richtigkeit|Session 094]]: Confirms to the party that the golden apples have side effects on Lorilla; described as uneasy but cooperative.
- [[Session 095 - Die vergessene Schwelle|Session 095]]: Party visits his shop in [[Aarum]] during downtime; they review newly acquired magic gear with him and continue using him as a local mana/portal knowledge contact.
