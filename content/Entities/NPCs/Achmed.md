# Achmed

## Known Information

Achmed is a ship captain and navigator who transported the [[Gold Daggers]] from [[Lerchen]] toward Helikonos. He speaks in dialect and has a distinct personality. He was present during a [[Tritons|Triton]] attack on the vessel and survived the session with no indication of death, making him a potential recurring contact for future sea voyages.
