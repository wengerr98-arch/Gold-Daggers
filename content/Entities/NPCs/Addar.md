# Addar

## Known Information

Addar is the Druckmeister (Printing Master) at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]], where he works on developing printing equipment and letter molds. [[Lionel]] serves as his assistant. He appears embedded in the monastery's daily operations and was present during the harvest feast and trial in Session 031.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 029]]: Named in session frontmatter and sidecar; appears in the monastery/Azkaban context.
- [[Session 031]]: Present at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]] during the harvest feast and trial; printing master role confirmed.
- [[Session 032]]: Confirmed as Druckmeister at the monastery; [[Lionel]] actively assists him in making letter molds and developing printing equipment.

## Known Facts

- Role: Druckmeister (Printing Master) at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]
- Assistant: [[Lionel]]
- Working on developing printing equipment and letter molds (Session 032)
- Present during the harvest feast and trial at the monastery (Session 031)
