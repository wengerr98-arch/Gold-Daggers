## Known Information

Addar is the Druckmeister (Printing Master) at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]], where he works on developing printing equipment and letter molds. [[Lionel]] serves as his assistant. He appears embedded in the monastery's daily operations and was present during the harvest feast and trial in Session 031.
