# Adryn

## Known Information

Adryn is a prostitute and informant encountered by [[Eloryn]] in Helikonos. She provided substantial intelligence about city conditions, human workers, escape routes, and confirmed that a rebel group still exists in the city. After [[Eloryn]] explained an escape route to her, she was considering using it herself.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 046]]: Named NPC; speaks directly to [[Eloryn]] in the nightlife district. Provides key plot intelligence about city conditions, human workers, and escape routes. Confirms the rebel group still exists. Considering using the escape route Eloryn described.
