# Agnes

![[agnes.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Agnes was an innocent 83-year-old human woman living in [[Dalior]] who was accidentally swept up in the party's escape from Murtada's assassination. She was killed by [[Barzan]] in a cellar to prevent the group's discovery — a morally significant act that haunted both BB and Barzan in subsequent sessions.

## Session Appearances

- Session 025: Bystander accidentally teleported with BB and Eloryn during the assassination escape; killed by Barzan in the cellar to cover the group's tracks. Story-significant death.
- Session 063: Referenced during BB's personal meditation sequence in the afterlife — BB processes grief tied to Agnes during the rune ritual that forces each party member to confront personal themes.
- Session 097: Referenced again when Barzan confesses to Anubis's scale (Waage) that he killed Agnes; the confession lightens Barzan's heart during the afterlife judgment sequence at the infernal gate.

## Known Facts

- Race: Human.
- Age: 83.
- Home: [[Dalior]].
- Killed by [[Barzan]] in a cellar during the assassination escape (Session 025) to prevent the group from being discovered.
- Her death is morally significant: BB processed grief over it in a meditation ritual (Session 063), and Barzan's confession about killing her served as the basis for the scale's judgment of his heart (Session 097).
