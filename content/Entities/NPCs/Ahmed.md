# Ahmed

## Known Information

Ahmed is a fisherman NPC who accompanied the party to the ocean floor in Session 051 with his crew. He reappears in Session 052 during the return toward the harbor, where he and three passengers were brought to safety by the group. His role is minor and it is unclear whether he recurs beyond these two sessions. "Achmed" in session text is the Swiss German dialect spelling of the same name.

## Session Appearances

- Session 051: Accompanied the party (along with his fishermen) to the ocean floor ("De Achmed und sini Fescher chöme ou zo eus abe"). Minor supporting role.
- Session 052: Accompanied the party toward the harbor following the ocean floor mission; brought to safety along with three passengers by the group.

## Known Facts

- Occupation: Fisherman.
- Alias: "Achmed" (Swiss German dialect spelling used in session text).
- Minor NPC; two confirmed session appearances.
