# Akaresh

![[akaresh.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Akaresh was a blue dragon who guarded the [[Vergessener Tempel]] under the control of [[Dr. Mason]], who repeatedly killed and resurrected him. The Gold Daggers ultimately defeated Akaresh atop the mountain, on the bridge before the memory temple. His spirit now lives within [[Koda]]'s [[Dragon Hammer]], which was forged from his horn and spine by the smith [[Glargrit]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 035: Akaresh's horn and spine are used to forge Koda's Dragon-Horn Hammer; his essence (soul) attempted to charm the party during the forging process.

## Known Facts

- Type: Blue dragon.
- Guarded the [[Vergessener Tempel]] under the control of [[Dr. Mason]].
- Repeatedly killed and resurrected by [[Dr. Mason]].
- Defeated by the Gold Daggers on top of the mountain, on the bridge in front of the memory temple.
- His spirit is bound within [[Koda]]'s [[Dragon Hammer]], forged from his horn and spine by [[Glargrit]].
