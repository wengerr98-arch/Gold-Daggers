# Akareth

## Known Information

Akareth is a dragon of extraordinary power described as close to godhood — a soul that cannot be destroyed by mortals. He communicates telepathically with [[Leumas]] and made a binding contract with [[Koda]] in Session 044 such that her arm acted independently in service to him, with Yorni's body promised as payment once the party kills him. Koda severed this contract in Session 045. Akareth is deeply interested in the party's Wishspell, its origin, and a massive entity larger than himself — likely a Leviathan or titan-fish — which he wants researched.

## Session Appearances

- Session 036: The hammer Koda wields self-identifies as "Akareth" — first indication that the weapon carries the dragon's name and possibly his essence. The hammer is described as sentient and angry ("angepisst").
- Session 044: Revealed as the entity behind a binding contract with Koda. Koda's arm acts on Akareth's behalf. Communicates telepathically with Leumas. Demands Yorni's body as payment. Aware of the party's Wishspell; inquires about who gave it to them.
- Session 045: Koda questions Akareth about Shen and learns Shen is described as very young and fish-like. Koda ends the arm contract with Akareth this session, severing the binding arrangement.
- Session 052: Referenced near Helikonos — Akareth mentioned Pandemonium and his history with Mason; the party learns the large fish/titan Akareth refers to is bigger than Akareth himself, and Akareth wants eyes (intel) for research on it.

## Known Facts

- Dragon; described as near-godlike in power — his soul cannot be destroyed by mortals.

- Has a history with [[Dr. Mason]] and references Pandemonium in that context.
- Wants intelligence on a massive entity (larger than himself) — possibly a Leviathan or titan-fish near Helikonos.
- Koda's hammer first self-identified as "Akareth" in Session 036.
