## Known Information

Akli is a famous kobold from [[Kobolddorf]] who earned wings after killing a dragon — a feat that made him a folk hero among his people. His act, however, created a lasting obligation for the kobold community, who have since undertaken an apology mission to a dragon on his behalf. Whether Akli is still living or is a historical figure remembered in legend remains unclear.

## Session Appearances

- Session 026: Referenced as a kobold folk hero. The kobolds are on a mission to search for and apologize to a dragon because Akli killed one long ago. This act is central to the settlement's current pilgrimage/apology quest.
