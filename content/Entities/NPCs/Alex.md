## Known Information

Alex is a woman who worked at the city office (Stadtamt) of [[Dalior]] and later took up a position in [[Helykonos]] at the gold exchange bureau (Umtauschamt für Gold). She survived the massacre in [[Helykonos]] by fortunate timing — she had returned to [[Dalior]] before it occurred.
