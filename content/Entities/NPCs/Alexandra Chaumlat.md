# Alexandra Chaumlat

## Known Information

Alexandra Chaumlat is a city official and clerk at the Stadtverwaltung (city administration) of [[Dalior]]. She serves as the party's point of contact for the [[Dr. Mason]] undead commission, and issued their access permit for the [[Zweite Platte]] to carry out the undead-clearing job.

## Session Appearances

- Session 007: Met at the Stadtverwaltung; manages the official commission for Dr. Mason's escaped undead. Provided access permit to the party for the zweite Platte.

## Known Facts

- **Affiliation:** Stadtverwaltung (Dalior city administration)
- **Role:** City clerk / official point of contact for the Dr. Mason undead commission
- **First name + last name confirmed:** Session 007
