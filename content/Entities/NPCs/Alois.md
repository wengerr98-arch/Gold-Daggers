# Alois

## Known Information

Alois is a named hydra kept as a pet by the [[Prinzessin|Queen]] of the [[Rosenpalast]]. She is housed in the castle stalls alongside giant geese (approximately 2–2.5m tall, wearing a red ribbon), and the party made use of those geese as cover during their infiltration of the wedding.

## Session Appearances

- Session 048: Named as the Queen's pet hydra; kept in the Rosenpalast stalls. The party used the geese in the same stalls as cover during the infiltration ("Die Hochzeit").

## Known Facts

- **Species:** Hydra
- **Owner:** [[Prinzessin|Queen]] of the [[Rosenpalast]]
- **Location:** Rosenpalast stalls
- **Co-inhabitants:** Giant geese (~2–2.5m tall, wearing a red ribbon)
- **Name confirmed:** Session 048 ("Alois, Name der Hydra")
