# Anubis's Jailers (The Three Suspects)

## Known Information

[[Anubis]] names three suspects responsible for his imprisonment, though he does not know which one is guilty. He asked the party: "esches de willelosi-köhnig, de soldat oder de rebell?" — calling on them to help determine who orchestrated his captivity. Their real names and identities remain unknown.

## Session Appearances

- Session 097: Named by Anubis as the three suspects responsible for his imprisonment; the last tether (Cerberus) was severed in this session.

## Known Facts

- **Suspect 1 — Der Willlose König (The Willless King):** A figure without will or agency; possibly a puppet or magically controlled entity
- **Suspect 2 — Der Soldat (The Soldier):** A military or warrior figure
- **Suspect 3 — Der Rebell (The Rebel):** A rebellious or revolutionary figure
- **Context:** [[Abrahim (Ephraim)|Ephraim]] is noted as having "sealed" the god of death (possibly Anubis), making him a related figure
