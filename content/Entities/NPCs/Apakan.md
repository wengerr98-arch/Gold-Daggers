# Apakan

## Known Information

Apakan was a former king of [[Skjall]], active approximately 500 years before the current campaign. He was named in Session 064 when the party asked about the former king, identified as a historical figure rather than a current NPC. He may be connected to the forgotten war lore and the black-star entries in the [[Logbook (Silent Dance Tap Room)]], though this has not been confirmed.

## Session Appearances

- Session 064: Referenced as the former king of [[Skjall]] (~500 years ago); mentioned alongside investigation of [[The Silent Dance Tap Room]] and its logbook.

## Known Facts

- Former king of [[Skjall]], approximately 500 years ago.
- Historical figure — not a current NPC.
- Mentioned in the context of the [[Logbook (Silent Dance Tap Room)]] investigation (Session 064).
