# Arachne

![[arachne.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Arachne is a large blue tarantula-type creature who operates as a tailor and seamstress in [[Dalior]], affiliated with the [[Diebesguilde]] (Thieves' Guild). She made the party their outfits and gifted [[Leumas]] an illusion-amplifying armband — which he was wearing the night he disappeared.

## Session Appearances

- Session 013: Made the party their outfits; gave [[Leumas]] an illusion-amplifying armband.
- Session 092: Referenced in the sidecar entities list as part of the party's Dalior-era connections during planning discussions.

## Known Facts

- **Race:** Spider (large blue tarantula)
- **Occupation:** Seamstress / Tailor
- **Location:** [[Dalior]]
- **Affiliation:** [[Diebesguilde]] (Thieves' Guild)
- **Notable:** Gave [[Leumas]] an illusion-amplifying armband (he wore it the night he disappeared)
