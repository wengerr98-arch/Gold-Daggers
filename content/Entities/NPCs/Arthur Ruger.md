# Arthur Ruger

## Known Information

Arthur Ruger, known as "der Babo" (the boss-of-bosses), was a powerful antagonist revealed in Session 047. [[Silk]] had previously fought him and only survived with [[Robin]]'s help, indicating an extreme power disparity. He was killed by [[Eloryn]] in Session 055 after the party destroyed his war camp near Nolia with a Comet strike.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 047]]: Revealed as "der Babo"; [[Silk]] previously fought him and barely survived with [[Robin]]'s help.
- Session 048: Referenced during the party's planning for the wedding-sabotage operation at [[Rosenpalast]]; his power level factored into their risk assessment.
- Session 055: Returns to the [[Helikonos]] region with elite soldiers, establishes a war camp and outpost near [[Nolia]]. Party attacks the camp with a Comet strike creating a massive crater and destroying the portal. Ruger revealed in the crater, defeated in combat; killed by [[Eloryn]]. Destroys his magic items before dying. Party interrogates his corpse the next morning.
