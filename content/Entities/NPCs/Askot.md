# Askot

## Known Information

Askot is a human male described as "fett" (fat) who leads the offensive branch of a guild. He is one of three swarm-leaders within the [[Diebesgilde]] / [[Sturm-Phönix]], responsible for directing the "Gangwars" arm of the organisation. He appears only briefly in Session 002 with no subsequent mentions.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 002]]: Introduced as "Askot, fetter Mensch" — fat human leading the offensive/Gangwars branch of the [[Diebesgilde]] / [[Sturm-Phönix]].

## Known Facts

- Race: Human
- Role: Swarm-leader (offensive branch / "Gangwars")
- Affiliation: [[Diebesgilde]] / [[Sturm-Phönix]]
- One of three swarm-leaders within the guild structure
