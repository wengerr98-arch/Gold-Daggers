# Aubonne

![[Aubonne.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Aubonne is a daughter of [[Felix]] and sister of [[Melissa]], approximately 40 years old, who runs the [[glückliche kleine Biene]] bar in [[Dalior]] — a front with a hidden backroom used by the revolutionary movement [[das Mehr]]. She is a long-standing ally of the Gold Daggers, offering them publication support, intelligence on disappearances, and logistical aid during the Dalior arc. By Session 047, she has fled to [[Helykonos]] alongside Felix, Robin, Silk, and Treth.

## Session Appearances

- Session 006: Encountered at the burning quarter (Brennendes Quartier); slapped Melissa during a dispute, then told BB to return to the bar in two nights.
- Session 007: Celebrates the party's achievements, offers to print their story anonymously in a publication, and is present when the group formally names themselves The Gold Daggers.
- Session 010: Referenced by name as likely related to Galatea; BB identifies Galatea as "95% a sister of Melissa and Aubonne." Aubonne does not appear in person this session but is cited as a family connection.
- Session 012: Mentioned as the co-party to a dispute involving a "tall blond man" repairing Felix's house; the dispute involved Aubonne and Melissa, establishing ongoing tension between the two sisters' circles.
- Session 013: Meets with the party and a group of dwarves (including [[Embrach]]) at the Tempel bar. Brings the group schnaps. Present for the discussion about Ophris trade routes and the escalating military situation.
- Session 014: Provides the party with notices about ~15 overnight disappearances, including [[Crank]] and an older female magic user. Warns the group about the scale of the kidnappings and the eerily empty Entertainment Distrikt.
- Session 017: Informed by the party about Herviar's discovery of the prison entrance and the planned assault in 5–6 days; brought into the information chain for the upcoming Oubliet storming operation.
- Session 018: Referenced as outside the city; Melissa instructs the party via Barzan's message to bring the rescued child to Aubonne. Noted as holding a different view from Melissa on how much magic should be used to change the city.
- Session 019: Receives [[Robin]] (brought to her by the party per Melissa's instructions); her bar appears empty from the outside. Warned that guards had been searching for Robin. Told that Mason, a former student of Felix, is pursuing Robin. Expresses anger that the Entertainment District only looks after itself, but ultimately accepts broader cooperation and aligns openly against Mason and the city. Agrees to organise food supplies through the bar for the next day.
- Session 024: Referenced in the context of city cleanup activities; the party's cleanup and regrouping takes place near her location in the Entertainment District area.
- Session 025: Found drunk, sick, and emotionally overwhelmed in her bar when the party arrives to hand over the prison logbook; Leumas and Lorilla help her recover with a Bloody Mary. Barzan discovers a hidden drawer in the back room containing a portrait with several sisters' names on the back. BB places the logbook in the back room with a letter. Aubonne is eventually carried upstairs to bed.
- Session 047: Present in [[Helykonos]] alongside Felix, Robin, Silk, and Treth in the temple area after fleeing Dalior; referenced by the party (via the alias "Obon") as part of the allied group reconnected with following their escape from Yorni's captivity.

## Known Facts

- Daughter of [[Felix]]; sister of [[Melissa]]; approximately 40 years old; human; non-magical.
- Has a likely third sister, [[Galatea]], identified by BB in Session 010 as matching the family resemblance; a portrait found in Session 025 suggests there may be more sisters.
- Runs the [[glückliche kleine Biene]] bar in [[Dalior]], which has a secret backroom used by the revolutionary movement [[das Mehr]].
- Long-standing ally of the Gold Daggers; offered to publish their story anonymously in Session 007.
- Operates the bar under a false appearance (looks empty from outside) to maintain cover.
- Disagrees with [[Melissa]] on how much magic should be used and whether the city should be changed (Session 018).
- As of Session 019, holds [[Robin]] in her care and is publicly aligned against [[Dr. Mason]] and the city authorities.
- As of Session 047, in [[Helykonos]] with Felix, Robin, Silk, and Treth — part of the allied group who fled from Dalior.
- Has connections to a group of dwarves in Dalior, including [[Embrach]].
