## Known Information

Azan Aeducan is the Emperor of Ophris, having ruled for nearly 300 years — a reign that raises questions about immortality or divine sanction. He shares the Aeducan surname with PC [[Barzan Aeducan]], strongly implying an imperial family connection (Barzan's father is reportedly a son of the Emperor, making Barzan the Emperor's grandson). His right hand is visibly blackened and transforming, concealing something dangerous in his glove connected to a destroyed island or volcanic force, and his health is described as worsening.
