# Bader

Bader is the Head of the Necromancy school at the [[Wizard Academy|Kasta]] magical academy. The session text uses both "Bader" (NPC list) and "Baader" (school label); "Bader" is used as the canonical form with "Baader" retained as an alias pending DM confirmation.
