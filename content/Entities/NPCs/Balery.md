# Balery

![[Balery.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Balery is a Duergar (Duergarn dwarf) blacksmith residing at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]. She is missing one arm. The party encountered her during a library and smithy visit, where she was asked about her missing arm and quoted a cost of 1500g to complete a half-plate commission.

## Session Appearances

- Session 034: Party visits the smithy during the library/smithy segment; Snix asks about her missing arm; Leumas asks her about completing a half-plate (cost: 1500g).

## Known Facts

- Race: Duergar (Duergarn dwarf).
- Occupation: Blacksmith at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]].
- Physical trait: Missing one arm.
- Quoted 1500g to complete a half-plate for Leumas (Session 034).
