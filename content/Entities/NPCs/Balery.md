Balery is a Duergar (Duergarn dwarf) blacksmith residing at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]. She is missing one arm. The party encountered her during a library and smithy visit, where she was asked about her missing arm and quoted a cost of 1500g to complete a half-plate commission.
