## Known Information

Beat (formerly Vokon) was a spy sent by [[Dr. Fitz Mason]] and his highest-ranking operative assigned to learn how the [[Skjall]] wall works. He carried an arcane curse rune on his arm that had reportedly grown since childhood; in Session 065, [[Barzan Aeducan]] used Revivify and BB cast Remove Curse to lift the curse, after which the party gave him the new name Beat. Now free of the curse, he volunteered intelligence on Mason's spy network — including naming his handler Nigea as the highest-ranking operative in their spy class — but the party regards him as a potential double agent whose loyalty remains unresolved.

## Session Appearances

- Session 065: Major appearance — captured cursed spy; [[Barzan Aeducan]] uses Revivify and BB casts Remove Curse to lift the arcane rune; party renames him Beat; provides intel on Mason's approach route via Limbo and on handler Nigea.
- Session 068: Approaches the party in the street during the Skjall planar breach; warns that the path to Skjall is dangerous; gives them a box of [[Wish-bone|Wishbones]] (portal-opening bones); Barzan becomes suspicious and suspects Yorni is behind the gesture.
- Session 078: Referenced as Vokon; party debates next steps (choosing Aarum) and Beat remains a background consideration as the group heads to Ophris and discusses resistance to Mason.
