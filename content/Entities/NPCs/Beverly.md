# Beverly

## Known Information

Beverly is the daughter of both [[Lady Ty]] and King [[Azan Aeducan]], a major dramatic revelation uncovered during the party's private meeting with Azan. She is also described in connection with [[Felix]] — Azan states that Felix's daughter was captured and later died in a collapsed building, with Beverly apparently being this daughter. Her fate remains deeply uncertain, as Felix is known to be unable to die, and the implications of this for Beverly's identity and survival are plot-significant.

## Session Appearances

- Session 037: Identity revealed during the party's private meeting with King Azan Aeducan; major plot revelation that Beverly is the daughter of both Lady Ty and Azan; Azan did not know Felix cannot die.

## Known Facts

- Daughter of [[Lady Ty]] and King [[Azan Aeducan]]
- Also connected to [[Felix]] — described as Felix's daughter who was captured
