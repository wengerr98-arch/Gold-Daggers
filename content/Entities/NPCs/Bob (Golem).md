## Overview

Bob is a colossal Golem — hundreds of meters tall — with a pyramid on its back that contains [[Anubis]]. Named by [[Abrahim (Ephraim)|Ephraim]], who built it but notes "not like this" — the current form uses different materials than the original design.

## Physical Description

- Hundreds of meters tall
- Giant swords, sandals
- No face
- Pyramid on back (Anubis containment)
- Runes covering body form a map that updates as borders change
- The earthquakes felt across the region are its footsteps

## Containment Function

The runes on Bob are the containment sigils for Anubis. Arcana check (31) revealed:
- Sigils are nearly indestructible (thousands of HP)
- A key is needed to open/release
- Anubis is neither fully contained nor free — described as "a bug" in the soul mechanics

## Construction

Ephraim explains the Golem was built differently than his original design:
- Original used Behemoth and Leviathan bodies as materials
- A man was involved with the Behemoth component
- A woman was involved with the Leviathan component
- A failsafe was built in — this is [[The Monument (Skjall)|the Monument in Skjall]]

## Known Facts

- Named "Bob" by Ephraim (Session 108)
- Contains Anubis in the pyramid
- Walking along the border of Kasta
- Runes form a map that follows national borders
- Party must move borders to trigger the failsafe
