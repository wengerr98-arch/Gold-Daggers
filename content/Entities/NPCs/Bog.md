# Bog

## Known Information

Bog is a minotaur and one of the Skjall faction leaders, assembled for meetings following major crises in the city. He represents the Minotaur faction within Skjall's multi-species leadership structure, appearing in discussions about both the defeat of The Mother and the later threat posed by the monument, curfews, and Wild Hunt crisis.

## Session Appearances

- Session 062: Named as a Skjall minotaur leader — "Bog minothauren" — in the assembly list following the defeat of [[The Mother]] in the throne room.
- Session 065: Present as one of the assembled Skjall clan leaders (Minotaur faction) during the king's public briefing about the monument threat, curfews, and [[Wild Hunt]] crisis.
