# Bradir

## Known Information

Bradir is the cousin of [[Bromar]], the horse trader in [[Krüsam]], and is based at the harbor there. He served as the drop-off point for the party's horses during the Helikonos front mission, though he had no direct interaction with them.

## Session Appearances

- Session 043: Mentioned as Bromar's cousin at the harbor in Krüsam; horses left with him; no direct interaction with the party.
