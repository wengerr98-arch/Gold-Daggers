# Bramir

## Known Information

Bramir is an NPC near [[Peoryn]] and a cousin of [[Brovin]], placing him within the broader "Bro/Bra-" family network in the region. He provided horses to the [[Gold Daggers]] during Session 050, serving as the party's access point to Brovin and the Lerchen community.

## Session Appearances

- Session 050: Named NPC near Peoryn; provided horses to the party; identified as Brovin's cousin; spoken with directly.

## Known Facts

- Located near [[Peoryn]]
- Cousin of [[Brovin]]
- Part of the "Bro/Bra-" family network in the Peoryn/Lerchen area
- Provided horses to the [[Gold Daggers]]
