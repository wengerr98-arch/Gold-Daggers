# Bratilda

## Known Information

Bratilda is both the mother of the young woman who provides banner information to [[Barzan]], and the bar owner/innkeeper that [[Leumas]] meets in secret — DM confirmation has established these are the same person. She appears to be a figure who holds useful knowledge about the Zhurek investigation, accessed through two separate party members via different angles.

## Session Appearances

- Session 012: Not seen directly — mentioned by name as a person [[Leumas]] deduces may have information ("vlt. weiss die Mutter (Bratilda)"). [[Barzan]]'s contact is her daughter. [[Leumas]] meets Bratilda (the bar owner) in secret.

## Known Facts

- Mother of the young woman with banner information whom [[Barzan]] speaks to (Session 012).
- Bar owner/innkeeper whom [[Leumas]] meets in secret (Session 012).
- DM confirmed these are the same person — Bratilda is simultaneously the mother of the banner-girl and the bar owner.
