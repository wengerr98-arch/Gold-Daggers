# Bravin

## Known Information

Bravin is a dwarf ancestor and founding figure of the [[Cousins]] family line, currently trapped in an astral sea city. He co-built the ship [[Calamitas]] alongside [[Barzan]] and played a major role in Barzan's arc during the astral sea phase of the campaign. The party initially called him "Jean-Jacques" before his true name was confirmed. His timeline predates King Zäsan's reign on the material plane, placing him in an ancient historical context.

## Session Appearances

- Session 090: Major role in Barzan's arc — met in the astral sea city; helped build the ship [[Calamitas]]; confirmed as ancestor of the [[Cousins]].
- Session 092: Referenced as "Brevin" in the sidecar; associated with the party's research into planar artifacts and time-travel routes — noted as a potential link to planarity and planar artifact knowledge.
