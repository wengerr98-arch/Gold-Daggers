# Brel

## Known Information

Brel is a Loxodon student based in room 207 of the Conjuration tower in [[Kasta]], introduced to the party by [[Torren]] as a local contact. He has become a recurring lore source for the party, particularly regarding Fey conjurations and titan history. He confirmed that surviving titan knowledge in Kasta is largely oral and that the library holds guarded magical knowledge with restricted access. Brel witnessed [[Leumas]] reveal himself as a changeling during one of their meetings.

## Session Appearances

- Session 078: Introduced by [[Torren]] as a student contact in Kasta.
- Session 080: Met in room 207 of the Conjuration tower; [[Leumas]] reveals himself as a changeling and asks Brel about Fey conjurations; established as a follow-up contact.
- Session 081: Serves as a lore source; explains that surviving titan history is largely oral and that the library holds guarded magical knowledge with restricted access.
- Session 082: Referenced as part of the broader Kasta investigation arc while the party follows blood-trail clues into the desert.
- Session 083: Visited at the school/library; party questions him about titans, Saul (Ephraim alias), and diviners — gathering lore before departing Kasta.
- Session 085: Active contact during the party's investigation of the blood archive and warded library.
- Session 090: Referenced in sidecar; remains a standing Kasta contact as the party navigates the Astral Sea arc.
- Session 095: Referenced during Aarum downtime research phase as a background standing contact for the Kasta/titan lore thread.
