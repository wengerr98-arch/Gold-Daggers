## Known Information

Brel is a Loxodon student based in room 207 of the Conjuration tower in [[Kasta]], introduced to the party by [[Torren]] as a local contact. He has become a recurring lore source for the party, particularly regarding Fey conjurations and titan history. He confirmed that surviving titan knowledge in Kasta is largely oral and that the library holds guarded magical knowledge with restricted access. Brel witnessed [[Leumas]] reveal himself as a changeling during one of their meetings.
