## Known Information

Brentan is an ancient, extraordinarily strong dwarf folk hero, freed from Dr. Mason's basement dungeon in Session 010. He is the uncle of Sixt and a grandfather figure to Brovin's community in Lerchen. Goliath archival texts at Sanktum Elend identify him as the only known person to have entered the Wild Hunt and returned alive. Roughly 200–300 years ago he participated in a lethal survival tournament in [[Skjall]], defeated a massive monster behind a special door, and walked away — an event that shapes the party's understanding of the Wall's activation mystery.

## Session Appearances

- Session 010: Freed from a cage in Dr. Mason's basement dungeon; described as a very old, possibly senile dwarf of extraordinary strength — throws boulders and digs an escape tunnel. Uncle of Sixt, another prisoner. He and [[Lady Ty]] appear to recognise each other.
- Session 037: Referenced by King [[Azan Aeducan]] during his private briefing with the party; more about Brentan's fate and connections to [[Lady Ty]] and Felix revealed.
- Session 050: Encountered in person at [[Lerchen]], living with Brovin's community as the community grandfather figure. Physically described as bare-chested and shredded. Recognised the party from prison ("Aaah, Gefängnis!"). Shared heroic stories including the Skjall tournament tale. Revealed family in [[Peoryn]] was attacked.
- Session 052: Present at the harbor; initially reacted with fear alongside Brovir and Bronils upon the party's arrival, then shifted to cooperative help and assisted with repairing the boat.
- Session 061: Referenced in archival research at [[Sanktum Elend]] — Goliath texts identify him as the only known person to have entered the Wild Hunt and returned; shapes the party's understanding of the Wall's activation mystery.
- Session 072: Name appears in historical entries of a logbook discovered in a hidden tavern space, connecting him to past visits and the party's current timeline puzzle.

## Known Facts

- Freed from a cage in Dr. Mason's basement dungeon in Session 010; uncle of Sixt.
- Extraordinary physical strength — throws boulders, digs escape tunnels.
- As of Session 050, lives in [[Lerchen]] with Brovin's community; functions as the community grandfather figure.
- Physically shredded and bare-chested; recognised the party from their shared prison stint.
- Approximately 200–300 years ago: visited [[Skjall]], participated in a lethal survival tournament — won all rounds, fought a massive monster behind a special door (Balgura-like, bigger than a church, four arms, snake head, brown fur, thumbed feet), and survived.
- Because he was not a Skjall citizen he could not claim the Torr title; received a prize of "walking through the door" instead.
- Has had family in [[Peoryn]]; expressed sadness when told Peoryn was attacked.
- Identified in Goliath archival texts at [[Sanktum Elend]] as the only known person to have entered the Wild Hunt and returned alive (Session 061).
- Name appears in a hidden tavern logbook, connecting him to past visits (Session 072).
