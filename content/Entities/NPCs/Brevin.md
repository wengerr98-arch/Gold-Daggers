# Brevin

## Known Information

Brevin is a named NPC briefly encountered in Session 101 who departs by boat toward the Tarrasque's location. Very little is known about their identity, faction, or fate.

## Session Appearances

- Session 101: Named in the boat departure scene; exits the boat heading toward the Tarrasque's location.
- Session 105: Appears at the Werft (Ophris Coast Shipyard) as Snix's older cousin (OG Bruder). Discusses the ship Calamitas — warns it should not be used as a normal ship. Party confirms Brevin as Snix's family connection.

## Known Facts

- Appeared in the boat departure scene in Session 101
- Travelled toward the Tarrasque's location
- Confirmed identity (Session 105): Snix's older cousin (OG Bruder); works at or is connected to the Werft (Ophris Coast Shipyard)
- Warned the party that Calamitas should not be used as a normal ship (Session 105)
