# Bromir

## Known Information

Bromir is an NPC encountered at the Helikonos shipyard (werft) during the party's relief operation. He reacted positively ("amazing") to the party's arrival. He is one of the "Cousins" — a group of NPCs sharing a "Bro-/Brah-" naming pattern — and is distinct from [[Bromar]] (the horse trader from [[Krüsam]]). See [[Cousins]] faction.

## Session Appearances

- Session 055: Present at the Helikonos shipyard; reacted positively to the party's arrival during the relief operation.

## Known Facts

- Location encountered: Helikonos shipyard (werft)
- Member of the [[Cousins]] faction
- Distinct NPC from [[Bromar]] (horse trader, [[Krüsam]], Session 043)
