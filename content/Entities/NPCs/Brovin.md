# Brovin

## Known Information

Brovin is a dwarf who owns and operates the shipyard in [[Lerchen]], a harbour town near [[Peoryn]]. He is a cousin of [[Bramir]] and part of the broader "Bro/Bra-" family network in the region. A warm and hospitable community leader, he arranged sea passage for the [[Gold Daggers]] toward [[Helykonos]] and hosted the party for the evening.
