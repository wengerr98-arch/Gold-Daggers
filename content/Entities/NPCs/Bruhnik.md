# Bruhnik

## Known Information

Bruhnik is a dwarf NPC encountered aboard [[Loid]] in Session 060. Barzan addresses him by name, indicating a personal acquaintance. He reports on attacks against healers in the quarter, mentions having had four bad shifts, and reveals he was hired by someone called "en lady namens Snix." He also references co-founding a village near a shipyard alongside [[Bromir]] and [[Bronils]].

## Session Appearances
