Calboretta is a notable NPC of [[Dalior]] who served as the wagon driver leading the merchant convoy (Wagen-Händler-Kolonne) the party travelled with on the road to [[Dalior]] in Session 003. She is accompanied by a figure referred to as "crank (grandchild)" and entered the city tavern with the group on their first arrival in Session 004. "Carboretta" is a known alias or variant spelling.
