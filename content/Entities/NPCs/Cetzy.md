# Cetzy

## Known Information

Cetzy is a powerful Rakshasa who serves as patron of the [[Entertainment District (Dalior)|Entertainment District]] in [[Dalior]] and owner of the [[Tempel (Dalior)]]. He is the patron of [[Silk]], [[Milly]], and [[Eloryn]], the latter of whom he bound through a formal warlock pact in Session 036, claiming her right arm as the price. In Session 014, Cetzy appeared in person as a tiger-form Rakshasa and formally declared the Entertainment District's independence, its intent to raise troops, and its entry into the war.

## Session Appearances

- Session 012: Named by BB when asking [[Silk]] about Cetzy and [[Amare]]; Silk does not know more. Identified as patron of the Entertainment District and the Tempel, and as [[Eloryn]]'s patron.
- Session 014: Appears in person alongside [[Shelandria Van Meehren]]. Speaking through Silk as translator, formally declares the Entertainment Distrikt will act independently, raise troops, and go to war. Described as a powerful Rakshasa tiger.
- Session 015: Contacted remotely via Message spell at the [[Tiger Stallungen]]; advises the party to disguise themselves as slave traders to smuggle freed hostages into the city through the slavers' entrance.
- Session 036: Appears in Eloryn's dream-vision; offers a warlock pact, demanding her right arm as collateral. Eloryn signs with blood and receives new powers through the replaced arm.
- Session 101: Referenced in passing as Eloryn's patron and patron of the Tempel entertainment network; noted under alias "Jetzy" in the sidecar entity list.

## Known Facts

- Race: Rakshasa; class uncertain (noted as "Dschin?" in old sources).
- Patron of the [[Entertainment District (Dalior)|Entertainment District]] and owner/patron of the [[Tempel (Dalior)]].
- Patron of [[Silk]], [[Milly]], and [[Eloryn]].
- Can be reached remotely via Message spell.
- Declared the Entertainment District's independence and intent to go to war in Session 014.
- Bound [[Eloryn]] through a warlock pact in Session 036; price was Eloryn's right arm, signed in blood.
