# Charon

## Known Information

Charon is the ferryman of the dead who operates on the [[River Styx]]. In Session 096 the party encounters him while crossing from [[Limbo]] toward [[Avernus]]— he ferries souls and charges a diamond or coin for passage. Notably, he gifted [[Eloryn]] a finger-bone that subsequent arcana investigation revealed to contain "more magic than bone."

## Session Appearances

- Session 096: Active scene NPC; grants the party passage across the [[River Styx]]. Barzan pays with a diamond. Charon gives [[Eloryn]] a finger-bone as a gift. BB performs an arcana check confirming it contains "more magic than bone."

## Known Facts

- Role: ferryman of the dead on the [[River Styx]].
- Operates between [[Limbo]] and [[Avernus]] (and other planar crossings).
- Charges a diamond or coin for passage.
- Gifted [[Eloryn]] a finger-bone with unusual magical properties (Session 096).
