## Known Information

Crank is a notable NPC of [[Dalior]] who first appears travelling with [[Calboretta]] on a wagon convoy heading to the city. He is referred to as "crank (grandchild)" in session text, though whether this is a proper name or a role description, and what his exact relationship to [[Calboretta]] is, remains unconfirmed. In Session 014 he was abducted from his bed by a large armed convoy and imprisoned, losing his flute in the process; the party subsequently rescued him. After the rescue, [[Snix]] carved him a replacement flute as a gift.

## Session Appearances

- Session 003: Referred to as "crank (grandchild)"; accompanies [[Calboretta]] on the wagon convoy to [[Dalior]].
- Session 004: Enters the city tavern with the group on the party's first arrival in [[Dalior]].
- Session 014: Abducted from his bed by a prisoner convoy (~40–60 armed soldiers) heading toward the mountains; stripped of possessions including his flute; imprisoned alongside [[Leumas]] and an older female magic user; rescued by the party after they stormed the camp house.
- Session 015: Part of the freed hostage group travelling with the party; [[Snix]] carves a replacement flute for him during the journey back to the city.
- Session 020: Referenced in passing — [[Leumas]] plays his flute and mocks Crank; no direct appearance.
