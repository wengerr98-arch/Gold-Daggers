# Dabri

## Known Information

Dabri is an older dwarven woman who operates the Giesserei (foundry) in [[Torrenviertel]], [[Aarum]]. The party negotiated a commercial deal with her in Session 039 — purchasing Ithrium/Mithril at a discounted price in exchange for ongoing arrangements. As part of the deal she provided a Sending Stone for use as a party contact channel, establishing a lasting commercial relationship.

## Session Appearances

- Session 039: Party negotiates Ithrium/Mithril purchase deal; Dabri provides a Sending Stone for ongoing contact; commercial relationship established at the Giesserei in [[Torrenviertel]].

## Known Facts

- Race: dwarf (older woman).
- Occupation: foundry operator at the Giesserei in [[Torrenviertel]], [[Aarum]].
- Provided the party with a Sending Stone as an ongoing contact tool (Session 039).
- Sells Ithrium/Mithril; offered a discounted rate in exchange for an ongoing arrangement.
