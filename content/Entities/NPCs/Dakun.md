# Dakun

![[Dakun.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Dakun is a young part-elven dwarf from [[Aarum]] who co-leads the [[Tower]] (Turm Gang) alongside his twin sister [[Nasri]]. He is recognisable by his mechanical arm and has developed a warm relationship with the party over many sessions, serving as a reliable contact, caretaker, and craftsman in Aarum. In Session 057 he took on a father-figure role for the Dragon Tortoise hatchling Baby-Boy, and as of Session 095 he is actively forging weapons for the party.

## Session Appearances

- Session 041: Met by the party inside the Tower; co-leads the organisation with his sister [[Nasri]]. Notable for having a mechanical arm. Party encounters both siblings together for the first time.
- Session 042: Attends the joint dinner in the Pocket Dimension; becomes noticeably more open after negotiations; agrees to shared governance of the former Pleasure District.
- Session 044: Present at the public government meeting with Torren and the government, alongside [[Nasri]]. Witnessed the card-and-tattoo confrontation between Barzan and Nasri that triggered the black cloud and lightning display.
- Session 056: Party briefly checks in with Dakun during resupply; he and [[Nasri]] are referenced as the party shops and checks in with contacts before the journey to Skjall.
- Session 057: Takes on a caretaker and father-figure role toward the Dragon Tortoise hatchling Baby-Boy; Barzan temporarily entrusts him with his Pick-Axe.
- Session 078: Referenced during the party's Aarum stop; Dakun and the Tower continue operations while the party receives business and military updates before departing for Ophris.
- Session 095: The party visits Dakun's tower during downtime; observed actively forging the party's requested weapons.
- Session 101: Broke down in tears when receiving gold from Barzan; had an active scene role.

## Known Facts

- Race: Dwarf, part-elven
- Age: 20
- Home: [[Aarum]]
- Affiliation: [[Tower]] (Turm Gang); co-leader alongside twin sister [[Nasri]]
- Has a metal (mechanical) arm
- In Session 057, took on a caretaker role for Baby-Boy (Dragon Tortoise hatchling); Barzan temporarily entrusted him with his Pick-Axe
- As of Session 095, actively forging weapons for the party
