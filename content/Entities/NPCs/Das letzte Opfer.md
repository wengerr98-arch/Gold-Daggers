# Das letzte Opfer

## Known Information

Das letzte Opfer ("The Last Sacrifice") is an imprisoned Halfling who serves as a central mystery figure in the campaign. First encountered in Session 022, they were held in a glass box in the [[Oubliette|Oubliettes]] — the same type of containment used in [[Dr. Mason]]'s cellar. They communicate telepathically with [[Snix]], claim ownership of the black smoke within Snix, the [[Combustion Sword]], and Mason's ring, and assert that [[Dr. Mason]] created both the ring and the mark on Snix's hand. Whether "Das letzte Opfer" is a personal name, a title, or a ritual designation remains unknown.


## Session Appearances

- Session 022: Central mystery figure — imprisoned in glass box in the Oubliettes; communicates telepathically with [[Snix]]; claims ownership of the black smoke, [[Combustion Sword]], and Mason's ring; names [[Dr. Mason]] as creator of the ring and the mark on Snix's hand.
- Session 028: Manifests as a smoky figure emerging from the glass prison; demands possessions back from the party, then announces a hunt for the eight swords. [[Snix]] sees through the eyes of multiple sword-bearers scattered across different locations after the encounter.
- Session 029: Visits [[Snix]] again as a smoky figure; speaks of the eight swords and a "final victim"; causes a red metallic ring to grow fused into Snix's palm — identified as one of the eight swords set's markers.
- Session 038: Manifests as an avatar and attacks the party repeatedly during night watch; appears to toy with them rather than kill, causing exhaustion (Barzan accumulates two levels); also appears alongside a "fancy Dude" antagonist in Snix's visions.
- Session 042: Referenced as "the last victim" in [[Snix]]'s conversation with Yazi; the Cithrul bible recovered from Madame Dimdelle's Bag of Holding deepens the mystery around Das letzte Opfer's connection to Cithrul's primordial structure.
- Session 045: Barzan scries on Das letzte Opfer and spots [[Dr. Mason]] and Yorni nearby; the scrying turns hostile and a fight breaks out, suggesting their location is accessible to remote viewing and that Mason and Yorni are actively guarding them.
