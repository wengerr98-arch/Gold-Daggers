# Dedev

![[Dedev.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Dedev is an orc leader who led a group of orcs that aided the Gold Daggers and [[Shelandria Van Meehren|Shelly]] in freeing [[Leumas]] after he was kidnapped in [[Dalior]].

## Known Facts

- Orc. Leader of an orc group.
- Assisted the Gold Daggers and [[Shelandria Van Meehren|Shelly]] in rescuing [[Leumas]] from [[Dalior]].
