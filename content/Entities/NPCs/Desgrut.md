# Desgrut

## Known Information

Desgrut is a named individual associated with the greenhouse area in [[Aarum]] who exchanges gold for seeds (samen). They appear in a single mention in Session 078 and have not been confirmed as a recurring character.

## Session Appearances

- Session 078: "De Desgrut nemmt s'Gold" — takes gold in exchange for seeds at the [[Aarum]] greenhouse area.

## Known Facts

- Location: [[Aarum]] greenhouse area
- Role: trades seeds (samen) in exchange for gold
