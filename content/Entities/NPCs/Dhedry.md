# Dhedry

![[dhedry.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Dhedry is a Triton NPC encountered at the [[Fightclub Untergrund]] in [[Ophris]]. She drinks with [[Kara]] and the party after the bouts and shows knowledge of [[Shens Garde]] and their operations in Shi Cheng ([[Helykonos]]), asking [[Eloryn]] directly how she escaped from Shen's forces — suggesting Eloryn's prior history with that faction is known beyond the party.

## Session Appearances

- Session 036: Met at the [[Fightclub Untergrund]] in [[Ophris]] after the fights. Drinks with [[Kara]] and the party. Asks [[Eloryn]] how she escaped from [[Shens Garde]] in Shi Cheng ([[Helykonos]]). Eloryn's relationship with Dhedry softens over the course of the evening.

## Known Facts

- Race: Triton.

- Has knowledge of [[Shens Garde]] and their activities in Shi Cheng ([[Helykonos]]).
- Aware of [[Eloryn]]'s history with Shens Garde.
