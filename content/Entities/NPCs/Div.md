# Div

## Known Information

Div is a kobold who appeared in Snix's future vision during Session 089, fighting in an arena within an old gravel mine (alte Kiesmine) alongside roughly 100 other kobolds. The vision context means Div may or may not exist in the current timeline.

## Session Appearances

- Session 089: Named in Snix's future vision in the kobold arena fight; one of several named kobold combatants alongside [[Kasimir]], [[Frezzy]], [[Gröbel]], [[Mixxa]].

## Known Facts

- Race: Kobold
- Context: Appeared in a prophetic vision, not confirmed as present in the current timeline
