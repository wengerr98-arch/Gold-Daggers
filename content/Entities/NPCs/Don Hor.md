# Don Hor

## Known Information

Don Hor is a dwarf cleric (unconfirmed class) based at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]] and affiliated with the [[Kirche der Herbstgöttin]]. He serves as a senior authority figure at the monastery — acting as judge, warden, and presiding officer over its rituals and trials. He came to understand that Yorni is corrupt after hearing Barzan's account of the Nolia situation, and shifted from open hostility toward the Gold Daggers to a formal warning, ultimately allowing them access to the temple.

- Session 031: Acted as judge and warden — interrogated the party about the stone plate, the key, and their actions in Nolia; declared them political prisoners of the monastery; proposed and oversaw a formal trial, revealing they had already passed the patience test before escalating to an animated-puppet and barrel-monster combat.
