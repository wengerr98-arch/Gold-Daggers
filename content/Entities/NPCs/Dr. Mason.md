# Mason

## Known Information

Mason (Dr. Mason) is the campaign's primary antagonist — a necromancer approximately 80 years old who is the continuation or descendant of [[Dr. Fitz Mason]], the historical founder of Necromancy. He operates out of Dalior, where he runs a network of undead experiments, a secret prison system (the Oubliettes), and political manipulation through Dominate Person stones; his research into souls, liches, and cosmological forces drives much of the campaign's threat. His necromantic theorem holds that he exists in a constant state of "dying" — neither fully alive nor dead — allowing him to control unlimited undead, but he would die if he lost concentration or fell unconscious.

## Known Facts

- Approximately 80 years old; continuation or descendant of the historical [[Dr. Fitz Mason]] who founded Necromancy
- Former student of Felix (Felix was his mentor); Felix says Mason "started well but is no longer on the right path"
- Researches undead bodies, resurrection, souls, and necromantic theory; views on undead ethics deviate from standard practice
- Necromantic theorem: exists in a constant state of "dying" — neither fully alive nor dead — allowing him to control unlimited undead; would die if he lost concentration or fell unconscious
- Operates out of the zweite Platte (second tier of Dalior); accessible via Alexandra Chaumlat at the Stadtverwaltung
- Has a burned second laboratory from which undead escaped; the party was originally hired to clear it
- Mason Mansion basement contains: multiple trap/monster rooms, a magically identity-locked library, a cage room with ritual circles, a water chamber, a corpse room with sorted bodies, and a dragon chamber holding an undead dragon
- Caged prisoners found in basement (Session 010): Lady Ty, Brentan, Sixt, Galatea
- Lab notes (Session 008): gnoles deemed "too unstable" for his intended experiments; plans noted to attempt the work on larger beings and liches
- Created the Oubliettes prison system beneath Dalior — contains multiple levels including an underground forest, desert chamber, and a barracks field (Session 022)
- Created the Combustion Sword, the ring associated with the black smoke inside Snix, and the mark on Snix's hand (per Das letzte Opfer, Session 022)
- Casually killed Lorilla during the Session 024 encounter (Barzan immediately revived her)
- Issued an ultimatum to the party in Session 024: no chaos in the city for the next three days
- Met privately with a fox (Yum Ka'ax) after Session 024; promised the fox that if the city is reshaped, the trees will be kept
- Refers to the glass-caged prisoner in the Oubliettes as "a failed experiment" (Session 024)
- As of Session 025, the three-day ultimatum has been violated; Mason remains in control of Dalior
- As of Session 037, Lady Ty has been recaptured by Mason
- Working with Yorni; as of Session 045, they are moving the Dominate Person stones south and starting a new project in Amare
- Appears capable of detecting scrying attempts (Session 045)
- Documents found in Madame Dimdelle's Pocket Dimension (Session 042) refer to Mason as "Drak"; include research notes, anatomical studies, and a Cithrul bible with possible Mason-cipher notes
- Linked to Cithrul research: 30 notebooks theorising about the 10 powers and the 11th primordial god (Session 042 Legend Lore)
- The party burned his human-operation plans (Session 042)
