# Dros

## Known Information

Dros is the Head Artificer of [[Kasta]], described as holding an official position akin to chief housemaster rather than a purely technical role. The Homunculi found in Kasta originate from [[Ja'kob]] rather than Dros, suggesting their areas of responsibility are distinct.

## Session Appearances

- Session 080: Named as Head Artificer (Loxodon) at Kasta; mentioned in passing with note that the Homunculi originate from Ja'kob.

## Known Facts

- Species: Loxodon
- Role: Head Artificer of Kasta
- Described as "official position, but more like chief housemaster"
- The Homunculi at Kasta come from [[Ja'kob]], not from Dros
