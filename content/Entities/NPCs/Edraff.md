# Edraff

## Known Information

Edraff is a Goliath blacksmith based in [[Dalior]] and the father of [[Treth]]. He works alongside [[Natok]] at the Dalior smithy and was directly involved with the party's equipment work there. By Session 056, Edraff had relocated among the Nolia refugees and craftsmen in Helykonos, where he was reunited with his son.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 012]]: Named as Goliath blacksmith and father of Treth; Natok's colleague at the forge. Party brings [[Donnerstein]] to the forge for work; Edraff notes more material is needed.
- Session 056: Found among Nolia refugees in Helykonos; reunited with Treth.

## Known Facts

- Species: Goliath

- Location (later): Helykonos, among Nolia refugees
