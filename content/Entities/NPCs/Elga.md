# Elga

## Known Information

Elga is a woman encountered just outside the Entertainment District. She provided the party with local knowledge about the cleanup monsters (Aufräumungs-Monster), confirming that they only appeared on that single day when the curfew (Ausgangssperre) was in effect.

## Session Appearances

- Session 024: Questioned by BB and Eloryn near the Entertainment District; confirmed the cleanup monsters appeared only on the one day of the curfew and provided local knowledge about the incident.

## Known Facts

- Encountered just outside the Entertainment District (Session 024).
- Confirmed the Aufräumungs-Monster appeared only on the day of the Ausgangssperre (curfew).
