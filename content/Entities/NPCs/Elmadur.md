# Elmadur

![[elmadur.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Elmadur is the Laborchef (Laboratory Chief) at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]], recognisable by his violet beard and hair. He distributes magic items to the party and played a central role in presenting and discussing items for their upcoming journey. In Session 034 he also showed Eloryn a protected item from the southern temple that turned out to be an illusion concealing a gold necklace/amulet bearing the note "sorry S." — later identified as a duplicate of the Vestige placed by [[Muryn]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 033 (trailing Session 030 block): Named in the "Granil Chef Labor" context — functions as the lab chief who distributes magic items to the party.
- Session 034: Presented and discussed magic items for the party's upcoming journey, facilitating individual item selection per party member; showed Eloryn a protected item from the southern temple which Barzan dispelled to reveal a gold necklace/amulet with the note "sorry S." (a duplicate of the Vestige placed by Muryn).

## Known Facts
