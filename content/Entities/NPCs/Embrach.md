# Embrach

![[embrach.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Embrach is a dwarf who leads a merchant group from [[Ophris]], encountered in [[Dalior]] in the tense period before the war. He arrived at [[Aubonne]]'s bar with roughly twenty men, reported that the city was hostile and movement restricted, and agreed to cooperate with the [[Entertainment District (Dalior)|Entertainment District]], where [[Silk]] housed and integrated his group into the district's defences.

## Session Appearances

- Session 019: Arrives at [[Aubonne]]'s bar leading a group of dwarves from [[Ophris]]. Reports the city is hostile and movement restricted; has limited intelligence from his trade route and roughly twenty men needing space and infrastructure. The party agrees to meet again the next day. Later, [[Silk]] houses Embrach and his group in the [[Entertainment District (Dalior)|Entertainment District]] and provides instructions for integration into its defence.
