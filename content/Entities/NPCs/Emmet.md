# Emmet

![[emmet.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Emmet is a child who was trapped with [[Robin]] in a dome during the attack on the Entertainment District. He was among three children rescued from a cellar during Session 018, and adopted the cover name Dema during the escape.

## Session Appearances

- Session 018: One of three rescued children found in the cellar; trapped with Robin in the dome during the Entertainment District attack; adopts cover name Dema (alias) during the escape.

## Known Facts

- Child rescued from a cellar during the attack on the Entertainment District (Session 018).
- Was trapped with [[Robin]] in the dome during the attack.
- Adopted the cover alias Dema during the escape.
