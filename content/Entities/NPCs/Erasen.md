# Erasen

![[Erasen.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Erasen is an orc who serves as co-leader of a married pair alongside [[Kiathen]], representing orc leadership within [[Skjall]]. Erasen appears across several sessions as a faction representative in multi-clan councils, including the leaders' meeting after the defeat of The Mother, the briefing on the monument threat and Wild Hunt crisis, and the defense response during the earthquake and soul-emergence crisis.

## Session Appearances

- Session 059: Mentioned in expedition-book research at the Goliath Library in [[Skjall]] as co-leader of an orc married-pair alongside [[Kiathen]]. Not directly encountered in person this session.
- Session 062: Referenced as part of the orc faction leadership assembled for the leaders' meeting following the defeat of [[The Mother]] in the Skjall throne room.
- Session 065: Present as one of the [[Skjall]] clan leaders for the king's public briefing on the monument threat and Wild Hunt crisis; represents orc leadership alongside [[Kiathen]] in the multi-faction council.
- Session 067: Referenced as "Erazen" in the sidecar; present in [[Skjall]] during the earthquake and soul-emergence crisis as part of orc leadership active during the defense and evacuation effort.

## Known Facts

- Race: Orc.
- Married-pair co-leader alongside [[Kiathen]] in [[Skjall]].
- Active participant in multi-clan councils during the Skjall crisis arc (Sessions 062–067).
- Referenced in library sources on orc matters in Skjall (Session 059).
- Also recorded under alias "Erazen" in session sidecars.
