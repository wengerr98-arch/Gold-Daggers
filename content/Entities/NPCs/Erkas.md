# Erkas

## Known Information

Erkas is a gnome and the brother of [[Yorni]] (family surname: Ambulan). He was found as a mummified corpse in Yorni's dedicated vault room at the [[Zentralbank]], having died of starvation approximately 60 years ago while living underground in [[Dalior]]. His identity was established via a Talk with Dead spell, which surfaced the name "Erkas…" In Session 106, [[Barzan Aeducan]] performed True Resurrection on Erkas using 25,000gp diamond dust — successfully reviving him. After resurrection, Erkas revealed that the family's surname is Ambulan, that they are originally from [[Lervik]], that his younger siblings are [[Yorni]] and Yaen, and that a half-orc named Sedal lived with the family when Yorni was approximately 8 years old. He recognised the name Ambulan when the party mentioned Sani Ambulan. Yorni was furious upon learning Erkas had been revived, and retrieved him from the party shortly after.
