# Espresso (Bialetti)

## Known Information

Espresso, surname Bialetti, is an engineer-type NPC based in Aarum. The Gold Daggers recommended him to Nasri in Session 078 as a useful contact. Little else is recorded beyond his name and profession.

## Session Appearances

- Session 078: The party recommends the engineer Espresso (Bialetti) to Nasri. Referenced as "de Espresso heisst Bialetti zom nachname."

## Known Facts

- Name: Espresso; surname Bialetti.
- Role: engineer-type NPC.
