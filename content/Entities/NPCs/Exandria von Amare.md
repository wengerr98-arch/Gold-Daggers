# Exandria von Amare

## Known Information

Exandria von Amare is a young sea merchant from [[Amare]] with a distinct pirate vibe, notable for her brunette hair and bright blue eyes. She appeared in Session 015 as a significant NPC contact for the party and reportedly knows [[Muryn]], though she denied it when asked. She is also listed as a notable NPC in the old vault's Tempel (Dalior) NPC list, connected to the Entertainment District.
