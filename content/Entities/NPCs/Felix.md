# Felix

![[felix.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Felix (full name Felix Kalamites, also known as Alfredo, Felix Calamitas, and Fixu) is the owner of the magic shop "Unfreiwillig Glücklich" in Dalior and a figure of immense, ancient power — appearing about 85 years old despite being thousands of years old. He is the former mentor of [[Dr. Mason]] and bears an involuntary happiness curse called the Theorem Felicitas, created by the goddess Aria approximately 800 years ago, which renders him essentially immune to harm. Felix died during Session 077 in the Wild Hunt arc, though the true nature of this death remains unresolved given his curse; [[Robin]] is identified as carrying his power forward.
