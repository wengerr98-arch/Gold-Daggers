## Known Information

Fred is a pacifist orc from [[Skjall]], first encountered at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]] where [[Barzan Aeducan]] grew up. Affiliated with the Kirche der [[Herbstgöttin Frelka]], he is committed to spreading a pacifist ideology among the warlike orc communities of Skjall, and has already built both a temple and a school there to support his mission. By Session 058 he runs a Yee-Ha Bar in Skjall that doubles as a venue for his nonviolent conduct seminars, demonstrating his culture-building work in practice.

## Session Appearances

- Session 031: First encountered at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]; present during the harvest feast; welcomed by the monastery community and remained friendly with the party.
- Session 032: Met BB in the monastery kitchen in the morning after the feast; introduced himself as a long-term monastery resident and pacifist; described Skjall as warlike and referenced the ongoing orc-goliath war; explained he has already built a temple and a school there; explicitly invited the Gold Daggers to visit him in Skjall.
- Session 033: Present at the monastery during the Gold Daggers' interrogation and trial period.
- Session 034: Met the group during lunch; was visibly stressed and left early after revealing that the goliath have split and a goliath civil war is imminent.
- Session 042: Named in Barzan and Leumas's correspondence — receives letters from the party as they settle the Pocket Dimension arrangement; remains an active contact in the wider correspondence network.
- Session 058: The party encounters Fred in Skjall, where he runs a Yee-Ha Bar. He holds a seminar on nonviolent everyday conduct in a side room, and later dances with the party and the crowd; his pacifist mission in Skjall is visibly active.
- Session 077: Named as one of the NPCs at the Volk settlement reunion; among those the party reconnects with after pushing back the Wild Hunt, consistent with his presence in the Skjall community.
