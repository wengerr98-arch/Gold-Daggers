# Frezzy

## Known Information

Frezzy is a kobold who appeared in [[Snix]]'s future vision in Session 089, fighting in an arena inside an old gravel mine (alte Kiesmine) alongside roughly 100 other kobolds. The vision context means Frezzy's existence in the current timeline is uncertain. DM has confirmed to track as an NPC.

## Session Appearances

- Session 089: Named in Snix's future vision during the kobold arena fight. One of several named kobold combatants alongside [[Kasimir]], [[Gröbel]], [[Div]], and [[Mixxa]]. Vision context — may or may not exist in the current timeline.

## Known Facts
