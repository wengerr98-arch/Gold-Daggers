# Fritora

## Known Information

Fritora is an elven Monk who was freed from a cell in the [[Oubliette|Oubliettes]] by the party in Session 022. Before departing, she told the party "wir sehen uns später" (we'll see each other later), leaving an explicit re-appearance hook, then ran off. Her current whereabouts are unknown.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 022]]: Named and freed from her cell in the [[Oubliette|Oubliettes]]. Made an explicit future-appointment promise ("wir sehen uns später") and ran off.
