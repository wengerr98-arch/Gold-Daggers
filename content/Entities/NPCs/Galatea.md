## Known Information

Galatea is one of three daughters of [[Felix]], and a sister of [[Melissa]] and [[Aubonne]]. She was found unconscious in a special isolation box in Dr. Mason's basement dungeon in Session 010 — a box BB suspected was designed for magic users. [[Lady Ty]] pledged to return her to [[Felix]] following her rescue, and the family connection was confirmed in Session 012.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 010]]: Found in a special isolation box in Dr. Mason's basement dungeon. Stabilised but unconscious throughout the session. Identified by BB as "95% a sister of Melissa and Auborne (Aubonne)." [[Lady Ty]] pledges to return Galatea to [[Felix]], her apparent father.
- [[Session 012]]: Referenced indirectly — the family connection between Galatea, [[Melissa]], and [[Aubonne]] (all daughters of [[Felix]]) is confirmed by session context.
