# Galzan

## Known Information

Galzan is Barzan's youngest uncle, referred to as the "son of his wayward brother," and a government official and political figure aligned with the establishment. At their first personal meeting, he was dismissive of Barzan. His adviser is [[Azan Aeducan]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 044: First personal meeting with Barzan; participated in the government meeting alongside the party. Described Barzan dismissively as the son of his wayward brother. Revealed that [[Azan Aeducan]] serves as his adviser.
