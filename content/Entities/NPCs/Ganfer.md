# Ganfer

## Known Information

Ganfer is a named Frost Giant present at [[Muryn]]'s camp, distinguished by holding the oldest memories among those gathered there. He appeared briefly in Session 101 and may be a one-session character.

## Session Appearances

- Session 101: Present at [[Muryn]]'s camp as a Frost Giant; noted as the one who recalls the oldest memories among those present.

## Known Facts

- Race: Frost Giant
- Location: [[Muryn]]'s camp
- Holds the oldest memories among those present at the camp
