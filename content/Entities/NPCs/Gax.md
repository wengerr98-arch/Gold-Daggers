# Gax

![[Gax.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Gax is a kobold NPC living in [[Kobolddorf]] who serves as guide and intermediary for the Gold Daggers throughout their dealings with the kobold settlement. He is trusted by both the party and his own community, and has repeatedly facilitated communication, logistics, and cover when the group needed it.

## Session Appearances

- Session 026: Primary kobold contact; guided the party through the tunnels, fetched [[Geppo]], translated kobold lore. Named the four disappeared kobolds: [[Tok]], [[Sik]], [[Nebu]], and [[Stefe]] (Dave's brother).
- Session 027: Welcomed the party back after their underwater expedition; led them to Geppo for gear repairs and modification.
- Session 028: Informed the party that four kobolds had gone missing (Tok, Sik, Nebu, and Stefe — Dave's brother); shifted from fearful and tense to relieved after the party defeated the aboleth and resolved the hidden tunnel situation.
- Session 029: Helped BB and Snix navigate back into the kobold village after the cave sequence; acted as trusted ally during burial preparations and the confrontation with the minotaur outside the settlement.
- Session 030: Organized the party's horses and spread a cover story that they had only stopped for supplies, providing the group with a plausible alibi.
- Session 054: Welcomed the returning Gold Daggers and explained recent changes in the settlement; accompanied the party via Dimension Door to eat turbi-soup in the mensa; helped plan the approach to the rift and used a pick-axe given by Barzan to break through a wall.
