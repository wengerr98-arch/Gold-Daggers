# Geppo

![[geppo.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Geppo is a kobold tinkerer and inventor living in the [[Kobolddorf]], recognisable by his unusually pink — almost light-red — skin. He is the party's primary technical contact among the kobolds, demonstrating and gifting inventions, repairing and improving party equipment, and welcoming the Gold Daggers on multiple return visits to the settlement.

## Session Appearances

- Session 026: Demonstrated multiple inventions to the party: a jewelry-making device, a large mixer, and a working setup for Barzan's unfinished pistol; also handled bomb-like devices and supplied Barzan with several bombs.
- Session 027: Repaired the incomplete clock, improved Barzan's makeshift firearm, and gifted Eloryn the kitchen-mixer weapon; accepted a bottle of blood from Jonathan (delivered by Koda) as a gift/trade item.
- Session 029: Consulted by BB and Snix after their return to the kobold village; referenced as a key kobold contact during the burial preparations and post-cave debrief.
- Session 054: Present in the kobold entrance hall to greet the party alongside Gax and Abla when the Gold Daggers return to address the rift situation.
- Session 072: Name appears among historical entries in a logbook examined in the hidden tavern space, alongside other figures connected to the party's timeline.

## Known Facts

- Race: Kobold; distinguishing feature: pink / very light red skin
- Home: [[Kobolddorf]]
- Role: Tinkerer / inventor

- Handles bomb-like devices; supplied Barzan with several bombs (Session 026).

- Accepted a bottle of blood from Jonathan (via Koda) as a trade/gift item (Session 027).
