# Gilgamesh

## Known Information

Gilgamesh is a Yuan-Ti and the first magic user in campaign lore — she is the creator of the first Titan, not a titan herself. She was instructed in titan creation by either Jetzy or the Succubus (likely the Succubus), and created the first and only titan she ever made. She has helped multiple times before and has been in the Chele (the cold/void?) more than once. The party encountered her 6,000 years in the past during a time jump, discovering her operating a workshop where Sarrukh figures and [[Urochar]] were present — she escaped via Thunderstep when the party arrived. She is 20 Ernte old and casts magic in a wizard style.

## Known Facts

- Yuan-Ti; female; 20 Ernte old (Session 101)
- First magic user in campaign lore — casts in a wizard style (Session 101)
- Creator of the first Titan — she is NOT a titan herself; Frankenstein not the monster (Session 101)

- The basin used for Mana/Spirit was located at a point connected to Laylanes (Session 101)
- Was instructed in titan creation by either Jetzy or the Succubus — Gilgamesh thinks it was likely the Succubus (Session 101)
- Has helped the world multiple times before; has been in the Chele more than once (Session 101)
- Her helper is Oda, a bear-shifter (Session 101)
- Operated a workshop 6,000 years in the past where Sarrukh figures and [[Urochar]] were present (Session 088)
- Escaped via Thunderstep when the party arrived at her workshop (Session 088)
- Major historical and cosmological figure connected to the four-titan myth
