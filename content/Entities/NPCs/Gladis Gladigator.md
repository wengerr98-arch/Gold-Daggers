## Known Information

Gladis Gladigator (also known as Gladigator) is an NPC encountered alongside [[Dave]] during early sessions in [[Dalior]]. She assisted with rear security during the assault on the [[Oubliet]] prison, coordinating with Herviar to keep the rear secure for the group's attack. Her background, race, and class are not recorded in available sources.

## Session Appearances

- Session 014: Went with [[Dave]] to [[Kobold-Dorf]] during the party split when the party divided to pursue different objectives.
- Session 021: Present at the fight-club entrance on the day of the prison assault; agreed with Herviar to keep the rear secure for the group's attack on the [[Oubliet]] prison.
