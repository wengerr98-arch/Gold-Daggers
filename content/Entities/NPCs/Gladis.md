# Gladis

## Known Information

Gladis (also known as Gladigator) is a large Lizard-Folk fighter, gladiator, and champion at the underground arena beneath [[Dalior]]. She accompanied the party to the spa and [[Bodenloser Krug]] during Session 013, suggesting a recurring social presence at that time.

## Session Appearances

- Session 013: Accompanying the party to the spa and [[Bodenloser Krug]]; recurring presence during that session.

## Known Facts

- Also known as Gladigator (alias)
- Race: Lizard-Folk
- Class/role: fighter and gladiator
- Champion at the underground arena beneath [[Dalior]]
- "Gladis" and "Gladigator" are confirmed as the same individual
