# Gogok

## Known Information

Gogok is a female Bugbear — furry, tall, and goblinoid — who was encountered as a freed prisoner in the [[Oubliet|Oubliettes]]. She presents a friendly facade that the party quickly saw through, being noted as duplicitous in nature. She was included in the escape group but flagged as a potential complication for the evacuation.
