# Goldie

## Known Information

Goldie is the golem worker at the [[Goldie Bräu]] brewery in [[Aarum]], which is now owned by the [[Gold Daggers]]. The brewery was formerly [[Fellbrün Volkam]]'s establishment; day-to-day operations are managed by [[Susra und Kafra|Kafra and Susra]], with Goldie and [[Jacques]] working there. The brewery and beer brand are both named "Goldie Bräu" — Goldie the golem is a distinct entity from the brand name.

## Session Appearances

- Session 041: Referenced as working at Goldie Bräu; party discussed the golem's role in brewery operations and Volkam's last order (from Oridan) directed against the party.
- [[Session 043]]: Party visits the brewery; Goldie is present and operational alongside Kafra and Susra.
- Session 078: Referenced during brewery expansion — Goldie Bräu expands and hires new staff; Goldie continues operations under Kafra and Susra's management.

- Day-to-day management: [[Susra und Kafra|Kafra and Susra]]
- Co-worker: [[Jacques]]
