# Gramnir

## Known Information

Gramnir is a Riesenzwerg (giant dwarf) standing at 1.65m — notably tall for a dwarf — encountered at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]. He is a member of Gregorius's team and competed in the monastery's drinking contest during a harvest feast, winning against Eloryn 3-0.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 031]]: Present at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]] during the harvest feast and trial period; part of the monastery community.
- [[Session 032]]: Named as a member of Gregorius's drinking-contest team; described as a Riesenzwerg (1.65m); won against Eloryn 3-0.

## Known Facts

- Race: Riesenzwerg (giant dwarf)
- Height: approximately 1.65m (notably tall for a dwarf)
- Affiliation: Gregorius's team at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]
- Won a drinking contest against Eloryn 3-0 (Session 032)
