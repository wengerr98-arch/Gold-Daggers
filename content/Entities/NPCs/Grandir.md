# Grandir

## Known Information

Grandir is a metalsmith and vendor operating in the Torren Marktgasse within [[Torrenviertel]], [[Aarum]]. The party consulted him regarding Ithrium (Mithril) pricing and market conditions; he was described as not fully honest but a useful source of trade information. He functions as a minor market contact in the Aarum scene.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 039]]: Party spoke with Grandir at the Torrenviertel market; he provided Ithrium pricing context and market information.

## Known Facts

- Occupation: metalsmith and vendor
- Location: Torren Marktgasse, [[Torrenviertel]], [[Aarum]]
- Provided Ithrium (Mithril) pricing and market information
