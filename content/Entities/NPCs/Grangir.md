# Grangir

## Known Information

Grangir is a dwarf mercenary encountered by the party on a mountain battlefield during Session 038. He was found wounded near a Yeti and healed by the party, describing himself as a former mercenary who had been hunting Yetis in the area. He spent time with the party but vanished overnight without explanation, leaving behind only a mat, a blanket, and a single copper piece — a notably mysterious departure.
