# Gregorius

![[gregorius.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Gregorius is the Braumeister (brewmaster) at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]], responsible for the monastery's market purchases and supply logistics. He is a boisterous and social figure, best known for organising and leading dwarf drinking contests at monastery feasts. He remains a reliable named contact at the Kloster into the later sessions.

## Session Appearances

- Session 031: Hosts a drinking contest at the harvest feast; challenges Koda's team; approaches [[Don Hor]] informally. Active and named across the drinking contest and morning scenes.
- Session 032: Arrives at the monastery feast with more dwarves; initiates a seven-on-seven drinking contest; leads his team through multiple duels and celebrates vigorously.
- Session 042: Named in Barzan and Leumas's correspondence — receives letters from the party while they settle the Pocket Dimension arrangement; remains a reliable contact at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]].

## Known Facts

- Role: Braumeister (Brewmaster) at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]
- Responsibilities: market purchases and monastery supply logistics
- Active organiser of drinking contests at monastery feasts; led a dwarf team in a seven-on-seven contest (Session 032)
- Named reliable contact at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]] as of Session 042
