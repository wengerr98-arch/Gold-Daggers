## Known Information

Helen Dravik is one of [[Odyss Trion]]'s four generals. She was captured in Odyss's villa during the Stygian Gala raid (Session 103) and later delivered to the Ophris magic prison on behalf of Emperor [[Azan Aeducan]] (Session 105), alongside [[Marius Wehl]]. Two of Odyss's four generals remain unnamed.

## Session Appearances

- Session 103: Named by Barzan during interrogation of captured personnel in Odyss Trion's villa. Named alongside [[Marius Wehl]]; two further generals remain unnamed.
- Session 104: Referenced as a captured general during the continued villa operation.
- Session 105: Delivered to the Ophris magic prison by the Gold Daggers as part of the handover to Emperor Aazan, which earned the party a 600,000 gold payout.
