# Herbstgöttin Frelka

## Known Information

Herbstgöttin Frelka (Autumn Goddess Frelka) is an autumn goddess figure known primarily from old vault records and a possible in-session appearance. During Session 024, an unnamed woman in archaic clothing appears to Leumas in a death-vision inside an autumn forest; she tells him "it is not yet your time" and gives him a wine pouch. This figure is thematically consistent with Herbstgöttin Frelka, though her identity has not been confirmed in-session. The old vault entry contained no written detail beyond a template infobox and a reference image.

## Session Appearances
