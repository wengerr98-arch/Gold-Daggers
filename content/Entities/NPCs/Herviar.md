## Known Information

Herviar is a Firbolg who runs the [[Fightclub Untergrund]] in the underground beneath [[Dalior]], and is an active member of the revolutionary group [[Das Mehr]]. He served as a key intelligence contact and tactical organiser for the Gold Daggers during their effort to storm the [[Oubliet]] prison, locating the prison entrance, supplying maps, and helping coordinate the assault. He also recognised Eloryn on first meeting and passed on information that her aunt may have gone north.

## Session Appearances

- Session 013: Runs the underground fight club (Fightclub Untergrund) beneath Dalior; welcomed the party to the arena; confirmed he is part of [[Das Mehr]] and promised to provide the Oubliet prison location and assault timing once available; recognised Eloryn and told her that her aunt may have gone north.
- Session 017: Met the party underground in the arena beneath the Entertainment District; revealed he now knows where the prison entrance is, linked to a storage room near the city center breached via a teleportation circle; showed the party arena runes forming a blood/storage/trigger pattern that mitigates the growing exhaustion effect; set the assault timeline at 5–6 days.
- Session 021: Arrived urgently with a map showing a hidden stairway below a plate in the underground passage; agreed with Gladigator to hold the rear during the prison assault; opened the barricaded fight-club door on the day of the prison break; present when the group cleared guards in a room with three pentagram teleportation circles; when asked about freeing the wrong prisoners, warned it would create chaos.
