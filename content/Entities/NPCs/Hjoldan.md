# Hjoldan

## Known Information

Hjoldan is the guesthouse manager (Chef Gästehaus) at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]. He was the party's first named contact upon arriving at the monastery, receiving them and assigning their rooms, and remains connected to the Gold Daggers' extended stay there across several sessions.

## Session Appearances

- Session 030: Received the party at the monastery and showed them to their guest rooms; first named contact on the Gold Daggers' arrival at the Kloster.
- Session 031: Present at the monastery during the harvest feast and the trial; part of the guesthouse context during the party's ongoing stay.
- Session 032: Referenced as part of the monastery guesthouse context as the party continues their stay following the drinking contest.

## Known Facts

- Role: Chef Gästehaus (guesthouse manager) at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]
- Welcomed the Gold Daggers and assigned them rooms on their first visit (Session 030).
