# Hylrus

## Known Information

Hylrus is an Archfey, one of three Archfeys connected to the party's Fey plane. Associated with a traveling circus. Named by Barzan in conversation with Emperor [[Azan Aeducan]] at the Ophris Palace in Session 104, when Barzan asked about a "Favor vom Archfey Hylrus." No further details are known.
