# Ja'kob

## Known Information

Ja'kob is the Head of the Transmutation school at the Kasta magical academy and the creator of the Homunculi found within and around Kasta. His creations — homunculi and golems — have been found active in desert sites outside the academy, and evidence suggests a deeper connection between his work and the Anubis pyramid complex. His name is canonically spelled "Ja'kob"; "Jakob" is an alternate form.

## Session Appearances

- Session 080: Named as Head of Transmutation at Kasta; identified as source of the Homunculi, distinct from Dros the Head Artificer.
- Session 081: Party encounters his homunculi during desert excursion from Kasta; combat breaks out against the homunculi and a golem linked to a desert workshop site.
- Session 082: Party fights the golem and homunculi again in a buried desert chamber along the route toward Anubis; a scroll recovered from the chamber's platform references the golem and a wizard figure, suggesting Ja'kob (or someone connected to him) sealed something below.

## Known Facts

- Head of the Transmutation school at Kasta magical academy (Session 080)
- Creator of the Homunculi found in Kasta; distinct from [[Dros]] the Loxodon Head Artificer (Session 080)
- His homunculi and a golem were found active in a desert workshop site outside Kasta; defeated by the party (Session 081)
- A buried desert chamber on the route to Anubis contained another golem and homunculi, a sealed glass cocoon, and a scroll platform; scroll fragments reference the golem and a sealing act (Session 082)
- Canonical spelling: "Ja'kob"; alias: "Jakob"
