# Jack the Rabbit

## Known Information

Jack the Rabbit is a named, speaking rabbit who appears in [[Lorilla]]'s forest vision in Session 089. He participates in a forest gathering scene presided over by [[Grosser Hirte]] (the Great Shepherd), speaking and asking about stories. His emphatic naming in the session source suggests intentional significance as a creature of the forest domain or [[Deep Wilds]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 089: Appears in Lorilla's future vision during a forest gathering scene presided over by [[Grosser Hirte]]; speaks and asks about stories. Location: forest domain, likely the [[Deep Wilds]].
