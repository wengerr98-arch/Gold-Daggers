# Jaena

## Known Information

Jaena is a historical figure whose name was found in the logbook of the time-shifted taverne during Session 072, written alongside [[Entris]]: "Jaena und Entris send zwei Näme womer fende." No further confirmed context exists in the session text, and no direct interaction with the party has been established. The DM has classified Jaena as a historical figure rather than an active NPC.

## Session Appearances

- Session 072: Name found in the taverne logbook alongside [[Entris]]; no direct interaction confirmed.

## Known Facts

- Historical figure only (DM classification); not an active NPC
- Name appears paired with [[Entris]] in the logbook entry
- Possibly one of a pair of androgynous elves encountered in the same taverne — those individuals possessed records about the first earthbreak and had contacts in the city administration (Stadtverwaltig); unconfirmed
