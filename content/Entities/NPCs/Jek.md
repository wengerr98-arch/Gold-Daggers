# Jek

## Known Information

Jek is a kobold jeweler living in the hidden kobold commune encountered by the party in Session 038. He is described as "almost completely white" and "fabulous," and he trades in gems and metals with visiting parties.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 038]]: Named kobold jeweler at the hidden commune; party purchases diamonds from him; [[Barzan Aeducan]] negotiates metals.

## Known Facts

- Race: kobold
- Occupation: jeweler
- Location: hidden kobold commune (Session 038)
- Appearance: "almost completely white," described as "fabulous"
- Traded diamonds with the party; negotiated metals with [[Barzan Aeducan]]
