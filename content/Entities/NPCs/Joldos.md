# Joldos

## Known Information

Joldos is a Triton captured during the Waterplane battle and held in the party's cells. Unusually, he woke without glassy eyes, indicating he was not mind-controlled — making him a potentially reliable intel source. He provided information about Waterplane rift locations near Offris (Ophris), Amare (Amarre), Kasta, and Rhino, though Lorilla suspects he may not actually know where the control stones are.

## Session Appearances

- Session 051: Captured Triton prisoner; provided rift location intel during interrogation; claims knowledge of control stone locations, though Lorilla is sceptical.
