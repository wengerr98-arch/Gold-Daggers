# Kalzam

## Known Information

Kalzam is a young orc girl of approximately eleven years old, encountered with [[Dedev]]'s orc caravan. She demonstrated magical ability during Session 014, casting Flaming Sphere, and participated as an active combatant in the raid on the captive camp alongside the party.
