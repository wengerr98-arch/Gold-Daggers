# Kara

![[kara.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Kara is a Shifter woman encountered at the [[Fightclub Untergrund]] in [[Ophris]]. She fought against Koda in the arena and afterwards drank with the party, sharing information about [[Kasta]] and the no-man's land surrounding it. She mentioned coming from a forest, suggesting a possible connection to the wilderness near Kasta.
