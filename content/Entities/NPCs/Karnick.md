# Karnick

## Known Information

Karnick is a dwarf gatekeeper stationed at the entrance of the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]], the mountain monastery. When the party arrived in Session 057 he recognised Barzan by name and admitted the group without difficulty.

## Session Appearances

- Session 057: Gatekeeper at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]; recognised Barzan and let the party in.

## Known Facts

- Race: dwarf
- Role: gatekeeper / door guardian at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]
- Acquainted with Barzan
