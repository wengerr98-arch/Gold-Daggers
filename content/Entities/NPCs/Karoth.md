# Karoth

![[Karoth.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Karoth is a Goliath who co-heads the Goliath Library (Goliath Bibliothek) in Skjall alongside [[Nameo]], and is regarded as a spiritual leader of the Goliath people. She also serves as gatekeeper and custodian for the Sanktum Elend archive, controlling access to its older restricted texts. Karoth has been a consistent presence in Skjall's faction leadership throughout the Wild Hunt crisis and its aftermath.

- Session 061: Provides the party access to the Sanktum Elend archive's restricted text area; operates a blättere/page-turn mechanism; the party consults her for materials about Vorad, the Giants, the Goliaths, and the Wild Hunt.

- Session 065: Among the Skjall clan leaders addressed by the king during the public briefing about the monument threat and the Wild Hunt crisis; represents the Goliath faction in the assembled leadership council.

## Known Facts

- Co-head of the Goliath Library (Goliath Bibliothek) in [[Skjall]] alongside [[Nameo]].
- Spiritual leader of the Goliath people.
- Serves as gatekeeper/custodian of the Sanktum Elend archive; controls access to older restricted texts and an archival page-turning mechanism.
- Granted the party access to the restricted Sanktum Elend materials when asked (Session 061).
- Engaged in setting traps and fall mechanisms as Skjall prepares for the anticipated assault following the Wild Hunt and Wintergöttin crisis (Session 066).
- Performed a ritual at the entrance during the Session 067 earthquake crisis; her soul rose and moved toward the Minotaure — suggesting a known or prepared ritual form rather than a death.
- Part of the broader Skjall resistance network allied with Ophris against the Wild Hunt.
