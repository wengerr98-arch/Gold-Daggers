## Known Information

Karoth is a Goliath who co-heads the Goliath Library (Goliath Bibliothek) in Skjall alongside [[Nameo]], and is regarded as a spiritual leader of the Goliath people. She also serves as gatekeeper and custodian for the Sanktum Elend archive, controlling access to its older restricted texts. Karoth has been a consistent presence in Skjall's faction leadership throughout the Wild Hunt crisis and its aftermath.
