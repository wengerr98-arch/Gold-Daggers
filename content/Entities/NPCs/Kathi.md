## Known Information

Kathi is a merchant encountered at the Boots-Markt in [[Dalior]]. She is distinctive for her eye-patch and large hairdo, and was cleaning a pipe when first met. Her stall sells small crystals, fireworks, and [[Schwarzpulver]] (gunpowder), which she describes as "one of the newest inventions from the wonderful neighbouring country" — hinting at awareness of a foreign nation's technological development.
