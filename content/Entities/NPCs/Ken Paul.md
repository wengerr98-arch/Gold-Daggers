# Ken Paul

## Known Information

"Ken Paul" is a false name used by a mysterious light-skinned, half-human Tiefling encountered at [[Levistus]]'s [[Bastion]] in [[Stygia]]. He is also known as "Kevin" and stated "mis läbe ghört ned mer" (my life doesn't belong to me), implying magical or fey servitude. He carries an unregistered Sigil that failed to be registered with city administration, and appears to know the party's weaknesses — suggesting he is an agent of an unknown power.
