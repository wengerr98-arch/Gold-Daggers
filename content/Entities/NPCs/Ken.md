## Known Information

Ken is a horned NPC who carries a seal from the military linked to [[Zariel]] (known by the alias "Zaria"), connecting him to the infernal command structure and the Stygia ball political arc. He has appeared across multiple sessions, with his seal and horns noted as distinguishing details.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 048 - Die Hochzeit|Session 048]]: Named in the entity list for the Rosenpalast wedding ("Die Hochzeit"); no specific events attributed to him in this session.
- [[Session 095 - Die vergessene Schwelle|Session 095]]: Named in the entity list during the party's [[Aarum]] downtime; present in Aarum as part of [[The Tower]]'s ongoing operations alongside Ron.
- [[Session 101 - 1-0 Auftakt|Session 101]]: Named with his horns and military seal noted; seal linked to [[Zariel]] (via the "Zaria" alias), connecting him to the infernal command structure and the Stygia ball political arc.
