## Known Information

Kevos (also known as "Keve" or "der Kiefer Keve") is a low-ranking Nohlia soldier or worker stationed in [[Helykonos]]. He introduced himself to [[Leumas]] (in disguise) and provided key intelligence about the portal connecting to a second camp on land, as well as details about the two-month rotation of human workers operating in the city. By Session 052 he was part of the revolutionary network, reuniting warmly with [[Eloryn]] alongside [[Stevos]] and [[Mahlyn]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 046: Encountered by [[Leumas]] in disguise. Reveals information about the portal and the human worker rotation schedule. Appears to be a low-ranking Nohlia operative with operational knowledge.
- Session 052: Reunited with [[Eloryn]]; hugged her after accepting her explanation of her situation; part of the Stevos/Mahlyn group welcoming Eloryn back to the revolutionary network.
- Session 105: Appeared at the Helykonos Rebell HQ, pleading with the party to be allowed to stay and help rebuild the rebel network.
