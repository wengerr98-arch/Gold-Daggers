# Kiathen

![[Kiathen.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Kiathen is an Orc leader in [[Skjall]], serving as one half of a married-pair leadership duo alongside [[Erasen]]. The pair co-lead in a context connected to the library expedition book material and represent Orc faction leadership in multi-faction councils. Kiathen was active during the earthquake and soul-emergence crisis, participating in the defense and evacuation effort.

## Session Appearances

- Session 059: Mentioned in expedition book research context as co-leader of an Orc married-pair alongside [[Erasen]]. Not directly encountered in person this session.
- Session 062: Referenced as part of the Orc faction leadership assembled for the leaders' meeting following the defeat of The Mother in the Skjall throne room.
- Session 065: Present as one of the Skjall clan leaders assembled for the king's public briefing about the monument threat and Wild Hunt crisis; represents Orc leadership alongside [[Erasen]] in the multi-faction council.
- Session 067: Referenced as "Kiaten" in the sidecar — present in Skjall during the earthquake and soul-emergence crisis; part of the Orc leadership active during the defense and evacuation effort.
