# Klereth

## Known Information

Klereth was the last Triton king of [[Helykonos]] (also called Tritonia). He was murdered by [[Shen]], who subsequently seized power and now holds public parades in the city. His death is a named structural fact establishing Shen's usurpation of power in Helykonos, recorded in a book about old monarchies read by [[BB]]: "Der letzte König hiess Klereth, er wurde von Shen ermordet."

## Session Appearances

- Session 046: Named as the last Triton king, deceased — murdered by [[Shen]]. Referenced in a written source within the game world (a book about monarchies).

## Known Facts

- Race: Triton
- Status: Deceased (murdered by [[Shen]])
- Title: Last Triton King of [[Helykonos]] / Tritonia
- Source of information: A book about old monarchies, read by [[BB]] in Session 046
