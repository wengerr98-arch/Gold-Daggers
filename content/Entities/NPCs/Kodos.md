## Known Information

Kodos is a Triton revolutionary described as the "former third hand" of the [[Helykonos]] revolution, operating within the command structure under [[Uthrin]]. He played a practical, logistical role during Session 052, providing the party with the Helikonos map and leading them to the armour exchange where gear was swapped in preparation for the stealth mission.
