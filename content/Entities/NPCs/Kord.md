# Kord

## Known Information

Kord is a former officer of [[Demogorgon]] who operates near [[Skjall]], described by Tautok in Session 077 as coming "from the Wilds." He has a satyr-like appearance with a red upper body and an aggressive character, making him a potential threat in the northern region. His former allegiance to Demogorgon and possible ties to the Giants still in the north mark him as an unresolved antagonist thread.

## Session Appearances

- Session 077: Named by Tautok as Demogorgon's former officer, currently a potential threat near Skjall. Mentioned alongside the Giants in the north as a possible future confrontation.

## Known Facts

- Former officer of [[Demogorgon]], from the Wilds.
- Satyr-like appearance; red upper body; aggressive character.
- Currently stationed at or operating near [[Skjall]].
- Possible connection to Giants/Ice-Giants still in the north.
