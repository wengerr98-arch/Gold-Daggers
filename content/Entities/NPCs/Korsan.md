# Korsan

## Known Information

Korsan is the father of [[Barzan Aeducan]], first identified in Session 089 when he appeared in BB's future vision. He is infected with the nothingness affliction — the anti-creation force (the "nüüüt"/Silence) spreading across planes — making his condition a significant campaign thread. His appearance marks the first time Barzan's father has been named in the campaign.

## Session Appearances

- Session 089: Appears in BB's future vision. Named as Barzan's father. Described as infected with nothingness (the "nüüüt"/Silence affliction).

## Known Facts

- Father of [[Barzan Aeducan]].
- Infected with the nothingness/Silence affliction.
- Connected to the broader nothingness thread; possibly linked to gauntlets found in the sea near Helikonos.
