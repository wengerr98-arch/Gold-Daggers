# Kuruki

![[Kuruki.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Kuruki is a half-orc warrior based in [[Skjall]], physically distinctive with a scarred face, shaved sides, and an athletic build. She has a romantic connection with [[Leumas]], with whom she shared an intimate reconnection at the sanctum in Session 067. Kuruki is aware of the Gold Daggers' alliance with the Wild Hunt and has expressed clear approval of the party.

## Session Appearances

- Session 066: Attended party dinner; physically described (half-orc, scarred, shaved sides, athletic build); noted as potential romantic interest for Leumas.
- Session 067: Leumas takes Kuruki to the sanctum with wine and a picnic blanket; they apologise and reconnect in a clearly romantic beat. Kuruki asks why Leumas hasn't come by; Leumas explains and reveals the party's alliance with the Wild Hunt. Kuruki expresses clear approval of the Gold Daggers.
- Session 077: Present at the Volk settlement reunion; among those the party reconnects with after pushing back the Wild Hunt.

## Known Facts

- Half-orc; physically described as scarred, shaved sides, athletic build.
- Romantic interest of [[Leumas]]; the two shared an intimate reconnection in the sanctum (Session 067).
- Expresses clear approval of the Gold Daggers.
- Aware of the party's alliance with the Wild Hunt; received the information calmly from Leumas.
- Based in [[Skjall]]; part of the Skjall social circle.
