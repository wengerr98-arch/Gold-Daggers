# Königin (Fey Queen)

## Known Information

The Königin is a Fey queen encountered by [[Leumas]] in a throne room during Session 090, seated with a regal bearing and polishing armour that closely resembles Leumas's own. Leumas addresses her as "Maaam," implying a maternal or queen-subject relationship. Her identity remains unresolved — she may be the same entity as [[The Arch-Fey]], and DM confirmation is required before this stub is promoted to canon.
