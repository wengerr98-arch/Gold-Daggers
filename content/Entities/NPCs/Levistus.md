## Known Information

Levistus is an archdevil figure whose domain lies in the infernal plane of Stygia, reached by the party via the Bastion. He is connected to ancient lore about the Genasi of [[Kasta]] and may be linked to the ancestral origin of that people through events of the Forgotten War. After a tense first contact — including a combat encounter in his throne room — Levistus entered a functional if uneasy cooperative relationship with the party, briefing them on the Erdbrecher, titans, the Silence, and a planar corruption threatening multiple planes, and equipping them with astral-anchor outfits for travel in the Astral Sea.

## Session Appearances

- Session 080: Referenced in the context of Kasta titan lore and early earth genasi history; Barzan's research (via [[Mahdi]] the market bookseller) surfaces a theoretical connection between Levistus and Genasi descent — specifically that the Genasi may trace their lineage through Levistus.
- Session 096: Named in the context of the party's infernal travel — Ysra references Levistus and Bragian in explaining the political and cosmological geography of the infernal region; their relevance to the wider setting is flagged as an open thread.
- Session 098: A Legend Lore-style vision experienced by the party in Stygia reveals layered imagery involving Levistus — tyranny, imprisonment, kingship, and recurring displacement. The party processes the vision and its meaning for Levistus's role in the infernal political order.
- Session 099: The party directly contacts Levistus via Sending, identifies a ball ticket and unregistered Sigil on a follower ([[Ken Paul]]) linked to him, then proceeds to his domain via the Bastion and lift — they navigate the audience queue before reaching the throne room where an ooze/dragon-style combat erupts, ending with Barzan unconscious.
- Session 100: The party is brought into a formal audience with Levistus; he explains the cosmic situation including titan-like bodies, ritual patterns, and a corrupting presence that may infect multiple planes. He gives the party astral-anchor outfits for travel in the Astral Sea and hints they must locate and deal with the threatening presence. The party's relationship with Levistus transitions from hostile unknown to a functional if tense line of contact.
