# Linsea

A Half-Elf and member of a 5-person patrol in [[The Unclaimed Lands]]. Encountered by the party in the past timeline (year 323 p.b.).

- Mother is from the elf city (unspecified which one)
- Can cast Sending (useful for long-distance communication)

- Prepares tea with pure alcohol during the campfire night
- Takes night watch with [[Lorilla]]
