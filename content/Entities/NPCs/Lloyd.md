# Lloyd

## Overview

Lloyd is the party's Nautiloid — a living ship/vessel that serves as their primary means of transportation. The name "Lloyd" is a nickname used with many spelling variations (Lloyd, Lloid, Loyd).

## Nature

Lloyd is a **companion**, not merely an item or vehicle. As a Nautiloid, it is a living creature-ship hybrid.

## Capabilities

- Flight (seen flying above storms)
- Bridge for navigation and observation
- Protective barrier (ghosts/souls pound against it from outside)
- Sanctuary space (party rests and prepares here)

## Known Facts

- Party's primary vessel
- Can fly above storms
- Has a bridge where party observes and navigates
- Has some form of barrier that keeps external entities out
- Nickname with multiple spellings: Lloyd, Lloid, Loyd
