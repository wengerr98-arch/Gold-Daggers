# Lotti

## Known Information

Lotti is a celestial or aasimar-adjacent entity connected to the legendary tavern known as the [[Silent Coin Taproom]] and its death-day logbook. She self-identifies as "Divienne" when asked for her name, and exists slightly out of phase with normal perception. She was forced to write a black-star death-day entry in the logbook, suggesting some form of binding or compulsion connected to the tavern. In Session 092 she revealed that [[Jetsi]] was her brother and shifted from guarded and fearful to more openly cooperative after the party's reassurance and the black flower pin, though she remained visibly alarmed about the apples.

## Session Appearances

- Session 065: Appears in the dream-state sequence in the legendary tavern (Silent Coin Taproom); responds to BB with the name "Divienne"; legend-lore confirms celestial/aasimar-adjacent nature; forced to write a black-star death-day entry in the logbook.
- Session 070: Wanda (alias for the Queen of Wands entity) places a statue of Lotti in Aasimar form at the base near the old tavern site — a representation of Lotti in her celestial form rather than the form the party knows; this is a distinct and significant act, suggesting Wanda has knowledge of Lotti's true nature.
- Session 073: Lotti's book (the original logbook) is a key object — the party investigates it, creates a Fabricate-copy to hand to the elves as a fake, and adds invisible-ink paw-print marks to both the real and fake versions. Lotti's book is then held by [[Muryn]], who carries it in a tin container after the exchange.
- Session 092: Lotti is followed by the party to the graveyard behind the tavern; she reveals that Jetsi was her brother and that his name is preserved on graves. She shifts from guarded and fearful to more openly cooperative after the party's reassurance and the black flower pin. She remains visibly alarmed about the apples.

## Known Facts

- Connected to the legendary tavern ([[Silent Coin Taproom]]) and its death-day logbook; forced to write a black-star entry in the logbook during the Session 065 dream encounter.
- Self-identifies as "Divienne" when asked for her name; the name Lotti is the historical or external reference to the same figure (Session 065).
- Celestial or aasimar-adjacent in nature, confirmed by legend-lore in Session 065.
- A statue of Lotti in her Aasimar form (not her familiar form) was placed by Wanda at the base near the old tavern site in Session 070; Wanda appears to know Lotti's true celestial form.
- Lotti's original book was studied and duplicated (Fabricate) by the party in Session 073; both real and fake copies were marked with invisible-ink paw prints; the real book was subsequently carried by [[Muryn]] in a tin container.
- In Session 092, Lotti confirmed that [[Jetsi]] was her brother and that his name is preserved on the graves; her attitude shifted from guarded/fearful to more cooperative after receiving the black flower pin, though she remained alarmed about the apples.
