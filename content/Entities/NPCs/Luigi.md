# Luigi

## Known Information

Luigi is a doorman and contact for [[Randrus Torren]] at [[The Tower]] quarter in [[Aarum]], acting as a gatekeeper who controls access to Torren. He let the party in and personally escorted them to Torren during Session 093, suggesting a trusted role in Torren's household operation.

## Session Appearances

- Session 093: Let the party in and escorted them to Torren.

## Known Facts

- Doorman and gatekeeper for [[Randrus Torren]] at [[The Tower]] quarter in [[Aarum]].
- Escorted the party to Torren on their arrival (Session 093).
