# Madame Dimdelle

## Known Information

Madame Dimdelle was the owner of a brothel in [[Aarum]] housed in a Pocket Dimension, and the leader of the White Rose faction operating there. She also engaged in covert magical and surgical modification of individuals and possessed a Cithrul holy text containing cipher notes associated with Dr. Mason, connecting her to broader portal and planar operations. She was killed by the Gold Daggers during their assault in the Entertainment District in Session 041, after which her Pocket Dimension facility was dismantled and approximately 120–130 sex workers were freed.

## Session Appearances

- Session 037: Named by King Azan Aeducan as one of four crime leaders in Aarum the Gold Daggers are sent to intimidate; identified as running a brothel in a Pocket Dimension.
- Session 038: Barzan scries on her; observed wearing a pink business suit and hermelin scarf while using magical tools and a scalpel to modify a dwarf — purpose unclear.
- Session 041: Spotted on a balcony in the Entertainment District watching the party after they pursue Yazi; appears at the end of the street watching the party leave the district; confirmed deceased at the session's end — killed and overkilled by the Gold Daggers during their offensive.
- Session 042: Her Pocket Dimension facility is discovered and dismantled by the Gold Daggers; approximately 120–130 former sex workers are freed; her Cithrul bible (containing FC-cipher notes and possible Dr. Mason annotations), blackmail documents, anatomical studies, and operation plans are recovered from her Bag of Holding; the human operation plans are burned by the party; her White Rose faction and district territory become a primary negotiation topic with the Tower, Torren, Nasri, and Dakun.
- Session 078: Referenced by Torren during his intelligence briefing; her prior connection to the Cithrul network and portal operations remains a live thread even after her death.
- Session 091: Referenced (as "Madame Dimdale") in the party's post-Skjall recap and re-planning discussion; her Cithrul bible and connection to the Mason/portal network remain relevant background context.
