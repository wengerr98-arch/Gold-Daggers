# Maer

## Known Information

Maer is a member of [[Gregorius]]'s team at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]], encountered during the monastery drinking contest in Session 032. He served as [[Barzan]]'s opponent in the contest and narrowly lost, making him a minor background figure within the monastery community.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 031]]: Present at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]] during the harvest feast and trial period; part of the monastery community.
- [[Session 032]]: Named member of [[Gregorius]]'s drinking-contest team; [[Barzan]]'s opponent; narrowly lost.

## Known Facts

- Member of [[Gregorius]]'s team at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]].
- Competed against [[Barzan]] in the monastery drinking contest; narrowly lost (Session 032).
