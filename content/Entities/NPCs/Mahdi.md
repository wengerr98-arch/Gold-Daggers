# Mahdi

## Known Information

Mahdi is an Earth Genasi bookseller and scholarly text dealer operating at the [[Genasi Market]] in [[Kasta]]. He shared detailed Forgotten War lore with [[Barzan]] — including information about [[Kaldera]] and the theoretical connection between [[Levistus]] and Genasi descent — and provided the party with a star chart and sextant to navigate to the [[Anubis]] pyramid site. His cartographic materials have remained central to the party's desert gate research through multiple subsequent sessions.

## Session Appearances

- Session 080: Earth Genasi vendor and scholar at [[Kasta]] Genasi market; gave the party a star chart and sextant; shared Forgotten War lore with [[Barzan]].
- Session 082: His map and blood-trail clues formed the basis of the party's desert route; the star chart guided the party toward the Anubis site, ultimately leading them into a buried chamber with a golem and homunculi.
- Session 086: The "Map of the Mahdi" referenced during the party's library research into ley-lines, Genasi history, and lore from the Kasta library raid.
- [[Session 087]]: Contacted by the party about books connected to the [[House of Wisdom]] and Genasi lore; his map continued to guide desert gate research.
