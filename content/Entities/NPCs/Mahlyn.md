# Mahlyn

![[Mahlyn.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Mahlyn is a female Triton revolutionary affiliated with the Stevos/Kevos group operating in [[Helikonos]]. She serves as a messenger and advance scout within the network, and went ahead during Session 052 to warn revolutionary leadership about [[Eloryn]]'s identity or presence — indicating she holds a trusted liaison role within the organisation.

## Session Appearances

- Session 052: Went ahead to warn revolutionary leadership about [[Eloryn]]; part of the Stevos/Kevos group operating in [[Helikonos]].
