# Mara

![[mara.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Mara is a child who was trapped with [[Robin]] in a dome during the attack on the Entertainment District. She was found with two other children in a cellar and rescued by the party, using the cover name Aram during the escape. Her mother reportedly works in some form of administration.

## Session Appearances

- Session 018: One of three rescued children found in the cellar; adopts cover name Aram (alias) during the escape.

## Known Facts

- Child rescued from captivity in the Entertainment District dome attack.
- Was trapped alongside [[Robin]] during the attack.
- Alias used during escape: Aram.
- Mother reportedly works in administration (specific role unknown).
