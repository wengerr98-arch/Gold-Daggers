# Maradak Dreamkiller

## Known Information

Maradak Dreamkiller — also known as the Torr, the Faceless King, and the Grave King — was the ruler of [[Skjall]] and uncle of [[Natok]]. He suffered a nightly transformation into a large deformed skeletoid figure that dragged corpses into the crypts, which he denied knowledge of when confronted; locals believed he was blocking their souls' path north after death. He was killed by Leumas in Session 075, though as of Session 091 his cosmological influence appears to persist through the portal split between "the Demogorgon side" and "the Grave King side."

## Session Appearances

- Session 049: Vault room bearing this name discovered in [[Zentralbank]] Hades sub-level; [[Key to the Door in Skjall]], bloody weapons, severed heads, and population documents referencing the [[Wild Hunt]] recovered.
- Session 058: The party meets Maradak Dreamkiller in person as the Torr (king) of [[Skjall]]. He appears exhausted and close to operating on instinct; revealed to be [[Natok]]'s uncle. That night, a large deformed skeletoid figure emerges from the crypts, drags a huge corpse, and attacks the hall — the party concludes this monster and the king are the same being. After a gong sounds, the armored figure removes his armor and resumes his kingly form.
- Session 059: The King is confronted about his nightly activities; gives a defensive explanation and denies knowing what is happening. Lorilla rolls a natural 20 Insight on him. He tells the party they are crossing a line and orders them to leave. BB asks if he can touch her cards. The party sneaks back and observes him opening a door by gesture, letting in four monsters he then fights off. After a short rest a horn sounds and a feathered figure appears through the gate; deformed humanoids emerge from the area. Locals believe he is blocking their souls' path north after death.
- Session 060: A massive battle (estimated 30,000–40,000 fighters) takes place in the Thronsaal; the Torr fights at the front and is slow to recognise the party. The party observes the Grave King (Skelettmann) form dragging a corpse toward the Wild Hunt, wearing a gladiator mask and red cloak with an aura that distorts the world around him. The gate behind the Grave King appears damaged and worn.
- Session 061: The king permits the party to hunt and capture the shapeshifter/impostor; reveals he does not want people dying the way other kings did; the party speaks with him about the Wall's malfunction and the need to recover collective knowledge.
- Session 062: The party presents The Mother (contained in an obsidian tube) and the red-haired Goliath woman; the king reacts strongly to her mother's name. Leumas meditates before the throne and senses a red thread connecting himself to the king through a death ritual. BB notices a shadow behind the king tied to moonlight. The throne room wall opens slightly to reveal a wall of many eyes and a larger hidden presence.
- Session 064: The party confronts Maradak in a winter-storm sequence attempting to communicate with the Winter Goddess; the scene destabilises. The name Apakan is raised as a prior or alternate royal reference.
- Session 065: The king publicly addresses all clan leaders — Karoth, Nameo, Kiathen, Erasen, Tazar, Bog, Aseter, Moo, and others — revealing the monument threat, the undead and living stone/trees pressing on Skjall, and the need to sacrifice people or monsters to the Wild Hunt monument. He imposes curfews, battle suspensions, patrol groups, guard rotations, and an evacuation/registration system. The party concludes the legendary tavern is known to both the king and Tazar.
- Session 067: The king speaks privately with the party via telepathic conversation; confirms the wall has been damaged and the forest beyond it is dying — both tied to Dr. Mason's activity. Coordinates a defense and evacuation after a major earthquake, avalanches, and a cold force strike the area; souls begin emerging from corpses.
- Session 068: Directs defenses from behind a barricaded Thronsaal hall as planar instability surges; reports the Wintergöttin is hostile, not approachable, and already wounded; has not yet fully rested.
- Session 069: Works alongside the party in the Thronsaal as Mason's undead army — zombies, ghosts, soulnados, and Dyrrn — assaults Skjall. On the front lines as the obelisk rises and the first assault begins; at session's end, on the ground alongside Mason and Felix.
- Session 070: The party survives the Thronsaal collapse and falls through a hole; the king's immediate fate after being downed in Session 069 remains unresolved during this session.
- Session 075: Leumas determines the king cannot be Mason's killer and makes a decisive choice — he kills the king. A giant eagle form (Lorilla's wild shape) carries Leumas and the king's body away. The king's death destabilizes Skjall's leadership.
- Session 076: The death of Maradak Dreamkiller is publicly announced to the people of Skjall; Leumas addresses the crowd and claims the new kingship/leadership role.
- Session 089: Referenced as "the king" in the session's future-vision sequence. Maradak's legacy and the Skjall kingship transition form background context for multiple-futures visions.
- Session 091: Referenced as "the Grave King" — the Great Hirt reveals that the portal is split between a Demogorgon side and a Grave King side, suggesting Maradak's death has not ended his cosmological influence.
