# Marius Wehl

## Known Information

Marius Wehl is one of [[Odyss Trion]]'s four generals, described as a Hauptstratege (chief strategist). He was captured in Odyss's villa during the Stygian Gala raid (Session 103) and later delivered to the Ophris magic prison on behalf of Emperor [[Azan Aeducan]] (Session 105). During transport or imprisonment, a concealed Glyph of Warding in a fake tooth triggered — he survived.

## Session Appearances

- Session 103: Named by BB and Barzan during interrogation of captured personnel in Odyss Trion's villa. Identified as Hauptstratege (chief strategist) among Odyss's four generals.
- Session 104: Referenced as a captured general during the continued villa operation.
- Session 105: Delivered to the Ophris magic prison by the Gold Daggers. A fake tooth containing a Glyph of Warding attempted to detonate — Marius Wehl survived. Part of the handover to Emperor Aazan at the Ophris Palace, which earned the party a 600,000 gold payout.

## Known Facts

- Role: Hauptstratege (chief strategist)

- Had a fake tooth with a concealed Glyph of Warding — detonation attempt in Ophris magic prison (Session 105), survived
- Delivered to Ophris magic prison and handed to Emperor Azan Aeducan (Session 105)
