# Melissa

![[Melissa.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Melissa is one of three sisters — daughters of [[Felix]] — along with [[Aubonne]] and [[Galatea]], connected to the Magic Shop in Dalior. She was involved in holding Koda and was capable of telepathic communication, but was later reported missing in Session 014 before ultimately being confirmed dead by Session 024. Her name appears smeared and crossed out on a family portrait found in Aubonne's hidden drawer, and her death forms a significant part of Aubonne's grief.

## Session Appearances

- Session 006: Argues with Aubonne, guides party to Magic Shop, is slapped by Aubonne during confrontation. Referred to as "Schwester von Aubonne vom Magic-Shop."
- Session 008: Snix receives a direct telepathic contact from Melissa's voice; she mentions that Koda is being "kept back for a few more hours," implying Melissa is holding or detaining Koda somewhere.
- Session 010: Referenced by the party as likely related to Galatea; BB identifies Galatea as "95% a sister of Melissa and Aubonne." Melissa does not appear in person this session but is cited as a connection for identifying Galatea.
- Session 012: Referenced indirectly — a tall blond man repairing Felix's house was present during a prior dispute involving Melissa and Aubonne; Melissa's family connection to Galatea and Aubonne further established this session.
- Session 014: Identified on a notice sheet distributed by Aubonne as one of roughly 15 people who disappeared overnight; named among the missing alongside Crank and an older female magic user.
- Session 017: Robin sends the party a message from a cellar saying Melissa is gone and people are approaching, urgently requesting help; Melissa's whereabouts remain unknown.
- Session 018: Contacts Barzan via message from outside the city; confirms everyone is safe and instructs the party to bring Robin to Aubonne. Is at odds with Aubonne over how much magic should be used and whether the city should be changed.
- Session 019: Reported by Aubonne to be away with Felix; whereabouts unknown to the party as of midday.
- Session 024: Confirmed dead by Felix, who informs the party this session. Her children are reported safe by Felix.
- Session 025: Referenced indirectly — Aubonne's emotional breakdown in her bar is connected to grief and the burdens related to Melissa's death and their shared history. The portrait found in Aubonne's hidden drawer lists Melissa's name (smeared and crossed out) among the sisters.
- Session 092: Referenced in the sidecar entities list; her death is part of the ongoing backdrop as the party revisits their connections and losses while planning future arcs.

## Known Facts

- Sister of [[Aubonne]] and [[Galatea]]; daughter of [[Felix]].
- Connected to the Magic Shop in Dalior.
- Capable of telepathic communication — contacted Snix directly in Session 008.
- Was holding or detaining Koda as of Session 008 and implied she would release them within a few hours.
- Listed as missing in Session 014 — among roughly 15 people who disappeared overnight, named on a notice sheet distributed by Aubonne.
- As of Session 018, was outside the city and safe; sent Barzan a message instructing the party to bring Robin to Aubonne.
- Held a different view from Aubonne on the use of magic and whether the city should be changed (Session 018).
- As of Session 019, reportedly away with Felix; whereabouts unknown to the party.
- Confirmed dead as of Session 024; Felix informs the party of her death. Her children are reported safe.
- Her name appears smeared and crossed out on a sisters' portrait found in Aubonne's hidden drawer (Session 025).
