# Millie

![[Millie.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Millie is the sister of [[Silk]] and a Tabaxi NPC who is deceased but continues to exist within a Pocket Dimension. Her survival in this form is made possible by [[Cetzy]], and [[Eloryn]] is able to enter the Pocket Dimension — accessed through a magical shell gifted to Eloryn by [[Cetzy]] — where Millie resides.

## Known Facts

- Sister of [[Silk]].
- Race: Tabaxi.
- Deceased; exists within a Pocket Dimension sustained by [[Cetzy]].
- [[Eloryn]] can enter the Pocket Dimension via a magical shell received from [[Cetzy]].
- Home listed as "Muschel" (the shell / Pocket Dimension).
