# Milly

## Known Information

Milly (also known as Chrazi, Kratzer, Dudette, and Mili) is [[Silk]]'s younger sister, approximately 14–15 years old. Originally living on the island of [[Safti]] in [[Amarre]], she was encountered by the party in Aarum in Session 044. By Session 071 she is identified as long dead but undead, communicable through a shell — the party used this connection to ask about BB's soul.

## Session Appearances

- Session 025: Named by Silk during the farewell scene; Silk entrusts the party with a diary to deliver to her sister on Safti. Named "Kratzer (Chrazi)" in session notes but the reviewer notes she is mostly referred to as "Milly."
- Session 044: Encountered in Aarum under her nickname "Dudette"; she is with Silk when Eloryn meets them. The party makes contact with her in Aarum, advancing the diary delivery mission objective.
- Session 065: Appears (as "Mili") in Eloryn's dream-state vision — the tea-party dream sequence includes her alongside Blobbert and a tabaxi resembling Silk. Her appearance in the dream imagery is unresolved.
- Session 071: Referenced as "Mili" — speaks from a shell and is identified by the party as long dead but undead; she cannot assist with the shell-based request, and the party also asks her about BB's soul through the shell communication.
- Session 077: Named as "Mili" in the sidecar; referenced alongside Silk in the context of Demogorgon-related lore being discussed and skryed by the party near the session's close.

## Known Facts

- Younger sister of [[Silk]]; approximately 14–15 years old.
- Originally based on the island of [[Safti]] in [[Amarre]].
- Known aliases: Chrazi, Kratzer (German: "scratcher"), Dudette, Mili; canonical vault name is Milly.
- The party accepted a mission to deliver Silk's diary to her (Session 025).
- Encountered in Aarum in Session 044 alongside Silk.
- Confirmed long dead but undead as of Session 071; communicable through a shell.
