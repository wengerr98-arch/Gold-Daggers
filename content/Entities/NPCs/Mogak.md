## Known Information

Mogak is a goliath approximately 2.40m tall who served as former Kommandant of the [[Chrom-Brigade]], one of Nolia's main offensive attack forces. He was imprisoned in the Oubliettes for killing his superior officer over unjust orders — described as fair-minded and blunt. In Session 022 he became a key coordinator of the prison escape.
