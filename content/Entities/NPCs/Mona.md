## Known Information

Mona (also known as Mona Fabrics) is a fabric merchant from [[Amarre]] who travels widely to source new trends and materials for markets such as the Herbstkirche trade. The party encountered her at Lerchen harbour, where she was waiting for her boat to be repaired before continuing her journey toward Ophris. She is known for sharing useful intelligence and proved a valuable contact, reporting that Wizard Island had resurfaced and arranging a clothing deal with the party.

## Session Appearances

- Session 042: Named in Barzan and Leumas's correspondence — receives letters from the party as they settle the Pocket Dimension arrangement; a contact in Amarre named in the session's correspondence arc.
- Session 050: Encountered in person at Lerchen harbour; her boat is being repaired at Brovin's shipyard. Travelling from Lerchen toward Ophris (the Amarrian capital) to explore new trends and fabric for the Herbstkirche market. Made a clothing deal with the party — including a specially made outfit for Koda that won't tear during wildshape shifts. Sold Eloryn a chain. Reported that Wizard Island has resurfaced. Arranged to send fabric and a tailor to Don Hor and Udir.
