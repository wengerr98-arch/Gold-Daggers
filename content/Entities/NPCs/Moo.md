# Moo

## Known Information

Moo is a named member of the Skjall council. He was present at the monument council gathering in Session 065, assembled alongside other clan leaders including [[Maradak Dreamkiller]], [[Karoth]], [[Nameo]], [[Tazar]], [[Kiathen]], [[Erasen]], [[Bog]], and [[Aseter]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 065: Named in the monument council gathering before the party's address to the Skjall clan leaders.
