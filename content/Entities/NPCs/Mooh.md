Mooh is the patron and apparent owner of [[Moohs Blues]], the premier jazz club inside [[Skjall]]. He was encountered sitting on a stage/pedestal in a suit, and gave [[Leumas]] a bottle of fancy rum ("Very Vanill") during the party's visit to Skjall.
