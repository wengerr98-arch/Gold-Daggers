## Known Information

Murtada is one of the kings (Könige) of [[Amare]], a maritime realm with twelve island rulers. He is primarily known in relation to geopolitical tensions between Amare, Ophris, and Moria — his envoy to Nolia was killed in an assassination in [[Dalior]], and the Amarier (his people) were used as scapegoats in the framing surrounding the Ophris–Moria war.

## Session Appearances

- Session 016: Referenced in connection with the Amarier and geopolitical tensions around Ophris and Moria; the Amarier (Murtada's people) are named as being scapegoated in framing related to the war.
- Session 021: Referenced indirectly when BB asks Amare-related questions at Silk's location during the pre-prison-break resupply; Murtada is one of the twelve kings of Amare whose affairs are discussed.
- Session 025: Referenced when Yorni discusses the parade assassination — the interior minister target is connected to Amare politics, and Murtada's name comes up as one of the island kings with interests in the region.

## Known Facts

- One of the twelve kings of [[Amare]].

- The Amarier (people of Amare / Murtada's subjects) were scapegoated in the framing surrounding the Ophris–Moria war (Session 016).
