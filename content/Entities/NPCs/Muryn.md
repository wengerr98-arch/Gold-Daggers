## Known Information

Muryn is [[Eloryn]]'s aunt, an elven woman who left [[Helykonos]] years before the campaign began in order to defeat [[Shen]] and free her people. She is a highly martial figure who joined a thieves' guild in Helikonos after departing the city, and she has been pursuing a plan to find a bendigs stone north of the Wild Hunt to bind Shen. By Session 101 she had allied with the Frost Giants and committed to helping defend [[Skjall]], representing a major milestone in her long-running mission.

## Session Appearances

- Session 015: Indirectly referenced — [[Exandria von Amare]] is identified as likely knowing Muryn, but does not confirm the connection.
- Session 056: Barzan scries Eloryn's aunt — a snowbound, highly martial vision is revealed, featuring a falchion, a shark, a beast, and blue-flame eyes; the party interprets this as pointing toward Skjall as a destination.
- Session 072: Encountered in person inside the hidden tavern space in the Minotaur quarter; initially confused by the time-shifted situation. Reveals she joined a thieves' guild in Helikonos, that the cloister events happened roughly half a year earlier from her perspective, and that she plans to acquire a familiar empowered by the dragon scale choker, then use it to bind Shen with the bendigs stone. Gives the party a compass needle and gives blood to Eloryn, strengthening their connection.
- Session 073: Reconnects with the party at the Magic Taverne ([[The Silent Dance Tap Room]]); is carrying the original logbook in a tin container. Passes the Page of Swords tarot card to Wanda (BB). Departs afterward.
- Session 078: Referenced as "Myrin" (variant spelling) as an active background thread in the party's planning around Aarum/Ophris.
- Session 089: Referenced as "Murin" (alias) in the session's future-vision sequence; Eloryn's lineage ties keep Muryn relevant as a background thread.
- Session 090: Referenced as a possible link for Eloryn following Eloryn's recovery at the Entertainment District.
- Session 101: The party travels to meet Muryn and the Frost Giants on the Astral Sea; Muryn and the Frost Giants commit to helping Skjall — a significant development in her mission and the broader war effort.
- Session 103: Target of a Sending from the party warning about Odyss Trion posing as Torr and/or Leumas at the Stygian Gala. Note: referred to as "Murin" in session text — confirmed alias (already noted in this stub).
