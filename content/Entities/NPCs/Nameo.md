## Known Information

Nameo is a Goliath spiritual leader and co-head of the Goliath Library (Goliath Bibliothek) in [[Skjall]], sharing leadership with [[Karoth]]. He has been a consistent presence across the party's dealings with the Goliath faction in Skjall, representing their interests at multi-faction councils and continuing to support the party following events such as the defeat of The Mother and the Wild Hunt crisis. The canonical spelling is "Nameo"; "Naemo" appears in early session notes and is an alias.
