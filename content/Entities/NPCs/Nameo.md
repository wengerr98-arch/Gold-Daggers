# Nameo

![[NAMEO.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Nameo is a Goliath spiritual leader and co-head of the Goliath Library (Goliath Bibliothek) in [[Skjall]], sharing leadership with [[Karoth]]. He has been a consistent presence across the party's dealings with the Goliath faction in Skjall, representing their interests at multi-faction councils and continuing to support the party following events such as the defeat of The Mother and the Wild Hunt crisis. The canonical spelling is "Nameo"; "Naemo" appears in early session notes and is an alias.

## Session Appearances

- Session 059: Co-leads the Goliath Library in the Goliath Viertel of Skjall; spiritual leader of the Goliath community; party (BB and Barzan) conducts research at the library.
- Session 062: Referenced as part of the Goliath faction leadership at the leaders' meeting following the defeat of The Mother in the throne room.
- Session 063: Referenced in context of the Goliath quarter and the party's investigation of the obelisk; present as part of the ongoing Goliath leadership structure.
- Session 064: Referenced as part of the Goliath leadership structure during the party's winter-storm and dream-state confrontations in Skjall.
- Session 065: Present as one of the Skjall clan leaders in the assembly convened by the king to reveal the monument threat and Wild Hunt crisis; represents the Goliath faction in the multi-faction council briefing.
- Session 066: Referenced in the Skjall context as the group returns to the Sanktum and interacts with the king and faction leaders following the Wintergöttin encounter.
- Session 077: Reunites with the party at the Volk settlement after the Wild Hunt is pushed back; among the Goliath allies present as the party sets new objectives.
