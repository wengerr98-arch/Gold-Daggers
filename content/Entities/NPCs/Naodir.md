# Naodir

![[Naodir.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Naodir is a dwarf who serves as the Forge Boss (Schmiedeboss) at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]. He is compact and stocky with distinctively red hair, and is notably hard of hearing and not very talkative.
