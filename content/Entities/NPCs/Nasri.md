## Known Information

Nasri is the dominant co-leader of [[The Tower]] (der Turm) in [[Aarum]], running the faction alongside her brother [[Dakun]]. A dwarf-elf of around twenty years old with artificer abilities, she is the sole possessor of the Tower's munitions recipes and production capacity, making her indispensable to its operations. Over time she has developed a cautious but warming working relationship with the party, marked by a tense magical confrontation in Session 044 that she ultimately de-escalated herself.

## Session Appearances

- Session 037: One of four wanted-poster crime leaders in [[Aarum]] that the party is sent to intimidate; co-leads "der Turm" with her brother; identified as the dominant figure of the pair.
- Session 041: Encountered inside the Tower; confirmed as co-leader alongside [[Dakun]]; revealed as the sole possessor of the Tower's munitions recipe and production capacity.
- Session 042: Attended the joint dinner in the Pocket Dimension with Torren and the government; became noticeably more open after negotiations; agreed to have the Tower care for the freed sex workers and participate in the new district governance structure.
- Session 044: Present at the public meeting organised by Torren; revealed weapon production plans including a revolver rifle design for mass production. Tattoo comparison with Barzan triggered a black cloud and lightning display; she lowered her weapon and removed the ammunition rather than escalating.
- Session 056: Party visits Nasri at the tower/library in [[Aarum]] during a resupply stop; session ends at a shooting range with Nasri and [[Dakun]]; working relationship becoming more comfortable.
- Session 057: Group resumes shooting range activity from S056; Nasri remains an active contact during the party's downtime and supply operations in [[Aarum]].
- Session 078: Party meets Nasri at the tower during a crossroads planning stop; she provides military and production updates ahead of the group's teleportation to [[Ophris]].
- Session 095: Referenced during the party's Aarum downtime; continues to operate the Tower with [[Dakun]] while the party researches and crafts during the post-Ylvar recovery period.
