## Known Information

Natok is a Goliath blacksmith first encountered working at a forge in [[Dalior]], notable for his imposing size (250 cm), heavy tattoos, and black, ash-grey hands from forge work. He was among the Nolia soldiers and craftspeople liberated by the Gold Daggers in [[Helykonos]], after which he joined the crew aboard [[Loid]]. He is the nephew of [[Maradak Dreamkiller]], the Torr (king) of [[Skjall]], and developed an ongoing pen-friendship with [[Snix]] after being sent to the front.

## Session Appearances

- Session 056: In Aarum, Natok is given gold by the party to buy materials for the forge; the group stocks up for the planned journey to Skjall. Natok is treated as a valuable contact and supplier for forge-related shopping.
- Session 057: During downtime in Aarum, Lorilla helps Natok set up the forge; he is actively integrated into the party's base operations.
- Session 058: Natok accompanies the party to Skjall and gives them practical insight into how Skjall works — its factions, layout, and social dynamics; confirmed to be the nephew of the Torr, [[Maradak Dreamkiller]].
- Session 067: [[Snix]] dances with Natok during the group's social gathering and music session in the Bierhalle before the earthquake strikes Skjall.
- Session 077: Named as part of the reunion at the Volk settlement; the party reconnects with Natok and others after successfully pushing back the Wild Hunt.
- Session 078: Referenced as part of the party's ongoing crew; the group is at Aarum/Ophris this session and Natok remains part of the background crew context.

## Known Facts

- Race: Goliath.
- Blacksmith (Schmied); originally worked at a forge in [[Dalior]].
- Physical description: 250 cm tall, heavy tattoos, hands are black and ashy from forge work, works shirtless.
- Nephew of [[Maradak Dreamkiller]], the Torr (king) of [[Skjall]] — family connection confirmed in Session 058.
- Part of a group of Nolia soldiers and craftspeople liberated by the Gold Daggers in [[Helykonos]]; [[Treth]]'s father was also in that group.
- Joined the party's crew aboard [[Loid]] after liberation.
- Began a pen-friendship (Brieffreundschaft) with [[Snix]] after being sent to the front.
- Received forge materials funded by the party (Session 056) and had his forge set up in Aarum with Lorilla's help (Session 057).
- Provided the party with practical local knowledge about Skjall's layout, factions, and social dynamics on arrival (Session 058).
