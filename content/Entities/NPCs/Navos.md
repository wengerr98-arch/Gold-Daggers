# Navos

![[Navos.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Navos is a middle-aged Triton revolutionary figure in [[Helikonos]], holding the position of "left hand" of the revolutionary army. He appears in the strategy meeting scene alongside Uthrin (the head of command) and [[Kodos]] (former third hand), forming part of the core Triton revolutionary command structure.

## Session Appearances

- Session 052: Appeared in the revolutionary strategy meeting in [[Helikonos]]; identified as the "left hand" of the revolutionary army.

## Known Facts

- Race: Triton

- Role: "Left hand" of the revolutionary army in [[Helikonos]]
- Part of the Triton revolutionary command structure alongside [[Uthrin]] (the head) and [[Kodos]] (former third hand)
