# Nigea

## Known Information

Nigea is the highest-ranking operative in [[Dr. Fitz Mason|Dr. Mason]]'s spy class assigned to learn how the [[Skjall]] wall works. She served as the handler for Beat (formerly Vokon) and was named by him after his revivification in Session 065 when he volunteered intelligence on Mason's network.

## Session Appearances

- Session 065: Named by Beat as the highest-ranking operative in Mason's spy class assigned to investigate the Skjall wall; identified as Beat's handler.
