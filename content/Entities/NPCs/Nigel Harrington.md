## Known Information

Nigel Harrington was the builder of [[El Loco]] in [[Dalior]], who died approximately eight years before Session 007. His daughter went missing during construction of the building at around 16 years of age; the skeleton of a young woman found entombed in El Loco's wall is believed to be her, showing two broken cervical vertebrae consistent with strangulation or murder. His son (also named Harrington) has since also died.

## Session Appearances

- Session 007: Named via city records during an inquiry into the skeleton found in [[El Loco]]'s wall. Both Nigel and his son are deceased.
