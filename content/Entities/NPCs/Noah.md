## Known Information

Noah (also called Noa) is a gardener who originally worked at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]], where he tended the monastery's herb garden and became the love interest of [[Lorilla]]. He later transitioned to working as one of the employed healers sent from [[Ophris]] to [[Skjall|Skjall (Stadt)]], lodging in the Gastquartier. Throughout the Skjall crisis he served as a medical operative and the party's key contact in the healer quarter, sending a blood-stained letter to Lorilla requesting urgent help and performing surgery to extract parasitic worms from Leumas and Barzan.

## Known Facts

- Gardener (Gärtner) at [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]; love interest of [[Lorilla]].
- Responsible for the monastery herb garden and fields.
- Old acquaintance of Barzan; the two reconnected warmly in Session 032.
- Sent from [[Ophris]] to [[Skjall|Skjall (Stadt)]] as one of multiple healers; lodges in the Gastquartier.
- Becomes Lorilla's friendly contact and lodging point in Skjall.
- The Ophris healers in Skjall appear embedded in an intelligence/spy network; many visitors at the spy meeting were Ophris representatives.
- Sends a blood-stained letter to Lorilla requesting urgent help during the Skjall healer-quarter attacks.
- Performed surgery removing parasitic worms from Leumas and Barzan (Session 063); both recovered.
- Confirmed safe after the Thronsaal collapse (Session 070).
