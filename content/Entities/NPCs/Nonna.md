## Known Information

Nonna is an elderly dwarf woman (boggel, approximately 1m15 tall) and the grandmother of Torren, associated with the Torren family restaurant in [[Aarum]]. She was seen kneading dough and calling for Torren during Session 078, making her a named recurring character tied to [[The Tower]] faction through her family connection.
