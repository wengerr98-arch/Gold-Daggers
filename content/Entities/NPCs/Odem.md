## Known Information

Odem is a near-blind rogue in his mid-40s, with dirty-blonde hair, decorative neck tattoos, and nearly white eyes. Remnant handcuff rings on his wrists hint at a history of captivity; he is originally from Dalior/Nolia and was freed from the Oubliettes by the party. He acted as an ally and guide through the underground forest, and the party encouraged him to team up with [[Kebasch]].
