# Odyss Trion

## Known Information

Odyss Trion is a bounty hunter who has been actively pursuing the Gold Daggers party since at least Session 044. He was re-encountered in a wealthy district of the Material Plane after the party returned from Stygia, suggesting he tracks them across planar boundaries. In Session 101, Leumas successfully impersonated him at the Stygia ball and gained access to his sphere, revealing that Odyss Trion holds an active role in Stygia's political landscape with connections to war planning.

## Session Appearances

- Session 044: Openly establishes himself as a bounty hunter actively pursuing the Gold Daggers party.
- Session 100: Encountered again in a wealthy district of the Material Plane after the party returns from Stygia; his reappearance at this juncture is flagged as a significant development.
- Session 101: Referenced as an active antagonist; Leumas impersonates Odyss Trion at the Stygia ball to observe war planning and gains access to his sphere through the impersonation.
- Session 102: Major session — two distinct reveals:
  1. **Backstory vision (BB):** BB experiences a detailed vision of Odyss's life. He grew up on a borderline tropical island in Amare (far south, Greek architecture), in a Tabaxi-like society. As a child he was larger, broader, and crueler than his peers — bullied others, was praised by parents and celebrated locally, but disliked in private circles. Left the island, repeatedly overreached: tried to storm a dragon turtle (repelled by spells), later attacked a dragon (crew killed, he alone survived). Began treating crews as disposable. Met [[Dr. Mason]] in Nolia — tried to kill him, was killed and revived by Mason instead. Mason was careless; Odyss stole a scalpel and escaped. Saw the third plate and returned to Mason's service anyway. Later shifted allegiance to [[Yorni]] (Jorni), spending years on procurement work for him. Goal: become the greatest and most powerful. When the war broke out he volunteered for the front — was refused — went anyway, massacred Tritons in the south. Now acts entirely on his own ambition with no restraint.
  2. **At the Ball:** Odyss arrived disguised as a Leonin in a Jute Sack (his cover at the Ball); [[Sani Ambulan]] (entourage affiliation unspecified) pointed him out to [[BB]]. He then shapeshifted into [[Leumas]] to challenge [[Asmodeus]] to a duel; [[Barzan]]'s Subtle Spell reversed the disguise. After the party won *Die Wette des Hadeses*, Odyss challenged [[Leumas]] directly to a dance battle (appearing as a broad, sun-bronzed Minotaur from a Stygian estate of ~650 slaves). The dance was a 15-round contested social HP battle; Odyss ultimately outlasted Leumas, who was ejected from the Gala with charisma depleted.
- Session 103: Major confrontation — the party raided Odyss Trion's villa during the Stygian Gala. Odyss was posing as both Torr and Leumas during the Gala to confuse targets. Party sent Sendings to Thautok and Muryn to warn about his deceptions. Party discovered his villa contained a Mystery Room with Yum Ka'ax imprisoned in fox form.
- Session 104: Continued confrontation at Odyss's villa. Party discovered Enara imprisoned inside the Void Shard. Party also fought the Hushes and looted the villa.
- Session 105: Final confrontation — Odyss defeated in combat (round 12). In Torr form at time of defeat; Snix acquired the [[Mantle of the Torr]] from him. BB attempted (and failed, Athletics 14 vs 30) to stuff Odyss in tortoise form into the Void Shard.
