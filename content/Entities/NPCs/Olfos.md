## Known Information

Olfos was a named [[Tritons|Triton]] warrior and combat leader, described as "the oldest but still strongest fighter." He functioned as a spearhead commander during the Triton attack on the party's vessel and carried a magical red Returning tattoo that contracted into a needle upon his death. He was beheaded by the party in Session 050; the tattoo then transferred to Koda, who became its new bearer.

## Session Appearances

- Session 050: Named Triton boss; led the Triton attack on the party's sea vessel; used magical tattoos with special abilities; defeated in combat (beheaded).
- Session 051: After death, his red tattoo contracted and left his body as a needle — identified as a Returning tattoo; the party decided Koda should bear it, and BB painted it onto Koda's hand.
- Session 052: Referenced in historical/contextual discussion as one of the former Triton admirals alongside Nemnis and Jinnis; mentioned in context of the revolutionary leadership around the Helikonos command structure.
