## Known Information

Oliver is a street food vendor operating in the Entertainment District of Dalior, renowned for his meat skewers (old vault: "Beste Spiessli"). He is a recurring background presence in the district across numerous sessions, selling skewers to partygoers, the Gold Daggers themselves, and festival crowds. Though a minor NPC, he is a consistent thread tying the party to the Entertainment District community.

## Session Appearances

- Session 007: Brief mention — Snix steals meat skewers from him in the Entertainment District.
- Session 019: Party purchases meat skewers from Oliver (or his stall) as part of their food acquisition run for the upcoming conflict.
- Session 020: Active in the Entertainment District during the street festival following the fireworks incident; sells skewers to partygoers while the district fills with crowds.
- Session 025: Present in the Entertainment District on the first festival day during the Stadtfest; Oliver's skewer stand is part of the market atmosphere.
- Session 055: Mentioned among the crew the party plans to check in on after the camp assault; his situation in the Entertainment District is flagged as a loose thread.
- Session 056: Referenced among the Entertainment District community contacts as the party passes through and resupplies.
- Session 092: Present as part of the background Entertainment District world in the Aarum/Goldie Bräu context.

## Known Facts

- Street food vendor in the Entertainment District of Dalior
- Speciality: meat skewers ("Beste Spiessli")
- Snix stole skewers from him in Session 007
- Sold skewers to the party as provisions in Session 019
- Among the first to reopen his business during the impromptu street festival triggered by the fireworks incident (Session 020)
