# Phönix

## Known Information

Phönix is an Earth Genasi and the twin sister of [[Sturm]]. She was not physically present in Session 022 but was recalled via memory — Lorilla previously saw a statue of her in the first cellar of [[Lervik]] during an earlier session, recognised [[Sturm]]'s face from it, and Sturm confirmed she has a twin named Phönix. She is likely co-namesake of the [[Sturm-Phönix]] guild.

## Session Appearances

- Session 022: Referenced only — Sturm confirms twin sister named Phönix; Lorilla connects her to the Lervik cellar statue.

## Known Facts

- Race: Earth Genasi
- Twin sister of [[Sturm]]
- A statue of her was seen in the first cellar of [[Lervik]]
- Likely co-namesake of the [[Sturm-Phönix]] guild
