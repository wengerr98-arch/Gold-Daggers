## Known Information

Pira Devei was a Director or senior official of the [[Zentralbank]], serving as the session antagonist during the heist in Session 049. She has red hair, beard stubble, and scars, and is a Monk by class. She was subject to a soul-switch during the heist and was killed by the end of the session. [[Silk]] retained her body, intending it for future use connected to the soul-switch ring setup.
