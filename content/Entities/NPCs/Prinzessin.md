# Prinzessin

> Note: Her real name has not yet been learned in-game. "Prinzessin" is a placeholder until the canonical name is confirmed by the DM.

## Known Information

Prinzessin is the wife of [[Yorni]] and lives in the [[Rosenpalast]]. She appeared prominently at the wedding in Session 048, where she rushed to see the new geese and was listed at the tribüne alongside [[Yorni]], [[Das letzte Opfer]], [[Arthur Ruger]], and [[Dr. Fitz Mason]]. She became visibly distressed after the Pit Fiend was killed. Eloryn and Leumas visited her tower during the session under the aliases "Ludmilla and Mathilde."
