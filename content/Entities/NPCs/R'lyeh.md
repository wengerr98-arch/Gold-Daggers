# R'lyeh

Note: "Herley" in the session protocol is a misspelling of R'lyeh — Herley is NOT a separate entity and does not become an alias representing a genuine alternate form. The alias entry here is for search/cross-reference only.

## Known Information

R'lyeh is a pirate known as the "Terror of the Sea" who holds a position in the chain of rule over the seas connected to [[Azatoth]] and the Waterplane. They made a pact of unclear nature and are associated with the Sword of Relay artifact (Schwert vo Relay). R'lyeh is historically linked to both [[Shen]] and [[Blobbert]] through familiar history described in the [[Book of Azatoth]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 052: Revealed through the [[Book of Azatoth]] — R'lyeh is named as connected to the Waterplane and forms part of a chain of rule over the seas tied to Azatoth; linked historically to both Shen and Blobbert through familiar history.
- [[Session 101 - 1-0 Auftakt]]: Named in connection with the Sword of Relay (Schwert vo Relay) found in the shrinking glass.
