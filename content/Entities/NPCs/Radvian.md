## Known Information

Radvian is a dwarf who grew up with King [[Azan Aeducan]] of Ophris, making her one of his most trusted confidants. She leads the Vigilantes, an armed network operating in the city, and used this force to detain and question the Gold Daggers before releasing them with a warning. Her close personal ties to Azan were confirmed when the two were seen drinking together and departing after a meeting in the Bar Tonne cellar.

## Session Appearances

- Session 035: Questioned the captured party in a separate room following the pub quiz ambush; released them with a warning not to cause trouble.
- Session 036: Visited the Gold Daggers in Ophris, delivered news of a royal invitation, then departed together with Leumas — suggesting a personal connection forming between them.
- Session 037: Seen drinking with King [[Azan Aeducan]] after the meeting in the Bar Tonne cellar room; the two departed together, confirming Radvian as Azan's trusted confidant.
- Session 101: Referenced as a background figure in the Skjall and Torren alliance thread; Torren and Skjall formally join the fight, with Radvian's connection to Azan making her relevant to the broader war coalition.
