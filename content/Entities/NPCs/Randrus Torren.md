## Known Information

Randrus Torren (alias "Daddy") is the head of the [[Torren Family]] crime faction, one of four major crime leaders in [[Aarum]] identified by King [[Azan Aeducan]]. He is a practical, expansion-minded operator who initially viewed the Gold Daggers cautiously before shifting into a working alliance — sharing intelligence, operational resources, and enchantment work. As of Session 093 he is confirmed infected and visibly unwell, and his Nessus base was destroyed with thirty people lost.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 037]]: Named by King [[Azan Aeducan]] as one of four wanted-poster crime leaders in Aarum that the Gold Daggers are sent to intimidate; leads the Torren family faction.
- [[Session 039]]: The party observes Torren family wappen (coat of arms) on every building in [[Torrenviertel]], their dedicated market district; the party conducts metal trade negotiations in Torren territory without direct contact with Randrus Torren himself.
- [[Session 040 - Deals like Daggers|Session 040]]: Encountered at the restaurant "Die gleichstehenden Monde" using the alias "Daddy"; immediately recognises the party as the Gold Daggers and requests to meet at the theater the next day; warns that [[Volkam & Partner]] were put onto the party by the material supplier connected to the foundry.
- [[Session 041 - Deals like Daggers|Session 041]]: Arrives for the scheduled business dinner with a wooden leg and walking stick; explains that his group wants to expand off the island and proposes that the party act as ambassadors in harbour towns. Describes tensions with White Rose and younger thieves who steal from him. Notes that the island's church situation has changed and only one church remains active there. Identifies a Winterkirche emblem shown by BB. Explains that removing the faction chiefs would collapse the Tower, produce a successor for his own position, and leave White Rose's future uncertain. States that materials are sent to the mainland but his group does not directly participate in the war.
- [[Session 042]]: Attends the joint dinner in the [[Pocket Dimension]] alongside Nasri, Dakun, and the government/Hurmond; agrees to a broader civic arrangement covering shared governance, controlled materials, food security via Pocket Dimension farming, and limited governmental presence. Offers the party corpse disposal, shared infrastructure, and a place in the new district management. Relationship with the Gold Daggers shifts to a practical working alliance.
- [[Session 043]]: Contacted by the party for advice on where to buy a normal wooden wagon in Aarum; provides a recommendation.
- [[Session 044 - All about different kind of souls|Session 044]]: Arrives at the public government meeting; presents a major proposal for industrial expansion and infrastructure. The government pushes back but the meeting ends in a deal benefiting all parties. Barzan is officially listed as Torren's intermediate/relations representative.
- [[Session 048 - Die Hochzeit|Session 048]]: Acts as an insider ally during the party's wedding-sabotage operation at the [[Rosenpalast]] — provides a room-plan map, hidden-route knowledge, illusion-defence warnings, and an amulet against magic detection bells. Expects repayment if his goose-exit route was not needed.
- [[Session 050 - 20'000 Leagues|Session 050]]: Referenced indirectly — Mona (fabric merchant from Amarre, a known Torren-associated contact) appears in Lerchen en route to Ophris; she makes a clothing deal and mentions Don Hor and Udir as delivery recipients, placing Torren-adjacent contacts in the coastal area near Helykonos.
- [[Session 057 - Catching Up|Session 057]]: BB contacts Torren via Sending; he appears stressed and busy with enchantment work. Later invites the party to dinner at [[Nonna]]'s restaurant in Aarum, discussing the disrupted wedding, trade, magical hotspots, monster attacks, and the wider political situation. Reveals that agents are being sent north toward Skjall on a destabilising mission. Demonstrates enchantment work — including a fire-based ring and a scrying ring — and Barzan uses the scrying ring to scry Tolteka.

- [[Session 095 - Die vergessene Schwelle|Session 095]]: Collaborates with the party on crafting a dimensional knife capable of opening portals; reveals he lost 30 people and his Nessus base was destroyed.
