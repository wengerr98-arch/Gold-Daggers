## Known Information

Robin is a child NPC in [[Felix]]'s household orbit, approximately 9 years old and described as Latino and cute. Initially appearing as a helpful household presence who brought the party their gear and guided them in Felix's absence, Robin was later rescued from a cellar alongside two other children and transferred to [[Aubonne]]'s care at [[Melissa]]'s instruction. Robin is part of Melissa's family and appears to be the target of Dr. [[Mason]]'s interest. Since Session 047, Robin has been visibly growing stronger while Felix weakens, and as of Session 056 has acquired significant new magical abilities — including self-healing and Teleport as a 7th-level spell — consistent with Felix's curse partially transferring to Robin.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 012]]: Present at Felix's house. Brought the party their gear. Played a stone game with [[Lorilla]]; described as inspired by the interaction.
- [[Session 013]]: Points the party to the stairs and trapdoor giving access to the underground level beneath the platform in Felix's absence.
- [[Session 014]]: Present at Felix's location when the party visits but Felix is not there; Robin acts as the household presence.
- [[Session 017]]: Sends the party an urgent message from a cellar — reports they are with others, that Melissa is gone, that people are coming, and requests help immediately.
- [[Session 018]]: Found in a cellar with two other children (Mara and Emmet) inside a failing Leomund's Tiny Hut, guarded by two uniformed casters attacking the dome. Rescued by Koda (who finds Robin first) and carried out by Barzan. Described as the smallest of the three children. Is not exhausted after the ordeal, unlike the other two. Recognises the book "Ervirs Reisen" and names a favourite passage. Placed under Koda's care in the temple cellar. Given the cover name "Dodo" at Silk's request to hide identity.
- [[Session 019]]: Brought to Aubonne by the party per Melissa's instructions — transferred from the temple to Aubonne's care. The party warns Aubonne that guards had been looking for Robin. Aubonne is told about Mason's interest in Robin. Robin travels under the cover name "Dodo."
- [[Session 024]]: Appears at the rebuilt house/shop area and is identified as a bard. Described as a strong magic-user who struggles to control his powers. [[Leumas]] reveals his changeling true form to Robin, Snix, and BB this session. Robin is identified as part of Melissa's family.
- [[Session 025]]: Found at Felix's shop baking cake with Koda when the party visits in the lead-up to the assassination. Receives the slavery information book delivery there.
- [[Session 047]]: Present in Helikonos alongside Felix, Aubonne, Silk, and Treth; appears visibly stronger than Felix. Gives BB "The World" tarot card through a touch.
- [[Session 056 - Teen Robin, Felix Shenanigns|Session 056]]: Visibly aged and older, with apparent signs that Felix's curse has shifted onto Robin. Can heal himself and demonstrates Teleport as a 7th-level spell, displaying many new abilities. Felix agrees Robin should travel with the party and confirms Robin can fly.
- [[Session 077 - Results|Session 077]]: Confirmed to be carrying Felix's power after Felix's death during the snowstorm/Wild Hunt arc; the nature of this power transfer remains unresolved.
- [[Session 090]]: Eloryn and Leumas are found lifeless at the Entertainment District stage — Robin's thread as a power-carrier in the district context remains active.
