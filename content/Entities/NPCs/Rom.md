# Rom

## Known Information

Rom is a human woman in her early sixties, with dark skin and short hair, who had been imprisoned in the Oubliettes for ten years on a charge of murder. She was discovered by the party hiding in a tree-hole in the underground forest, sheltering from cannibals. Healed by Eloryn, she joined the group's escape attempt.
