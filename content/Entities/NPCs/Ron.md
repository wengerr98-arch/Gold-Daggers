# Ron

## Known Information

Ron is a recurring door guard at [[The Tower]] in [[Aarum]], consistently paired with [[Ken]] as entry guards. The two are frequently mentioned together and are known for refusing to open the door for the party, suggesting they are a reliable fixture of Tower security rather than major players within its hierarchy.

## Session Appearances

- Session 042: Named alongside [[Ken]] as recurring Tower door guards; again do not open the door for the party.
- Session 048: Named in the entity list for Session 048 ("Die Hochzeit"); present in the broader Tower context.
- Session 095: Named during the party's Aarum downtime; present in Aarum as part of the Tower's ongoing operations alongside [[Ken]].

## Known Facts

- Role: door guard at [[The Tower]] in [[Aarum]]
- Consistently paired with [[Ken]]
- Known for refusing to open the Tower door for the party
