# Sai

## Known Information

Sai is the daughter of a gardener who works at a garden center in [[Dalior]]. She met [[Lorilla]] in Session 012, exchanged introductions with her, and gifted Lorilla a lily — marking her as a potential recurring contact for Lorilla in Dalior.

## Session Appearances

- Session 012: Met [[Lorilla]] at the garden center in Dalior. Named explicitly. Gave Lorilla a lily; her mother (unnamed) was also present.

## Known Facts

- Location: garden center in [[Dalior]]
- Father: unnamed gardener at the garden center
- Mother: unnamed older woman, also present at the garden center
- Gave [[Lorilla]] a lily during their first meeting
