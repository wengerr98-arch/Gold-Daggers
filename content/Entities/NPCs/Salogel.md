# Salogel

## Known Information

Salogel is an elven NPC who appeared in [[Barzan Aeducan]]'s future vision during Session 089. In the vision, Barzan is brought to Salogel — described as occupying a reception or front-desk role in the elven library of the [[Red-Walled Elven City]]. The DM has confirmed Salogel is an NPC (elven), not a location or Swiss German term.

## Session Appearances

- Session 089: Appears in Barzan Aeducan's future vision; Barzan is brought to Salogel at the front desk of the elven library in the Red-Walled Elven City.

## Known Facts

- Race: elf
- Role: receptionist or greeter figure at the elven library in the [[Red-Walled Elven City]]
- Appeared in a future vision experienced by [[Barzan Aeducan]]
- DM-confirmed NPC (not a location name or Swiss German word)
