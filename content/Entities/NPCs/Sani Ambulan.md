## Known Information

Sani Ambulan (also known by full name Lervick Ambulan) is a Duergar dwarf with one arm. She is a court smith for the Abadon family and has connections to Odyss Trion. She was present at the Stygian Planar Ball (Session 102), where she pointed out Odyss Trion (disguised as a Leonin in a jute sack) to BB. In Session 103, her full name Lervick Ambulan was revealed — she works for Fam. Abadon and was working for Odyss. The Ambulan surname connects her to the Ambulan family from [[Lervik]] (Yorni, Erkas, Yaen) — exact family relationship unconfirmed but surname match is noted. Note: "Lervick" in her name likely refers to the town of Lervik (the Ambulan family's origin), not a personal first name.

## Session Appearances

- Session 031: Listed in the monastery character roll-call (Persönlichkeiten im Kloster).
- Session 032: Named in the monastery entity list; present at the monastery during the feast and subsequent activities.
- Session 102: Appears at the Planar Ball as part of [[Asmodeus]]'s entourage. [[BB]] asks about Sani Ambulan; she confirms she belongs to an entourage (whose is unspecified) and then points through the crowd to a Leonin in a Jute Sack — who is revealed to be [[Odyss Trion]] in disguise. BB is specifically tasked with making contact with both Asmodeus and Verus at the event.
- Session 103: Real name revealed as Lervick Ambulan — she panicked and tried to flee at the Stygian Gala; held in place by Eloryn using an Immovable Rod. Confirmed to be working for [[Odyss Trion]].
