# Sarim

## Known Information

Sarim is an ancient or historical figure referenced during the party's visit to [[The Eladrin Library]] in [[Kasta]]. The suggestion enchantment on the library's books is attributed to Sarim, or possibly someone even older (*"de Spell esch vom Sarim, oder älter!"*), implying Sarim may predate the current institution and could be associated with the founding of Kasta's magical tradition.

## Session Appearances

- Session 081: Single mention — attributed as the possible origin of the Suggestion enchantment on [[The Eladrin Library]]'s books.

## Known Facts

- Attribution: Possibly responsible for the Suggestion enchantment on [[The Eladrin Library]]'s books
- Era: Ancient or pre-library historical figure; possibly older than Kasta's current magical institution
- Entity type: Unconfirmed (may be a historical wizard, titan-era figure, or other ancient entity)
