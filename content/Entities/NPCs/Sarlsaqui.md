# Sarlsaqui

## Known Information

Sarlsaqui is a legendary assassin from the Forgotten War, uncovered by BB through a high-difficulty research roll. They were notorious for systematically infiltrating and undermining entire military camps, killing all occupants simultaneously. Whether Sarlsaqui is currently alive, imprisoned, or long dead remains unknown.

## Session Appearances

- Session 099: Referenced in research documents about the Forgotten War as a legendary assassin who "systematically undermined entire camps and killed everyone at once."

## Known Facts

- Era: active during the Forgotten War
- Specialty: simultaneous elimination of entire military camps
- Discovered via BB's research roll (nat 29) in Session 099
- Current status: unknown (alive / imprisoned / deceased)
