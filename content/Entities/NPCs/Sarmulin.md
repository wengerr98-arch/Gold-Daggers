# Sarmulin

## Known Information

Sarmulin is a dwarf from [[Skjall]] and a devotee of the [[Wintergöttin]]. The party met him aboard the boat to [[Aarum]] during the night-visitor incident involving a black smoke entity ([[Das letzte Opfer]]). He actively helped defend the party during the attack, earning their trust — particularly through [[Eloryn]]'s judgment of his character. As a gesture of goodwill, he gave [[BB]] a religious emblem made of Kaltstahl, and in return Eloryn gifted him a bracelet with claw beads.
