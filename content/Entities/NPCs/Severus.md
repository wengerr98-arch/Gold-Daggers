# Severus

## Known Information

Severus holds the role of Vorsteher der Novizen (head of novices) at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]. He appears in the monastery's personnel lists for both Sessions 031 and 032 but has no recorded speaking scenes or direct interaction with the party.

## Session Appearances

- Session 031: Present at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]] during the harvest feast and trial period; named in the monastery personnel list.
- Session 032: Named again in the monastery personnel list as Vorsteher der Novizen (head of novices).

## Known Facts

- Role: Vorsteher der Novizen (head of novices)
- Affiliation: [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]]
- No speaking scenes or party interaction recorded
