## Known Information

Shen is [[Eloryn]]'s former divine patron — a multi-colored underwater dragon who rules the Triton city of Helikonos. Originally a mortal Koi fish who climbed a waterfall for a thousand years before transforming into a dragon, Shen actively conceals his mortal origins. He forced [[Eloryn]] to publicly execute a revolutionary leader when he discovered her involvement in the resistance, and has since sent her on a secret mission to find and destroy certain things; her parents, also part of the revolution against him, remain under his reach.

## Known Facts

- Multi-colored underwater dragon; type uncertain — old vault filed under NPCs but content describes a dragon deity.
- Origin: a mortal Koi fish that climbed a waterfall for 1,000 years, transforming into a dragon; spent another 1,000 years transforming fully.
- Actively conceals his mortal origins; the party suspects this is significant.
- Ruler of Helikonos, the underwater Triton city; lives in the former king's house there.
- Described as very young and fish-like in appearance (Session 045).
- Murdered the last Triton king [[Klereth]] to seize power over Helikonos (Session 046, referenced in a book about old monarchies).
- Holds periodic public parades in Helikonos to maintain public presence (Session 046).
- Enforces oppressive rule: control stones, stratified society, human laborers (Session 046).
- Orchestrates a Waterplane rift network; appeared telepathically aboard the Nautiloid to direct rift placement (Session 051).
- Linked to [[R'lyeh]] through the [[Book of Azatoth]]'s chain of rule over the seas (Session 052).
- As of Session 052, described as increasingly aggressive ("hässiger").
- As of Session 055, Helikonos is in active civil war; Shen has been rampaging, retains some public support, but the army is disorganized and Nolia soldiers have withdrawn.
- [[Eloryn]]'s former divine patron; their relationship is described as "compromised."
- Forced [[Eloryn]] to publicly execute a revolutionary leader upon discovering her involvement in the resistance.
- Sent [[Eloryn]] on a secret mission to find and destroy certain things.
- Dragon turtles were transformed into half-dragons similarly to Koi dragons — both were originally mortal.
- "Garden / Wachgarde von Shen" (his city guard) — may share a name with the [[Garden]] faction in Dalior but appears to be a distinct entity.
