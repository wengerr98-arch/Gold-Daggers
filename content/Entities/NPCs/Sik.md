# Sik

## Known Information

Sik is a kobold NPC, one of four kobolds who disappeared after the sealed lower tunnel was opened in [[Kobolddorf]]. Those who returned from the tunnel came back "completely different," and Sik's current fate is unknown.

## Session Appearances

- Session 026: Named by [[Gax]] as one of four disappeared kobolds. The kobold community is frightened; those who returned from the tunnel came back "completely different."

## Known Facts

- Kobold NPC from [[Kobolddorf]].
- One of four kobolds who disappeared after the sealed lower tunnel was opened (Session 026).
- Named by [[Gax]] as one of the missing.
