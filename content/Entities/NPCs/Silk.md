# Silk

![[silk.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Silk (full name: Smooth Silk) is a Tabaxi NPC who was formerly captured and brought to [[Dalior]] against his will. He grew up in [[Safti]] on the [[Amare]] island group and now serves as the right-hand of [[Cetzy]], running the [[Entertainment District (Dalior)|Entertainment District]] of Dalior from the [[Rakshasa Tempel]] bar. Lamp-bound by a broken contract, Silk can only exist within her lamp's boundary — she was revived by an old patron and cannot leave that space. Despite this constraint, Silk played an instrumental role in the district's defence during the Dalior crisis and remains one of the party's most trusted long-term contacts.

## Known Facts

- Tabaxi NPC; formerly captured and brought to [[Dalior]] against his will.
- Full name: Smooth Silk (confirmed by DM correction note).
- Grew up in [[Safti]], part of the [[Amare]] island group (specifically Hubhe island within Amarre).
- Right-hand of [[Cetzy]]; runs the [[Entertainment District (Dalior)|Entertainment District]] of Dalior.
- Bartender at the [[Rakshasa Tempel]] bar; serves as Cetzy's translator when Cetzy communicates with the party.
- Lamp-bound: cannot leave her lamp's space due to a broken contract; was revived by an old patron and is only alive inside the lamp (Session 041).
- By Session 017, holds direct leadership of the Entertainment District's defensive strategy, delegated by Cetzy.
- Killed the final enemy (Blackguard-led assault with two ogres) at the end of Session 017.
- Actively protects Robin's identity; requested the party keep it secret (Session 018).
- Has a younger sister named Milly (also called Chrazi / Kratzer) living on Safti in Amare; entrusted the party with a diary to deliver to her (Session 025).
- Has expertise in golem lore and construct contracts; explained the golem contract alteration ritual in Session 040.
- Tracks trade route conditions between Dalior, Offris, and Amare.
- Works with [[Exandria von Amare]] at the Tempel, coordinating intake of freed hostages.
- Previously fought Arthur Ruger and barely survived — only made it out with Robin's help (Session 047).
- In Session 049, retained Pira Devei's body after the soul-switch sequence, with stated intent to use it for future plans.
- As of Session 056, district trade network and supply lines are under pressure from the ongoing war.
