# Sixt

## Known Information

Sixt is a young dwarf NPC who was imprisoned in Dr. Mason's basement dungeon and freed by the party in Session 010. He is the nephew of [[Brentan]], who was caged alongside him. Sixt helped open other cages during the escape but has thin session detail and has not appeared in later sessions.

## Session Appearances

- Session 010: Freed from a cage in Dr. Mason's basement dungeon alongside his uncle [[Brentan]]. Helps open other cages during the escape. Named once; acts in the background with thin session detail. Likely the same individual referred to as "der junge Zwerg" (the young dwarf) in the session text, though this is not fully confirmed.
