# Snigli

## Known Information

Snigli is a kobold NPC and confirmed sibling of [[Snix]], closely resembling her. She first appeared in Snix's future vision as an opponent in an arena fight at an old gravel mine (alte Kiesmine) surrounded by roughly 100 kobolds. Snigli has been trapped in [[Pandemonium]] for a significant period, and by Session 091 the two siblings appear to have stabilised a bond through a switching event.

## Session Appearances

- Session 089: Appears in Snix's future vision in an arena fight. Named explicitly: "Snigli macht spirit guardians? Cleric?" and "Snigli attackiert meteme Wisdom save - Hold Person und de Snix gohot doown." Described as resembling Snix ("like a sister?"). Confirmed as Snix's sibling, currently trapped in Pandemonium.
- Session 090: Snix is rescued from Pandemonium after a cave collapse and brought back aboard the ship — Snigli remains trapped in Pandemonium while Snix escapes.
- Session 091: Explicitly confirmed as linked to Snix; Snigli is briefly teleported away, then the two switch/bond in a more stable way, with the kobold situation in Pandemonium shifting during the session.
- Session 095: Snix sends a message to Snigli during downtime about a kobold village north-west of Gnolia, re-establishing contact and implying Snigli's situation in Pandemonium is being actively tracked.

## Known Facts

- Race: kobold
- Sibling of [[Snix]]
- Currently trapped in [[Pandemonium]]
- Acts as a cleric (observed using Spirit Guardians and Hold Person)
- As of Session 091, Snix and Snigli have formed a more stable bond/switch
- Contact re-established via message in Session 095
