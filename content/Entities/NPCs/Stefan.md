# Stefan

## Known Information

Stefan is a named human spy from [[Gnolia]], operating in [[Skjall]] in disguise beneath an "orc greenface." He meets with spies between 2–3am at an unspecified location and was present in a scene involving roughly 45 gathered human spies from various cloisters and regions, with strong [[Ophris]] representation. The spies reportedly do not clearly know their own mission.
