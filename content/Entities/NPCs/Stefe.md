# Stefe

## Known Information

Stefe is a kobold NPC who disappeared along with three others after the sealed lower tunnel in [[Kobolddorf]] was opened. He is the brother of [[Dave]], giving this disappearance direct relevance to an active companion's backstory.

## Session Appearances

- Session 026: Named by [[Gax]] as one of four kobolds who vanished after the lower tunnel was unsealed; identified as [[Dave]]'s brother.

## Known Facts

- Race: kobold
- Location last known: [[Kobolddorf]] (disappeared into or near the sealed lower tunnel)
- Family: brother of [[Dave]]
- Source of information: [[Gax]]
