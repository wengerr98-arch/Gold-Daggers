## Overview

A Kobold and the main speaker of a 5-person patrol in [[The Unclaimed Lands]]. Encountered by the party in the past timeline (year 323 p.b.).

## Known Facts

- Kobold, heavily cloaked with a red snout visible
- Grew up in his tribe in the Unclaimed Lands
- Main spokesperson for his patrol group
- Trades with [[Kasta]]; mentions that elves still exist (in 323 p.b.)
- Perceptive — made a good Insight check on [[Barzan]], noticed he grew up outside of cities
- Comments that he would never kill a dragon (in response to Snix's dragon-killing reputation)
- Initially uncertain whether the party has potential or will be a problem
- Interested in city life, asks [[Barzan]] about it

## Patrol Members

Works alongside:
- [[Linsea]] (Half-Elf)
- Kelpi (Oger Shifter)
- Huton (Warforged)
- Uossa (Hyena-faced shifter scout)

## Session Appearances

- **Session 109**: First encounter; approaches the party after they land in the Unclaimed Lands; converses with [[Barzan]] about religion and city life; camps overnight with the party before city scouts attack
