## Known Information

Stevos is a Triton revolutionary operative based in Helikonos, part of the close-knit group that includes [[Kevos]] and [[Mahlyn]]. He reconnected with [[Eloryn]] upon her return to the city after a period of separation, and was confirmed to be actively fighting in the [[Revolutionary Army]]'s ongoing conflict against [[Shen]]'s forces.

## Session Appearances

- Session 052: Reconnected with Eloryn after her return to Helikonos; part of the Stevos/Kevos/Mahlyn group that greets and supports her in the city.
- Session 053: Confirmed to have been actively fighting in the ongoing conflict against Shen's forces alongside the [[Revolutionary Army]].
