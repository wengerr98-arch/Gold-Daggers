# Sturm

## Known Information

Sturm is an Earth Genasi described as "made of stone" who possesses limited flight. She was freed from the Oubliettes in Session 022, at which point Lorilla recognised her face as matching a statue previously seen in the first cellar of [[Lervik]]. Sturm confirmed she has a twin sister named [[Phönix]], and her connection to the [[Sturm-Phönix]] faction — of which she is likely the namesake — makes her a campaign-critical figure.

## Session Appearances

- Session 022: Freed from the Oubliettes; Lorilla recognises her face from the Lervik cellar statue; reveals twin sister Phönix; guild connection confirmed.
- Session 023: Escaped with the evacuated crowd during the ladder-based prisoner evacuation; present during the uprising on the upper prison levels.

## Known Facts

- Race: Earth Genasi
- Description: "made of stone"; can fly a little
- Twin sister: [[Phönix]]
- Associated faction: [[Sturm-Phönix]] (likely namesake)
