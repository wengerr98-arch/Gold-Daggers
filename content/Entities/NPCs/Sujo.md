## Known Information

Sujo is a Loxodon contact based in Kasta, introduced to the party by Torren. A trusted local figure with a stand in the city, Sujo serves as the party's primary on-the-ground guide in Kasta — explaining local pricing, student access rules, and the magical-school hierarchy. He provides the party with safe harbour at his Safe House during their most critical operations in the city.

## Session Appearances

- Session 078: Introduced by Torren as a contact in Kasta (Loxodon with a stand).
- Session 080: Active as the party's main local guide; explains local pricing, student access, and the magical-school structure; accompanies the party to the Genasi market and helps navigate Kasta's social hierarchy.
- Session 081: Continues as an active presence in Kasta during the party's investigation of titan lore and their desert excursion; provides ongoing local hospitality and support.
- Session 085: Serves as the party's safe-harbour contact; the group regroups through Sujo and rests at the Safe House between the Ethereal Plane scouting of the library and Snix's infiltration attempt.
