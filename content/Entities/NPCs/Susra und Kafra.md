## Known Information

Kafra and Susra are identical twins hired by the Gold Daggers to manage the [[Goldie Bräu]] brewery in [[Aarum]] after Folkam's departure. Kafra took on the formal management role — originally at 3 silver per week, later raised to 20 gold per week as the brewery grew — while Susra joined as additional support. Under their management the brewery became profitable, expanded its orders, and developed into a financially healthy enterprise.

## Session Appearances

- Session 041: Kafra accepts Folkam's former management role at [[Goldie Bräu]] and brings twin sister Susra as support; both hired at 3 silver per week; a new sign is made for the brewery.
- Session 042: Named in the party's Aarum operations; both twins continue their work under the party's oversight in the new civic arrangement.
- Session 043: The party visits the brewery to check on Susra, Kafra, and Goldie; purchases food for Snix's date preparations.
- Session 056: Both working well at [[Goldie Bräu]]; the business is profitable; future investment discussed during the party's resupply stop.
- Session 078: Brewery gains more large orders and stronger finances; Kafra's salary raised to 20 gold per week; party receives positive update during the crossroads planning session.
- Session 092: Referenced as "Susra" and "Kaffra" in the sidecar; party discusses Goldie Bräu AG business, security upgrades, and personnel during their return to Aarum.
