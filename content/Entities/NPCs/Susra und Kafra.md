## Known Information

Kafra and Susra are identical twins hired by the Gold Daggers to manage the [[Goldie Bräu]] brewery in [[Aarum]] after Folkam's departure. Kafra took on the formal management role — originally at 3 silver per week, later raised to 20 gold per week as the brewery grew — while Susra joined as additional support. Under their management the brewery became profitable, expanded its orders, and developed into a financially healthy enterprise.
