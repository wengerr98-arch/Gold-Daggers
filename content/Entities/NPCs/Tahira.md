# Tahira

## Known Information

Tahira is a Dragonborn described as mute. Snix asked Yorni about her in Session 025, and Yorni replied that he does not know her current location. She may be connected to Snix's missing sister thread — the sister who disappeared after being taken in by the [[Sturm-Phönix]] / Diebesgilde alongside Snix.

## Session Appearances

- Session 025: Snix asks [[Yorni]] about "Tahira (dragonborn)"; Yorni says she was mute and her location is unknown to him.

## Known Facts

- Race: Dragonborn
- Described as mute
- Her whereabouts are unknown (as of Session 025)
- Possible connection to Snix's missing sister (taken in by [[Sturm-Phönix]] / Diebesgilde)
