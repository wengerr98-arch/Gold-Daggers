# Tavira

## Known Information

Tavira is a named female Dragonborn who was found imprisoned on the second prison level, unable to speak. [[Snix]] recognised her by name, suggesting a personal connection tied to his missing sister backstory thread. She was rescued by the party in Session 023 but confirmed dead by Session 025, closing the lead on Snix's search.

## Session Appearances

- Session 022: Mentioned in passing — the party notes Tavira as someone they have not seen in a long time, raising the question of her current whereabouts and condition.
- Session 023: Found on the second prison level; unable to speak; recognised by name by Snix; rescued but condition unresolved.
- Session 025: Checked as a contact point while the party camps in the forest after fleeing Dalior; the attempt reveals that Tavira is dead. This ends the lead for Snix's missing sister search thread.
- Session 092: Named in the sidecar entities list; referenced as part of the party's ongoing backstory threads and connections as they plan future arcs.
