# Tazar

![[Tazar.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Tazar is a Bugbear NPC based at [[Skjall]], serving as project lead and school principal (director of the Lehrlingsausbildungszentrum) for the city's Wall-repair engineering effort. He is a key figure in the Wall activation arc, speaking directly with BB and Lorilla about the engineering work, and was present at the public briefing on the monument threat and Wild Hunt crisis alongside the assembled clan leadership.

## Session Appearances

- Session 061: Bugbear project lead / school principal for wall-repair engineering; speaks directly with BB and Lorilla.
- Session 064: Referenced in the context of the ongoing Wall investigation in Skjall; Tazar's wall-repair engineering effort remains active background to the party's king-and-throne-room focus this session.
- Session 065: The group concludes from logbook and dream-state evidence that the legendary tavern ([[The Silent Dance Tap Room|Silent Coin Taproom]]) is a legend that the king and Tazar both knew about; Tazar is also present as part of the assembled Skjall clan leadership in the public briefing about the monument threat and Wild Hunt crisis.
- Session 076: Present in Skjall as the leadership transition unfolds following the king's death; Tazar's role in the new post-king power structure is part of the session's unresolved open threads.
