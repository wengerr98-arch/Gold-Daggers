# Teana

## Known Information

Teana is a narrow-built elderly Loxodon who serves as Rektorin and Archivarin of the Divination school at [[Kasta]], and is the last surviving active member of that school's leadership. She reads possible futures and visibly reacted with agitation during her reading for the party. She aided the party by giving them an amulet to prevent detection and showing them a secret exit from the Divination Tower, and was astonished to learn that [[Abrahim (Ephraim)|Ephraim]] — known to her as Isaak, a former expelled Divination member — had survived for 400 years.

## Session Appearances

- Session 083: Consulted by the party at the Divination Tower; gave party escape amulet; revealed secret exit; reads futures and reacted with agitation; expressed shock at Ephraim's (Isaak identity) continued existence after 400 years.
- Session 085: Referenced in the context of the party's continued investigation of Kasta's warded library and blood archive; the party studies Ethereal Plane access and traces a hidden magical structure tied to the city barrier.
