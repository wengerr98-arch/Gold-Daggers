# Tec

## Known Information

Tec is one of two elderly kobold women who co-lead the hidden kobold commune encountered by the party in Session 038. She and [[Vic]] jointly received the party and shared lore about eastern kobold tribes. The commune is notable for its white-coloured kobolds, educational system, mud baths, and resident jeweler.

## Session Appearances

- Session 038: Received the party alongside [[Vic]] at the hidden kobold commune; community co-leader who shared lore about eastern kobold tribes.

## Known Facts

- Elderly kobold woman; co-leader of a hidden kobold commune (Session 038).
- Leads jointly with [[Vic]].
- The commune features white-coloured kobolds, an educational system, mud baths, and a jeweler.
