## Known Information

Tec is one of two elderly kobold women who co-lead the hidden kobold commune encountered by the party in Session 038. She and [[Vic]] jointly received the party and shared lore about eastern kobold tribes. The commune is notable for its white-coloured kobolds, educational system, mud baths, and resident jeweler.
