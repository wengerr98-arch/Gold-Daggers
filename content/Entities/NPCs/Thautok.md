## Known Information

Thautok (also known as Eradrin, König, and The First Torr) is the ancient first monarch of [[Skjall]], encountered in the throne room during Session 062 performing soul-shredding on corpses. She is connected to moonlight and the Wild Hunt, and was later encountered by BB directly in the mushroom realm, where she revealed her identity as an early ruler of Skjall and passed knowledge of the city's history to BB. As of Session 077, she has spoken with BB about monument mechanics, planar seals, and plane traversal. Her historical significance extends to the party's research in Stygia, where she appears in records as the first Torr of Skjall.

## Session Appearances

- Session 062: Referred to as "de König" — seen in the Skjall throne room performing soul-shredding on corpses. BB observes a shadow behind the King connected to moonlight, potentially linked to Felix's "true face." Leumas senses a red thread connecting him to the König via a death ritual.
- Session 075: Referenced as "Eradrin"; Eradrin confirmed as an alias for Thautok (the first Torr of Skjall). Appears in the context of BB's mushroom-realm experience and the party's investigation of Wild Hunt traces.
- Session 076: BB meets Eradrin (Thautok) directly in the mushroom realm; Eradrin/Thautok reveals she was an early ruler of Skjall, and BB gains a direct personal connection to her history with the city and the Wild Hunt.
- Session 077: Referenced as Tautok (alias); BB speaks with Thautok about monument mechanics, the planar seals, and plane traversal as the party prepares to push back the Wild Hunt and reach the Volk settlement.
- Session 078: Referenced as Tautok; Thautok's knowledge of planar mechanics (from prior mushroom-realm encounters) remains relevant context as the party discusses portals, planes, and resistance to Mason.
- Session 099: Referenced as "The First Torr" in the context of the party's library research in Stygia into forgotten peoples and the elven past — Thautok as the first monarch of Skjall is part of the historical and planar lore the party pursues in the Stygian library.
- Session 103: Target of a Sending from the party warning about Odyss Trion posing as Torr and/or Leumas at the Stygian Gala.
