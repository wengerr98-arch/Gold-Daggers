## Known Information

[[Thavyra]] is a Dragonborn member of the [[Diebesguilde]] (Thieves' Guild). The party first met her in [[Lervik]], and later freed her from the [[Oubliette]] in [[Dalior]]. After her rescue she became unreachable, and all attempts to contact her have failed — she is presumed dead, with [[Yorni]] suspected of possible involvement.
