# The Arch-Fey

## Known Information

An unnamed Arch-Fey who runs an unmarked, nameless circus tent that is "not usually set up," implying it appears and disappears unpredictably. King [[Azan Aeducan]] explicitly warns the party against making any deals with this figure, marking him as dangerous. He is described as "somewhat a vampire" and is likely a future major encounter on the road to [[Aarum]].
