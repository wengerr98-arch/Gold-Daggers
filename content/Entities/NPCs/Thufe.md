# Thufe

## Known Information

Thufe is one of the names found crossed out on the back of a hidden portrait discovered in [[Aubonne]]'s concealed drawer. The crossed-out name implies she is deceased or estranged from the group. She is part of the 7 sisters mystery alongside Melissa (also crossed out), Aubonne, Gutetama, and Wilisith (also crossed out).
