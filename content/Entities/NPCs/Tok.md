# Tok

## Known Information

Tok is a kobold NPC who disappeared after the sealed lower tunnel was opened in [[Kobolddorf]]. Named by [[Gax]] as one of four kobolds who went missing, with those who returned from the tunnel reportedly coming back "completely different," suggesting something sinister occurred below.

## Session Appearances

- Session 026: Named by [[Gax]] as one of four disappeared kobolds. The kobold community is frightened; those who returned from the tunnel came back "completely different."

## Known Facts

- Race: Kobold
- Affiliation: [[Kobolddorf]]
- One of four named kobolds who disappeared after the sealed lower tunnel was opened
- Named by [[Gax]] as part of the missing group
- No known relation to [[Natok]]
