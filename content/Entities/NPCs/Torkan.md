## Known Information

Torkan is a minor dwarf associated with the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]] monastery community. He appeared in Sessions 031–032 as a member of [[Gregorius]]'s team during the monastery drinking contest, where he became extremely drunk (sturzbetrunken).

## Session Appearances

- Session 031: Present at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]] during the harvest feast and trial period; part of the monastery community.
- Session 032: Named as a member of [[Gregorius]]'s drinking-contest team; noted as becoming extremely drunk.

## Known Facts
