# Treth

![[treth.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Treth is a blacksmith in [[Dalior]], working at the forge alongside his colleague [[Natok]] and his father [[Edraff]]. He crafted a magic-concealment amulet for the party to allow spellcasting in Dalior, and later played a coordination role during the refugee evacuation — brought in by Natok as "Papa Treth" to organise the transport of refugees and craftsmen to the shipyard in small groups. In Session 092, BB and Barzan connected a robed figure bearing Erdbrecher imagery and rune tattoos to Treth, opening the possibility that he has cultist ties and that the Erdbrecher may have a split internal structure. Note: this stub was merged from a "Trev" stub (Session 015) — "Trev" was a transcription misread of Treth.

## Session Appearances

- Session 012: Named as son of [[Edraff]]; colleague of [[Natok]] at the [[Dalior]] forge. Party brings [[Donnerstein]] to the forge; Edraff notes more material is needed.
- Session 013: Left alone at the forge when most workers were sent west to Offris by the military. Reports workers were deployed to Offris to smith and repair machinery/infrastructure after negotiations involving the queen. Tells the party about the underground route — ladders down or jumping/flying from the platform edge.
- Session 015: Returns [[Leumas]]' sword to the party; gives a letter and a patterned shell gift for [[Snix]].
- Session 047: Present in Helikonos alongside Felix, Robin, Aubonne, and Silk after the group fled the camp battle; one of the allies the party reconnects with.
- Session 055: Referenced — Natok brings "Papa Treth" in to coordinate transport of refugees and handworkers to the shipyard in small groups.
- Session 056: Reunited with his father [[Edraff]] in [[Helykonos]], among the Nolia refugees and craftsmen.
- Session 092: BB and Barzan identify a cultist figure tied to Erdbrecher imagery and rune tattoos as potentially connected to Treth; party opens possibility of separate cult structures and a Treth–Erdbrecher link.
