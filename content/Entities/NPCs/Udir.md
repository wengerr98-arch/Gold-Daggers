# Udir

## Known Information

Udir is a good-natured, peaceful elderly dwarf who lives at the [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]] and serves as [[Barzan Aeducan]]'s mentor — the figure who shaped Barzan's faith and upbringing at the monastery. He co-presides over the monastery's ceremonies and trials alongside [[Don Hor]], with whom he shares a close romantic partnership. His warmth and spiritual depth make him one of the most trusted contacts the Gold Daggers have within the monastic world.
