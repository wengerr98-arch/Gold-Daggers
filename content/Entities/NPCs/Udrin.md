# Udrin

## Known Information

Udrin is an NPC associated with the Helikonos revolutionary forces, mentioned after a demanding operation in which both he and the guards under his watch were left exhausted. He appears to be a leader or guard-level figure within the revolutionary command structure, distinct from [[Udir]] (Barzan's dwarven monastery mentor at the Kloster).

## Session Appearances

- Session 055: Mentioned as exhausted alongside the guards after the Helikonos operation.

## Known Facts

- Associated with the Helikonos revolutionary forces.
- Described as exhausted after an operation, alongside the guards.
- Confirmed as a distinct NPC from [[Udir]].
