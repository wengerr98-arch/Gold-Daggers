## Known Information

Uthrin is the Triton revolutionary leader in Helikonos, described as the "former left hand, now the boss" of the revolutionary command structure. He hosted the party for a strategy meeting and was visibly heartbroken after 24 comrades failed to return from an afternoon mission. He is a practical logistics figure as well as a command authority, providing the party with supplies and a map for their approach to the gorge.

## Session Appearances

- Session 052: Led the strategy meeting with the party in Helikonos; emotionally affected by the loss of 24 revolutionary fighters; coordinated the mission to find the arrival point of the mind-control stones.
- Session 053: Provided the party with a map of the gorge and gave them rations for the journey; served as logistics contact for the approach to the gorge.

## Known Facts

- Triton; head of the Helikonos revolutionary command structure.
