# Verus

## Known Information

Verus is a mysterious figure who attended the Auktion der Tugenden ball in Stygia in disguise alongside [[Asmodeus]]. The name first surfaced in Barzan's memory when he noticed a reflection connected to [[Lady Ty]] in Stygia's chromium surfaces, suggesting an unexplained link between Verus and Lady Ty. Verus was flagged as an unresolved thread across multiple sessions before making a physical appearance at the Stygian gala.

## Session Appearances

- Session 098: Name surfaces in Barzan's memory via a reflection connected to [[Lady Ty]] in Stygia's chromium surfaces; precise significance unresolved.
- Session 100: Listed among entities with open threads; flagged as unresolved going into the next session.
- Session 101: Attends the Auktion der Tugenden ball in Stygia in disguise alongside [[Asmodeus]].
- Session 102: Present at the Planar Ball; has not found [[Levistus]] himself. [[BB]] is specifically tasked with making contact with both Asmodeus and Verus during the event.

## Known Facts

- Attends the Auktion der Tugenden (Stygian gala) in disguise alongside [[Asmodeus]]
- Connected to [[Lady Ty]] via a chromium reflection in Stygia (nature of link unknown)
- True identity and faction unknown
- Has not found [[Levistus]] despite attending the Planar Ball (Session 102)
- BB is tasked with reaching both Asmodeus and Verus at the Ball (Session 102)
