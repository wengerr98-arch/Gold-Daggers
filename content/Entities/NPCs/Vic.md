# Vic

## Known Information

Vic is one of two elderly kobold women who jointly lead the hidden kobold commune encountered by the party in Session 038. She and [[Tec]] co-lead the community and received the party, sharing lore about eastern kobold tribes. The commune is notable for its white-colored kobolds, educational system, mud baths, and jeweler.

## Session Appearances

- Session 038: Received the party alongside [[Tec]] at the hidden kobold commune; shared lore about eastern kobold tribes as co-leader.
