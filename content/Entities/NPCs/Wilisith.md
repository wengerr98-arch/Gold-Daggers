# Wilisith

## Known Information

Wilisith is one of the names inscribed on the back of a hidden portrait discovered in [[Aubonne]]'s concealed drawer. Her name is crossed out, implying she is deceased or estranged. She is part of the seven sisters mystery alongside [[Melissa]] (crossed out), [[Aubonne]], [[Gutetama]], and [[Thufe]] (crossed out).
