# Winfried


## Known Information

Winfried is the butler of [[Dr. Mason]]'s household, first named in Session 009 ("Butler heisst Winfred"). He is unfriendly toward the party and exerts a controlling influence over their movements ("er mag uns nicht sehr, kontrolliert uns"). He reappears in Session 010 as a named member of the household staff who questioned the party informally in a servants' dining room.

## Session Appearances

- Session 009: Named as the butler of the building/dungeon complex the party was navigating. Hostile and controlling attitude toward the party.
- Session 010: Present as part of Dr. Mason's household staff; the party was escorted to a servants' dining room and questioned informally by household staff, of which Winfried is a named member.

## Known Facts

- Butler in [[Dr. Mason]]'s household.
- Unfriendly toward the party and controlling in his staff role.
- Spelled "Winfried" (Raphael, 15 Nov 2026); "Winfred" and "Winford" in the protocols are the same person.
