## Known Information

Yaenne is [[Yorni]]'s sister and an active threat to the party. She was named by Yorni as his intended replacement agent for the current mission, and the party was explicitly warned that she must not find them first — implying she is dangerous and actively searching for them. She has been confirmed as hostile: in Session 073, she planted a poisoned pink-wrapped cake in the sewers to trick [[Snix]], who failed a Con save and gained a compulsion/obsession trail. She is also connected to a book that [[Dr. Mason]] is actively seeking.

## Session Appearances

- Session 071: Named twice by [[Yorni]]. Identified as his sister, his replacement agent for the mission, and as a figure connected to a book Mason is searching for. The party is warned she must not find them first.
- Session 073: Retroactively identified as the one who planted a mysterious pink-wrapped cake in the sewers to trick [[Snix]]. The cake caused a compulsion/obsession effect on Snix after a failed Con save. Her hostile intent toward the party confirmed.
- Session 074: Referenced in the context of the Kanalisation and Lagerhalle exploration; the alias "Jörns" (Yorni's childish nickname / Chenderspetzname) emerges in session, confirming her continued presence as an active threat operating in or around Skjall.
