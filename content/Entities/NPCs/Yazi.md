## Known Information

Yazi is a green-scaled Dragonborn who initially appeared as an antagonist pursuing the party in Session 038, with a burned arm possibly linked to the drug Zunder. She stole [[Okam|Okam's Kurzschwert]] (Snix's named short sword) and tracked the party near the coast. Over subsequent sessions, her relationship with [[Snix]] shifted from hostile to something more complicated — culminating in a formal date in Session 043 — though she ultimately did not join the party's southward journey. She continues to appear in Snix's visions and dreams, and as of Session 078 Lorilla wrote her a farewell letter, indicating the party considers her an important contact.

## Session Appearances

- Session 038: Active antagonist; pursues the party across the travel arc; attacks [[Snix]]; steals [[Okam|Okam's Kurzschwert]]; has a burned arm connected to the drug Zunder; tracked near the coast.
- Session 041: Spotted on a balcony in the Entertainment District; Snix notices her and the party repositions. Snix grabs Yazi's swords and his hands begin to hurt as the rings in them grow. Yazi teleports away before the party can fully engage.
- Session 042: Snix meets Yazi again; they discuss the last victim and the stolen weapons, and Snix invites her to the garden. A tentative conversational truce begins.
- Session 043: Snix arranges a formal date with Yazi; the party prepares food and sets up a separate space. Yazi arrives dressed up and visibly nervous. They discuss her past, her weapon, and whether she wants to stay with the party. The session ends with their relationship open and unresolved — Yazi signals she does not want to stay in one place too long.
- Session 044: Met by the party in a tree in Aarum; Snix invites her to travel south with the group.
- Session 045: Left behind by the party, who buy horses and depart toward Peoryn without her. Last reportedly heading toward Skjall.
- Session 052: Appeared in Snix's dream during the voyage toward Helikonos — seen moving through a snowy place and fleeing from something unidentified; the vision's meaning is unresolved.
- Session 055: Listed among the people the party intends to check in on after the camp assault; whereabouts and status remain open.
- Session 057: Appears in Snix's dream — Snix dreams of Pandemonium and sees Yazi in the southern mountains; the vision's significance is unresolved.
- Session 065: Briefly visible outside the window of the legendary tavern ([[Silent Coin Taproom]]) during the party's shared dream state; Snix recognised her immediately. Whether she was truly present, a projection, or connected to the tavern's legend-lore is unknown.
- Session 078: Lorilla writes a farewell letter to Yazi before the party's departure; the letter expresses support and marks the separation as temporary — confirming the party considers Yazi an important contact.
- Session 089: Snix is affected by the "succubus / Yazi thread" during the fight against Enoch in the [[Gilgamesh Workshop]], becoming socially influenced during combat. Yazi and the Succubus patron are referenced as a linked influence thread on Snix.
