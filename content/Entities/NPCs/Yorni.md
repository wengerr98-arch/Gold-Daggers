# Yorni

![[yorni.webp|260]]

## Known Information

Yorni is a gnome and the chief of the thieves' guild (Diebesgilde) in Nolia, holding the title of Aussenminister (Foreign Minister). He is one of the campaign's most central antagonists and manipulators — he commissioned the Gold Daggers to assassinate the Interior Minister, then publicly branded them as international terrorists to consolidate his own political position. Despite appearing as an enemy, Session 077 revealed he functions as a double agent who does not favor [[Dr. Mason]], and he temporarily allied with the party, providing keys to his extra-dimensional spaces and explaining Mason's objectives. His mother is a Succubus — also the mother of [[Demogorgon]] — giving him infernal lineage and a sibling connection to a major demonic entity.

## Known Facts

- Is a gnome (Session 002).
- Chief of the thieves' guild (Diebesgilde) (Session 002).
- Leads one of three internal guild swarms; the other swarms are led by Askot (offensive/gang wars focus) and Dragenborn (shadow/stealth focus) (Session 002).
- Holds the title Aussenminister (Foreign Minister) of Nolia (Session 048).
- Is the groom at the Rosenpalast state wedding in Session 048.
- Counterspelled Leumas's concealment attempt in Session 048, demonstrating high-level spellcasting ability.
- Publicly labelled the Gold Daggers as terrorists in broadcasts following the Interior Minister assassination and Eloryn's Gold Daggers accusation (Sessions 025, 048).
- Commissioned the assassination of the Interior Minister of Nolia as part of a larger plan to reorganise Nolia's political structure; paid 500 platinum plates (half the total payment in advance) (Session 025).
- Orchestrated a trap/manipulation: commissioned the party to commit the assassination, then publicly branded them as international terrorists while positioning himself as the law-and-order response.
- Was seen with [[Dr. Mason]] discussing moving stones south and working on a new project in Amare (Barzan's scrying, Session 045).
- Can place a spellcaster's astral orb into a metal box to sever their connection to their body; demonstrated against Barzan in Session 045.
- Can manifest physically at a distance to retrieve or deliver specific individuals; demonstrated with Koda in Session 069.
- Is coordinating with [[Dr. Mason]] on a larger plan involving the winter goddess and events tied to the Wild Hunt and final sacrifice (Session 069).
- Functions as a double agent, openly opposed to or non-cooperative with [[Dr. Mason]] despite outward appearances (Session 077).
- Maintains ownership of a significant Meat-House/Meat-Shredder facility (Session 077).
- Provides keys and access to extra-dimensional spaces for allies (Session 077).
- Maintains an office in Nolia where he can imprison people (Session 047).
- Works with or has a relationship with a dark red female succubus figure, observed by Barzan via scrying in Session 036.
- May control or influence the queen of Nolia through a magical ring (suspected by Barzan, Session 037; unconfirmed).
- Yorni's body has become a proposed contract object in a dragon binding initiated by Koda in Session 044; implications remain unresolved.
- May operate under or be identical to the alias/identity "Vokon" during planar instability events (Session 068; unconfirmed).
- Babo 1 (an influential figure in Helikonos) knows Yorni and maintains a working relationship with him (Session 046).
- Does not know Tahira's current location or status as of Session 025.
- Uses magical communication methods (magic phone calls) to contact party members (Session 012).
- Operates through the Diplomatie Ministry for investigations and assignments (Session 012).
- Has a brother — Erkas — who died from starvation (mummified remains identified via speak-with-dead in the Zentralbank vault, Session 049); Yorni had not revived him (Session 071); Barzan true resurrected Erkas in Session 106 using 25,000gp diamond dust — Yorni was furious (Insight 30 vs Nat 1) and retrieved Erkas from the party.
- [[Yaenne]] is Yorni's sister, designated as his replacement agent for the current mission (Session 071); confirmed as younger sibling by Erkas in Session 106.
- Family surname is Ambulan (confirmed by Erkas in Session 106); family originally from Lervik.
- When Yorni was ~8, a half-orc named Sedal lived with the Ambulan family (Session 106).
- Yorni secretly enchanted one of the party's coins as a tracker (discovered Session 106, arcana 22).
- Yorni's mother is a Succubus; she is also the mother of [[Demogorgon]], indicating infernal lineage and a sibling connection to a major demonic entity (Session 092).
- Historically recruited operatives formerly handled by [[Dr. Mason]]; [[Odyss Trion]] spent years doing procurement work for Yorni after escaping Mason in Nolia, before defecting to pursue personal ambition (Session 102 vision)
