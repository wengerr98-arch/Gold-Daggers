# Ysra

## Known Information

Ysra is a mysterious figure from [[Bragian]], a historical dwarven city-state later absorbed by [[Ophris]], who was discovered frozen and imprisoned in the ancient sunken city of [[Ylvar]] beneath [[Aarum]]. She was freed by the [[Gold Daggers]] in Session 094, agreed to join the crew, and subsequently served as navigator and guide on their infernal journey. She possesses knowledge of infernal travel cosmology and owns the Brandcreme from [[Jårnfestning]], which serves as a portal destination anchor.

## Session Appearances

- Session 094: Discovered frozen in the Sunken City of [[Ylvar]] (ancient sunken dwarven city beneath [[Aarum]]); the party descended underground specifically to rescue her; she was freed and agreed to join the crew, becoming a new party member.
- Session 095: Serves as navigator and boat-side support during the party's downtime in [[Aarum]]; the party returns to her on the boat after looting the Old Cathedral in [[Ylvar]].
- Session 096: Travels with the party as guide and navigator on the infernal route; explains the cosmology of the [[River Styx]], [[Limbo]], and [[Elysium]]; reveals that [[Bragian]] (a historical dwarven homeland) was her former home; provides the Brandcreme from [[Jårnfestning]] as a portal anchor; her connection to Lorilla is established during their shared infernal journey.
- Session 101: Waited with [[Brevin]] during the Astral Sea journey; was safely delivered to the Elves — a significant completed mission outcome.
