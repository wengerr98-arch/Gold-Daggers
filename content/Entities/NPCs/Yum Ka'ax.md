## Known Information

Yum Ka'ax is a divine or semi-divine fox figure whose name was revealed via Legend Lore cast by Barzan ("Der Fuchs heisst Yum Ka'ax"). He is described as representing all mammals and having always existed, and is capable of transforming into other animal forms including a sea leopard. He has traveled with the party and is closely bonded with Lorilla, who first encountered him as a constrained nature spirit in the [[Pocket Dimension]]; he also had a private conversation with [[Dr. Mason]] regarding trees should a city be reshaped.
