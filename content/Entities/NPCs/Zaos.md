# Zaos

## Known Information

Zaos is a space elf who responded to Barzan's sending spell in Session 070, introducing himself by name and warning the party to leave "because gravity," addressing Barzan as "mon ami." His casual use of French and named self-introduction suggests a recurring contact rather than a one-off voice. He is confirmed as a recurring figure by a DM correction note on the Session 070 report. The space elves as a group are known to live outside the [[Wheel of Planes]], per a legend lore vision seen by Barzan during the same session.
