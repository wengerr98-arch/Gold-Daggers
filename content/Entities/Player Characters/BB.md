## Character Summary

BB (Big Brush), a Tabaxi and member of the Sturm-Phönix guild, is one of the more intellectually driven members of the Gold Daggers — a researcher, codebreaker, and tactician whose most defining trait is her obsession with Amare and the mysterious Tarot cards that keep finding their way to her. She serves as the party's arcane investigator, having demonstrated abilities ranging from Suggestion and Firebolt to reading runes, identifying magical items, and parsing fragments of Deep Speech. Her most significant arc so far has been the slow unravelling of her personal origin story: the Tarot cards have physically teleported her across multiple planes to a distant island in Amare, her father has been located alive on the island group Quilti, and a Feywild Shard recovered from an Aboleth's lair resonates with the same supernatural source as her own strength — suggesting her roots lie somewhere between the Feywild and Amare. She died during a gelatinous cube encounter and returned with a supernatural afterlife experience whose full meaning remains open.
