## Character Summary

Barzan Aeducan is a dwarf from Ophris, a pragmatic and capable member of the [[Gold Daggers]] whose defining trait is his combination of martial effectiveness, craftsmanship, and political awareness. He moves through the world as a communicator and tactician — maintaining family ties back to Ophris, warning allies of danger before it arrives, and negotiating deals when others reach for weapons. His Ophris origin has made him a wanted man, carrying a 10,000 gold bounty that confines his freedom of movement, yet he continues to operate as one of the party's most reliable members. Over the course of the campaign he has grown from a guild operative into a fugitive revolutionary — killing Agnes to cover the group's escape, helping free hundreds of prisoners, and surviving encounters with Aboleths, an undead dragon, and Dr. Mason himself. His emotional bond to his homeland and his growing arsenal (a custom shield, bombs, and an unfinished pistol) reflect a character preparing for war.

## Character Arc

- **Origins as a guild operative:** Barzan began as a Sturm-Phönix member embedded in a competent party, defined by his Dwarvish identity, craftsmanship, and communication skills — traits that made him valuable but not yet tested.
- **Naming and belonging:** The formal adoption of the Gold Daggers name (Session 007), including Barzan commissioning the emblem shield, marked his investment in the group as a genuine identity rather than a job.
- **Becoming a wanted man:** The revelation of a 10,000 gold bounty tied to his Ophris origin (Session 015) transformed his freedom of movement into a strategic liability — a dwarf who once moved freely now must calculate every exit.
- **The war and family pressure:** Sending a message to his grandfather about military mobilization (Session 014) and experiencing nightmares about the war (Session 015) reveal that the Nolia/Ophris conflict is personal — not just political background. His family is inside it.
- **The Agnes killing:** Killing Agnes to cover the party's escape (Session 025) is the starkest moral line Barzan has crossed — a deliberate, calculated act to protect the group that cannot be undone.
- **Revolutionary from fugitive:** Fleeing Dalior as an international fugitive (Session 025) while having just helped free ~700 prisoners and participated in an assassination marks a transformation from mercenary operative to committed revolutionary actor.
- **The arsenal and the weapon project:** The ongoing pistol project with Geppo (Sessions 026–027) represents a craftsman's ambition — Barzan is not merely wielding weapons, he is building one. The bombs received alongside it suggest he is preparing for larger-scale conflict.
- **Udir's gift:** Receiving the ancient Basalt Tablet from Udir (Session 023) and being deeply moved by it suggests a bond and significance to that relationship that has not yet been fully explained — a personal thread with mythic weight.
- **The Last Sacrifice and theological knowledge:** Barzan's lore about Zima, God of Justice (Session 020) and the emergence of The Last Sacrifice hunting the eight swords (Session 028) positions him as someone whose background knowledge may become directly relevant to the campaign's supernatural stakes.
