## Character Summary

Eloryn Agarath is a Triton Warlock/Sorcerer who carries the weight of a revolution gone wrong — exiled from her people after being forced by her dragon deity [[Shen]] to publicly execute a revolution leader, she is now regarded as the greatest traitor in Triton society. Defined by a wry irreverence and a talent for improvisation (fireworks, demonic summons, using colored water to strip an enemy's invisibility), she nonetheless carries a deep private grief: her parents have been executed, and the mission to save her people grows heavier with each revelation. Within the [[Gold Daggers]] she occupies an unusual dual role — both a meticulous planner (she coordinated the prison evacuation logistics and engineered the interior minister's assassination through her familiar [[Blobert]]) and the party's social wildcard, equally capable of goofing around in a library or crawling through a sealed kobold tunnel toward a telepathic voice in the dark. Her most significant ongoing arc is the tightening web between her personal quest to defeat Shen and the emerging supernatural threats — the Deep Speech presence in the kobold depths and [[The Last Sacrifice]] — that seem to address her specifically.

## Character Arc

- **Exile and guilt:** Eloryn's defining wound is being forced by Shen to publicly execute a revolution leader — an act that branded her the greatest traitor in Triton society and ended her connection to her people before the campaign began.
- **First concrete lead on Muryn:** Lady Tyburn recognizing her aunt (Session 010) was the first sign that Eloryn's private quest has threads inside the city — followed by HErvir's confirmation that Muryn went north (Session 013) and Exandria von Amare's likely-but-denied knowledge (Session 015).
- **Blobert as an extension of self:** The arc from testing spellcasting through Blobert (Session 026) to transforming him into a familiar (Session 024) to using him to assassinate a minister (Session 025) traces a character learning to operate through a proxy — a parallel to her forced complicity under Shen.
- **Disclosure to the party:** Revealing the Shen connection and the forced killing to the full party in Session 026 was a significant trust moment — Eloryn stopped carrying the secret alone.
- **Parents confirmed dead:** Learning from the Triton lieutenant that her parents were executed (Session 043) forecloses a core part of her original quest ("save her parents"). The quest to "save her people" remains, but its personal stakes have deepened and sharpened.
- **The Deep Speech pull:** The telepathic voice in the kobold depths addresses Eloryn directly, telling her she will never free whoever is below — a supernatural antagonism that mirrors the Shen relationship and may be testing whether she can resist being used again.
- **The Last Sacrifice:** The emergence of Das letzte Opfer hunting the eight swords (Session 028) introduces a threat that intersects with both the kobold underworld arc and Eloryn's supernatural entanglements; its relationship to the Shen thread is unresolved.
