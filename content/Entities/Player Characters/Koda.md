# Koda

![[koda.webp|260]]

## Character Summary

Koda is a wolf-person Shifter and member of the [[Storm-Phönix]] guild, known within the [[Gold Daggers]] for her raw physical dominance and fiercely protective instincts. She has a reputation for aggression, but that same force has been consistently directed at protecting those who cannot protect themselves — most visibly in her role as primary caretaker for the three rescued children Robin, Mara, and Emmet. Her most significant ongoing arc centres on a deeply personal connection to the slave trade: she began recognising places and names in the slavery information book recovered from the [[Auktionshaus]], suggesting her own backstory is entangled in the network the party has been dismantling. Whether she will choose to confront that past — and what the slave-trade records that specifically referenced her actually contain — is the defining unresolved thread of her story.

## Character Arc

- **Protector instinct:** Koda's aggression is consistently channelled into protection — she was one of the first to reach Robin's shop during the city crisis (Session 018), became primary caretaker for the rescued children, and gave money to a child thief in Aarum rather than punishing her (Session 039). Her violence is purposeful, directed at threats rather than exercised for its own sake.
- **Physical identity:** Her defining combat style — raw physicality over subtlety — is established early (killed two zombies alone, Session 009) and repeatedly confirmed (arm-wrestling Snix, throwing out the guards, grabbing the kobold). Being a Shifter with a reputation for aggression frames this as both a gift and a social complication.
- **The slave-trade thread:** The moment Koda began recognising places and names in the slavery information book (Session 025) represents the most significant shift in her arc — her personal backstory is directly entangled in the network the party has been dismantling. This was foreshadowed by the slave-trade records in the Auktionshaus that specifically referenced her (Session 023). Whether she pursues this thread is the central question of her current arc.
- **Demon wound:** Koda was found unconscious with burns and corrosive injuries described as a demon wound (Session 024) — the origin of this wound is unresolved and may connect to the slave-trade network or the Oubliette events.
- **Recurring absence:** Koda has been notably absent from several sessions due to player absence (Sessions 019, 024) — each time the party has continued without her, and each time she has returned to find something has changed. This pattern is becoming part of her story.
