# Snix

![[snix.webp|260]]

## Character Summary
Snix is a small, scaly, and agile reptilian rogue who began as a member of the thieves' guild Sturm-Phönix before finding her place at the heart of the [[Gold Daggers]]. Her defining trait is the supernatural weight she carries: a combustion sword of unclear origin, a black smoke entity that intrudes on her dreams and body, and a bone-ring growth now physically fusing to her hand — all tied to a mysterious antagonist called [[The Last Sacrifice]] who hunts the Eight Swords and has recognized Snix from shared dreams. In the party she fills the role of nimble infiltrator and opportunist, contributing lockpicking, acute perception, and light fingers alongside heavier combat, but her personal arc has grown increasingly fraught: her recurring chain-and-smoke nightmares have worsened to the point of forcing the party to change houses, and her missing sister — her only surviving family thread — has lost its last known contact. Snix is becoming a character defined by things latching onto her from outside: a smoke entity, a sword with its own history, a tribal recognition she cannot explain, and a half-halfling prisoner who insists on claiming pieces of her.

## Character Arc

- **Entering the Gold Daggers:** Began as a Sturm-Phönix guild member and transitioned into the named party identity — present for the formal adoption of the Gold Daggers name (Session 007).
- **The sword and the smoke:** Found her sword with Mason; the sword began producing physical evidence of supernatural interference — dirt matching dream landscapes, smoke in her room, ceiling burns. The sword is now confirmed as one of the Eight Swords and a direct conduit for The Last Sacrifice's claim on her (Sessions 019, 022, 028).
- **The Last Sacrifice encounter:** Snix confronted an imprisoned halfling in the Oubliette's stacked cell mountains who recognized her from a dream, demanded the black smoke back, and refused to be ignored. This entity is now free, hunting the Eight Swords, and has escalated from dream-intrusion to growing bone into Snix's hand (Sessions 022, 028). In Session 029, the entity made direct contact, demanding its belongings and speaking of a "final victim."
- **The missing sister:** Snix's backstory of growing up with a sister in the guild was surfaced publicly at Yorni's awkward dinner. Tavira — the only remaining lead — is now confirmed dead, leaving the search thread without direction (Sessions 025).
- **Dave's tribal recognition:** An unresolved identity thread — Dave sees something of his own tribe in Snix, gave her the tribal cloak, and connected her presence to stolen eggs. What Snix is, or was, to that tribe remains open.
- **The prison break:** Played an active role in dismantling Mason's Oubliette — encouraging prisoners to rise, pulling up ladders under pressure, freeing ~700 people. A moment where Snix's opportunistic nature aligned with something larger (Session 023).
- **Leumas' true form:** Being one of the first to witness Leumas voluntarily reveal his changeling nature marks a deepening of inner-circle trust within the party (Session 024).
- **The bone growth:** A ring-like bone structure is physically fusing to Snix's hand — the supernatural claim is no longer just in her dreams. Something is taking root in her body (Session 028). Session 029 shows escalation: a red metallic ring grew into her palm, and the entity made direct contact (Session 029).
