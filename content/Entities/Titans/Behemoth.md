# Behemoth

## Known Information

Behemoth is the Earth Titan, one half of the named titan pair "Leviathan und Behemoth" from Kasta cosmological lore. Named alongside [[Leviathan]] (the Water Titan) via a History check in Session 080, Behemoth is a campaign-specific lore entity — distinct from the generic D&D creature of the same name. Its existence forms part of the cosmological backdrop the party must now actively engage with.

## Session Appearances

- Session 080: Named as the Earth Titan, companion to [[Leviathan]], revealed through BB's History check (dirty 20) as part of Kasta titan cosmology.
- Session 100: Referenced during Levistus's briefing on titan-like bodies and the wider planar threat; Behemoth as Earth Titan is part of the backdrop framing the party's next objectives.
- Session 101: Named in the Gilgamesh titan history discussion; the erased memory of the four titans (including Behemoth as Earth Titan) is a central topic, connecting Gilgamesh's explanations of early titan creation to the current threat.

## Known Facts

- Domain: Earth Titan
- Part of the named pair "Leviathan und Behemoth" in Kasta lore
- Counterpart: [[Leviathan]] (Water Titan)
- Referenced in Gilgamesh's account of the four titans and their erased history
