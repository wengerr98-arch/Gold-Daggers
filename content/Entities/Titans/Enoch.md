## Known Information

Enoch was named "der erste Titan" (the first Titan) and served as the primary antagonist of Session 089. He was encountered in the [[Gilgamesh Workshop]] and killed there by [[Leumas]], who received the tarot card "The Fool" at the moment of Enoch's death. Enoch appears connected to the nothingness/Silence spreading across the campaign world — the party fought him at the climax of a session whose central thread is the spread of anti-creation force.
