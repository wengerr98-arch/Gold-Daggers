## Known Information

Leviathan is the Water Titan, one of the Warforged Titans described by Tautok in Session 077 as fallen/dead. Also known as the "Crab Titan" — an alias confirmed by a DM correction note from Session 080. Leviathan appears in Kasta cosmological lore paired with [[Behemoth]] as the Earth Titan, and is referenced across multiple sessions in library texts and lore discussions as part of the broader titan history the party is investigating.

## Session Appearances

- Session 051: Barzan uses Legend Lore on a mysterious fish in the Waterplane and connects it to a Titan-like opposing force alongside a reference to Yog Sototh; the fish is subsequently released by Lorilla. The connection to Leviathan remains unresolved.
- Session 077: Named by Tautok as a Warforged Titan ("Crab Titan"); BB is mentioned as having previously encountered it. Warforged Titans described as fallen/dead.
- Session 080: Referenced as "The Crab" (confirmed alias) during the party's discussion of Kasta titan lore; named alongside [[Behemoth]] as the Water Titan in the "Leviathan und Behemoth" pair. BB's History check (dirty 20) surfaces this pairing; the party discusses the possibility of a sky titan as a third figure.
- Session 086: Named in library books recovered during the Kasta library reading challenge; Leviathan's role as a Titan is part of layered historical lore revealed alongside Genasi ancestry, archfeys, and ley-line information.

## Known Facts

- Domain: Water Titan
- Alias: Crab Titan (DM-confirmed)
- Part of the named pair "Leviathan und Behemoth" in Kasta lore
- Counterpart: [[Behemoth]] (Earth Titan)
- Status per Tautok (Session 077): fallen/dead (as part of the Warforged Titans)
- Referenced in Session 086 Kasta library texts
