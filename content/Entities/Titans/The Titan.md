# The Titan

## Known Information

A powerful cosmic entity connected to the Waterplane. During the whale fleet battle, a voice spoke telepathically — "HOW DARE YOU" — attributed to the Titan. Barzan's Legend Lore on a mysterious fish revealed that the fish's true form is "very similar to the Titan," suggesting the unnamed fish is the counterweight to the Titan in a Waterplane balance mechanic. The Titan appears to be a major cosmological antagonist or force, and may be related to "Golem / Fourth Titan / [[Enoch]]" — though the DM notes this identity should be confirmed before merging.

## Session Appearances

- Session 051: Speaks telepathically during the Waterplane whale fleet battle ("HOW DARE YOU"). Identified indirectly through Barzan's Legend Lore as connected to the fish/[[Yog Sototh]] balance conflict.
