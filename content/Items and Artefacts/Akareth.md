# Akareth

<div class="gd-seen"><p><span>Mentioned in 1 session</span> <span><a href="../sessions/session-036">Session 36</a></span></p></div>


## Known Information

Akareth is [[Koda]]'s sentient hammer, forged from the horn of a dragon also named Akareth. The weapon self-identified to Koda during Session 036 and is described as "angepisst" (angry/irate) in session notes, indicating a strong and forceful personality. The hammer carries the dragon's name and may carry aspects of its spirit or power.

## Session Appearances

- Session 036: [[Koda]] interacts with the hammer; it identifies itself as Akareth. Described as sentient with a distinct, angry personality.

## Known Facts

- Type: sentient hammer
- Current owner: [[Koda]]
- Origin: forged from the horn of a dragon named Akareth
- Personality: described as angry/irate ("angepisst")
- Self-identified its name to Koda in Session 036
