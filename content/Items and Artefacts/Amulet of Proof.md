## Known Information

The Amulet of Proof is a magic item that protects against scrying and detection, identified by [[BB]] in Session 015. It was purchased by the party from forge smith Trev in Session 024 and is also referred to in-session as "Amulett gegen Magiedetektion." By Session 072, Lorilla attuned to the amulet — her appearance became visibly paler and more desaturated after attunement, and the party discussed who should carry it as part of their strategic setup.

## Session Appearances

- Session 015: Identified by [[BB]] as providing protection against scrying; current holder at that time was [[BB]].
- Session 024: Purchased by the party from forge smith Trev; also called "Amulett gegen Magiedetektion" in-session.
- Session 037: Listed as part of the party's existing gear (in [[BB]]'s possession); referenced as "Amulet of Proof Against Detection and Location."
- Session 072: Lorilla attuned to the amulet; her appearance became visibly paler and more desaturated after attunement. Party discusses who should carry it.

## Known Facts
