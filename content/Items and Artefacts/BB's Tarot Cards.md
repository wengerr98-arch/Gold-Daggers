# BB's Tarot Cards

## Known Information

A set of story-critical magical tarot cards held by [[BB]] (Big Brush). The cards have teleportation properties — individual cards transport BB to specific locations. The deck has been growing steadily since Session 007, collected through discovery, gifts, and unexpected encounters. [[Felix]] discouraged further investigation into their meaning, suggesting hidden significance. [[Barzan Aeducan]] connected a throne-room vision triggered by one card to [[Zima]], the God of Justice.

The Queen of Wands card is unique: it is also the name and embodiment of a divine entity (Wanda) with a direct link to BB's soul — see [[Queen of Wands]] for its full stub.

Card no. 4 also has its own stub — see [[Card no 4]].

## Cards in the Deck

### Queen of Wands
- **Type:** Major Arcana
- **Acquired:** Present before Session 012; confirmed connection explored from Session 031 onward
- **Status:** Own stub — [[Queen of Wands]]
- **Notes:** Also the identity of Wanda, a divine entity soul-linked to BB. Invokes Daylight when BB and [[Lorilla]] activate it together; triggers Wild Magic surges when BB activates it alone. Wanda's staff was taken by [[Zima]] — active recovery quest as of Session 077.

### Card no. 4 (The Emperor — uncertain)
- **Type:** Numbered card / possibly Major Arcana IV
- **Acquired:** Session 019 — BB grabbed it mid-air during the night at the new hotel
- **Status:** Own stub — [[Card no 4]]
- **Effect:** Transported BB to an open field upon grabbing; caused a solo session-ending teleportation event.
- **Notes:** "Card no. 4" may correspond to The Emperor (IV) in standard tarot, but this is unconfirmed.

## Session Appearances

- Session 007: Tarotkarte der Sonne discovered on [[Lorilla]]'s forehead during a rest at the Rakshasa Tempel; ownership not confirmed.
- Session 012: BB shows at least two cards to [[Felix]] for inspection; Felix discourages further investigation. BB receives a new card from [[Leumas]] at the Bodenloser Krug — described as feeling "immediately right." Receives a second card, ending session with at least three.
- Session 019: BB grabs flying card no. 4 mid-air at the new hotel; immediately teleported to an open field — session-ending event.
- Session 020: BB lays cards at the Tempel; one card levitates and teleports nearby party members outside the entrance. Individual cards transport BB to specific locations: a meadow, a snowy mountaintop, a mountain throne (linked to [[Zima]] by Barzan), and the Amare coastline. BB collects shells at the coast. Takes the last card, ending the session with four confirmed cards. Also purchases non-magical tarot cards at the Boots-Markt for comparison.
- Session 031: BB and [[Lorilla]] activate the Queen of Wands card together, producing a Daylight effect before the monastery trial.
- Session 033: Queen of Wands produces Daylight again when BB and Lorilla are jointly involved.
- Session 035: [[Barzan Aeducan]] researches the Queen of Wands and wild magic at the library in Shicla.
- Session 037: Barzan uses a crystal ball to observe the Queen of Wands — sees her associated with a small shifting island near [[La Cadena]].
- Session 038: BB experiments alone with Queen of Wands — first activation causes all hair to fall out; second triggers a 10× Wild Magic result, leaving BB temporarily blue and hairless for ~24 hours.
- Session 044: BB researches the card system in the Aarum library, learning about negative cards, minor cards, major cards, and the Queen of Wands. Gains [[Azan Aeducan]]'s card.
- Session 047: Robin physically passes The World card to BB through direct touch.
- Session 063: [[Karoth]] gives BB the High Priestess; the negative cards The Emperor, The Hierophant, Wheel of Fortune, and The Hanged Man also go to BB; Death and Judgement are missed at this time.
- Session 071: Wanda (operating as BB) takes the Judgement card from [[Yorni]] at their meeting.
- Session 073: BB (operating as Wanda) receives the Page of Swords from [[Muryn]] at the Magic Taverne.
- Session 074: [[Fred]] gives BB The Lovers card at the Volk settlement reunion.
- Session 077: The Empress, Page of Swords, and The Lovers are referenced in the extra-dimensional scene. Wanda appears in person, restores BB's body, and tasks the party with recovering her staff (taken by [[Zima]]).
- Session 092: BB draws The Chariot card after shaking [[Lotti]]'s hand at the graveyard.

## Known Facts

- **Collection size:** At least 4 cards confirmed by Session 020; continued growth through Sessions 044, 047, 073, 092.
- **Named cards confirmed in deck:** Queen of Wands, The High Priestess, The Emperor, The Hierophant, The Empress, The Lovers, Card no. 4, Wheel of Fortune, The Hanged Man, Page of Swords, The Chariot, Judgement, The World, The Fool, Karte Nr. 6, Karte Nr. 7.
- **Additional unidentified cards:** Card from Leumas (Session 012), Azan Aeducan's card (Session 044), possibly Tarotkarte der Sonne (Session 007 — ownership uncertain).
- **Teleportation mechanism:** Individual cards transport BB to specific locations — meadow, snowy mountaintop, mountain throne, Amare coastline (Session 020).
- **Group teleportation:** One card levitated and transported nearby party members when placed at the Tempel (Session 020).
- **Non-magical comparison set:** BB purchased ordinary tarot cards at the Boots-Markt in Session 020 to compare against her magical ones.
- **Active connection to a divine entity:** The Queen of Wands is also Wanda, a soul-worker with a direct link to BB's soul (see [[Queen of Wands]]).
- **Zima link:** The throne-room vision triggered by one card was connected by Barzan to [[Zima]], God of Justice (Session 020). The card deck and Zima appear cosmologically interlinked.
- **Felix discouragement:** Felix explicitly discouraged BB from investigating the cards further (Session 012) — reason unknown.
- **Related stubs:** [[Queen of Wands]], [[Card no 4]]
