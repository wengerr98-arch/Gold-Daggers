# Bag of Holding

## Known Information

A Bag of Holding belonging to [[Lorilla]], used as persistent party-critical storage equipment. First confirmed in Session 012 when Robin returned party belongings from it. As of Session 047, the party holds at least two Bags of Holding — a second was recovered from under the city administration office.

## Session Appearances

- Session 012: Robin returns party belongings stored in a "bag of holding / Infinity Bag"; Barzan distributes 740 gold, leaving 630 gold in the bag.
- Session 020: [[Lorilla]] searches for the Bag of Holding and fails (failed roll); whereabouts unclear at session end.
- Session 029: Listed among party equipment during the kobold settlement stay; Lorilla stores water plants from the underground cave in the bag.
- Session 030: Listed among party equipment during travel toward the monastery and into Ophris.
- Session 042: The party is pulled into a Bag of Holding found in Madame Dimdelle's Pocket Dimension and fights the Dyre Troll inside before recovering books, gold, research, anatomical studies, and related materials.
- Session 047: BB, Snix, and Barzan recover stolen belongings from under the city administration office; a second Bag of Holding is found among the recovered goods. The party now holds at least two.
- Session 052: Referenced as party equipment; Loid's stored gear and kitchen gear recovered, likely via the Bag of Holding.
