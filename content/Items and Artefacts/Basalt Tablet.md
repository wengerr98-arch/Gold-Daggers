# Basalt Tablet

## Known Information

The Basalt Tablet is an ancient basalt artefact — described also as a basalt book — hundreds to thousands of years old. It was delivered magically by [[Udir]] to [[Barzan]] and functions as an attunement spell focus. Only the first three words have been deciphered so far: *"nahe der Sonnenwende"* (near the solstice), making it a story-critical mystery item with undetermined full contents.
