# Beets of Nurishment

## Known Information

The Beets of Nurishment is a glass jar containing 10–15 small spherical beets with magical sustenance properties. One sphere provides a full day's nourishment for a person. The item was found by the party during a dungeon crawl and is a campaign-specific named item.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 009]]: Found by the party during the dungeon crawl; one sphere provides a full day's nourishment.
