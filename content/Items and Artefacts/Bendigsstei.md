# Bendigsstei

## Known Information

The Bendigsstei (Swiss German: *bindigs* = binding, *stei* = stone) is a specific object [[Muryn]] is seeking, located north of [[Wild Hunt]] territory. It is intended to be used to bind [[Shen]] as part of a multi-step plan: first obtain a familiar without a patron, then empower it using the [[The Vestige|dragon scale choker]], and finally approach [[Shen]] and bind him using the Bendigsstei.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 072 - Protokoll: BB, Leumas|Session 072]]: Referenced by [[Muryn]] as her goal object; located north of the [[Wild Hunt]]. [[Muryn]] describes her three-step plan to the party.
