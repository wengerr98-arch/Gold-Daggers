# Blade of Avernus

<div class="gd-seen"><p><span>Mentioned in 1 session</span> <span><a href="../sessions/session-105">Session 105</a></span></p></div>


## Known Information

The Blade of Avernus is a Legendary Vorpal Sword artifact — a cursed longsword comparable to the R'lhey Schwert. Barzan was trying it out in Session 105 after it was acquired as Avernus loot. Full curse mechanics not yet fully explored.

## Session Appearances

- Session 105: Part of the Avernus loot distribution. Barzan fascinated and tried it out.

## Known Facts

- Type: Longsword; Legendary Vorpal Sword artifact
- Cursed blade — full curse mechanics not yet confirmed
- Comparable to the R'lhey Schwert in power level
- Current owner: Barzan (as of Session 105)

### Properties (as recorded)
- 2x Minor charges + cast one 3rd level spell (Fireball) — recharge 1-5: no recharge till dawn; 6: auto recharge
- 1x Minor charge + cast one 3rd level spell (Laeral's Silver Lance) — same recharge mechanic
- 1x Major charge + cast one 5th level spell (Transmute Rock) — same recharge mechanic
- 1x Major charge — all Potions within 10ft lose their effect
