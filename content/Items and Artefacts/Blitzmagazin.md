# Blitzmagazin

## Known Information

The Blitzmagazin (Lightning Magazine) is a magical weapon accessory sold by [[Nasri]] at [[The Tower]] quarter weapons dealer. When loaded with normal bullets, the enchanted magazine converts them into lightning ammunition, making it a specialised offensive upgrade for firearms-users.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 093 - Lured|Session 093]]: Mentioned as a product available from [[Nasri]] at [[The Tower]] weapons dealer.

## Known Facts

- Type: magical magazine / weapon accessory
- Effect: converts normal bullets into lightning ammunition when loaded
- Sold by [[Nasri]] at [[The Tower]] quarter weapons dealer
- Current holder: [[Nasri]] (vendor)
