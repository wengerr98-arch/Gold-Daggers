# Blobbert's Ring

## Known Information

Blobbert's Ring is a golden ring left behind by [[Blobbert]] upon his death/transition. It contains a one-time summoning of [[Blobbert]] in octopus form and now represents [[Blobbert]]'s binding to [[Eloryn]]'s pocket dimension (the [[Former White Rose]]). The ring is currently in [[Eloryn]]'s possession.

## Session Appearances

- Session 052: [[Blobbert]] leaves behind the golden ring after his death/transition; contains a one-time summoning in octopus form; represents [[Blobbert]]'s permanent bound state to [[Eloryn]]'s pocket dimension shell.

- Current holder: [[Eloryn]]
