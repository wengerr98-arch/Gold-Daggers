# Blue Stone

## Known Information

A blue stone found at the [[Ha'roshon Mesugo]] desert pool obelisk site. It is made of the same material as the [[Torr Key]], though confirmed to be distinct from that item. When brought near the obelisks in conjunction with the Torr Key, it resonates — suggesting some functional or ritual relationship between the two objects.

## Session Appearances

- Session 084: Blue stone resonates with the [[Torr Key]] at the [[Ha'roshon Mesugo]] obelisk site. Identified as the same material as the key but distinct. Questioned via Lorilla's fox familiar; no answers obtained.

## Known Facts

- Found at: [[Ha'roshon Mesugo]] obelisk site
- Material: same as the [[Torr Key]], but confirmed "not the same stones"
- Behaviour: resonates when combined with the [[Torr Key]] near the obelisks
- Associated reference: a "flower source" was mentioned in connection with this stone (no details obtained)
- Current holder: unknown
