# Book of Azatoth

## Known Information

A large 2-metre tome recovered from the sarcophagus of a giant crab creature at the bottom of the [[Mariannengraben-Spalt]] fissure near [[Helikonos]]. The book is a story-critical item containing cosmological lore about the Waterplane power structure, detailing the origins of [[Azatoth]]'s first warlock R'Lhey and the chain of rule leading to [[Shen]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 052: Recovered from the sarcophagus inside the crab structure at the bottom of the [[Mariannengraben-Spalt]] fissure; identified as the Book of Azatoth; contains key lore about the Waterplane power structure.

## Known Facts

- Recovered from: sarcophagus of giant crab creature, [[Mariannengraben-Spalt]], near [[Helikonos]]
- Physical description: large 2-metre tome
- Content — [[Azatoth]]: described as the "blind idiot god"; rules the Waterplane
- Content — R'Lhey: documented as Azatoth's first warlock; conquered the seas
- Content — Waterplane succession: implies [[Shen]] is R'Lhey's successor ("less personality")
- Content — cosmological link: notes the connection between the Obsidian Tempel and the Waterplane as important
- Content — cosmological counterpart: references [[Yogsototh]] as a counterpart to [[Azatoth]]
- Current holder: party (recovered in Session 052)
