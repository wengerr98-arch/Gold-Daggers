# Bragi Book

## Known Information

A lore tome dedicated to the ancient dwarven god [[Bragi]], found on the altar of the sunken dwarven cathedral in [[Ylvar]]. The party recovered it during their Session 094 exploration of the Sunken City. The book appears to contain historical and religious information about [[Bragi]] and dwarven cosmology.

## Session Appearances

- Session 094: Party discovers the book on the altar of the sunken dwarven temple in [[Ylvar]] and takes possession of it.

## Known Facts

- Found at: altar of sunken dwarven cathedral, [[Ylvar]]
- Subject: lore about the ancient dwarven god [[Bragi]] and dwarven cosmology
- Current holder: party (recovered Session 094)
