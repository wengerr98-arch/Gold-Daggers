# Combustion Sword

## Known Information

A story-critical sword acquired by [[Snix]] in Session 022, dramatically emerging from his stomach in a vomit scene. It had previously appeared in Snix's dream. According to [[Das letzte Opfer|The Last Sacrifice]], the sword was created by [[Dr. Mason]] and is associated with the black smoke entity inside Snix.

## Session Appearances

- Session 022: Snix acquires the sword (emerges from his stomach); detailed physical description given; [[Das letzte Opfer|The Last Sacrifice]] claims ownership and names [[Dr. Mason]] as creator.

## Known Facts

- Current holder: [[Snix]]
- Creator: [[Dr. Mason]] (per [[Das letzte Opfer|The Last Sacrifice]])
- Physical description: round grip, metal tube, leather-wrapped, round guard; blade black at the base (like soot) transitioning to clean silver at the tip
- Previously appeared in Snix's dream before manifesting physically
- Associated with the black smoke entity inside Snix
- Linked to Mason's ring and experiments
