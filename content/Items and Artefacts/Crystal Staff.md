# Crystal Staff

<div class="gd-seen"><p><span>Mentioned in 1 session</span> <span><a href="../sessions/session-051---book-of-yavin">Session 51 · Book of Yavin</a></span></p></div>


## Known Information

A unique staff found on the Nautiloid bridge resting on a table next to a map. BB identified it as having a "slimy attraction" and the ability to cut openings into the [[Waterplane]] and close them again, as well as teleport itself 45 feet. It is directly connected to the Waterplane rift network. Known in Swiss German as "Krestallstab." Ownership after Session 051 is unresolved.
