# Die Wege zur Industriellen Nutzung von Magie

## Known Information

*Die Wege zur Industriellen Nutzung von Magie* (approximate English: *The Ways Towards Industrial Use of Magic*) is a scholarly book authored by a figure named **Jakob**. It describes tower-like machines that store ambient energy — including emotions and life force — drawn from their surroundings, connecting the item to the industrial magic era of the Forgotten War. The book was recovered from the Verliess Ephraim vault and later reviewed by the party in Session 079, where [[Barzan Aeducan]] extracted its key contents with a natural 20 History check.

## Session Appearances

- Session 049: Recovered from Verliess Ephraim in the Zentralbank Hades sub-level.
- Session 079: Reviewed by party; key information extracted via History check ([[Barzan Aeducan]], Nat 20).

## Known Facts

- Author: Jakob
- Type: scholarly/technical book
- Recovered from: Verliess Ephraim (Effraim's dungeon/vault), Zentralbank Hades sub-level
- Content: describes tower machines that store ambient energy (emotions, life force) from surroundings
- Campaign-critical connection: links industrial magic era (Forgotten War) to [[Kasta]]'s eight towers and potentially to [[Sentinel]] constructs (which contained a mana/life-essence core)
