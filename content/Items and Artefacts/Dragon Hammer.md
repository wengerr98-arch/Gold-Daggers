# Dragon Hammer

## Known Information

The Dragon Hammer is [[Koda]]'s signature weapon, forged from the horn and spine of the dragon [[Akaresh]] by the smith [[Glargrit]] and the [[Gold Daggers]] in [[Ophris]]. The forging was a major campaign milestone: during the process the dragon's soul — still residing in the horn — attempted to charm the party multiple times, but the group succeeded despite the interference. The hammer has been in [[Koda]]'s possession since its creation.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 035]]: Forged at the smithy in [[Ophris]]. [[Leumas]] and [[Eloryn]] channelled lightning; [[Eloryn]] went unconscious multiple times and [[Barzan]] collapsed near the end. [[Snix]] and BB waited in the Tiny Hut. Dragon's soul attempted to charm the party during forging but was overcome. [[Koda]] kept her focus throughout.

## Known Facts

- Material: constructed from the horn and spine of the dragon [[Akaresh]]
- Forged by: [[Glargrit]] (smith) with assistance from the [[Gold Daggers]]
- Forged in: [[Ophris]]
- Current owner: [[Koda]]
- The dragon's soul was present in the horn during forging and attempted charm effects
