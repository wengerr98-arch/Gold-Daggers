# Eloryn's Pact with Chetzi

## Known Information

Eloryn's Pact with Chetzi is a physical parchment contract — signed in blood — formalising [[Eloryn]]'s warlock pact with her patron [[Cetzy]] (Chetzi). The document was drafted by [[Silk]] during Session 036. As part of the agreement, [[Eloryn]] gave her right arm as collateral; it was replaced with a golden arm. The full terms of the pact are currently secret.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 036]]: Pact formalised. [[Silk]] drafts the scroll. [[Eloryn]] signs in blood and surrenders her right arm as collateral — it is replaced with a golden arm. Full terms remain secret.
