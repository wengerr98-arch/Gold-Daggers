# Engraved Rock

## Known Information

The Engraved Rock is a carved stone bearing the symbol of the outer planes. Leumas purchased it from Ysra for 600 gold during Session 094. Its exact purpose and the significance of the outer planes symbol remain unknown.

## Session Appearances

- Session 094: Leumas purchases the engraved rock from Ysra for 600 gold.
