# Erwirs Reisen

## Known Information

*Erwirs Reisen* ("Erwir's Travels") is a picture book owned by [[Snix]]. It tells the story of a bard who journeys through the land and repeatedly encounters kings; in one key episode, the bard meets a king living alone in a small cottage who no longer remembers where his realm is or what his purpose was — the bard takes him along to find his kingdom again. The king then abruptly vanishes from the story, and the narrative accelerates through the remaining 13 kings. The book is speculated to hold possible cosmological relevance, with a suggested connection to [[Amarre]] and its 13 royal houses.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 018]]: [[Snix]] shows the picture book to the group. The story and its "forgotten king" motif are noted as potentially significant.
