# Feywild Shard

## Known Information

The Feywild Shard is a shard linked to the Feywild, found by [[BB]] in the aboleth chamber during Session 028. Its essence is described as connected to [[BB]]'s innate source of strength, suggesting a deep link between the shard and BB's personal power origin.

## Session Appearances

- Session 028: Found by [[BB]] in the aboleth chamber. Described as having an essence linked to BB's source of strength.
