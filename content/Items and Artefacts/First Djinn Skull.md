# First Djinn Skull

## Known Information

The First Djinn Skull is a skull belonging to the First Djinn, an ancient and cosmologically significant entity. It is currently held by Ysra, who discovered it in the Astral Sea before her imprisonment. When [[Barzan]] performed Legend Lore on the skull in Session 094, he received a vision depicting the First Djinn as a flame older than the universe itself, responsible for creating Djinns and fundamentally shaping reality.

## Session Appearances

- Session 094: Ysra shows the skull to the party. [[Barzan]] uses Legend Lore and receives a vision of the First Djinn as an ancient creative force predating creation itself.

## Known Facts

- Current holder: Ysra
- Discovered by Ysra in the Astral Sea prior to her imprisonment
- Legend Lore reveals the First Djinn as a flame older than the universe, creator of Djinns
- Classified as a lore-critical cosmological artefact
