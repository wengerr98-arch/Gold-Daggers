# Gargantuan Horn

## Known Information

The Gargantuan Horn is a rare and powerful artefact claimed by the party after defeating a Kraken in the sunken city of Ylvar during Session 094. Barzan identified it by name after the encounter, and the party's initial reaction suggests the item will have significant campaign relevance. It is currently held by Barzan Aeducan.

## Session Appearances

- Session 094: Party defeats the Kraken and claims the Gargantuan Horn as spoils of victory. Barzan identifies the item by name.

## Known Facts

- Current holder: Barzan Aeducan
- Obtained from the Kraken in the sunken city of Ylvar (Session 094)
- Identified by Barzan by name after the encounter
- Classified as a rare and powerful artefact
