# Golem Contract (Volkams Brauerei)

## Known Information

A magic scroll/contract that controls the brewery golem "Goldie," who works at [[Goldie Bräu]]. The contract was retrieved by the party from Volkam's safe after killing Felbrim Volkam. The golem's commands can be rewritten by performing a ritual using magical paper, ink, and 1,000gp worth of incense per line.

## Session Appearances

- Session 040: Retrieved from Volkam's safe. The party is considering modifying the golem's contract to serve as a mainland house servant. Silk was consulted about constructs and golem contract mechanics.

## Known Facts

- Current holder: [[Gold Daggers]]
- Rewrite ritual requires: magical paper, magical ink (500gp purchased by BB), and 1,000gp worth of incense per line
- An additional 500gp worth of incense was found in the brewery itself
