# Kryptex

## Known Information

A named puzzle-box device with encoded contents, currently held by [[Barzan Aeducan]]. Three wrong attempts to open it trigger a controlled explosion. Inside are two objects of unknown but adaptive power — their nature appears to change depending on whoever contacts them. It is a campaign-specific prop, not a standard D&D magic item, and was inspected by [[Felix]] in Session 012.

## Session Appearances

- Session 012: Carried by [[Barzan Aeducan]]; inspected by [[Felix]]. The two interior objects have no defined power but appear to adapt to whoever contacts them. Identity and purpose of contents remain unknown.

## Known Facts

- Type: puzzle-box / encoded container
- Security: three wrong attempts to open it cause a controlled explosion
- Contains: two objects of adaptive, undefined power
- Current holder: [[Barzan Aeducan]]
