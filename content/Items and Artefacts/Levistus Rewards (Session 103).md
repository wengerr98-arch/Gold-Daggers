# Levistus Rewards (Session 103)

## Known Information

A set of personalised magic rewards given to each member of the Gold Daggers by [[Levistus]] following the Stygian Gala in Session 103. Each item is tailored to its recipient.

## Session Appearances

- Session 103: Levistus distributed personalised magic rewards to the Gold Daggers after the Stygian Gala.

## Known Facts

### BB — Periapt of Wound Closure
- Twice a day Metamagic Option: Quickened Spell (referred to as "Quick'n'Spell")

### Barzan — Amulett of the Devout
- 5% (rounded up) of every heal is stored in a pool (max 30 hp); pool released as a Bonus Action targeting a creature within 30ft

### Eloryn — Metallic-Blender-Arm
- Once per long rest: force an enemy to re-roll a saving throw

### Leumas — Steady-Aim
- Bonus Action: gain advantage on one attack roll this turn; cannot move this turn

### Snix — Tattoo Distraction
- Once per long rest: use Dexterity saving throw instead of another save type

### Lorilla — Excellerate (One-Time)
- Once per long rest: gain 2 Actions this turn
