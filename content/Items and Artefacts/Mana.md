# Mana

## Known Information

Mana is a handball-sized glowing ball of liquid blue energy extracted by [[Snix]] from inside a destroyed [[Sentinel]] construct in the desert ruins outside [[Kasta]] during Session 079. [[BB]] identified it as "pure essence of life" belonging to no known school of magic, making it a distinct form of energy unlike anything in the standard eight magical schools. Its presence inside the Sentinel suggests that the industrial constructs of the Forgotten War era were powered by pure life essence, possibly connected to the tower-machines described in *[[Die Wege zur Industriellen Nutzung von Magie]]* that harvest ambient life force and emotions.

## Session Appearances

- Session 079: Extracted from the destroyed Sentinel construct by [[Snix]]; identified as pure life essence by [[BB]].

## Known Facts

- Form: handball-sized glowing ball of liquid blue energy
- Alias: Blue Life-Energy Core
- Identified by [[BB]] as "pure essence of life" — not from any of the eight known schools of magic
- Extracted from a destroyed [[Sentinel]] construct in desert ruins outside [[Kasta]]
- Current holder: [[Snix]] (as of Session 079)
- Possible connection to *[[Die Wege zur Industriellen Nutzung von Magie]]* and the Forgotten War's energy-harvesting technology

Al Bidaya ('The Beginning') is the ancient name for Mana used by Ephraim. He is searching for Al Bidaya again as of Session 106, intending to use it to create life (golems, titans, new gods, new races). Farid is said to have stolen Mana/Al Bidaya at some point (Session 107).
