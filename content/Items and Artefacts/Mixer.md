# Mixer

## Known Information

The Mixer (also called "Küchenmaschine") is a large bladed cylinder gifted to [[Eloryn]] by [[Geppo]] during a visit to his workshop. The gift was presented with great fanfare — announced as "FIRST PRESENT!" — though its precise purpose, whether as a weapon or a kitchen implement, has not been confirmed.

## Session Appearances

- Session 026: Gifted to [[Eloryn]] by [[Geppo]] in his workshop. Described as a large bladed cylinder. Eloryn cuts her hand on it.

## Known Facts

- Description: large bladed cylinder
- Also referred to as "Küchenmaschine" (kitchen machine)
- Current holder: [[Eloryn]]
- Origin: gifted by [[Geppo]] from his workshop
