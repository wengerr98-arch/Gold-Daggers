# Nihil

## Known Information

Nihil is a sword received by [[Snix]] at the start of Session 077, given alongside [[Albert]] as one of two new blades. It is confirmed as part of [[The Eight Swords]] by DM correction note, making it one of a set of eight significant weapons.

## Session Appearances

- Session 077: Received by Snix at session start; one of two new swords (companion item to [[Albert]]).

## Known Facts

- Type: sword
- Current holder: [[Snix]]
- Belongs to the set: [[The Eight Swords]]
- Companion item to [[Albert]] (both received together in Session 077)
