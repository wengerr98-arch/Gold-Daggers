# Obsidian Flint Dragon Plate

## Known Information

The Obsidian Flint Dragon Plate is a Legendary armor obtained from the Loot of Odyss (Session 106). The party noted it was too weak for their current power level and left it unclaimed for now.

## Session Appearances

- Session 106: Found in the Loot of Odyss; deemed "too weak" for the party's current tier — unclaimed.

## Known Facts

- Legendary rarity.
- Type: plate armor (dragonscale/obsidian flint construction).
- Currently unclaimed — in Gold Daggers' possession.
- Origin: Loot of [[Odyss Trion]] (Session 106).
