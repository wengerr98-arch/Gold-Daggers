# Okam

## Known Information

Okam is [[Snix]]'s named short sword, which appeared in Snix's dream sequence during Session 019 — found alongside [[Mason]] and seemingly part of a set of blades. The sword's grip features a notable gap suggesting it belongs to a paired or larger set, and a red-bladed sword also appeared in the dream as a possible counterpart. As of Session 038, Okam was stolen from Snix by the green Dragonborn Yazi during a road ambush.

## Session Appearances

- Session 019: Appears in Snix's dream. Snix hands the sword to Trev (the smith) during this session. The blade's dirt matches the dream imagery. The grip gap implies a sword set.
- Session 038: Stolen from Snix by the green Dragonborn Yazi during a road ambush; Snix loses the sword when Yazi attacks and takes it.

## Known Facts

- Named short sword belonging to [[Snix]]

- Blade had dirt matching imagery from the dream

- Currently held by Yazi (green Dragonborn) after being stolen in Session 038
