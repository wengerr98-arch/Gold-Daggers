# Planar Bomb

<div class="gd-seen"><p><span>Mentioned in 1 session</span> <span><a href="../sessions/session-093---lured">Session 93 · Lured</a></span></p></div>


## Known Information

The Planar Bomb is a weapon of mass destruction being constructed by Randrus Torren. It functions by opening portals to multiple planes simultaneously, scattering targets and body parts across different planes. As of Session 093, Torren has been conducting anti-Nothingness missions using this weapon and crafting is still in progress.

## Session Appearances

- Session 093: Torren discusses the Planar Bomb with the party; reveals he has been conducting anti-Nothingness missions using this weapon and that it is still under construction.

## Known Facts

- Constructed by Randrus Torren
- Function: opens portals to multiple planes, scatters targets across different planes
- Primary material: [[Kaltstahl]] (cold steel)
- Additional component: golden apples ([[Eden Apples]] / Ziegelsteistoub component)
- Crafting was in progress as of Session 093
- Intended for use against the Nothingness (implied by Torren's anti-Nothingness missions)
