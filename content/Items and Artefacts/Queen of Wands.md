# Queen of Wands

## Known Information

The Queen of Wands is one of [[BB]]'s tarot cards and is also the name taken by a divine entity (Wanda) who is directly linked to [[BB]]'s soul. The card has demonstrated magical properties including invoking Daylight when BB and [[Lorilla]] use it together, and triggering Wild Magic surges. As of Session 077, Wanda appeared in person, restored BB's body, and tasked the party with recovering her staff (taken by [[Zima]]) as a named side-quest to restore her full power.

## Session Appearances

- Session 031: BB and [[Lorilla]] activate the Queen of Wands card together, producing a Daylight effect during preparation for the monastery trial.
- Session 033: The card produces a Daylight effect when BB and Lorilla are both involved; the shared magical connection between the two through this card is unexplained.
- Session 035: Barzan researches the Queen of Wands and wild magic at the library in Shicla.
- Session 037: Barzan uses a crystal ball to observe the Queen of Wands — sees her associated with a small shifting island near [[La Cadena]] (Lakadena).
- Session 038: BB experiments with the card — first activation causes all hair to fall out; second activation triggers a 10× Wild Magic result, leaving BB temporarily blue and hairless for ~24 hours.
- Session 044: BB researches the card system in the library, learning about negative cards, major cards, and the Queen of Wands; a pouch of cards held by BB is referenced.
- Session 057: BB assigns Lionel (in [[Skjall]]) the task of researching the Queen of Wands.
- Session 070: Wanda introduces herself by name after the Queen of Wands reference; places a statue of Lotti in Aasimar form inside a base; Wanda and BB appear linked after BB loses a leg to wild magic.
- Session 071: Wanda reappears with the regrouped party in the Minotaur quarter; confirmed as a soul-worker with an active link to BB's soul; party discusses reaching BB's soul through dreams via the card's connection.
- Session 072: Wanda joins the party's investigation of the hidden tavern space in the Minotaur quarter; she realises her chosen soul may be destroyed, raising the stakes around the Bands of Imprisonment and the dragon scale choker.
- Session 073: Wanda operates as BB's alias throughout; she and Eloryn investigate the original logbook (natural 20), use Fabricate to copy Lotti's book, mark both books with invisible-ink paw prints, and connect with [[Muryn]] — from whom Wanda receives the Page of Swords card; the party exchanges a fake book with the elves at the Magic Taverne; Wanda hugs Muryn and says goodbye.
- Session 074: Wanda operates in [[Skjall]] alongside Leumas; investigates the Kanalisation and Lagerhalle areas, encountering a chest, logbook, eggs, and a blue dragon fish amid ongoing Wild Hunt pressure.
- Session 076: Referenced as an active entity during the Skjall throne-hall transition; Wanda's ongoing connection to BB positions the Queen of Wands as a continuing presence in the leadership transition arc.
- Session 077: Wanda appears in person, restores BB's body, explains concepts about her wand and soul-marking; tasks the party with recovering her staff (vanished by [[Zima]]) as a named side-quest.
- Session 089: Referenced in a future-vision sequence — Wanda, [[Zima]], and Helikonos are tied to the expanding nothingness thread; Wanda's connection to nothingness deepens.
- Session 092: Referenced as "Wanda" in the entities list; part of the party's active cosmological framework as they plan time-travel routes and research planar artifacts.
