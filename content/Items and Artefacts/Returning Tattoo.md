# Returning Tattoo

## Known Information

The Returning Tattoo is a magical tattoo that allows its bearer to recall a non-living object at the end of an action. It was originally found on the dead body of Olfos at the ocean floor and was retrieved by Eloryn. BB painted the tattoo onto Koda's hand in Session 051, after which it began to glow upon activation.

## Session Appearances

- Session 051: Koda receives the Returning Tattoo from Olfos's dead body via Eloryn; BB paints it onto Koda's hand; it glows upon activation.

## Known Facts

- Property: allows bearer to recall a non-living object at end of an action
- Original holder: Olfos (found on his corpse at the ocean floor)
- Current holder: Koda
- Applied by: BB (painted onto Koda's hand)
- Activation visual: glows when used
