# Rhythm Maker's Drum

## Known Information

The Rhythm Maker's Drum is a +3 magic item purchased for Leumas at Myrolox (the Ophris magic shop) in Session 105. Part of the equipment acquisition funded by the 600,000 gold payout (100,000 per person) received from Emperor [[Azan Aeducan]] after delivering the captured generals [[Marius Wehl]] and [[Helen Dravik]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 105: Purchased at Myrolox (Ophris magic shop) for Leumas. Part of the post-delivery shopping trip funded by the 600k Ophris payout.

## Known Facts

- Type: Drum (instrument-based magic item)
- Bonus: +3
- Current owner: Leumas
- Purchased at Myrolox, Ophris (Session 105)
