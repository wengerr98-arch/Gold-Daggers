# Ring of Bone Growth (Snix)

## Known Information

The Ring of Bone Growth is a supernatural binding manifesting on [[Snix]]'s hand as a reddish-metallic ring fused to the bone, formed as bone growth after the smoky figure [[Das letzte Opfer]] visited Snix. It is connected to [[The Eight Swords]] set and to [[Das letzte Opfer]]'s broader claim over the eight sword-bearers and their marks. [[Das letzte Opfer]] has stated that [[Dr. Mason]] created both the ring and the [[Combustion Sword]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 012: Snix tests a ring with an unknown spell or binding; Felix warns that something trapped inside is trying to take over the wearer; the ring takes no visible damage from any tool when Natok strikes it at the smithy.
- Session 022: [[Das letzte Opfer]] (imprisoned halfling in the Oubliettes) claims ownership of the ring associated with Mason's experiments and the mark on Snix's hand; states that Mason created both the ring and the Combustion Sword.
- Session 028: The smoky figure ([[Das letzte Opfer]]) visits Snix; a reddish-metallic ring/bone growth begins forming in Snix's hand, growing from within the bone itself.
- Session 029: The ring continues growing into Snix's palm; Snix is physically and emotionally affected; [[Das letzte Opfer]] references it alongside "the eight swords" and "a final victim" during its visitation.

## Known Facts

- Appearance: reddish-metallic ring fused to bone, growing from within Snix's hand
- Current holder: [[Snix]] (involuntary)
- Origin: manifested after [[Das letzte Opfer]]'s visitation
- Creator (per [[Das letzte Opfer]]): [[Dr. Mason]]
- Connection: part of [[The Eight Swords]] set binding
- Cannot be damaged: took no damage from smithy tools in Session 012
- Something is described as "trapped inside" the ring, capable of trying to take over the wearer
