# Ritual Knife (Ephraim)

## Known Information

A ritual knife given by [[Ephraim]] to [[Barzan Aeducan]] at the end of their meeting in the 8th Tower of Kasta (Session 106). High quality and well-maintained, but not a magic item in the traditional sense. Ephraim used a similar knife to draw Barzan's blood for circle magic during the Astral Dreadnaught transformation ritual. The knife's ceremonial or cosmological significance is unclear.

## Session Appearances

- Session 106: Ephraim used a dolch (knife) to draw Barzan's blood for his Zyrkel-Magic (circle magic). Barzan was sent back with a ritual knife. [[BB]] identified it — "well made, very high quality, much used but well maintained — not a magic item." Barzan returned to the party with it.

## Known Facts

- High quality, well-used but well-maintained craftsmanship.
- Not a magic item (confirmed via identification).
- Given by [[Ephraim]] to [[Barzan Aeducan]] at the end of their meeting (Session 106).
- Ephraim used it (or a similar knife) to draw Barzan's blood for circle magic before the Astral Dreadnaught transformation.
- Current owner: [[Barzan Aeducan]].
