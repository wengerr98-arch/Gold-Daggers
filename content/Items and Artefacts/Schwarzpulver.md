# Schwarzpulver

## Known Information

Schwarzpulver (Black Powder / Gunpowder) is a campaign technology item confirmed as generic black powder by the DM. It is sold by [[Kathi]] at the Boots-Markt in Dalior, who describes it as "one of the newest inventions from the wonderful neighbouring country," implying it originates from an unnamed neighbouring nation and may represent emerging campaign-world technology rather than standard equipment.

## Session Appearances

- Session 020: Sold by [[Kathi]] at the Boots-Markt in Dalior; Barzan buys a box for 10 Gold; Eloryn also considers buying Dynamit (60 gold/stick) from the same stall but finds it too expensive.

## Known Facts

- Type: generic black powder (confirmed by DM)
- Seller: [[Kathi]] at the Boots-Markt, Dalior
- Price: 10 Gold per box
- Origin: described as coming from an unnamed "wonderful neighbouring country"
- Related product at same stall: Dynamit (60 gold/stick)
- Purchaser in Session 020: Barzan
