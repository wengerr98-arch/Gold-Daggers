# Scroll of the Comet

## Known Information

The Scroll of the Comet is a unique, legendary one-time-use scroll with devastating area-of-effect damage. It was identified during the party's meeting with King [[Azan Aeducan]] in Session 037 and is estimated to be worth 50,000 gold or more. It is currently carried by [[Lorilla]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 037: Identified during the party's meeting with King [[Azan Aeducan]]; described explicitly with stats and estimated value; [[Lorilla]] carries it.

## Known Facts

- Type: legendary, one-time-use scroll (unique campaign artefact)
- Damage: 30d10
- Blast depth: 15 metres
- Blast radius: 155 metres
- Effect: destroys all structures and non-carried items within the radius
- Estimated value: 50,000 gold or more
- Current holder: [[Lorilla]]
