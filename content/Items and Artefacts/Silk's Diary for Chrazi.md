# Silk's Diary for Chrazi

## Known Information

Silk's Diary for Chrazi is a personal diary that [[Silk]] entrusted to the party during a farewell tea scene at the end of Act 1. The party accepted the mission to deliver it to Silk's younger sister [[Milly]] (also known as Chrazi / Kratzer), who lives on the island of [[Safti]] in [[Amarre]].

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 025]]: Silk gives the diary to the party during the farewell tea; they accept the personal errand to deliver it to Milly / Chrazi on Safti.

## Known Facts

- A personal diary written by or for [[Silk]]
- Intended recipient: [[Milly]] (aliases: Chrazi, Kratzer), Silk's younger sister
- Delivery destination: [[Safti]] (island in [[Amarre]])
- Current holder: the Gold Daggers party
- Accepted as a personal errand at the end of Act 1
