# Sonnenbibel

## Known Information

The Sonnenbibel (Sun Bible) is a religious text purchased by [[Barzan Aeducan]] from [[Randrus Torren]]'s book collection, which was itself originally part of the [[Sonnenkirche]]'s library. Barzan acquired it specifically to research the [[Sommergöttin]] (Summer Goddess), indicating the text contains theological or lore content related to sun or summer deity worship.

## Session Appearances

- Session 041: Purchased by [[Barzan Aeducan]] from [[Randrus Torren]]'s collection (acquired from the dissolved [[Sonnenkirche]]); used to research the [[Sommergöttin]].

## Known Facts

- A religious/theological text about the [[Sommergöttin]] (Summer Goddess)
- Originally from the [[Sonnenkirche]]'s library
- Acquired by [[Barzan Aeducan]] from [[Randrus Torren]]'s book collection
- Current holder: [[Barzan Aeducan]]
