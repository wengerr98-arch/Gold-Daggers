# Soul Externalisation Objects (Session 108)

## Overview

During the storm passage to reach [[Bob (Golem)|Bob]], each PC faced their greatest fear about death and externalised part of themselves into a physical object. This occurred after wisdom saves (only Snix and Elo saved) following church bells ringing.

## The Ritual

Each PC was transported to a formative/breaking moment from their past and found an object in their hand. They then chose which part of themselves to place into that object.

## Objects by Character

### Leumas
- **Object:** Small hand with long red nails
- **Memory:** Transported to the Feywild moment — close to but far from his mother, when he was slapped
- **Fear:** No purpose, unfinished business
- **Part Given:** Heart

### BB
- **Object:** Chalk (Kriide)
- **Memory:** The moment that broke her — the alternate timeline
- **Fear:** Was it all for nothing? Failure at saving the world
- **Part Given:** Lightness

### Barzan
- **Object:** Torch (Fakle)
- **Memory:** A talk with Ephraim where they realised he wasn't so different from them
- **Fear:** Are we doing the right thing? Uncertainty about choices
- **Part Given:** Self-confidence / Conviction (Selbstsicherheit/Überzügig)

### Lorilla
- **Object:** Snow globe (Schneechugele)
- **Memory:** Wildhunt alone
- **Fear:** Can only influence yourself, not everyone around you (moral limitation)
- **Part Given:** Primal Trust (Urvertrauten)

## Effect

After the ritual, the party broke through the storm with parts of themselves externalised. The mechanical/narrative consequences are not yet clear.
