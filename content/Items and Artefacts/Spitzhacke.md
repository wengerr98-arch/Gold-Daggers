# Spitzhacke

## Known Information

The Spitzhacke is a magical or enchanted pickaxe discovered during an underwater cave loot exploration. It creates a 4-meter hole and functions only on stone, indicating clear mechanical enchantment properties. It was found during the kobold cavern expedition and claimed as part of the loot division.
