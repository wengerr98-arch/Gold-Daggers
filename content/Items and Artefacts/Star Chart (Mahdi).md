# Star Chart (Mahdi)

## Known Information

The Star Chart is a navigation document drawn by [[Mahdi]] and given to the party to help them locate the [[Anubis]] pyramid and the dead arch-mage's expedition site. It was provided as part of a lore exchange and is connected to Epizentrum data referenced in the storm weather research scroll, making it a potentially campaign-critical navigation tool.

## Session Appearances

- Session 080: [[Mahdi]] draws and gives the star chart (along with a sextant) to the party as part of a lore exchange; points toward the wandering grave site of [[Anubis]] and an active quest lead.
