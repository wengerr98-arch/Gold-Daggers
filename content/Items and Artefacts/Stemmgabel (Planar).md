# Stemmgabel (Planar)

<div class="gd-seen"><p><span>Mentioned in 1 session</span> <span><a href="../sessions/session-078---crossroads">Session 78 · Crossroads</a></span></p></div>


## Known Information

The Stemmgabel is a planar tuning fork used to close portals between planes. One tuning fork is required per plane — a separate version exists for the Material Plane, and others exist for individual planes such as the Wild Hunt's plane. [[Torren]] agreed to obtain the Material Plane version within a week, making this a campaign-critical item tied to the effort to close the north portal near [[Skjall]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 078: Torren offers to obtain a Stemmgabel for closing the north portal near Skjall; the mechanic of one tuning fork per plane is introduced.

## Known Facts

- Functions as a planar tuning fork used to close portals.
- One tuning fork is required per plane.
- A Material Plane version exists; separate versions exist for other planes (e.g., the Wild Hunt's plane).
- [[Torren]] agreed to obtain the Material Plane version within one week (Session 078).
- Campaign-critical item linked to the north portal near [[Skjall]].
