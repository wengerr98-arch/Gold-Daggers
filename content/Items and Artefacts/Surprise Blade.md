# Surprise Blade

<div class="gd-seen"><p><span>Mentioned in 1 session</span> <span><a href="../sessions/session-095---die-vergessene-schwelle">Session 95 · Die vergessene Schwelle</a></span></p></div>


## Known Information

The Surprise Blade is a magical spiral three-blade shortsword custom-forged by [[Dakun]] from a vibrating hilt that Snix recovered. It was commissioned and completed in Session 095 at Dakun's workshop in the Schmiedi Tower in [[Aarum]]. BB identified the finished weapon: it subtracts 2 from a target's AC, making enemies easier to hit.

## Session Appearances

- Session 095: Snix commissions Dakun at his workshop in the Schmiedi Tower (Aarum) to forge the vibrating hilt into a complete weapon; BB identifies the finished blade and its AC-reducing property; delivered to Snix.
