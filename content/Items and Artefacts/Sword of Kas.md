# Sword of Kas

<div class="gd-seen"><p><span>Mentioned in 1 session</span> <span><a href="../sessions/session-052---book-of-r'lyeh">Session 52 · Book of R'Lyeh</a></span></p></div>


## Known Information

The Sword of Kas is a named campaign artefact and one of the most powerful swords encountered by the party. It has the Spirit of Titan Kas imprisoned within the blade and can dominate monsters at will (DC 25). The sword was broken during the Mariannengraben-Spalt encounter in Session 052, introducing a chance of backfire on future use; it can only be destroyed by a Wish spell. Note: this sword is **not affiliated** with [[The Eight Swords]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 052: Used during the [[Mariannengraben-Spalt]] encounter; broken during this session, introducing a backfire chance on future use. The party currently holds the sword.
