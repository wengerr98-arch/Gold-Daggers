# Talisman of Ultimate Evil

## Known Information

The Talisman of Ultimate Evil is a very spiky, intensely evil amulet found on a skeleton inside a small crypt beneath a mountain peak in the far north (Session 106). [[BB]] unearthed it using Mold Earth and picked it up — immediately upon contact her appearance began shifting: her eyes became more evil-looking and her fur/hair wilder. The item has 6 charges and is described as "absolutely crazy."

## Session Appearances

- Session 106: Discovered on a skeleton in a crypt dug out by BB using Mold Earth at a northern mountain plateau. Identified via distance Identify spell. BB picked it up; her appearance immediately shifted (eyes more evil, hair wilder). Lorilla's Perception check (22) sensed bad vibes before the crypt was opened.

## Known Facts

- Appears very spiky and radiates intense evil.
- 6 charges.
- Magic action, range 120 ft, DC 20 Dexterity saving throw — on a failed save, target falls into a nearby chasm (exact effect text unrecorded).
- Picking it up caused immediate appearance changes in [[BB]]: eyes more evil, hair/fur wilder.
- Found on a skeleton inside a buried crypt at a northern mountain peak.
- Current owner: [[BB]].
- Identified as "Talisman of Ultimate Evil" via BB's distance Identify.
