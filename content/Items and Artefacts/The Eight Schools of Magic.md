## Known Information

A book recovered from the vault "Verliess Ephraim" in Session 049, in the party's possession as of Session 079. It is approximately 440 years old and contains childish writing with a heavy focus on Abjuration magic, along with extensive notes added over the years — suggesting it was a working study document rather than a mere archival text. Its age and Abjuration focus may connect it to the founding era of [[Kasta]] and the figure of [[Ephraim|Saul/Ephraim]], who believed mortals should control magic. Note: this item is distinct from [[The Eight Swords]] despite the similar name.

## Session Appearances

- Session 049: Recovered from Verliess Ephraim in the Zentralbank Hades sub-level.
- Session 079: In party inventory; noted in session review.
