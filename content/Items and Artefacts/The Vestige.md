# The Vestige

<div class="gd-seen"><p><span>Mentioned in 1 session</span> <span><a href="../sessions/session-072---protokoll-bb,-leumas">Session 72 · Protokoll: BB, Leumas</a></span></p></div>


## Known Information

The Vestige is a choker adorned with dragon scales that shimmer blue, confirmed by DM as the item referenced in earlier sessions as "The Choker / Vestige" (Session 034). As of Session 072, [[Snix]] is wearing it. [[Muryn]] plans to use the item to empower a familiar as part of her scheme to bind [[Shen]] using the [[Bendigsstei]], though whether Snix's wearing it conflicts with that plan remains unresolved.

## Session Appearances

- Session 072: [[Snix]] puts on the choker; it has dragon scales and shimmers blue. [[Muryn]] identifies it as the item she needs to empower a familiar in her multi-step plan against [[Shen]].
