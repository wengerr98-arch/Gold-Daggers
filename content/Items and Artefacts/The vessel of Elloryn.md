# The vessel of Elloryn

## Known Information

The vessel of Elloryn is an item connected to [[Eloryn]]'s Pact of the Djinn warlock contract. During the battle at The Monument in Session 069, the vessel was observed destabilizing — described as "mehr am leake" — though its precise function within the pact and the lasting consequences of that leakage remain unknown.

## Session Appearances

- Session 069: The vessel linked to [[Eloryn]] is described as destabilizing/leaking during the battle at The Monument.

## Known Facts

- Part of [[Eloryn]]'s Pact of the Djinn warlock contract
- Current holder: [[Eloryn]]
- Noted destabilizing/leaking during Session 069
