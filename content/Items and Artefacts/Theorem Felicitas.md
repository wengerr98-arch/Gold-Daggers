# Theorem Felicitas

## Known Information

Theorem Felicitas is a book discovered during the Session 049 heist of the [[Zentralbank]], found inside a vault. Written by or commissioned by [[Dr. Mason]], its content centres on [[Felix]] and his curse, documenting Mason's significant investment of time and resources in attempting to break it.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 049 - Crime against the currency (The Heist)|Session 049]]: Discovered in the vault during the heist; reveals [[Dr. Mason]]'s deep involvement with [[Felix]]'s curse.

## Known Facts

- Written by or commissioned by [[Dr. Mason]]
