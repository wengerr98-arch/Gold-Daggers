# Titans and I

## Known Information

*Titans and I* is a lore book authored by a bard described as someone who loved animals, discovered by BB during research in Kasta via a nat-20 history roll. It contains at least two distinct titan lore entries: one describing a rumour about a sea titan, and another describing "a colossal burning man who burned in vain" alongside a reference to a man who froze a titan — implying a frozen titan whose location is unknown.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 087]]: Found by BB during research in Kasta (nat-20 history roll); described as the "perfect book" for the party's research into titans, ley-lines, and the desert gate.

## Known Facts

- Author: an unnamed bard described as someone who loved animals
- Contains sea titan lore (described as a rumour)
- Contains fire titan / frozen titan lore: "a colossal burning man who burned in vain… somewhere there is an eternal flame"; references a man who froze a titan
- Discovered in Kasta, [[Session 087]]
