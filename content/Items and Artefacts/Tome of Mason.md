# Tome of Mason

## Known Information

The Tome of Mason is a book attributed to [[Dr. Mason]], found and studied by the party in Session 054. It contains a magical flaw: a spell using time magic flavoured with death magic that constitutes a fatal error. Don Hor examined it (rolled 25) but could not fully crack it; BB noted that Mason made a mistake. The flaw appears tied to [[Felix]] Kalamites' curse.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 054 - Under the water, just killed Watergod|Session 054]]: Party finds and studies the tome; [[Don Hor]] and BB identify a fatal magical flaw — a spell combining time magic and death magic, linked to [[Felix]] Kalamites' curse.
