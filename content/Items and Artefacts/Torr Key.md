# Torr Key

## Known Information

The Torr Key (referred to in session as "de Schlössel vom Thor/Torr") is a key associated with [[Maradak Dreamkiller]] (Torr), with "Thor" being a misspelling or misspronunciation of "Torr." The party carries this key and used it at the [[Ha'roshon Mesugo]] desert pool site to test resonance with the obelisks; it and the [[Blue Stone]] are described as being made of the same material and resonating when combined near the obelisks.

## Session Appearances

- Session 084: Party tests the Torr Key against the [[Blue Stone]] at the obelisk site; resonance observed. Key noted to be made of the same material as the [[Blue Stone]].
