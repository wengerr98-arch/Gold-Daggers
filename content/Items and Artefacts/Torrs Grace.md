# Torr's Grace

## Known Information

Torr's Grace is a two-component artefact associated with the kingship of [[Skjall]], extracted by [[Leumas]] from the [[The Monument (Seelenschredder)|Monument (Seelenschredder)]] in the [[Thronsaal]] after he claimed the throne in Session 076. It comprises the Throne Cube and a suit of Dark Plate Armor; together they represent the mantle of kingship and are linked to the [[Mantle of the Torr]]. Wearing the armour causes Leumas to grow to approximately 1.5× his normal size.

## Session Appearances

- Session 076: Leumas receives the Throne Cube and extracts the Dark Plate Armor from the Monument (Seelenschredder) after publicly claiming the kingship following [[Maradak Dreamkiller]]'s death (Performance roll 32).

## Known Facts

- Two-component artefact: the **Throne Cube** and the **Dark Plate Armor**
- Throne Cube: received from the throne/monument upon Leumas's kingship proclamation; described as similar in nature to a stone from the kobold village
- Dark Plate Armor: dark red-shimmer steel described as "mega dexy und skinny" with light red thread-like elements running throughout
- Wearing the armour makes Leumas approximately 1.5× larger
- Current holder: [[Leumas]]
- Associated with the kingship of [[Skjall]] and connected to the [[Mantle of the Torr]]
- DM confirmed canonical name: "Torr's Grace"
