# Tracker Coin

## Known Information

[[Yorni]] secretly enchanted one of the Gold Daggers' coins as a tracking device, allowing him to know the party's location at all times. The party discovered this in Session 106 via an Arcana check (22) — identified as "Warlock Stuff with a tracker coin." [[Barzan Aeducan]] returned Yorni's money (including the coin) when the party gave [[Erkas]] back. In Session 107, Barzan attempted to dispel the tracking magic with various numerical inputs, all of which failed. Current status of the tracker is unknown.

## Session Appearances

- Session 106: Discovered by BB (Arcana 22) after Yorni arrived to retrieve Erkas — revealed how Yorni always knew the party's location. The coin was returned to Yorni along with his gold when Barzan gave it back.
- Session 107: Barzan attempted dispel magic on various numbers (341092, 4571, 327380, 129631, 8, 450219) — all failed. Tracker coin status remains unknown.

## Known Facts

- Enchanted by [[Yorni]] using Warlock abilities.
- Functioned as a location tracker — allowed Yorni to always know where the party was.
- Discovered via Arcana check 22 (Session 106).
- Returned to [[Yorni]] along with his gold when Barzan gave Erkas back (Session 106).
- Dispel magic failed on multiple attempts using various number codes (Session 107) — tracking may still be active.
