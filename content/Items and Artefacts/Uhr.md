## Known Information

The Uhr is an incomplete clock ("noch nicht komplette Uhr") that has been in the party's possession and is under ongoing repair. In Session 027, [[Geppo]] partially repaired it, getting one gear ("das eine Zahnrad") to turn. Its full function when completed remains unknown. This item is likely the same object as the [[Uhr (unfinished)|Uhr (unfinished)]] stolen from [[Tigerauge]] in Session 025.
