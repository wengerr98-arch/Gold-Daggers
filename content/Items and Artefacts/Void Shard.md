# Void Shard

> ⚠️ Lore-significant artifact. [[Enara]] was found trapped inside. Do not canonize identity of Enara without DM confirmation.

## Known Information

The Void Shard is an artifact-level item found in [[Odyss Trion]]'s villa during the Session 104 raid. It is capable of imprisoning living beings — [[Enara]] was found trapped inside it. In Session 105, BB attempted to stuff Odyss (in tortoise form) into the Void Shard, but failed an opposed Athletics check (14 vs 30).

## Session Appearances

- Session 104: Discovered in Odyss Trion's villa. [[Enara]] was found imprisoned inside it — wearing robes in the same style as [[Eloryn]]. Looted by the Gold Daggers.
- Session 105: BB attempts to stuff Odyss Trion (in tortoise form) into the Void Shard during combat. Opposed Athletics check result: BB 14 vs Odyss 30 — attempt fails.
