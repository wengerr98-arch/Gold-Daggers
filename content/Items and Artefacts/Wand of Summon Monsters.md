# Wand of Summon Monsters

## Known Information

The Wand of Summon Monsters is a named magic wand explicitly identified in Session 018, capable of summoning monsters. It is currently held by [[Lorilla]] and has remained in the party's possession through at least Session 024.

## Session Appearances

- Session 018: Named and identified in session; given to or held by [[Lorilla]].
- Session 024: Referenced in the entity list as still in the party's possession; [[Lorilla]] confirmed as current holder.

## Known Facts

- Type: wand (magic item)
- Function: summons monsters
- Current holder: [[Lorilla]]
- First appeared: Session 018
