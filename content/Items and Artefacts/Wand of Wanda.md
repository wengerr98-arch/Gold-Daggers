# Wand of Wanda

<div class="gd-seen"><p><span>Mentioned in 1 session</span> <span><a href="../sessions/session-077---results">Session 77 · Results</a></span></p></div>


## Known Information

The Wand of Wanda (also known as the Staff of Wanda) is the personal staff of [[Queen of Wands]], required to restore her full power. It was made to vanish by [[Zima]] (God of Justice), and recovering it is an active side-quest assigned to the party by Wanda in Session 077.

## Session Appearances

- Session 077: [[Queen of Wands]] reveals that [[Zima]] made her staff vanish; she tasks the party with recovering it as a named side-quest.

## Known Facts

- Type: staff / wand (magic item)
- Owner: [[Queen of Wands]]
- Current status: missing — hidden or banished by [[Zima]]
- Purpose: restores Wanda's full power when recovered
- Active side-quest: party tasked with retrieval as of Session 077
