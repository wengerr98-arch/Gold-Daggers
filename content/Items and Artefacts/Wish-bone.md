# Wish-bone

## Known Information

The Wish-bone is a campaign-specific item that was cracked by [[Eloryn]] and [[Leumas]] together in Session 069. As a result of cracking it, [[Leumas]] gained an at-will effect. Whether it is a unique artefact or a one-session consumable remains unclear.

## Session Appearances

- Session 069: [[Eloryn]] and [[Leumas]] crack the wish-bone together; [[Leumas]] gains an at-will effect as a result.

## Known Facts

- Type: consumable or unique artefact (unclear)
- Used by: [[Eloryn]] and [[Leumas]] (cracked together)
- Effect: [[Leumas]] gained an at-will ability upon use
- First appeared: Session 069
