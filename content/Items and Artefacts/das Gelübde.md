# das Gelübde

## Known Information

Das Gelübde (The Vow / The Oath) is an item — or unified concept — combining aspects known as Aegis and Kazan. In Session 093, [[Barzan]] made a History check (DC 31 with Smith Tools) on the Rings of Aegis, determining they were "definitely forged," indicating exceptional craftsmanship. A possible connection to [[Zima]] (God of Justice, stonemason and smith) has been suspected but not confirmed.
