## Overview

Aarum is the major port city on the island of [[Ophris]], and the Gold Daggers' primary operational base throughout the campaign. Located on a harbor with mercantile, martial, and magical infrastructure, it has become the nexus for the party's activities, trade networks, and political relationships. The city has proven resilient despite periodic monster attacks and supernatural incursions, and remains strategically vital to the region's stability.

## Character

Aarum presents as a thriving maritime hub with practical, well-developed quarters. The city features a bustling harbor with waterfront activity, a functioning brewery with a visible workforce, and an established Tower that serves as both a magical academy and political seat. The atmosphere is one of organized commerce and institutional order, punctuated by sudden danger — the city has experienced monster attacks and portal incursions that the party has had to actively defend against. The underground cathedral within [[Ylvar]] beneath the city hints at older, submerged history of dwarven grandeur.

## Known Facts

- Located on the island of [[Ophris]], serves as the Gold Daggers' current operational base
- Teleportation circle connects Aarum to the front-line town of [[Krüsam]]
- Hafen (harbor) is the primary commercial and maritime hub
- Subject to periodic monster attacks; Dragon Tortoises are noted as natural predators controlling the local jellyfish population
- In Session 057, a Dragon Tortoise hatchling ("Baby-Boy") appeared near the lift and harbor
- Beneath Aarum lies the Sunken City of [[Ylvar]]: ancient dwarven settlement with violet/purple fachwärk-style architecture, accessible via underground passages
- A sunken dwarven cathedral within [[Ylvar]] contains religious artifacts and significant treasure
- In Session 093, a portal near the city emitted imps; the party was later drawn through it into [[Nessus]]
- In Session 100, a tarrasque attacked near Aarum and was defeated by the full party
- The island itself showed signs of structural instability noted in Session 078
- Multiple NPCs were discovered to be infected by a dominate person network affecting the city and wider region
- [[Ylvar]] contains the skull of the First Djinn and an Aria Bible
- Aarum hosts a shooting range and extensive downtime facilities
