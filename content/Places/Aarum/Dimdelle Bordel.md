# Dimdelle Bordel

## Overview
The Dimdelle Bordel is an establishment in [[Aarum]] operated by [[Madame Dimdelle]]. Despite its name suggesting a brothel, its true purpose appears to be surgical body modification and enhancement rather than conventional services. The establishment functions as a front for advanced physical alteration operations using magical tools and techniques.

## Character
A professional, clinical space designed for surgery and body modification work. The atmosphere suggests clinical efficiency rather than vice; Madame Dimdelle conducts her surgical work dressed in formal, uncanny-valley business attire. The facility is equipped with magical tools suitable for precision surgical procedures.
