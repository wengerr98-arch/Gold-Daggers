# Nonna

## Overview
Ristorante Nonna is a casual dining establishment in [[Aarum]] run by [[Nonna]], an elderly dwarf woman and grandmother of [[Randrus Torren]]. The restaurant serves as both a neighborhood gathering place and a location for significant faction business meetings.

## Character
A warm, unpretentious restaurant with brown tile flooring and brown wooden tables. The atmosphere is family-oriented and comfortable, befitting a establishment run by an older dwarf woman who takes pride in her work. The space conveys a sense of home cooking and neighborhood hospitality.
