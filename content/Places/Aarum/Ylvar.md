## Overview
Ylvar is an ancient sunken dwarven city located in the underground passages far beneath [[Aarum]]. Once a thriving settlement with distinctive fachwärk architecture and violet/purple aesthetics, it now lies in ruin in the deep undercity. The city contains a significant cathedral and various treasures, making it both historically important and a source of valuable artifacts.

## Character
The sunken ruins feature fachwärk-style dwarven construction with a distinctive violet and purple colour scheme throughout. The architecture suggests a sophisticated, prosperous dwarven civilization. The atmosphere is ancient and haunted, with the cathedral particularly imbued with a sense of divine strangeness—notably the presence of a chamber of divine darkness that defies normal sensory perception.

## Notable Areas
- [[Old Cathedral]] — the sunken dwarven cathedral within Ylvar, containing the chamber of divine darkness and papal regalia

## Known Facts
- Location: underground passages far beneath [[Aarum]]
- Architecture: fachwärk-style dwarven with distinctive violet/purple colour scheme
- Contains: ancient sunken dwarven cathedral ([[Old Cathedral]]) with significant religious and material treasures
- Items recovered: skull of the First Djinn, Aria Bible (Bragi holy text), church treasures and regalia worth 50,000 gold
- Notable encounter: Kraken defeated in the city (Sessions 094)
- The city has been submerged for an unknown period, predating the current settlement of Aarum above
