# Old Cathedral

## Overview
An ancient sunken dwarven cathedral located within [[Ylvar]], the underground city beneath [[Aarum]]. Characterized by violet and purple fachwärk dwarven architecture, it holds both spiritual significance and substantial treasure, including papal regalia and a mysterious threshold chamber imbued with divine darkness.

## Character
The cathedral exhibits distinctive dwarven construction with violet and purple fachwärk detailing. The air within carries the weight of ancient ritual spaces. A threshold chamber contains an inexplicable void of divine darkness surrounding a suspended stone chair—a space that strips sensory perception from those who enter directly. The overall atmosphere blends dwarven craftsmanship with something divine and unsettling.

## Notable Areas
- **Divine Darkness Chamber**: A threshold room featuring a chair suspended in a void of absolute darkness; direct entry causes sensory deprivation (confirmed 10+ minute duration); traversable via Ethereal Plane
- **Wardrobe/Pope Room**: Chamber containing ceremonial vestments and papal regalia

## Key Figures
None confirmed.

## Known Facts
- Location: within [[Ylvar]], underground beneath [[Aarum]]
- Architecture: fachwärk dwarven, distinctive violet and purple coloration
- Contains sacred and ceremonial objects of dwarven religious significance
- Church treasures recovered and valued at approximately 50,000 gold
- The Divine Darkness Chamber contains mundane furnishings (ashtray, chair) despite its otherworldly properties
- Accessible via the main passage systems of [[Ylvar]]

## First Appearance
- Session 095: Party discovers the cathedral after recovering from kraken encounter; explores the Divine Darkness Chamber threshold and recovers ceremonial items and treasures
