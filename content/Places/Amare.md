# Amare

## Overview

Amare is the southernmost nation on the continent 00_Hamata, stretching along a sun-drenched coast of sparkling beaches, olive groves, and vineyards. Roughly half of its territory is a vast archipelago, with each island possessing its own distinct culture and history. It is governed by a council of twelve kings, making it one of the most politically distinct regions on the continent.

## Character

Sun-drenched coastal nation with clear seafaring traditions. The islands feature beaches suitable for landing tarot-card-teleported adventurers, with shells found on the shores. A long, deep history (2000–3000 years recorded) gives the region an ancient, settled character compared to younger kingdoms like Dalior. Primarily trade-focused and diplomatic rather than militaristic. (Physical sensory details sparse in session records; characterization inferred from geography and cultural role.)

## Known Facts

- Southernmost nation on the continent 00_Hamata; first introduced in Session 001.
- Approximately half of Amare's territory is an archipelago of islands, each with distinct culture and history.
- Recorded history spans 2000–3000 years (far longer than Nolia/Dalior's ~400 years).
- Governed by a council of twelve kings.
- Seafaring, exploratory, island-based nation tied to port cities.
- Trade routes to Amare are open (confirmed Session 014).
- Tensions exist between Amare and Offris in geopolitical/trade contexts.
- A Waterplane rift exists near Amare, part of Shen's rift network (Session 051).
- Canon spelling: "Amare" (session usage); "Amarre" appears in old vault records.
- Island coasts feature shells suitable for souvenir collection (Session 020).

## First Appearance

- Session 001: Introduced in world-building notes as the southern region/nation of Hamata; described as seafaring, exploratory, island-based, and tied to port cities.
