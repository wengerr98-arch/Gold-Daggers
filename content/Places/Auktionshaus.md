# Auktionshaus

## Overview
The Auktionshaus is a public auction house in [[Dalior]] that serves as a front for a slave trafficking operation. Located in the wealthy second tier (zweite Platte) of the city, it conceals a hidden tunnel system connecting to an underground network of enslaved captives. The building came to the party's attention when they discovered records linking the crime lord [[Koda]] to the slave trade operation.

## Character
An elegant, functional auction hall designed to attract wealthy clientele. The main floor appears professional and well-appointed, with display areas for goods up for bidding. The atmosphere is deliberate and orderly on the surface, but beneath lies a secret—hidden passages reveal crude holding cells and a tunnel system designed for the covert movement of captives. The building's design suggests both legitimate commerce and deliberate concealment.

## Notable Areas

- Holding cells (revealed by secret passage)

## Known Facts
- Located in [[Dalior]], likely on the zweite Platte (second tier).
- Functions as a public auction house for legitimate sales.
- Conceals a secret tunnel leading to a slave operation.
- Records found inside link [[Koda]] to the slave trade.
- An unnamed auctioneer was present at the building and fled upon seeing [[Barzan]] during Session 023.
- Holds enslaved captives (at least two known: a Wildfire Druid and an Aasimar, freed during Session 023).

## First Appearance
- Session 023: [[Barzan]] and [[Leumas]] enter the building during the uprising in Dalior; discover the secret tunnel and enslaved captives; find records implicating [[Koda]] in the slave trade; the auctioneer flees.
