## Overview
Avernus is an infernal plane region accessible from the material plane via an active portal network and by crossing the River Styx with Charon's aid. The landscape is one of desolation and arcane resonance, serving as a strategic mobilization point for infernal forces in a broader coalition forming for a coming conflict. The party first entered Avernus in Session 096 while traversing a route through the Lower Planes guided by Ysra.

## Character
Avernus presents as a bleak, ashen wasteland: sand, raw broken stone, ash rising in the wind, and charred remnants of trees scattered across the desolate terrain. The air carries a sense of scorching desolation. A network of cultic ritual circles and arcane obelisks marks the landscape, suggesting deliberate construction and ongoing use for planar purposes. The environment is harsh and unwelcoming—a place of pure infernal character, raw and barren.

## Notable Areas
- Cultic Ritual Circle (portal nexus destroyed in Session 096, but additional circles reported by Snix—at least 9 distinct circles detected)
- Limbo Obelisks and associated arcane circles (encountered beyond the first portal network)

## Key Figures
- [[Ysra]]: Guided the party through Avernus, explaining the planar routes and mechanics of infernal travel
- [[Charon]]: Facilitated passage across the River Styx, granting access to Avernus

## Known Facts
- Infernal plane region accessible via portal travel and River Styx passage
- A network of cultic ritual circles operates within Avernus, used for planar transit
- Limbo obelisks and arcane circles are present in the landscape
- At least 9 distinct ritual circles have been identified within Avernus
- Avernus forces were being mobilized as part of a war coalition by Session 101
- The portal network is active and functional for cross-planar movement
- The landscape is barren and hostile, characterized by ash, sand, scorched stone, and burned vegetation

## First Appearance
- Session 096: The party crossed the River Styx with Charon's aid and entered Avernus, discovering a cultic ritual circle and portal network. A destroyed circle and Limbo obelisks marked the deeper regions of the plane.
