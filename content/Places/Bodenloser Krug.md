# Bodenloser Krug

## Overview

Bodenloser Krug is an inn and tavern in [[Dalior]] that serves as a lodging house for travelers. It was the Gold Daggers' first arrival stop in the city during Session 004 and their primary base of operations throughout their extended time in Dalior through at least Session 014. The establishment offers both food service at the bar and private rooms for guests.

## Character

A traditional inn tavern with a welcoming front. The sign is marked with a mug to identify the location. The bar area displays a "Rooms available" sign above it, indicating its dual function as both a dining establishment and lodging house. The interior has a lived-in, comfortable atmosphere befitting a reliable waystation for adventurers and merchants.

## Known Facts

- Full name: Bodenloser Krug (translates roughly to "bottomless mug" or "endless mug").
- Located in [[Dalior]].
- Functions as an inn with rooms for overnight lodging.
- Functions as a tavern with a bar and food service.
- Identified by a sign with a mug symbol.
- The Gold Daggers' first overnight destination upon arrival in Dalior in Session 004.
- Served as the party's base of operations in Dalior from Session 004 through at least Session 014.
- The party stayed here across multiple sessions (Sessions 004, 007, 012, 013, and 014).
- In Session 012, Leomas awakened Big Brush late at night and showed her a new tarot card.
- In Session 013, Coda received a tailor-appointment message while en route to the establishment, confirming it as their ongoing Dalior base.
- In Session 014, the party woke here to discover that Leomas had been abducted, prompting their investigation into mass disappearances across the city.

## First Appearance

- Session 004: The party's first overnight destination and lodging upon arrival in [[Dalior]]; they encounter it after arriving in the city and asking for accommodation at another tavern.
