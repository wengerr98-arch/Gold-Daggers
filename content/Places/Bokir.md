# Bokir

## Overview
Bokir was an ancient northern settlement and civilization, home to air and water genasi who possessed significant arcane knowledge. Its culture was completely eradicated in antiquity, leaving behind only ruins and fragments of historical reference scattered across the world—most notably the [[House of Wisdom]] and cryptic mentions in burned documents.

## Character
A lost civilization, no longer inhabited or active. Once a center of genasi culture and learning; now known only through archaeological evidence and dusty library records. (Inferred from type and content.)
