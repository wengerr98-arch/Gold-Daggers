# Bragian

## Overview
Bragian was a historical dwarven city-state located on the continent, eventually absorbed into [[Ophris]]. It holds deep personal significance as the homeland of Ysra, one of the party's companions. The nature of its absorption into Ophris—whether through conquest, treaty, or gradual assimilation—remains a point of intrigue in the party's campaign.

A dwarven settlement of durable construction and industrial character, befitting dwarven craftsmanship.
