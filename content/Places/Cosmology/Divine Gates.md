# Divine Gates

## Overview
The Divine Gates are a cosmological barrier or threshold that once separated the gods from the mortal realm and the Astral Sea. Before the current era, the gods resided in the Astral Sea behind the Divine Gates; they were referenced by [[Ophris]] and [[Zima]] in the context of how the gods came to occupy their current roles after [[Wanda]] distributed divine offices to them roughly 1500 years ago, following the Leviathan's death.

## Known Facts
- Cosmological barrier or boundary separating the gods from the material plane (or formerly separating the Astral Sea from the mortal realm).
- The gods held offices/roles in the astral city before the current era; the Divine Gates were part of that prior cosmological arrangement.
- Referenced in connection with [[Wanda]]'s role in distributing jobs to the gods after the Leviathan died ~1500 years ago.
- Also spelled "Devine Gates" in session notes (Swiss-German phonetic spelling).
- First mentioned in Session 090 when the party learned about the historical relationship between the gods and the astral city.
