## Overview
Dalior is the capital city of [[Nolia]], built in a expansive valley near the [[Otronus]] mountain range approximately 5 days' travel from [[Lervik]]. This ancient settlement—with approximately 400 years of recorded history—is an engineering marvel: stacked on four vertical levels connected by a central lift and grand staircases, each tier serves distinct social classes and functions. The city is perpetually illuminated by magical light-plant blooms (Licht-Blumen) that glow without natural day-night cycles, creating an eternal twilight across the platforms.

## Character
Dalior's atmosphere is one of stark magical modernism layered with rigid social hierarchy. The air carries the constant hum of magical light and the smell of mineral stone from the carved plateaus. The upper tiers are bright, orderly, and populated almost exclusively by humans, while the lower levels grow darker and more labyrinthine. Magical enforcement is pervasive—a bell alarm system triggers instantly if commoners attempt magic on the higher plates, though the upper *zweite Platte* has no such detectors. Rivers and fountains run along the city's edges, and street markets cluster near the central water. The city feels built for function over beauty, though grand fountains and statues (including one of city founder Nedda) break the monotony.

## Known Facts
- Capital city of [[Nolia]]; located in a large valley near the [[Otronus]] mountain range, ~5 days' travel from [[Lervik]].
- Built on 4 levels: Untergrund (lowest), Hauptplatte, zweite Platte, and [[Palast (Dalior)]] (palace, highest); connected by a central lift and grand staircases.
- Rivers and fountains line each side of the city; the market on the Hauptplatte runs along a river.
- Magical light-plant blooms (Licht-Blumen) illuminate the city 24/7 — no natural day/night cycle; triggering one bloom with fireworks causes ~100 new blooms to spawn at chain-reaction speed.
- Magic is forbidden for the common population; a bell alarm system activates on magic use on the higher plates. No detectors on the zweite Platte.
- The zweite Platte is home to the wealthy and nobility; also contains the [[Sklavenmarkt]] (slave market). Humans almost exclusively on the upper plates.
- Fountain/statue of Nedda (a city founder) is located in the main square area.
- Boots-Markt (boat/dock market) located in Dalior; vendor Kathi sells fireworks, crystals, and Schwarzpulver (black powder).
- As of Session 013: trade routes to [[Offris]] are blocked due to forming war fronts; city is increasingly dangerous. Military has conscripted workers to [[Offris]] for weaponry repair and infrastructure work.
- Dalior is approximately 400 years old with recorded historical records.
- The underground arena (Fightclub Untergrund) is run by [[HErvir]] and is populated mostly by humans with some non-human fighters; matches are organized in teams of three.
- Street festivals occur spontaneously, especially around the Entertainment District when large crowds gather.
- The Wächter / Garden faction patrols the city and responds to magical disturbances.
