# Fightclub Untergrund

## Overview
The Fightclub Untergrund is an underground arena and fighting venue located deep beneath [[Dalior]]'s main city plate, within the broader [[Untergrund (Dalior)]] district. Managed by [[Herviar]], this establishment serves as both a neutral ground for organized combat and a hub of information for the region's underworld factions—particularly [[Das Mehr]]. It first came to prominence as a meeting place and point of contact between the Gold Daggers party and key figures like [[Dave]] and [[Gladis Gladigator]].

## Character
The descent into the fightclub is oppressive and disturbing, with corpses and spoiled food evident along the stairs leading down from the city platform. A thick magical darkness hangs in the air around the arena proper. The fighting pit itself is a crude, brutal structure designed for spectacle and raw combat, frequented by a motley crowd drawn largely from Dalior's human population. An adjacent bar area provides a more social space where management and patrons conduct business away from the arena floor.

## Notable Areas
- Arena pit (primary fighting venue)
- Bar area (adjacent to arena floor)
- Lower access levels and trapdoors

## Key Figures
- [[Herviar]] (owner/manager; also connected to [[Das Mehr]])
- [[Dave]] / Gabriel Comte Esteban de Morena (notable visitor; non-human attendee)
- [[Gladis Gladigator]] (notable visitor; non-human attendee)

## Known Facts
- Located beneath the main city plate of [[Dalior]], within the [[Untergrund (Dalior)]].
- Managed and operated by [[Herviar]].
- Predominantly human clientele, making non-human visitors notable and memorable.
- Connected to [[Das Mehr]] faction and their operations within [[Dalior]].
- Fight tournaments organized with team-based matches (teams of three noted in Session 013).
- Accessible via trapdoor and ladder system from the upper platform.
- The fightclub operates as a neutral ground and information hub for underground networks.

## First Appearance
- Session 013: The party descended into the arena, participated in organized fights, and met [[Herviar]], who confirmed his connection to [[Das Mehr]] and promised future collaboration regarding a planned prison operation.
