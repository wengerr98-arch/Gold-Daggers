## Overview
Die Villa is the Gold Daggers' secure safehouse and covert base of operations, located on a mountain slope above the city of [[Ophris]]. Assigned by King [[Azan Aeducan]] as part of their role in the [[Schwarzes Gänseblümchen]] assignment, it serves as both a home base and a sanctuary during operations in and around the Ophris region.

## Character
A mountain villa with a fortified private atmosphere. The structure contains comfortable residential quarters alongside military-grade security features—including a hidden panic room stocked with supplies, weapons, and an emergency exit tunnel. The villa maintains an air of quiet isolation, perched above the bustle of the city below, with a focus on self-sufficiency and discretion. Traces of the party's presence—including names and symbols left in hidden tunnels—mark it as an active stronghold.
