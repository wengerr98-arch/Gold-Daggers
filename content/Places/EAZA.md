## Overview
EAZA is a prominent restaurant and dining establishment located in [[Stygia]], serving as a hub for socializing, gathering information, and observing Stygian society. The establishment draws a diverse clientele and is known for its regional specialties from across planes and regions, making it a central venue in the city's social landscape.

## Character
EAZA features a warm, bustling atmosphere with engaged staff and patrons conducting conversations and business. The establishment has an open layout where diners are visible to the street, and the space maintains a flame (noted as "scho lang nömme gseh"—not seen in a long time), suggesting both history and a welcoming yet observant environment. The dining experience is characterized by attentive service and culinary presentation, with food plating and tea service in wooden cups reflecting a deliberate aesthetic.
