## Overview
El Loco is a restaurant and bar located near the market in [[Dalior]]. Originally constructed by builder Nigel Harrington in the early 1900s, the establishment has since changed hands multiple times. The party discovered a significant historical mystery here: the remains of a young woman entombed in the building's walls, whose identity may connect to Harrington's missing daughter.

## Character
A working-class bar and dining establishment with a layered architectural history. The building contains an original section (Harrington's construction), a middle section added during a later ownership transfer, and a newest section from recent renovations. The place serves straightforward food and drink to locals and travelers. (Physical sensory details inferred from building type and context.)
