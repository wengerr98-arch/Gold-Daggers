## Overview

The Entertainment District is a self-governing, renegade district within [[Dalior]], operating independently under the protection of [[Cetzy]]. Renowned for hosting the finest entertainment and food skewers on the continent, it refuses entry to city guards and functions as a community-like city within the city, with its own customs, defences, and cultural identity. Over the course of the campaign, it served as the Gold Daggers' primary base of operations before becoming a besieged holdout during the wider Dalior conflict.

## Character

A vibrant, festival-like atmosphere with densely packed buildings, multiple terraced platforms, and a strong sense of defiant independence. The district features colorful banners (black marking deaths, white marking births of importance), bustling entertainment venues, food stalls, and narrow streets filled with crowds. It has a scry-proof temple at its center, a fortress-like main entrance heavily defended, underground passages and fall-tunnels, and a largest fountain at its heart. The tallest structure, the Silberner Vorhang, rises above the district offering vantage points over the surrounding city.

## Known Facts

- Functions as a self-governing community district — refuses entry to city guards.
- Under the protection of [[Cetzy]]; operates as a "renegade/outlier district" independent of Dalior's main government.
- Renowned for hosting the best entertainment and food skewers ("Spiessli") on the entire continent.
- Black-and-white banner custom: black banners mark a death; white banners commemorate the birth of someone important when the mother died in childbirth.
- Kohle-Höhle casino: owned by Dragonborn couple; co-owner died in childbirth (Session 012); three healthy eggs in incubation.
- Der silberne Vorhang is the district's tallest building; used as a firing platform for fireworks (Session 020).
- The district has underground access points and a fall-tunnel system connecting to areas beneath the city administration.
- Main entrance converted into a defensive bottleneck; originally guarded by smaller gates, sealed during lockdown (Session 017).
- Contains a largest central fountain used as a gathering point.
- [[Cetzy]] formally declared the Entertainment District would act independently, raise troops, and go to war (Session 014).
- Residents can be evacuated toward the far end of the district; Syarodin temple area used as defensive meeting point.
- As of Session 056, under active war-related strain with barricades, zombie threats, and trade network disruption.
- Party's mint-green house base claimed and used as primary residence (Session 019 onward).
- Allied dwarf group (~20 warriors under Embrach) integrated into the district's defences and housed there (Session 019).
- Ogres that attacked the district in Session 017 confirmed to have originated from Mason's Platte (the second plate of Dalior).
