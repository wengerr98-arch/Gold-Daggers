# Former White Rose

## Overview
The Former White Rose is the Gold Daggers' primary operational base, repurposed from Madame Dimdelle's former pleasure district facility in [[Aarum]]. A mystical pocket dimension accessible via the Bubble Street portal, it serves as the party's headquarters and long-term infrastructure project. The space blends natural gardens with crystalline structures and serves as a center for governance, agriculture, and recovery.

## Character
The Former White Rose merges the remnants of an urban vice facility with surreal, otherworldly architecture. The pocket dimension features vast fields of glass boxes, towering glass palace structures, emerald meadows, and enchanted gardens. The atmosphere feels both eerie and hopeful—tainted by its past as a prison but gradually being transformed into something livable through nature magic and careful rebuilding. The land itself is magically constrained but responsive to druidic influence.

## Notable Areas

- Sacrifice altar (tied to fleshberg ritual)

- Sams garden (designated farming area)

## Key Figures
- [[BB]] — artificer who modified the Teleportation Circle; found sleeping on an operating table upon first entry
- [[Lorilla]] — nature mage who bonded with the land's guardian fox/nature spirit and cultivated edible plants
- The Fox — a fox-like creature/nature spirit bound to the land's vitality; appears as the pocket dimension's guardian
- [[Noah]] — considered as potential chief gardener for farming operations
- Thorren — external ally who negotiates on behalf of city interests regarding shared governance and resource management

## Known Facts
- Located in or adjacent to the former Pleasure District of [[Aarum]].
- Formerly operated as Madame Dimdelle's pleasure district facility, housing roughly 120–130 sex workers.
- Currently serves as the Gold Daggers' base of operations and a shared governance project with the Tower, government, and Thorren.
- Access is through the Bubble Street portal; also known informally as "die Bubble" or "the Pocket Dimension."
- Features gardens, two large buildings, an altar, a Teleportation Circle (magically modified by BB to prevent old pattern access), and extensive glass structures.
- The land was magically constrained; Lorilla's nature magic enabled seedlings and edible plants to flourish.
- Established as the party's base in Session 042; conversion from hostile facility to operational headquarters occurred over the course of roughly a week.
- BB's curse was removed while at this location.
- Freed sex workers were transferred to Tower care under agreed gentle conditions.
- Shared governance agreement established among the party, the Tower, government officials, and Thorren regarding materials, food security, and limited governmental presence.

## First Appearance
- Session 042: Party enters the pocket dimension via Bubble Street portal, liberates trapped workers, establishes the space as their operational base, and negotiates a long-term governance arrangement with city factions.
