# Gilgamesh Workshop

## Overview
A sprawling underground workshop operated by [[Gilgamesh]] 6000 years in the past, during an ancient era before recorded history. The party was transported here during a time jump and encountered [[Gilgamesh]], [[Jetsi]], [[Dako]], and hostile forces. Whether this location persists in the present-day timeline (perhaps as ruins) remains unknown.

## Character
An expansive subterranean complex with stone halls that echo with the sounds of active metalworking. The air carries the burnt smell of clay and working stone, mixed with the metallic scent of blood. Large cauldrons and forges suggest sustained craftsmanship and construction. The atmosphere is cold, utilitarian, and distinctly ancient—(inferred: the workspace of a master craftsperson operating in an era long before known civilization).

## Notable Areas
None confirmed.

## Key Figures
- [[Gilgamesh]] (operator/owner of the workshop)
- [[Jetsi]] (young, resident of the workshop in the ancient era)
- [[Dako]] (devoted follower, operates in support of Jetsi)
- A Sarrukh (the first humanoid species; observed working within the workshop)
- A Succubus (present during the party's visit; connection to Patreon/Jorni unclear)

## Known Facts
- The workshop exists 6000 years in the past, in the same geographical location as the cave system used to access it.
- The workshop contains large cauldrons and evidence of metalworking and forge activity.
- A Sarrukh (humanoid creature resembling a homunculis) resides here, representing the first recorded humanoid species in the timeline.
- The party was transported here during a massive time jump in Session 088 and encountered both allies and enemies.
- [[Gilgamesh]] and [[Dako]] escaped the workshop via Thunderstep during the combat encounter.
- Whether the workshop survives into the present-day campaign timeline is unconfirmed.

## First Appearance
- Session 088: Site of the 6000-year time jump arrival; base of operations for [[Gilgamesh]] in the ancient past; location of the Urochar combat encounter.
