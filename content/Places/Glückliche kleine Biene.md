## Overview
A small bar in [[Dalior]] operated by [[Aubonne]], who is one of two sisters. Despite its unassuming appearance, it serves as the clandestine headquarters of the revolutionary movement [[Das Mehr]], making it a politically significant location despite its modest exterior. The bar is where the party first encountered Aubonne and later returned to deliver crucial evidence and supplies.

## Character
An intimate, modest drinking establishment with a lived-in atmosphere. The bar contains at least an upper floor with sleeping quarters and a carefully concealed back room with a hidden drawer—suggesting safe-keeping and covert operations. The interior has enough space for small meetings and provides privacy through hidden areas, appropriate for its role as a revolutionary hub.

## Notable Areas
- Back Room: A concealed rear chamber containing a hidden drawer; discovered to hold a portrait linked to Aubonne's family history.

## Key Figures
- [[Aubonne]]: Owner and operator of the bar; one of two sisters; head of Das Mehr operations from this location.
- [[Melissa]]: Sister of Aubonne (relationship confirmed Session 006); status and current role in bar operations unclear.

## Known Facts
- Small bar in [[Dalior]], operated by [[Aubonne]].
- Serves as the headquarters of the revolutionary movement [[Das Mehr]].
- Has a secret back room with a hidden drawer containing a portrait linked to Aubonne's family history (discovered Session 025).
- Prison logbook deposited in the back room by BB in Session 025, accompanied by a personal letter from BB to Aubonne.
- Upper floor contains at least one room used as sleeping quarters or private space.
- Aubonne was found intoxicated and emotionally distressed during Session 025 visit; party members Leumas and Lorilla assisted her recovery.

## First Appearance
- Session 006: Aubonne tells Big Brush at the burning quarter to return to the bar in two nights; established as Aubonne's regular base of operations.
