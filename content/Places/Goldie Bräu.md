## Overview

A brewery in the western quarter (Westliches Viertel) of [[Aarum]] on [[Ophris]], formerly known as Volkam's Brauerei. The Gold Daggers seized it in Session 040 after eliminating Fellbrün Volkam and his construct partner, and have since maintained it as their primary operational base and commercial enterprise in the city. It produces beer, supplies the party, and serves as their Aarum headquarters.

## Character

The brewery occupies a run-down quarter that resembles the Torren-controlled areas but appears more neglected. A main entrance leads inside; downstairs contains storage and safe areas. The building has an elaborate underground system with a labyrinth of corridors and hidden passages, including a hatch-like exit from the main floor. The space still functions as an active brewery with brewing equipment and production capacity. Sensory atmosphere: industrial, functional, somewhat weathered. (drawn from Session 040 field descriptions)

## Notable Areas

- Entrance hall (ground level, guarded or monitored)
- Underground storage and safe room (downstairs, contains valuables and bulk stores)
- Labyrinth of cellar/laboratory corridors (beneath the brewery, formerly used by Volkam and Partner for private operations)
- Brewing hall (with active equipment and fermentation capacity)

## Key Figures

- **[[Goldie]]** — Golem; brews beer here under contract with the Gold Daggers. Originally Volkam's construct, now the party's property.
- **[[Susra und Kafra|Kafra]]** — Brewery manager and operations lead. Formerly Volkam's employee; rehired and promoted by the party. Twin sister of Susra. Pay raised to 20 gold per week as of Session 078.
- **[[Susra und Kafra|Susra]]** — Kafra's identical twin sister; works as support staff and general assistant. Hired alongside Kafra; wages tracked with her sister.

## Known Facts

- Located in the western quarter (Westliches Viertel) of [[Aarum]] on [[Ophris]].
- Formerly known as Volkam's Brauerei; seized by the Gold Daggers on Session 040 after Fellbrün Volkam's death.
- Resident golem "[[Goldie]]" produces beer here; operates under a modified arcane contract (ritual-altered in Session 041 by Eloryn and BB).
- Kafra serves as manager; Susra works as her support staff. Both hired at baseline wages, with Kafra's later raised to 20 gold per week.
- As of Session 078, the brewery is profitable, hiring additional staff, and filling large commercial orders.
- Used continuously by the party as a supply base, planning center, and social gathering point across multiple sessions.
- Safe found in the downstairs area contained 9,900 gold and 500 gp worth of incense (Session 040 loot).

## First Appearance

- Session 040: Party learns of the brewery's location from Daddy, storms the building, defeats Fellbrün Volkam and his golem construct Partner, and claims it as party property. Kafra rehired as manager.
