# Ha-lefaním

<div class="gd-seen"><p><span>Visited in 1 session</span> <span><a href="../sessions/session-087">Session 87</a></span></p></div>


<p class="session-meta gd-tl-meta"><span class="gd-tl-link" data-to="timeline" data-q="place=ha-lefan%C3%ADm" data-label="On the timeline →"></span></p>

## Overview
Ha-lefaním is an ancient hidden city located on the far side of a massive stone gate set into the desert mountains. Approximately 6,000 years old, it predates the first world-breaking and remains home to a thriving mixed-race settlement sustained by underground gardens, fields, and water sources fed by ley-line confluence. The site is sacred to the deities Nergal and Ereshkigal, whose shrines mark the gate and lakeshore. Access is guarded by a treacherous pyramid complex with reality-warping properties.

## Character
Upon arrival, the city resonates with the sound of hammer-strikes on anvils — evidence of active craftsmanship and habitation. The air carries the scent of water and green growing things, a striking contrast to the arid desert outside. The settlement is built within a vast cavern lit by an unknown natural source, creating an atmosphere of perpetual twilight. The architecture appears ancient yet maintained, with evidence of both pre-calendar construction and ongoing adaptation by the current inhabitants.

## Notable Areas
- The Desert Gate: massive stone gate set into a mountain face, marked with seven eyes and inscriptions in an unknown language; serves as the sole known entrance/exit to the city
- Pyramid Complex: six ancient pyramids surrounding the gate approach; each induces age alteration, form-shifting (transmutation to animal parts), or planar effects (jumping between Planes A and C); spawns [[Shakali]] creatures and was guarded by a Sphinx guardian (destroyed in Session 087)
- Hidden City proper: mixed-race settlement with dwarves ("chle zwärg"), humans, loxodons, and elves; populated by hundreds of inhabitants
- Agricultural Terraces: illuminated fields with grains and other crops
- Central Lake: sits adjacent to a mountain cliff within the cavern; supports fishing and water access
- Shrine of Ereshkigal: consecrated site by the lake, predating the first world-breaking
- Shrine of Nergal: shrine on the mountain face near the main gate; also pre-world-breaking

## Key Figures
- Sphinx: guardian of the pyramid approach; encountered and destroyed by the party in Session 087
- [[Shakali]]: creatures or type spawned by the pyramid complex; encountered during approach

## Known Facts
- Ha-lefaním is approximately 6,000 years old and predates the first world-breaking.
- The settlement is accessible only through the Desert Gate, a massive stone gate carved into a mountain in a distant desert region.
- The gate is described as "a gate to nowhere, dried out" — historically disconnected from the wider world.
- Entry to the site is guarded by six pyramids with reality-warping effects: age alteration, animal/creature transmutation (bunny ears, snake hands, pig noses, crab hands, pigeon form), and planar sliding (Planes A and C confirmed).
- The pyramid effects are non-lethal but cumulative; the city's inhabitants have presumably adapted to or learned to navigate these effects.
- The hidden city is populated by mixed races (dwarves, humans, loxodons, elves) — approximately hundreds of inhabitants according to party observation.
- The site contains gardens, fields, and a lake fed by underground water sources, supporting a self-sufficient agrarian settlement.
- Ley-lines converge at Ha-lefaním, treating the mountain/gate as a "well/spring" nexus point rather than a simple location; some ley-lines are described as "like fountains" when they have a vessel to pool in.
- Shrines to [[Nergal]] and [[Ereshkigal]] are present at the gate and lakeshore respectively, both predating the first world-breaking and apparently the source of earlier settlement lore.
- The settlement's language and customs are unknown to the party; inhabitants do not speak Common or known trade tongues.
- The site shows evidence of both ancient construction and continuous habitation — not a ruin but a functioning, if isolated, civilization.

## First Appearance
- Session 087: Party researches Ha-lefaním via legend lore and maps, travels through the desert pyramid complex, survives reality-warping pyramid effects, destroys the Sphinx guardian, and breaches the gate to discover the hidden city on arrival.
