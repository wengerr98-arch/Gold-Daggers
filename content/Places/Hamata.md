# Hamata

## Overview
Hamata is the campaign's root continent, encompassing the entire known world where the Gold Daggers operate. It is home to five major regional powers—Chasta (east), Offris (west), Amare (south), Norden (north), and the central regions—each with distinct cultures, geography, and political influence. The party's adventures span across its diverse landscapes and settlements.

## Character
Hamata is a large, geographically varied continent marked by distinct environmental zones: eastern oasis deserts with magical properties, western mountain ranges and river systems filled with apparatus and buildings, southern seafaring territories with islands and port cities, and a northern warlike region. The continent is characterized by cultural diversity, from peaceful oasis dwellers to exploratory maritime peoples, with scattered monsters and powerful rulers presenting ongoing threats to stability.

## Known Facts
- Hamata is the primary campaign continent and world setting for Gold Daggers.
- The continent is divided into five major regions with distinct cultures: Chasta (eastern, oasis-based, peaceful), Offris (western, mountain and river-rich), Amare (southern, seafaring and exploratory), Norden (northern, warlike), and central areas.
- Chasta is described as an oasis culture with a large magical desert.
- Offris features mountains, rivers, and numerous buildings and apparatus.
- Amare is oriented toward seafaring and exploration, with islands and port cities.
- Multiple city-states and settlements exist across the continent, each with local governance.
- A map of Hamata was produced and is known to travelers (purchased by the party in Session 025).
- Monsters and powerful, cruel rulers (gemeine Herrscher) exist as widespread threats across the continent.
- The continent exists in a world year 997 timeline.
