# Mariannengraben-Spalt

## Overview
The Mariannengraben-Spalt (also known as "The Rift") is a massive underwater fissure in the seabed near the city of Helykonos. This Mariana Trench-like chasm served as the lair of a colossal crab creature and contained artifacts of immense power. It was sealed by the party during Session 052 using the Staff of Power.

## Character
A deep-sea fissure with crushing pressures and darkness, accessible only with magical underwater breathing support. The rift descends to tremendous depths where colossal bioluminescence and unnatural forces dominate. The crab structure and the clouded edges of the fissure suggest otherworldly origins, possibly connected to eldritch powers from the Waterplane and beyond.

## Notable Areas
- The crab structure — central guardian creature at the bottom depths
- Black, gold-flecked mass chamber — containment site for artifacts
- Tunnel beneath the crab structure — approach passage sealed after closure
