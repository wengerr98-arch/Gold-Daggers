# Helykonos

## Known Information

Helykonos (also known as Helikonos, historically as Shi Cheng and Tritonia) is an underwater Triton city built on the back of a colossal living crab, accessible only through underwater travel and located beneath the waters of an ancient seabed. Ruled by the tyrant [[Shen]] since his murder of the previous king [[Klereth]], the city has endured strict monarchic control, the mass use of control stones to dominate key professionals, and the forced import of Nohlia human laborers on two-month rotations. As of Session 055 the city is in active civil war, with an active revolutionary movement, fleeing refugees, and a mixed Triton-refugee community beginning to form at the shipyard.

## Known Facts

- Location: Underwater, built atop and within the body of a giant living crab; accessible only via underwater turbines or magical transportation.
- Historical names: "Shi Cheng" (confirmed via DM note, Session 036) and "Tritonia."
- Ruled as a strict Triton monarchy with inherited succession until [[Shen]]'s violent seizure of power following his murder of King [[Klereth]].
- Control stones are used to psychologically dominate key professionals; teachers were controlled en masse for two years prior to Session 045 to prevent anti-state education.
- Human laborers from Nohlia imported on two-month rotations via an underground portal connecting to a land-based military camp; the Nohlia military camp was destroyed by the party in Session 055.
- Bioluminescent plants and coral structures provide lighting and serve as vertical transport throughout the city.
- Sand robot guards patrol the outer trenches and approaches.
- [[Tongath Schlucht]] — large chasm adjacent to the city; rumored to produce dangerous monsters.
- [[Mariannengraben-Spalt]] — major rift and geographical landmark near the city; point of extra-planar danger.
- Waterplane entrance lies within the city, guarded by Babo 1; connects to an extra-planar dimension.
- The revolutionary movement includes teachers, workers, and former military personnel; key figures include [[Stevos]], [[Kevos]], [[Mahlyn]], [[Navos]], [[Uthrin]], and [[Kodos]].
- [[Morlos]] — food truck operator and former general; contact with revolutionary networks.
- [[Ronross]] — Triton refugee who offers shelter to outsiders and has connections to teachers.
- [[Adryn]] — street-level intelligence contact (prostitutes guild informant).
- Mixed refugee and Triton community forming at the shipyard as of Session 055.
- Notable sub-locations: [[Mariannengraben-Spalt]], Entertainment District Temple (scry-free sanctuary), Nightlife District, the Crab Structure interior tunnels.
