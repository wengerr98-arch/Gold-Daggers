# House of Wisdom

## Overview
The House of Wisdom is an ancient knowledge collection originating from [[Bokir]], a northern culture of air and water genasi that has been eradicated. Stolen from Bokir, it is now stored in the eladrin library in [[Kasta]], where the party encountered references to it and later contacted [[Mahdi]] for books connected to the collection as part of their research into titans, ley-lines, and the desert gate.

## Character
The House of Wisdom is not a physical building but rather a curated collection of books and knowledge. It currently exists as a secured archive within the greater eladrin library system, suggesting scholarly preservation and restricted access. (inferred from context)

## Notable Areas
- Eladrin Library in [[Kasta]] (current location of the collection)

## Key Figures
- [[Mahdi]] (librarian/knowledge keeper who can access books from the House of Wisdom collection)

## Known Facts
