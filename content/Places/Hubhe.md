# Hubhe

## Overview

Hubhe is an island within the [[Amare]] archipelago, a sun-drenched coastal settlement with deep ties to the sea. It serves as the birthplace and ancestral home of [[Silk]], one of the party's early companions. The island represents Amare's characteristic blend of trade, culture, and ancient history.

## Character

A seaside island community shaped by generations of maritime tradition. Hubhe shares the sun-drenched, coastal character of its parent region, with clear waters and established settlements. The atmosphere suggests a place of some significance and stability within Amare's island network, though sensory details from visits remain sparse. (Physical character partially inferred from regional context and Silk's association with the place.)

None confirmed.

## Key Figures

## Known Facts

- Hubhe is an island within the [[Amare]] archipelago (also known as "Amare die Inselsammlung," the island collection).
- Silk originated from Hubhe and was captured and brought away from the island at some point in the past.
- Confirmed by DM as a distinct, named location within Amare's island group.
- Part of Amare's sun-drenched, seafaring cultural sphere.

## First Appearance

- Session 007: Mentioned by Silk as her home island during a conversation at the Rakshasa Tempel bar; described as part of the Amare archipelago.
