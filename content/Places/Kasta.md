# Kasta

## Overview

Kasta is the western region of [[Hamata]] and the only democracy on the continent, governed by a ruling body known as the "Seven" — a political council shaped by magical ability and school affiliation. Bounded by the sea on one side and endless desert on the other, Kasta is home to the renowned Wizard Academy comprising eight schools of magic. The city enforces a strict social hierarchy where magical power determines political standing and legal rights, making it a center of arcane learning and political intrigue.

## Character

Kasta greets visitors with a dramatic landscape: the sea glimmers on one flank while dunes stretch into haze on the other. The city itself is built without walls, relying instead on magical defense and its geographic isolation. Eight towers representing different schools of magic punctuate the skyline, visible from miles away. Over different quarters hang billowing sail-cloth shade structures that create a patchwork canopy, reducing the intense desert heat. The air shimmers with magical energy, and the streets near the harbor bustle with activity — merchants, mages, and travelers from across the known world conducting business with practiced sophistication. The wealthier inner city contrasts sharply with impoverished outer districts where the magical "lower classes" struggle against Kasta's rigid social hierarchy. Sunsets are particularly striking, the sun sinking low over the endless water while the towers catch its dying light.

## Known Facts

- Western region of [[Hamata]]; only democracy on the continent
- Governed by the "Seven" (Kasta Ruling Seven / Council of Seven) — political power tied to magical school affiliation and magical strength
- Eight magical schools with equal standing: Abjuration, Conjuration, Divination, Enchantment, Evocation, Illusion, Necromancy, Transmutation — each maintains its own tower
- Founded by a legendary Arch-Mage named Saul, who first established the university and later the city; was over 400–450 years old at some point
- Another renowned Arch-Mage (Ephraim) is associated with Kasta's history
- Ancient legends speak of two great beasts — one guardian of the land, one of the sea — and a powerful Golem regarded as the city's supreme protector
- Social hierarchy is strictly enforced: magical power determines legal rights, political voice, and legal authority; mortals with greater magical ability can legally punish weaker individuals
- The Genasi market is a notable trade district with earth genasi merchants like [[Mahdi]] dealing in rare navigation tools and Forgotten War lore
- The Eladrin Library holds guarded magical knowledge; books carry a Suggestion enchantment (attributed to Sarim or an older entity)
- The desert immediately surrounding Kasta contains magical hazard fields (darkness, heavy sand), buried chambers, blood traces, magical glass formations, and hidden workshops
- A buried desert chamber contains a powerful golem, homunculi guardians, a glass cocoon, a gnome-like corpse, and an encoded scroll platform
- Hidden obelisks in the desert near [[Ha'roshon Mesugo]] resonate with the [[Torr Key]] and [[Blue Stone]]
- The city library is warded with a hidden magical structure connected to the city's primary barrier; a blood archive/cellar suspected beneath the library test circle
- The library tower's steel door (DC 30 lock) was guarded by Ed and Og (Beholder-type constructs), both defeated by the party (Session 086)
- The party is no longer welcome in Kasta as of Session 083 after infiltrating the library
- A hidden desert gate beyond Kasta leads through mountain passages to an ancient green civilisation, connected to the city's pre-history
- No aliases recorded for "Chasta" misspelling in Session 001 (confirmed as Kasta)
