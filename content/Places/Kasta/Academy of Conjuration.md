# Academy of Conjuration

## Overview
The Academy of Conjuration is one of seven active schools of magic within the [[Wizard Academy]] of [[Kasta]]. It occupies its own dedicated tower within the Academy and serves as the primary institution for the study and practice of conjuration magic. As a major component of Kasta's magical and political structure, the Academy of Conjuration represents one of the ruling powers in the city and is led by [[Farid]], the Head of Conjuration.

## Character
The Conjuration tower features a first-floor vestibule with student clothing and robes for formal occasions. The interior atmosphere is studious and active, with students and their magical familiars present throughout; some students are accompanied by summoned creatures including ogres and other magical beasts. Upper floors contain teaching spaces, including room 207, which features a large wall-mounted board or tafele inscribed with magical formulae and conjuration circles—the physical tools of the craft.

## Notable Areas
- Room 207 — Brel's teaching and meeting space, with formulas and conjuration circles on the walls
- First floor vestibule — student robing and entrance area
- Lower levels
- Library or archives

## Key Figures
- [[Farid]] — Head of Conjuration, representative of the school on Kasta's ruling council
- [[Brel]] — Talented tutor and instructor, works in room 207; described as a small, chubby Loxodon

## Known Facts
- Sub-institution of the [[Wizard Academy]] in [[Kasta]]
- One of seven active magical schools that shape [[Kasta]]'s ruling structure and political power
- Led by [[Farid]], Head of Conjuration
- Houses a dedicated tower with multiple floors and instructional spaces
- [[Brel]] frequently teaches in room 207 and specializes in conjuration instruction, particularly fey summoning
- First-floor vestibule contains student robes and formal attire
