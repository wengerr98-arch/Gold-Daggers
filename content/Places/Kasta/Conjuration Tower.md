# Conjuration Tower

## Overview
The Conjuration Tower is one of the seven active magical school towers in [[Kasta]], housing the School of Conjuration. It serves as a center for arcane study and instruction in summoning and conjuration magic. The tower contains lecture halls, student quarters, and research areas, with established faculty overseeing training in conjuring disciplines.

## Character
A structured academic building with visible magical atmosphere. The tower's interior contains areas set aside for students to change robes and prepare for study, and teaching spaces are equipped with large wall-mounted boards or surfaces for writing formulas and magical diagrams. Studious energy pervades the building, with students frequently encountering instructors and conjured familiars moving about the hallways.

## Notable Areas
- Room 207: A primary teaching and research space for conjuration study, equipped with large wall-mounted formula boards and chalk-marked diagrams

## Key Figures
- [[Farid]]: Head of Conjuration; oversees the school
- [[Brel]]: Resident tutor in room 207; teaches enchantment to some of Torren's students despite being assigned to Conjuration

## Known Facts
- One of the seven active school towers in [[Kasta]]
- [[Farid]] is the Head of Conjuration and holds administrative authority over the tower
- [[Brel]] is based in room 207 and teaches enchantment despite ostensibly being part of the Conjuration school
- The tower has robing/preparation areas for students on the first floor
- [[Brel]] has prior experience with Fey summoning, which ended disastrously — he was lost in the desert for a week as a result
- The relationship between [[Farid]] (Head) and [[Brel]] (teacher) appears unorthodox, suggesting internal school dynamics are complex

## First Appearance
- Session 080: Party enters the Conjuration Tower and meets [[Brel]] in room 207; [[Leumas]] reveals himself as a changeling during this meeting
