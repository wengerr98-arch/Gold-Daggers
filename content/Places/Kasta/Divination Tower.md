# Divination Tower

## Overview

The Divination Tower is one of eight magical academies within [[Kasta]]'s Wizard Academy, dedicated to teaching and practicing divination magic. Unlike its sibling towers, it stands notably poorly maintained and shabby, its once-proud sigil faded and ghostly — a physical reflection of the school's decline. [[Teana]], the Rektorin and Archivarin, is the last active leader of the Divination school and operates from within this tower.

## Character

The tower's exterior is visibly run-down compared to the other academy structures — its sigil ("Sägel") is faded, colorless, and appears thin and insubstantial, as if drained of magical resonance. The interior presents an atmosphere of abandonment: the main reception area is dusty and neglected, with an air of vacancy. The space feels magically diminished, lacking the vibrancy and maintained precision of the other towers. (General atmosphere inferred from session and condition.)

## Known Facts

- One of eight schools within [[Kasta]]'s Wizard Academy

- Visually distinct from other towers: sigil is faded, colorless, and thin; the building is poorly maintained
- Teana is the Rektorin (director) and Archivarin (keeper of records)
- The Divination school is in visible decline; the last previous leader (Isaak) withdrew
- Entrance is via the main tower entrance, leading to a dusty, abandoned reception area
- The tower contains a secret exit used by the party to flee Kasta in Session 083
