## Overview
The Genasi Market is a Bedouin-style tent market that sprawls at the city limits of [[Kasta]], serving as a trading hub for merchants, travelers, and craftspeople. It is particularly frequented by Genasi who maintain strong cultural and commercial ties to the desert, and it functions as both a market and a social gathering place. The market connects the ordered city of Kasta with the open desert beyond, offering goods and knowledge not always readily available within the city walls.

## Character
The market occupies a small plaza where canvas tents rise on the edge of the city's sprawl, gradually transitioning to open desert. It is a busy, bustling place with constant activity and the sound of haggling and trade. The air is somewhat dusty and humid, the ground occasionally muddy despite the desert location. Loxodon herds pass through the area. The setting feels more open and alive than the city proper, with merchants working in semi-covered spaces and a tangible sense of freedom and movement—people are always in motion, engaging with goods, stories, and each other.
