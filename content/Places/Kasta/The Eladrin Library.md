## Overview
The Eladrin Library (also known as Zentralbibliothek or Eladrin Bibliothek) is a major academic and research institution within [[Kasta]]. It serves as a center of magical knowledge and learning, housing extensive collections of books and archives. The library also contains restricted magical infrastructure—including the Blood Archive in its cellar—that binds its users to Kasta's city barrier dome.

## Character
A grand, multi-tiered structure of increasing elegance and antiquity as one ascends. The building features a bustling public atrium filled with scholars and activity during normal hours. As visitors climb via spiral staircases, each platform becomes progressively more ornate—advancing from ordinary furnishings to gold-trimmed chairs with cloud-like cushions, and eventually to ancient tombs and increasingly older, larger books. Phase spiders inhabit the building throughout, distributed deliberately throughout multiple floors. The atmosphere is scholarly and well-maintained, with magical wards and patrols, creating an air of controlled access and hidden authority.

## Notable Areas
- Room 207: Teaching classroom where [[Brel]] holds classes on magical theory and history
- Main Atrium: Bustling scholarly hub with multiple entry points and constant foot traffic
- Upper Platforms: Series of ascending platforms, each more elaborately furnished than the last
- Tower Top (Steel Door): A sealed chamber at the apex of the spiral staircase (DC 30 pick to open), containing a dark textured room with an illuminated object and heavy magical wards
- Blood Archive (Bluetarchiv): Underground cellar beneath the test circle platforms; houses blood contracts binding persons to Kasta's city barrier; contains a Möbius band rune structure connected to the city's barrier magic

## Key Figures
