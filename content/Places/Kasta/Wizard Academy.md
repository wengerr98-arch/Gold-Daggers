# Wizard Academy

## Overview
The Wizard Academy is the premier institution of magical education on [[Hamata]], located in [[Kasta]]. Founded centuries ago by [[Saul]] (alias [[Abrahim (Ephraim)|Ephraim]]), an Arch-Mage who believed mortals should control magic rather than be controlled by it, the Academy serves as the intellectual and political heart of Kasta's governance. The institution is organized into schools of magic, each housed in its own tower, and its leadership directly influences the ruling structure of the city.

## Character
A place of austere magical grandeur and strict hierarchy. The Academy's architecture reflects its founding principle—towers of crystalline magic and stone rising over the city, each dedicated to a school of magic with its own distinct aesthetic. The atmosphere is formal and politically charged; students and faculty move with purpose, and social rank matters intensely.
