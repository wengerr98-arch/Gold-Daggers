# Kloster der Herbstgöttin

## Overview

The Kloster der Herbstgöttin (Monastery of the Autumn Goddess) is a functioning religious and administrative community situated in a mountain range near [[Ophris]], reached by ascending a long stone stairway. It serves the divine order of [[Herbstgöttin Frelka]] (the Autumn Goddess) and has become a significant waypoint and sanctuary for the Gold Daggers, particularly because [[Barzan Aeducan]] grew up within its walls.

## Character

The monastery rises from steep mountains in autumn golden light, with visible autumn colors illuminating its stone walls at sunrise. The setting evokes a place of austere discipline tempered by hospitality — the community maintains strict rules around weapons and magic at formal meals, yet offers warmth through feast celebrations and guesthouse accommodations. The atmosphere is one of quiet contemplation mixed with scholarly and practical work (brewing, gardening, printing, teaching).

## Known Facts

- Located in a mountain range near [[Ophris]], accessed via a long stone stairway; the birthplace of [[Barzan Aeducan]].
- Affiliated with the divine order of [[Herbstgöttin Frelka]] (Autumn Goddess).
- Operates as a functioning religious and administrative community with diverse internal operations: brewing, gardening, printing, library, laboratory.
- Feast hospitality requires guests to remove weapons and magic items during formal meals.
- Don Hor declared the Gold Daggers political prisoners in Session 031 and oversaw their combat trial at the training ground, testing patience, will, strength, and understanding.
- The Gold Daggers underwent a formal monastery trial at the Übungsplatz (training ground), where Udir animated practice puppets and Don Hor fused barrels into a large barrel monster for the party to defeat.
- The monastery has a functioning printing press operation under printmaster Addar, with [[Lionel]] assisting in letter mold casting and printing technology research (Session 032).
- [[Fred]], an orc pacifist, is a long-term monastery resident and has built a temple and school for his mission to promote peace among the orc clans of Skjall.
- The autumn harvest festival is a major ceremonial event presided over by Don Hor and Udir, featuring feasting, drinking contests, and community gathering.
- The monastery has access to maps, research materials (dragon books), and mysterious artifacts (stone plate, cryptex).

## First Appearance

- Session 028: The party uses the monastery and surrounding kobold village as a base of operations, discovering hidden tunnel systems beneath the settlement.
