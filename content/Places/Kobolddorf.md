# Kobolddorf

## Overview
A large underground kobold settlement built in a honeycomb-structure of tunnels and caverns, home to approximately 1,200 kobolds of multiple tribal groups. Located a few hours outside of [[Dalior]] toward the mountains, the settlement serves as [[Dave]]'s home village and a major hub of kobold civilization. The community is accessible through a hidden rock-door entrance and contains numerous sub-chambers including a communal eating hall (mensa), residential areas, and industrial workshops.

## Character
The Kobolddorf sprawls through extensive underground stone tunnels carved with deliberate labor over generations. The settlement features honeycomb-like architecture with multiple intersecting passages, some naturally lit by glowing crystals and plant-filled caverns near subterranean water routes. The air is thick with the sounds of industry and community—metalwork, conversation, and ritual activities—with a palpable sense of organized family groups living in interconnected domestic spaces. Deep within lie sealed lower tunnels—old blocked sections that suggest history and containment beyond current settlement life.

## Notable Areas
- **Mensa** — communal eating hall where the settlement gathers for ritual meals and soup feasts (turbi-root broth documented)
- **Sleeping Quarters** — large residential chambers housing the community; notable for varied belly colors among children and residents
- **Geppo's Workshop** — a crafting space where gear is repaired, modified, and created; Geppo serves as the settlement's primary craftsperson
- **Sealed Lower Tunnels** — blocked stone passages apparently sealed from the kobold side; a telepathic presence speaking Deep Speech can be detected through the barrier
- **Entrance Hall** — the primary receiving area where strangers are brought after entering through the rock-door
- **Lakeside / Shoreline Area** — a subterranean water route with submerged passages, aquatic plant life, and connection to the Water Plane; site of multiple aboleth encounters
- **Schlammbad** — a mud-bath or water-filled chamber within the complex
- **Crystal-Lit Chambers** — caverns and rooms illuminated by naturally growing or placed blue, orange, and pink crystals

## Key Figures

## Known Facts
- Underground honeycomb-structure housing ~1,200 kobolds of multiple tribal groups organized around family groups and work roles.
- Located a few hours outside of [[Dalior]], toward the mountains.
- The settlement is accessed through a hidden rock-door entrance, typically revealed by knocking and approached through algae and plant-filled terrain.
- A fur trail west of the settlement leads to a hole connecting to broader kobold tunnel territory (Session 029).
- The community holds ritual meals and burial ceremonies; a major burial was held for 25+ kobold dead and slain wild animals with memorial items sealed in a dedicated cave (Session 029).
- [[Snix]]'s possible connection to this village is unconfirmed.
- A related secret kobold city is hidden inside a mountain rock face on the route to Aarum; residents there trade dragon materials for diamonds and rare metals (Session 038).
- The sealed lower tunnels contain or are guarded against something that communicates in Deep Speech and claims to block those seeking to "free" something below.
- At least two aboleths have been encountered in the subterranean water passages beneath or near the settlement (Sessions 026/027, 054).
- Residents who first found the sealed tunnel passages experienced strange changes, and some disappeared afterward.
- An enslaved connection or rift situation existed as of Session 054, motivating the party's visit to address it.
- The community expelled visitors by knocking out a hole in a wall with a pick-axe to access deeper water-linked areas (Session 054).
- Portrait drawing by [[Agnes]] was intended for the burial cave memorial by [[Barzan]] and BB.
