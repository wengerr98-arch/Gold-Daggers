# Kohle-Höhle

## Overview

Kohle-Höhle (Coal Cave / Charcoal Den) is a casino and game hall in the [[Entertainment District (Dalior)]], housed in an establishment themed to resemble a mine shaft. Originally owned by a Dragonborn couple, the venue was temporarily closed in mourning after one co-owner died in childbirth (Session 012), but reopened shortly after (Session 013). It remains a central entertainment venue in the district.

## Character

The interior is designed to evoke a mine shaft aesthetic, with multiple levels connected by narrow passages, all accented with deep red carpets. The entrance is marked by distinctive white and yellow flags. The atmosphere is lively and sophisticated, with gaming tables arranged throughout, live music provided by a singer, and the hum of cards, dice, and roulette wheels. The establishment caters to gamblers of all skill levels, from casual patrons to serious players.

## Notable Areas

- Main gaming floor (with roulette, poker, and blackjack tables)
- Performance stage (where a singer performs)

## Key Figures

- Surviving Dragonborn partner (formerly co-owner; name not yet confirmed)
