# Krüsam

## Overview

Krüsam is a staging town accessed by teleportation, positioned as a supply and staging point for military operations toward the front lines and Helikonos. The settlement serves the adjacent harbor and is likely aligned with the kingdom's war effort against the Tritons.

## Character

Krüsam is characterized by muddy, waterlogged terrain and frequent harsh storm weather that drives the population indoors. The architecture features military-style houses reflecting the town's functional role as a military staging area. The presence of a pub serves as a gathering place for locals who discuss weather conditions and praise the king's active leadership at the front, though they are critical of his children's competence. The atmosphere is workmanlike and austere, with visible military organization.

## Notable Areas

- Pub (unnamed tavern where locals gather)
- Harbor (where Bradir operates, connected to horse commerce)

## Key Figures

- [[Bromar]] — horse vendor; sells mounts from Krüsam; can be contacted through his cousin Bradir at the harbor

- Accessed by teleportation in Session 043
