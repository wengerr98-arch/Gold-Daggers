# La Cadena

## Overview
La Cadena is an uninhabited, shifting island located in or near the region of [[Amarre]]. The island is notable for its association with the [[Queen of Wands]] entity and a collection of dangerous, unexplained phenomena. It has been observed to move rather than remain in a fixed location, suggesting it may be anchored to planar or magical forces rather than purely physical geography. The island appears to be a place of interest and danger for adventurers and scholars alike.

## Character
The island is surrounded by an atmosphere of otherworldly strangeness and dread. Accounts describe air rifts ("Risse in der Luft") that defy explanation, an observable connection to the Fey Wild, and a history of unexplained disappearances among those who approach it. One particularly disturbing account speaks of a sailor who burned for an entire day — whether due to magical fire, divine punishment, or fey curse remains unknown. The shifting nature of the island itself adds to the sense of unreality and danger.
