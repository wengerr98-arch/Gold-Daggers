# Lehrer Grotte

## Overview

The Lehrer Grotte (also known as Grotte der Lehrer, meaning "Grotto of the Teachers") is a cave complex located near [[Helykonos]] that serves as a safe house and waypoint for the revolutionary movement. The grotto is stocked with supplies and equipment, and [[Rektor]] maintains residence there, overseeing operations and offering shelter to those who arrive. The "Teachers" connection likely refers to the resistance faction within the city, representing educators and intellectuals who oppose the tyrant's control-magic regime.

## Character

The grotto is a practical, well-maintained cave space that has been developed with shelving, storage racks, and organized supplies including rations, survival kits, rope, canvas/tent materials, and ship fittings. The atmosphere is austere but functional—a waypoint rather than a luxury refuge. Damp air and the cool stone walls create a sense of security and isolation from the city above.
