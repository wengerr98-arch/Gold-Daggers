# Lerchen

## Overview
Lerchen is a working coastal harbor city, larger than [[Peoryn]], located near it on the sea. It serves as a major port and shipbuilding center, home to a thriving community of sea-people and a prominent active shipyard. The party visited briefly in Session 050 to arrange sea passage toward [[Helykonos]].

## Character
A busy harbor city shaped by wind and sea, populated by a hardy maritime community. The atmosphere is maritime and industrial—salt-worn, weathered, bustling with activity. The shipyard dominates the locale with active construction and repair work underway. The streets and docks reflect the working nature of the settlement, oriented toward maritime commerce and vessel operations.

## Notable Areas
- Shipyard (Brovin's operation—working docks, construction, repair facilities, central bureau/office)

## Key Figures
- [[Brovin]] (shipyard owner/operator; captain of vessels under construction)

## Known Facts
- Located on the coast near [[Peoryn]], larger than Peoryn
- Active working shipyard with ongoing construction and expansion
- Home to a community of sea-people (Seemenschen)
- [[Brovin]]'s shipyard serves as the primary vessel-building and operation base
- The shipyard bureau is the administrative center, with maps, designs, supplies, and workspace
- Used as an embarkation point for parties traveling to [[Helykonos]] by sea

## First Appearance
- Session 050: The party arrived and met [[Brovin]] at his shipyard to arrange sea passage toward Helykonos.
