## Overview
Lervik (also called Dorf Lervik) is a small lakeside village in the [[Nolia]] region and the founding location of the Gold Daggers. The party first assembled here under the banner of the [[Storm-Phönix]] thieves' guild, meeting in a warm tavern cellar that became their de facto headquarters. It serves as the symbolic birthplace of the campaign.

## Character
A quiet lakeside settlement with an intimate, warm tavern cellar notable for its unusual warmth even in early morning hours. The main tavern sits directly on the shore of a large lake, with modest architecture befitting a small trading village. The atmosphere is subdued and provincial — a place where guild business could be conducted away from larger city attention. (inferred from village setting and guild activity)

## Notable Areas
- Taverne der [[Diebesguilde]] — the main tavern building, with the guild cellar beneath
- Guild Cellar — subterranean meeting space containing a large central table, two seated statues, and seven empty seats; described as notably warm
- [[Ambulan]] Haus — large estate property situated near the village (now associated primarily with the theft mission of Session 001)

## Known Facts
- Region: [[Nolia]]
- Type: village / small settlement
- Significance: founding location of the Gold Daggers; where the party first assembled
- Faction affiliation: [[Storm-Phönix]] Thieves' Guild (at time of founding)
- Primary location: lakeside tavern with guild cellar beneath
- Cellar features: large communal table, two seated statues, seven empty chairs
- The cellar statues depict [[Sturm]] and [[Phönix]], both Earth Genasi, and both later found to be imprisoned in the Oubliettes prison system
- Guild cellar is unusually warm even in early morning hours
- The [[Ambulan]] family estate (a large, walled property with magical protections) is situated within or very near Lervik
