## Overview

Mason Haus is the residence and laboratory of [[Dr. Mason]], located on the [[Zweite Platte]] (Second Tier) of [[Dalior]]. The mansion appears grand but notably dilapidated compared to neighbouring estates on the tier. Beneath the residence lies an extensive underground dungeon and laboratory complex—a sprawling maze of rooms, puzzles, and experimental chambers filled with undead constructs and creature preparations. The basement served as the primary setting for the party's first major dungeon delve in Session 008.

## Character

The mansion itself presents a facade of faded nobility: tall windows, elaborate stonework, and ornate fixtures mark it as an estate of significant wealth, though dust and neglect suggest its owner's focus lies elsewhere—deep underground. The basement is oppressive and clinical, reeking of death and chemical experimentation. Anatomical drawings line the walls; ritual circles mark holding cells; machinery for raising and lowering bridges clatters from above; and the deeper chambers sink into stagnant, fetid water. The air grows colder as you descend, thick with the smell of preservation fluids and decay.

## Notable Areas

- **Entrance & Ground Floor**: The grand foyer with living quarters; guarded by Winfred, an elderly butler
- **North Basement Entrance**: Magically marked door (necromancy ward) leading to the laboratory levels
- **Kitchen Chamber**: First basement room; contains animated severed hands and crude cooking equipment
- **Central Cross-Layout Room**: Hub area with multiple passages branching to laboratory sections
- **Library Rooms**: Behind magically sealed doors (Transmutation, Illusion, and other schools); contents largely unexplored
- **Bridge & Trench Room**: Mechanical chamber with raised bridge, floor strewn with skeletal remains
- **Cage Chamber**: Anatomical study room with ritual-bound undead in containment circles
- **Deep Water Chamber**: Final explored area; slow-moving water zombies and strange aquatic creature inhabit the pool

## Key Figures

- [[Dr. Mason]]: Owner, necromancer; residence and laboratory operator
- Winfred: Elderly butler; stationed at the mansion entrance to discourage wandering visitors

## Known Facts

- Location: [[Zweite Platte]] (Second Tier), [[Dalior]]
- Owner/resident: [[Dr. Mason]]
- Features: grand mansion (dilapidated); extensive multi-level dungeon and laboratory beneath
- Basement prisoners freed: [[Lady Ty]], [[Galatea]], [[Melissa]], [[Brentan]], and Brentan's nephew
- Dungeon encounters: animated severed hands (including one named Jacques), gnole witherlings, large hand-based creature, zombie woman (dressed as nurse), skeletal remains, water zombies, frog/anglerfish-like creature
- Lore paintings in mansion: [[Grosser Hirte]] and [[Arakan der Kobold-Slayer]]
- Item found: Ring of Jumping (discovered by [[Barzan]])
- Laboratory notes indicate gnole witherlings were "not stable enough" for the intended work; suggest experimentation with larger beings and liches
- Magically sealed library doors suggest multiple school-based laboratory sections (Transmutation, Illusion, etc.) remain unexplored
