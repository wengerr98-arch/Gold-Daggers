# Mogon Distrikt

## Overview
Mogon Distrikt is a distinct district within [[Dalior]], the major city of [[Nolia]]. It is geographically and administratively separate from the [[Entertainment District (Dalior)|Entertainment District]], though both serve as urban quarters. The district contains taverns and bars serving the local population, including establishments with underground cellars.

## Character
A working-class urban district with standard city architecture and street-level commerce. Taverns and bars indicate a social hub for residents. The presence of a rat-plagued cellar suggests older infrastructure with underground connections or deterioration. (Physical atmosphere inferred from type and Session 006 context.)
