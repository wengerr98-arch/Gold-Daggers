# Nedda Brunnen

## Overview
The Nedda Brunnen is a prominent fountain monument located in the main square area of [[Dalior]], commemorating [[Nedda]], one of the city's founding figures. This public landmark serves as a visual anchor in [[Dalior]]'s bustling central district, providing a reminder of the city's 400-year history and early leadership.

## Character
The fountain stands as a break from [[Dalior]]'s utilitarian architecture of stone platforms and magical infrastructure. Its water features and sculptural elements offer relief from the otherwise stark, function-over-beauty design aesthetic of the city. The statue depicts Nedda herself, serving as a recognizable focal point for the populace and travelers alike in the main square.
