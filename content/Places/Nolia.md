## Overview
Nolia is a major nation in the wider world with significant political and military reach across the region. It maintains a sophisticated political infrastructure, including a royal household and formal government ministries. The nation projects power through military presence, labour agreements with neighbouring states (notably [[Helikonos]]), and major diplomatic events. By the campaign's timeline, Nolia's direct military involvement in regional conflicts has largely withdrawn, though political interests and independent operatives continue to act in Nolia's favour.

## Character
A nation-state with formal governmental structure and ceremonial importance. Nolia conducts major state events (such as royal weddings) and maintains both military and administrative presences in neighbouring territories. The character reflects a organized, hierarchical power with bureaucratic reach—emphasized by named ministerial positions and coordinated labour systems rather than ad hoc occupation. ## Known Facts
- Major nation in the wider world; referred to by name "Nolia" and also by alias "Gnolia."
- Contains the [[Rosenpalast]], a royal palace serving as the seat of the Nolia royal household (Königshaus).
- Maintains a formal governmental structure including the office of Aussenminister (Foreign Minister).
- Sends human labourers to [[Helikonos]] on a two-month rotation schedule via a portal system.
- Operated a military camp connected to a portal inside [[Helikonos]], coordinating military personnel with the labour rotation.
- The arrival of Nolia's labour rotation altered [[Helikonos]]' social fabric, making oppression more subtle but pervasive.
- Nolia military forces formally withdrew from [[Helikonos]] as of Session 055; independent operatives (e.g., [[Arthur Ruger]]) continued to act afterward.
- Imports weapons from [[Offris]]; trade relationships tied to broader regional military balance.
- Geographical landmark used for orientation in party planar travel and research (Session 095); kobold villages referenced in relation to Nolia's position.

## First Appearance
- Session 046: Referenced as the nation maintaining labour rotation and military presence in [[Helikonos]].
