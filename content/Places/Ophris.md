# Ophris

## Overview
Ophris is an independent city-state governed by King [[Azan Aeducan]], with a powerful military presence and fine dwarf-run facilities. It maintains diplomatic standing as an important player in planar politics and resistance efforts against [[Mason]]. The city granted the Gold Daggers formal authority to speak in its name and announced a formal alliance with [[Skjall]].

## Character
A snow-capped, refined city with visible dwarf engineering and high standards. Upon entry, visitors encounter a monumental statue at a gateway guarded by military personnel who screen visitors. The air is cold, and snow falls regularly. The interiors are marked by fine architecture and impeccable hospitality—a very fine, boutique ambiance. Walking earthquakes (or the reference to one) suggest either geological instability or a named landmark. The overall feel is organized, exclusive, and politically engaged rather than commercial.
