# Fightclub Untergrund

## Overview
An underground fight club venue located somewhere beneath [[Ophris]], distinct and unaffiliated from the similarly-named fight club in [[Dalior]] run by [[Herviar]]. It operates as a spectator and participant venue, attracting various patrons and fighters for staged combat events.

## Character
A fancy underground venue with a professional fighting pit and viewing areas. Described as polished and upscale rather than crude. The atmosphere suggests organized, sanctioned competitions rather than street-level brawling. (Further sensory details not yet confirmed from sources.)
