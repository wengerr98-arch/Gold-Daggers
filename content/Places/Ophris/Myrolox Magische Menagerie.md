A magic item shop located in [[Ophris]], operated by Myrolox. The shop serves as a purveyor of enchanted goods and magical artifacts to adventurers and wealthy patrons passing through the city. The party conducted a major shopping expedition here during Session 054.

A well-stocked purveyor's establishment dedicated to magical merchandise. The shop maintains an extensive inventory of enchanted items ranging from minor trinkets to powerful artifacts, catering to adventurers and wealthy clientele. (inferred from merchant-class establishment in a prosperous city)
