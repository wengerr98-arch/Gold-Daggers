# Otronus

## Overview
Otronus is a mountain range located south of [[Dalior]] that forms a geographic barrier to travel from the southern regions. The party crossed it on their approach to the city. It divides territories and must be traversed to reach the capital.

## Character
A substantial mountain range with passages suitable for wagons and travel. The crossing is manageable but notable—significant enough to mark the party's journey into Dalior's territory. (Detailed sensory description not yet documented in sidecars.)
