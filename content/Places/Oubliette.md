## Overview

The Oubliette is a vast, multi-level underground prison complex approximately 600 metres beneath the city of [[Dalior]], created and operated by [[Dr. Mason]] as an experimental confinement and control system. Originally holding roughly 700 prisoners across three distinct environmental zones, the facility was completely evacuated by the Gold Daggers in Session 023, sparking widespread riots across Dalior's upper levels.

## Character

A cold, inhumane place of isolation and despair, structured with brutal efficiency. The upper antechamber is sterile and administrative; the middle levels transition from a strange, bright underground meadow with daylight-like ceilings and verdant forests to an oppressively hot, desert-like environment where prisoners suffer severe dehydration; the lowest level shifts to a raw, improvised camp with hunting grounds and earthen structures. The air grows progressively hotter and more stifling as one descends, punctuated by the sounds of distant creatures (hydras, barlguras, apes) and the thin cries of confined prisoners.

## Notable Areas

- **Antechamber** (Level 1): Administrative entry point featuring a logbook and three teleportation circles; the middle circle serves as the primary entrance to the rest of the facility.
- **Underground Meadow** (Level 1): A vast, bright, grassy field beneath a daylight-like white ceiling, separated from the surrounding forest by open grassland; inhabited by two five-headed hydras serving as primary guards.
- **Underground Forest** (Levels 1–2): A sprawling wilderness filled with ancient trees, barlguras, ape-like creatures, and scattered prisoner hideouts—both intentional (tree-holes, small sanctuaries) and emergent (hiding spots built by long-term inhabitants). Approximately 600 metres below ground.
- **Cell Mountains** / **Stacked Cells** (Level 1–2 border): Towering structures of stacked steel prison cages housing hundreds of prisoners in cramped conditions; accessible via narrow earth tunnels with groundwater.
- **Cannibal Zone** (Unconfirmed level): A distinct region within the complex where prisoners have organized into a separate, hostile cannibal community; avoided by rescue efforts.
- **Glass Chamber** (Level 2): A special isolation cell holding [[Das letzte Opfer]], a halfling imprisoned separately from the general population.
- **Desert Room** (Level 2): An extremely hot, arid environment where prisoners suffer from severe dehydration; appears to serve as a secondary containment zone.
- **Field with Barracks** (Level 3 / Above Ground): An open field surrounded by military-style barracks, visible from Dalior's upper plate; includes a small forest and stone walls, functioning as the surface-level extension of the prison complex.
- **Lowest Level Encampment** (Level 3 Deep): An improvised settlement area with hunting grounds and communal structures where the long-term imprisoned population had established subsistence living.

## Key Figures

- [[Dr. Mason]]: Creator and primary operator of the Oubliette; runs the facility as an experimental system for control and study.
- [[Das letzte Opfer]] / The Last Sacrifice: A halfling prisoner held in a special glass isolation chamber; has connections to [[Snix]] and Mason's experiments involving magical rings and entities.
- [[Mogak]] / Kommandant Mogak: A Goliath and former commander of the Chrom-Brigade, imprisoned in the facility; became a key ally during the escape effort and coordinated prisoner evacuation.
- [[Herviar]]: An operative who provided intelligence to the Gold Daggers regarding the facility's entrance and location (Session 017).

## Known Facts

- A vast underground prison system beneath [[Dalior]], created and operated by [[Dr. Mason]] as an experimental confinement project.
- Divided into at least three distinct environmental zones, each with different conditions and prisoner populations.
- Level 1 Antechamber: Contains a logbook and three teleportation circles; middle circle serves as the primary entrance.
- Level 1 Meadow and Forest: A bright underground meadow (~600m below ground) with a daylight-like ceiling, guarded by two five-headed hydras; adjoins a large, ancient underground forest filled with barlguras, ape-like creatures, cannibals, and scattered prisoners in tree-holes and cell structures.
- Level 2: Extremely hot, desert-like environment with severe dehydration conditions; includes a special glass isolation chamber holding Das letzte Opfer.
- Level 3: A deep tunnel network leading upward to a field with military barracks, a small forest, and stone walls.
- Level 3 / Above Ground: Additional barracks visible from Dalior's upper plate, located outside the city proper.
- Prisoner population: Approximately 700 individuals held across all levels (documented in Session 023), including Goliaths, Earth Genasi, dragonborn, halflings, and other races; intentionally mixed groups designed to prevent organized resistance.
- A distinct cannibal population existed within the complex; the escape effort intentionally excluded them from evacuation (Session 022).
- Entrance access: Via a teleportation circle located in a storage room (Lagerraum) near Dalior's city centre.
- Infrastructure: Steel prison cages, obsidian-stone structural variants, narrow earth tunnels with groundwater channels, and daylight-simulating ceiling systems.
- First escape and liberation: Executed by the Gold Daggers in Session 023 over approximately 24 hours; resulted in approximately 700 freed prisoners being evacuated upward via a long ladder assembly spanning all levels.
- The evacuation and escape sparked widespread riots across Dalior's second plate and upper levels, with newly freed prisoners incited to rise against the upper-class populations.
