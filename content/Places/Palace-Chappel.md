## Overview
The Palace-Chappel is a secret chapel hidden behind [[Yorni]]'s office in the [[Rosenpalast]], accessible only via a cipher hidden in a trapped drawer. It serves as a sacred space devoted to [[Yorni]]'s infernal warlock patron, containing an active blood-filled altar and a statue of a succubus. This is the palace's ancient chapel, repurposed for dark purposes.

## Character
A small, hidden chamber accessed through a stone-grinding hidden door. Hot air streams from within when opened. The space holds a red statue of a succubus positioned before a wooden chair that stands in a pool of blood—the ritual altar itself. The atmosphere is deliberately infernal and cramped, designed for secrecy rather than comfort.

## Notable Areas
- Ritual altar (blood-filled chair and floor)
- Statue alcove (succubus statue)

## Key Figures
- [[Yorni]] — warlock patron of the chapel, maintains the infernal ritual
