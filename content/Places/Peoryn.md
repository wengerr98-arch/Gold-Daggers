## Overview

Peoryn is a coastal town that serves as a critical travel hub and staging point for journeys to [[Helykonos]]. Built near the sea, it provides access to underwater travel routes and supply stops for the party's waterborne expeditions. The town has been recently attacked, leaving scars on both its infrastructure and its people.

## Character

A bustling maritime town with an active harbor and fishing culture. The atmosphere is one of recovery—cleanup and rebuilding work are ongoing. The town smells of salt, nets, and the sea. The presence of trade activity (particularly Samira's well-stocked shop) suggests it is a hub for adventurers, merchants, and sailors. (Waterside structures, docks, modest architecture adapted to coastal life.)

## Notable Areas

- Samira's shop (supplier of harpoons, nets, grenades, and travel gear)
- Battle site (nearby Admiral's tent with military equipment and loot)
- Harbors and docks (access to underwater travel devices)
- Bramir's residence (in the Peoryn agglomeration)
