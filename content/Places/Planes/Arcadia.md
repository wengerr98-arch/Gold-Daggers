# Arcadia

## Overview

Arcadia is an outer plane known to the Stygian Trading Group as a potential new trade destination. A planar split tunnel discovered by Stygian merchants connects Arcadia to both [[Elysium]] and [[Gehenna]], making it strategically valuable to established trading networks. The plane appears to hold significance in the broader cosmology, though its exact nature and inhabitants remain largely unknown to the party.

## Known Facts

- Connected to [[Elysium]] and [[Gehenna]] via a planar split tunnel
- Site of active trading interest for Stygian merchants
- First mentioned in the context of the Stygian Trading Group's expansion and exploration of new outer-plane trade routes (Session 098)
