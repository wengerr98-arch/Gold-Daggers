## Overview

The Astral Sea is a cosmological plane housing a red-stone astral city inhabited by elves and governed by [[Barzan]]. It serves as a transit nexus between the Material Plane and other realms, and was the site where the legendary ship [[Calamitas]] was constructed in the party's campaign.

## Known Facts

- Cosmological plane that lies between the Material Plane and other planar destinations
- Contains a red-stone, elf-inhabited astral city (catalogued as [[Red-Walled Elven City]])
- Astral city features a library, a MonDieu Quartier (where gods maintain offices), and a shipyard
- [[Bravin]] constructed the ship [[Calamitas]] at the astral city's shipyard
- The Gold Daggers spent approximately three months in the Astral Sea during their campaign before returning to the Material Plane
- [[Barzan]] served as guide and leader during the party's astral sojourn
- Related planes include [[Pandemonium]], [[Limbo]], and [[Deep Wilds]]
- First party encounter: Session 090
