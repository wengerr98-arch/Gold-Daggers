# Deep Wilds

## Overview
The Deep Wilds is a plane of cosmological significance, defined by the eternal duality of [[Yum Ka'ax]] (the Fox) and [[Luchs]] (the Lynx). It is the domain of fate spiders, where [[Arachne]] is said to dwell, and according to lore from the [[Queen of Wands]], this plane is where all the "card souls" were created. Its true nature and accessibility to mortal creatures remains unclear.

## Known Facts
- Plane of duality, eternally defined by the opposition of [[Yum Ka'ax]] (the Fox) and [[Luchs]] (the Lynx)
- Domain of fate spiders; [[Arachne]] is strongly associated with this plane
- [[Queen of Wands]] is said to have created all "card souls" here
- Also known as "the Wilds" (alias)
- Lore source: DM long-rest exposition in Session 070; canonical status unconfirmed
- First appearance: Session 070 during DM exposition
