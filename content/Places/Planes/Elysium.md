# Elysium

## Overview
Elysium is an outer plane in the campaign's cosmology that serves as a potential afterlife destination for souls. It is accessible from the Material Plane via the River Styx and Limbo, where the dead may cross over to Elysium or other realms depending on their nature and alignment. Part of a planar tunnel system that connects [[Gehenna]] and [[Arcadia]], Elysium exists as a node in the outer planar trade and spiritual routes.

## Known Facts
- Outer plane accessible from the River Styx through Limbo; souls can travel to Elysium after crossing the ferry
- Connected via planar tunnel system to [[Gehenna]] and [[Arcadia]], with both tunnels leading to Arcadia as the hub
- Functions as an afterlife destination for souls aligned with or destined for this plane
- Part of the outer planar system accessible from [[Stygia]] as a known trade destination
