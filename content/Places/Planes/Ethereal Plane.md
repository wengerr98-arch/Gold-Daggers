## Overview

The Ethereal Plane is a plane of existence with a distinctly religious (rather than wizard-made) origin, known to the campaign world for approximately 100–200 years. Inhabited by unknown watchers who appear unfriendly to outsiders, it is accessible via spells like Etherealness and serves as a transit point for creatures like phase spiders between itself and the Material Plane.

## Known Facts

- Accessible via the Etherealness spell; first actively explored by the party in Session 085
- Described as "religious" in origin, distinct from planes created by wizards
- Inhabited by unknown watchers — appeared unfriendly to [[Barzan Aeducan]] during his reconnaissance
- An unknown entity attempted to Charm [[Barzan Aeducan]] while he was present on the plane
- Phase spiders are known to transit between the Ethereal Plane and the Material Plane (encountered at [[The Eladrin Library]])
- Described as difficult for construction within, similar to [[Limbo]]
- Known to the campaign world for approximately 100–200 years
- First session appearance: Session 085, when [[Barzan Aeducan]] entered to scout [[Kasta]]
