# Fury's Heart

## Overview

Fury's Heart was the original demonic kingdom of [[Demogorgon]] within the Abyss. This chaotic plane served as the seat of the demon prince's power before his forces relocated to the [[Nature House Plane]]. Named by Tautok in Session 077, it represents Demogorgon's ancient stronghold in the lower planes.

## Known Facts

- Located within the Abyss
- Former primary seat of [[Demogorgon]]'s power
- [[Demogorgon]] has relocated his forces and demonic kingdom to the [[Nature House Plane]]
- Named by Tautok during Session 077 as part of divine lore explanation
- The shift represents a strategic repositioning of demonic forces in the campaign
