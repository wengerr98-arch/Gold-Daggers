# Gehenna

## Overview
Gehenna is an outer plane within the campaign's cosmology, positioned as part of the planar trade infrastructure accessible from [[Stygia]]. It forms part of a greater planar network via a split tunnel system, where both Gehenna and [[Elysium]] connect through to [[Arcadia]], integrating Gehenna into the outer planar system.

## Known Facts
- Outer plane connected to [[Elysium]] and [[Arcadia]] via a split tunnel system
- Part of the outer planar system accessible from [[Stygia]]
- Relevant to Stygian trade route discussions
- First appeared in Session 098 as part of planar trade route briefings
