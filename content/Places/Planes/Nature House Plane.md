# Nature House Plane

## Overview

The Nature House Plane is an extraplanar domain and the current seat of [[Demogorgon]]'s power. After abandoning [[Fury's Heart]] in the Abyss, Demogorgon relocated his demonic kingdom to this plane, where he now commands his forces and anchors the [[Wild Hunt]] threat that threatens the material realm and its settlements. The plane is campaign-critical to the party's unfolding conflict.

## Known Facts

- Current planar base of [[Demogorgon]] following his departure from [[Fury's Heart]]
- Home to Demogorgon's demonic kingdom and [[Wild Hunt]] forces
- Campaign-relevant: the Wild Hunt conflict is anchored here
- Previously Demogorgon occupied [[Fury's Heart]] in the Abyss before relocating to this plane
- First identified and named by Tautok in Session 077
