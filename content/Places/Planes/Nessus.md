# Nessus

## Overview
Nessus is the 9th and deepest layer of the Nine Hells planar system. It is the infernal seat of archdevil power and was encountered by the party during Session 093, where they confronted [[Moloch]] in a major planar conflict. The layer is known to host powerful fiends and has become strategically significant in the party's ongoing conflict with infernal entities.

## Known Facts
- 9th and deepest layer of the Nine Hells planar hierarchy
- Part of the Nine Hells cosmological structure (see also [[Avernus]], the first layer)
- [[Moloch]] (Archdevil) maintains presence here; was encountered and defeated by the party in Session 093
- The party was pulled to Nessus via a planar portal opened during Session 093
- The party formed a pact with four lesser demons during their Nessus encounter (Session 093)
- [[Torren]] maintained a base in Nessus that was subsequently destroyed; 30 of his personnel were killed there (referenced Session 095)
- Planar portals connecting material and infernal planes can be opened via crafted items (e.g. dimensional knife)
