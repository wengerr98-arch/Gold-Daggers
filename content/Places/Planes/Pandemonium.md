# Pandemonium

## Overview
Pandemonium is a cosmological plane of chaos and madness, confirmed campaign-relevant and directly linked to [[The Eight Swords]]. Multiple party members, particularly Snix, have experienced visions within this plane—dreams of deserts scattered with statues and floating chains, connected to personal mysteries and prophetic journeys.

## Known Facts
- Campaign-relevant plane; confirmed by DM to be linked to [[The Eight Swords]]
- Snix experienced visions here during Session 052 and Session 057
- Known sub-location within Pandemonium: a desert with statues and floating chains ("Wüste mit Statuen und schwebenden Ketten")
- Visions experienced in this plane are connected to personal arcs—particularly Snix's mystery involving a smoke figure and his sister
- Party members (Koda, Eloryn) have also experienced Pandemonium visions during shared psychic encounters
- Snix remains affected by Pandemonium "flickers" as of Session 078
