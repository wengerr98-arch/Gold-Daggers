# River Styx

## Overview
The River Styx is the river of souls and a major cosmological boundary between the material realm and the infernal regions, bordering [[Limbo]] at the 5th Circle. Crossing it typically results in death, though the ferryman [[Charon]] grants passage to those who can pay. In Session 096, the party physically crossed the Styx with Charon's aid en route to [[Avernus]].

## Known Facts
- Major cosmological feature; the river of souls
- Borders [[Limbo]] at the 5th Circle
- Serves as the boundary between the material realm and the infernal regions
- Crossing the River Styx typically causes death
- [[Charon]] is the ferryman of the dead who transports souls (and living travellers) across
- Party crossed it in Session 096 with Charon's aid, heading to [[Avernus]]
- Drinking Styx water absorbs infernal souls and triggers a charisma save (DC unknown); Leumas rolled 23
- Drinking the water caused Leumas's armor to transform
