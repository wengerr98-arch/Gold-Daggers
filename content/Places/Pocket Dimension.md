# Pocket Dimension

## Overview
An extradimensional pocket space originally created as a private domain for [[Madame Dimdelle]] and the [[Whiterose]] organisation. Following Madame Dimdelle's defeat in Session 042, the party seized the dimension and transformed it from a hostile facility into a shared base and productive farming site. The space now operates under mixed governance with Thorren, the Tower, [[Dakon]], and [[Nasri]], serving as a secure location for food production and party operations.

## Character
A surreal mix of industrial oppression and nascent cultivation. The dimension features sprawling expanses of meadow interspersed with glass structures—some monumental (glass palaces), others grimly mundane (glass boxes that once held captives). The air is thick with the strange liminal quality of magically-confined space. When [[Lorilla]] applied her nature magic, constrained plants and seedlings suddenly flourished, suggesting the land itself was magically suppressed under Madame Dimdelle's rule. (inferred: The fox-like nature spirit that emerged suggests deep connection to the land's awakening.)

## Notable Areas
- Large glass palace (grand structure, contains operating table where BB was discovered)
- Green meadow with glass boxes (former containment area; party freed ~120–130 captives here)
- Forest section with glass boxes (secondary containment area)
- Second building (discovered by party during initial exploration; contains sacrifice altar and fleshberg ritual apparatus)
- Teleportation Circle chamber (upper floor of second building; modified by BB to prevent unauthorized use)
- Farmland and cultivated gardens (party-designated production area; Noah considered as chief gardener)

## Key Figures
- [[Madame Dimdelle]] (original creator and owner; defeated by party in Session 042)
- [[Lorilla]] (bonded with nature spirit; maintains magical cultivation of the dimension)
- [[Noah]] (proposed/considered as chief gardener for food production)
- Fox-like nature spirit (nature entity bound to the land; introduced to party by Lorilla; connection to the dimension's cultivation potential)

## Known Facts
- Originally a hidden extradimensional facility created by [[Madame Dimdelle]] and operated by the [[Whiterose]] organisation as a center for human trafficking and exploitation.
- The dimension contained approximately 120–130 captive sex workers when the party discovered it; all were freed and transferred to Tower care.
- The facility featured a large glass palace, glass boxes used for containment, a sacrifice altar tied to fleshberg ritual magic, and a Teleportation Circle in the upper level.
- The land and vegetation in the Pocket Dimension were magically constrained or stunted under Madame Dimdelle's rule; [[Lorilla]]'s nature magic caused seedlings and edible plants to flourish rapidly once the oppressive magic was lifted.
- A fox-like nature spirit exists within the dimension and is connected to the health and cultivation of the land; [[Lorilla]] bonded with this spirit and introduced it to the party.
- After the party's victory, the Pocket Dimension was negotiated into shared control between the party, Thorren, the Tower, [[Dakon]], and [[Nasri]], with agreements for food security, farming, and limited governmental presence.
- BB modified the Teleportation Circle to prevent anyone using the old access pattern from entering; the kingdom later gained authorized access through Hurmond as liaison.
- The dimension is described as primarily farmland, particularly after the party's conversion of the space into a cultivation and storage base.

## First Appearance
- Session 042: The party discovers and breaches the Pocket Dimension, defeats hostile forces, frees captives, and claims the space as a base and farming area.
