# Quilti

## Overview

Quilti is an island or island group located in the archipelagic region of [[Amare]], serving as the distant homeland of [[BB]]. It is a place of personal significance as the location of BB's childhood home and the residence of her father. BB has maintained correspondence with her father from afar, and he knows she is safe.
