# Rhino

## Overview
Rhino is the northernmost city on the southernmost continent, according to intel provided by the Triton interrogee Joldos. A Waterplane rift is located near Rhino, making it geopolitically significant within the rift network that spans the campaign. The reliability of Joldos's intel is uncertain, as it was extracted under interrogation.
