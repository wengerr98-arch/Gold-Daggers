# Rosengarten

## Overview
Rosengarten is a crime-driven and trade-dominated island located near the west coast of [[Ophris]], close to [[Aarum]]. The settlement itself is built within a cave system, creating a hidden, defensible location for its criminal and commercial enterprises. The island is also home to Rosengarten guards, a security and escort organization that operates across the region.

## Character
A hidden island settlement operating in semi-secrecy within a cave. The atmosphere is criminal and commercial — pragmatic, profit-driven, and wary of outsiders. The physical setting is defined by cave architecture and the contrast between underground caverns and the populated areas within. The presence of trade caravans and escort services suggests a well-organized if morally gray operation.
