# Rosenpalast

## Overview
The Rosenpalast (Rose Palace) is the royal palace complex of [[Nolia]], located on the highest tier of [[Dalior]]. It is the official residence of the [[Prinzessin|Princess of Nolia]] and serves as the seat of government, housing the offices of senior officials like [[Yorni]]. The palace is a grand political and ceremonial center, dealing with matters of state, diplomacy, and royal administration.

## Character
A imposing, elegant palace built on the highest level of Dalior with grand architecture befitting a royal residence. The palace features formal reception areas adorned with blue "Vedalke" statues, elegant foyers and waiting rooms for official business, and multiple towers and gardens. The atmosphere is one of formal ceremony and power, with a sense of both grandeur and political intrigue. Access is controlled and ceremonial.

## Notable Areas
- [[Palace-Chappel]] — secret hidden chapel devoted to infernal worship, hidden behind Yorni's office
- Jorni's office — located within the palace, contains a cipher-locked drawer concealing the hidden chapel entrance
- Garten (garden) — multi-level gardens within the palace grounds, including rooftop and ground-level areas with statuary and water features
- Stallungen (stalls) — castle stables for housing livestock (including geese and the hydra Alois)
- Turm (tower) — residence tower(s), including the princess's private quarters
- Toilette / WC — palace bathrooms
- Gästequartier (guest quarters) — rooms for visitors and dignitaries
- Reception foyer — formal entrance area with ceremonial statues

## Key Figures
- [[Prinzessin]] — Princess of Nolia, resident of the palace
- [[Yorni]] — official with office in the palace, manages diplomatic and administrative functions from the Diplomatie Ministry

## Known Facts
- Location: highest level (Palast tier) of [[Dalior]], [[Nolia]]
- Home to the [[Prinzessin]] (Princess of Nolia)
- [[Yorni]]'s office is housed here; the palace serves as a seat of the Diplomatie Ministry
- Three blue "Vedalke" reception figures guard the entrance (Vedalke is a race/species)
- The palace contains multiple internal levels and structures (gardens, towers, stables)
- [[Palace-Chappel]] is a hidden infernal chapel within the palace, accessed via cipher from Yorni's office
- Session 048: major royal wedding held at the palace; the party infiltrated the event to recover stolen swords
- A Pit Fiend was summoned or guarded in the Palace-Chappel and was destroyed during the Session 048 wedding infiltration
- The Palastplatte is referenced as the name for this tier/level
