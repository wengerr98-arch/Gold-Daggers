# Safti

## Overview
Safti is an island city within the [[Amarre]] archipelago, known as the home island of the priest [[Silk]]. The party became aware of Safti during their time in [[Dalior]] when [[Silk]] entrusted them with a personal delivery mission to bring her diary to her younger sister Chrazi on the island during Act 2.

## Character
A quiet island settlement, likely smaller and less cosmopolitan than the major cities of the mainland. The island's significance lies in its domestic and personal ties rather than major political or commercial prominence. (inferred)

## Notable Areas
None confirmed.

## Key Figures
- [[Silk]] — priest, native resident of Safti
- Chrazi (also called Kratzer, Millie) — [[Silk]]'s younger sister

## Known Facts
- Island location within the [[Amarre]] island group/region
- Home island of the priest [[Silk]]
- The party undertook a delivery mission to bring [[Silk]]'s diary to Chrazi on Safti
- Chrazi is referred to by multiple names: Chrazi, Kratzer, Millie (canonical form uncertain)
