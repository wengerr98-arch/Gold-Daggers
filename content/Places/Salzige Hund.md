# Salzige Hund

## Overview
The Salzige Hund is a modest inn and tavern located in the village of [[Tulip]], serving as a social hub for local residents and travelers. It features attached stables and is known for welcoming both visitors and local youth. The party first visited during their overland journey east and found it a friendly, relaxed gathering place.

## Character
A warm, unpretentious village inn with a tavern atmosphere and working stables. The space appears to cater to a mix of travelers and locals, particularly appealing to younger residents of Tulip. No detailed architectural or sensory descriptions are currently documented. (inferred: likely rustic, functional, with the smell of wood smoke and beer, and the sounds of horses from the adjacent stables)
