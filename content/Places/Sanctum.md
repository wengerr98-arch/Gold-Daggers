# Sanctum

## Overview
The Sanctum is a sacred refuge invoked by [[Barzan Aeducan]] aboard the Nautiloid, the party's vessel and mobile base of operations. It functions as a persistent, named safe space aboard the ship where the party returns to rest, recuperate, and conduct private rituals after operations. The Sanctum provides both practical shelter and magical protection for the crew.

## Character
A dedicated, warded interior space with a calm, contemplative atmosphere suitable for rest and magical work. (inferred from its use as a ritual sanctum and safe haven aboard an active vessel)

## Notable Areas
None confirmed.

## Key Figures
- [[Barzan Aeducan]] — the cleric who invokes and maintains the Sanctum

## Known Facts
- Location: aboard the Nautiloid (the party's ship)
- Invoked and maintained by [[Barzan Aeducan]]
- Functions as the party's persistent base/refuge aboard the ship
- Serves as a place for the crew to rest, read, and wait between operations
- Barzan performed a magical ritual within the Sanctum in [[Session 060 - Exhaustion|Session 060]], experiencing a lore vision related to Skjall and the wall's power
- Also referred to as "Barzan's Sanctum" and "Sanctum (Nautiloid)"
- The party first returned to the Sanctum after the Helikonos operation in [[Session 055 - Punching up|Session 055]]
