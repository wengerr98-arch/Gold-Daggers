## Overview

The Saphir Menagerie is a small jewellery shop located in [[Dalior]]'s Gänggele alley and market area. Specializing in diamonds, sapphires, and bespoke jewellery commissions, it has served as a regular vendor for the party during their time in the city. The shop is run by Jarosla, a distinctive figure known for her black hair, large pale-brown eyes, thick glasses, and long blue dress.

## Character

A modest establishment with the character of a specialist jeweller's workshop. The shop gives the impression of careful craftsmanship and curated inventory, populated with fine gemstones and delicate metalwork. Jarosla maintains an active hand in negotiations and pricing, suggesting personal investment in her business and clients.
