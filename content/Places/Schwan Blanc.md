# Schwan Blanc

## Overview

The Schwan Blanc (also known as Cheval Blanc) is a high-end leisure venue in [[Ophris]], combining a luxury spa with a beer garden. Located directly opposite the royal palace, it caters to the city's affluent clientele and serves as a fashionable destination for those seeking refined relaxation and hospitality.

## Character

An establishment of notable elegance and refinement, matching Ophris's high standards for hospitality and luxury. The venue features fine amenities including spa facilities and cosmetics services. The atmosphere suggests exclusivity and comfort—a gathering place for the city's elite seeking leisure and indulgence. (inferred from luxury spa context and Ophris's refined character)
