# Shipyard (Ophris Coast)

## Overview
A coastal facility on the shores of [[Ophris]], serving as a mixed settlement where refugees, displaced persons, handworkers, and a growing community of [[Tritons]] and [[Cousins]] established themselves following the Helikonos evacuation in Session 055. This location has become the informal nucleus of a new, multi-species community formed in the aftermath of the relief operation.

## Character
The shipyard bustles with construction and settlement-building activity. Handworkers construct shelters on land while Tritons build structures in the water, creating a unique dual-element settlement. Food is cooked communally, and the atmosphere is one of relief and reconstruction—a moment of respite after chaos. The site has a mixed, welcoming character bolstered by the presence of the [[Cousins]], who bring provisions and beer.

## Notable Areas
None confirmed.

## Key Figures
- [[Bromir]] — warmly receives arrivals and coordinates activities at the shipyard.

## Known Facts
- Located on the Ophris coast; geographically in Ophris territory rather than Helikonos proper.
- Primary residence of most of the [[Cousins]].
- A mixed community composed of refugees, handworkers, [[Tritons]], and [[Cousins]].
- Handworkers build shelters; Tritons construct structures in the water.
- Community formation and provisioning are coordinated with support from revolutionaries and local groups.

## First Appearance
- Session 055: Evacuation destination and site of mixed community formation during the Helikonos relief operation.
