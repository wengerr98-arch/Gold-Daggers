## Known Information

The Silent Coin Taproom is the party's portable carried tavern, accessed via BB's beer glass (bierglas) — when BB takes out the glass, the tavern begins to expand around the party. It exists in a pocket dimension or dream-space and can be deployed anywhere. It is distinct from [[The Silent Dance Tap Room]] (a mobile dream-house manifestation containing the Nightmare Overseer encountered in Session 064). The tavern is home to the entity [[Lotti]] (also known as Divienne), who communicates with the party via Comprehend Languages, and contains a logbook with centuries-spanning black-star death-day entries tied to a forgotten war.

## Session Appearances

- Session 065: Expanded during the monument council meeting in Skjall; used as the gathering space for the party's meeting with clan leaders. BB spoke with [[Lotti]]/Divienne inside; the logbook was read via Comprehend Languages.

## Known Facts

- Portable tavern accessed via BB's beer glass; expands when BB produces the glass.
- Exists in a pocket dimension or dream-space; can be deployed anywhere the party travels.
- Home to the entity [[Lotti]] / Divienne.
- Contains a logbook with centuries-spanning black-star death-day entries referencing a forgotten war.
- Recognised by the Skjall king and Tazar as a legendary tavern they knew of.
- NOT the same location as [[The Silent Dance Tap Room]] (Session 064 Nightmare Overseer dream-house).
