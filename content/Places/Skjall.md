# Skjall

## Known Information

Skjall is a major fortified city in the frozen north, enclosed by a massive steel wall held aloft by four warforged Titans. The city is divided into four racial districts — Orcs, Goliaths, Bugbears, and Minotaurs — each with its own culture, architecture, and traditions; Goliaths handle funerary rites and the city's necromantic labour. The city held deep significance as both a military stronghold and a spiritual nexus tied to the Wild Hunt and the Wintergöttin, and became the focal point of the party's northern arc across dozens of sessions.

A Salamander called Trafalgar is freed during the rodeo.

Party consults Karoth at Sanktum Elend and reads archival texts about Vorad, Goliath/Giant history, the Wintergöttin, and Wild Hunt beliefs. The king authorises the party to hunt shapeshifters; the party tracks, defeats, and delivers one. Barzan dies and is revived through the Monolith with Lady Ty's restoration.

Lorilla and Koda disappear after the battle (blood and moonbeam traces; sending returns only hostile breathing). The party presents The Mother (in an obsidian tube) and a red-haired Goliath woman to the König. The throne room wall opens to reveal a wall of eyes.

After returning, they confront and turn a goliath spy (Beat). The party briefs the king and all clan leaders; the king publicly reveals the monument threat and imposes curfews, battle suspensions, patrol groups, guard rotations, and evacuation/registration systems. Vokon (Beat) gives the party portal-opening Wishbones. Barzan receives Mason's Sending reply about needing Skjall artifacts.
- Session 074: Split-party session (BB/Leumas) in Skjall — the pair explore the Kanalisation and Lagerhalle areas through the Goliath, Bugbear, and Minotaur districts. Items discovered include a chest, logbook, large egg, lizard egg, blue dragon fish, diamond, and emeralds. Yaenne remains an active threat; Thronsaal aftermath and Wild Hunt pressure continue to shape conditions.

Lorilla and Snix retrieve diamonds and emeralds from a storage chest. Leumas kills the king (Maradak Dreamkiller/Torr); a giant eagle form carries Leumas and the body away. The king's death destabilizes Skjall leadership.
- Session 076: The death of the Torr is publicly announced; Leumas addresses the crowd and claims the new kingship/leadership role. The party investigates the throne monument/cube chamber and fights through a mushroom-land battle sequence. The session concludes with the party victorious and a new leadership era beginning for Skjall.
- Session 077: The party receives new items, enters the Wild Hunt area from Skjall, scouts camps/tents/patrols/field hospitals, then pushes back the Wild Hunt using Leumas's wind/wall effect. The Volk settlement is now a day's march away. Session closes with the party reuniting with allies.
- Session 078: Referenced — the king of Ophris confirms an alliance between Ophris and Skjall and grants the party diplomatic standing to speak in Ophris's name.
- Session 092: Referenced (no additional detail on file).
- Session 101: Skjall mobilises for the coming fight — Torren and Skjall formally join the coalition; the Frost Giants commit to helping Skjall following the party's Astral Sea audience with Muryn; Avernus reinforcements prepared, framing Skjall as the focal point of the gathering war effort.

## Known Facts

- Dotryn is located in or associated with Skjall as of Session 045.
- Yazi headed toward Skjall after departing from the party in Session 045.
- ~200–300 years ago, Skjall held a lethal Torr-selection tournament; non-citizens could participate but not become Torr — they received a prize of "walking through a door" instead (Brentan's account, Session 050).
- The door prize in the tournament led to a chamber containing a massive monster: Balgura-like, four arms, snake head, brown fur, thumbs on feet, larger than a church. Brentan survived killing it (Session 050).
- As of Session 057, the settlement has four districts. Noah and Lionel are present there. Torren reveals agents are being deployed northward toward Skjall on a destabilising mission.
- The city is enclosed by a massive steel wall held up by four warforged; large enough to house Loid (the Nautiloid) inside a section of the wall shell (Session 058).
- Former Torr: Maradak Dreamkiller — Natok's uncle; killed by Leumas in Session 075 (Sessions 058, 075).
- Current leader: Leumas claimed the kingship/leadership role publicly after the Torr's death (Session 076).
- Four racial factions reside inside: Orcs (Yee-Ha area), Goliaths (funerary rites and necromantic labour), Bugbears, Minotaurs (Moohs Blues area) (Session 058).
- Ophris healers stationed in the Gastquartier; embedded in a broader spy network with ~45 attendees at a late-night meeting (Session 058).
- The Krypten (crypts) contain many damaged skeletons, including several less than two years old (Session 058).
- A black obsidian monument stands in the Eingangshalle; the party also observed a black obsidian key in the king's possession (Session 058).
- The steel wall is currently in an OFF state — BugBear library mechanical notes confirm this (Session 059).
- The wall was originally built by early tribes; Frost Giants and Goliaths constructed it first, BugBears later added craft and structure (Session 059).
- A Trämmli (tram/cable car) connects the Goliath Viertel to the BugBear Viertel (Session 059).
- The surrounding mountain environment is full of life and plant growth despite the extreme cold (Session 059).
- Records in the Goliath Library predate the current King by more than 250 years; burials changed at some point in that history (Session 059).
- The corpse-boiling funerary tradition derives from older cannibalistic/tribal practices; Skjall locals believe souls travel north after death and enter the Wild Hunt (Session 059).
- A large flame approximately 3 km from the wall is powered by a magical metal-ring construct with rotating components; its purpose is unknown (Session 059).
- A doppelgänger infiltrated the healer community aboard Loid during Session 060, impersonating a murdered cleric; identified as an Orklady who died 8–12 months earlier (Session 060).
- The wall's power is tied to the Thronsaal and may require recharging via an obsidian crystal, per Barzan's ritual vision (Session 060).
- The black obsidian Monument in the Eingangshalle absorbs those who touch it like liquid; Leumas was drawn in and lost maximum HP (Session 061).
- Sanktum Elend archive contains ancient texts about Vorad (first king of the Wall), the four Warforged Titans, Goliath/Giant history, the Wintergöttin, and Wild Hunt beliefs (Session 061).

- A massive corrupted flesh creature known as The Mother was fought and subdued in the Thronsaal (Session 062); BB polymorphed it into a turtle and it was contained in an obsidian tube.

- The throne room wall opened during Session 062 to reveal a wall of many eyes and a larger hidden presence — the nature of this presence is unknown.
- A red-haired Goliath woman appeared in tears immediately following The Mother's defeat; her connection to The Mother and the king is unresolved (Session 062).
- An obelisk in the Goliath quarter requires blood and rune-seals to open the associated door; identified as magical and ancient (Session 063).
- The party crossed through The Veil (boundary between life and death) in Session 063, entering an afterlife/limbo and fighting a giant and worm-like creatures there.
