# Dreamhouse im Ork-Viertel

## Overview

The Dreamhouse is a mobile tavern that serves as the Gold Daggers' base of operations. During Session 068, it was situated in [[Skjall]]'s Ork-Viertel (Orc Quarter). The tavern possesses an extraordinary property: those within its walls can share and enter each other's dreams, making it both a sanctuary and a window into the party's collective subconscious. The location became a focal point of planar instability during the campaign's northern arc.

## Character

A multi-story tavern structure with an ethereal, almost liminal quality—walls between planes grow thin within its confines. The interior features multiple levels, including at least a second floor, and during planar disturbances exhibits unsettling phenomena: sand, crystalline shards, and planar tentacles materialize in the air around the structure. The tavern serves as both a practical gathering space and a threshold between worlds, its architecture seemingly designed to accommodate the movement and dreams of its inhabitants.

## Notable Areas

- Second floor (residential level — housed [[Yum Ka'ax]] before liberation)

## Key Figures

- [[Yum Ka'ax]] (Fuchs) — a large shapeshifting creature freed from the second floor by [[Lorilla]] during Session 068

## Known Facts

- Type: mobile tavern and party asset
- Location during Session 068: Ork-Viertel (Orc Quarter), [[Skjall]]
- Special property: those inside can share dreams with one another
- Multi-level structure; at least a second floor exists
- In Session 068, a planar breach destabilized the tavern, manifesting planar instability (sand, shards, tentacles)
- The walls of the Dreamhouse experience planar thinness — the boundaries between planes become permeable during periods of cosmological disturbance

## First Appearance

- Session 068: Party's base of operations at the beginning of the planar breach crisis in [[Skjall]]; a Fox-like shapeshifter ([[Yum Ka'ax]]) discovered on the second floor and freed by [[Lorilla]].
