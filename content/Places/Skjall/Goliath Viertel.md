## Overview
The Goliath Viertel (Goliath Quarter) is one of four major racial districts within [[Skjall]], serving as the cultural and spiritual center of the city's Goliath population. As the keepers of funerary rites and necromantic labour, the Goliaths have shaped this district into a place of deep significance, blending sacred burial practices with arcane knowledge. The quarter houses [[Sanktum Elend]], an important archive and sanctuary, and is connected to the broader Skjall mystique through ancient obelisks and sealed crypts.

## Character
The Goliath Viertel carries an atmosphere of reverent solemnity mixed with pragmatic cultural power. The air feels heavy with age and purpose — stonework dominates, with architectural choices reflecting Goliath traditions of honouring the dead. Crypts and archive spaces suggest layers of preserved history beneath the quarter's surface. A tram line connects this district to the Bugbear Viertel, marking it as an active hub within Skjall's infrastructure despite its solemn character.

## Notable Areas
- [[Sanktum Elend]] — archive, funerary sanctum, and music venue combined; contains crypts with restricted archival texts
- Obelisk (Goliath Quarter) — ancient, magical structure requiring blood and rune-seals to access (location of deeper investigation in Session 063)
- Goliath Library — archival repository containing texts on Vorad, Goliath/Giant history, the Wintergöttin, and Wild Hunt beliefs; records predate current king by 250+ years
- Kanalisation — subsurface passages explored in Session 074
- Lagerhalle — warehouse or storage area accessed through the district in Session 074

## Key Figures

## Known Facts
- Sub-district of [[Skjall]]; one of four racial districts (Orcs, Goliaths, Bugbears, Minotaurs)
- Goliaths serve as keepers of funerary rites and perform necromantic labour for Skjall
- Connected to Bugbear Viertel by a Trämmli (tram/cable car)
- Contains Sanktum Elend, which blends funerary/necromantic function with emo/nu-rock/pop-punk music venue operations
- Crypts beneath the quarter contain many damaged skeletons, some less than two years old
- An obelisk in the quarter is identified as magical and ancient, sealed with blood and rune-seals
- Archival texts at Sanktum Elend reference Vorad (first king of the Wall), the four Warforged Titans, and Goliath/Giant history
- Beliefs in the district hold that souls travel north after death and enter the Wild Hunt (traditional Skjall funerary philosophy)
- Affected by wall damage and seismic activity during Session 067
