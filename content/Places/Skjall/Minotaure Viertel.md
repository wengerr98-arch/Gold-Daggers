## Overview

The Minotaure Viertel (Minotaur Quarter) is one of four major racial districts within the fortified city of [[Skjall]], serving as the residential and cultural center of the minotaur population. The district became strategically significant during the late campaign when it fell under assault by combined undead and planar forces, and currently bears signs of severe structural damage from the conflict.

## Character

The district is characterized by ruined streets and shattered architecture following the planar assault. Survivors report eerie atmosphere with undead rising from the rubble and a sense of hidden direction controlling the chaos. Within the district lies a hidden tavern area accessible through narrow, steamy corridors marked by red-flame indicators in the piping systems—a liminal space resembling [[Silent Coin Taproom]] from the dream sequence shared by the party in earlier sessions.
