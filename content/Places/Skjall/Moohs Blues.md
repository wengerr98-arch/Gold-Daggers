# Moohs Blues

## Overview
Moohs Blues is a premier jazz club and music venue located within the Minotaur Quarter of [[Skjall]]. Run by or closely associated with the patron [[Mooh]], it serves as both a cultural gathering place for the city's elite and a nexus of diplomatic intrigue—hosting musicians, visiting dignitaries, and operatives from various factions. The venue operates as a de facto meeting ground for regional intelligence networks and supernatural entities.

## Character
Moohs Blues combines an upscale, refined atmosphere with an undercurrent of mystery and political significance. The venue features live music performances (notably the band fronted by Spike), well-stocked drinks and cuisine, and an ambient culture of mingling and conversation. The interior architecture is described as sophisticated, with refined furnishings and an air befitting a place of rank and influence. A secondary access point exists—a fancy marble alley lined with trimmed bushes that leads beyond the city's normal geography into a separate, snowy, mossy-floored space, suggesting the location has planar or dimensional properties beyond its surface appearance.

## Notable Areas
- Marble alley approach (leads to snowy, mossy city threshold; possible planar portal)
- Club floor (main performance and mingling space)
- Bar area
- Hidden tavern space in the Minotaur Quarter (connected to or linked beneath the club, mentioned in Session 072)

## Key Figures
