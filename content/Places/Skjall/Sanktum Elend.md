# Sanktum Elend

## Overview
Sanktum Elend is a hybrid institution in [[Skjall]]'s Goliath district that combines sacred funerary and necromantic functions with an active emo, nu-rock, and pop-punk music venue. Its dual purpose—serving both as an archive of ancient Goliath history and spiritual knowledge, and as a modern performance space—makes it a distinctive and complex place within the frozen city's landscape. The crypts beneath hold secrets tied to [[Skjall]]'s history and the [[Wintergöttin]].

## Character
The building reflects its dual nature: formal archival spaces with solemn, reverent atmosphere contrasted against vibrant, energetic performance areas. The crypts are cold and deeply unsettling, lined with damaged skeletons and the decay of ages. Above, the music venue thrums with bass and energy, populated by an eclectic mix of musicians, venue-goers, and visitors seeking the city's nightlife. The archive itself is hushed and careful—vitrines of precious texts, page-turn mechanisms controlling access to restricted sections, and the persistent chill of stone and ancient knowledge.

## Notable Areas
- Crypts (accessed via secret passage; contains damaged skeletons and old funerary remains)
- Archive and Restricted Text Section (guarded by [[Karoth]]; vitrines with page-turn mechanism; holds historical texts on Vorad, Goliath and Giant history, the [[Wintergöttin]], Wild Hunt beliefs, and Wall construction)
- Music Venue / Performance Floor (emo/nu-rock/pop-punk stage and dance space)

## Key Figures
- [[Karoth]] — religious keeper of the archive; controls access to restricted older texts and operates the vitrines' page-turn mechanism

## Known Facts
- Located in the Goliath district of [[Skjall]]; sub-location of Skjall
- Serves dual functions as both funerary/necromantic archive and active music venue
- Contains crypts accessible via secret passage
- Archive holds ancient texts on Vorad (first king of the Wall), Goliath and Giant history, [[Wintergöttin]], Wild Hunt beliefs, and the Wall's origin and construction
- [[Karoth]] controls access to the restricted older text area and operates the page-turn mechanism (blättere) for the vitrines
- Crypts contain many damaged skeletons, including several that appear to be less than two years old
- An obelisk in the nearby Goliath quarter requires blood and rune-seals to open an associated door; identified as magical and ancient
- The crypts may be connected to the Faceless King's nightly transformations and disposal of bodies
