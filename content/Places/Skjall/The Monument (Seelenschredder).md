# The Monument (Seelenschredder)

## Overview

The Monument, known locally as the **Seelenschredder** ("Soul Shredder"), is a campaign-critical magical monument standing in the Eingangshalle of [[Skjall]]. This liquid-like structure functions as a gateway to an inner dimension where souls—captured by the Wild Hunt—exist as spores within the [[Mushroom Realm (Seelenschredder)|Mushroom Realm]]. The Monument is tied to the Wall's power system and activation requirements, making it central to understanding Skjall's defensive and cosmological mechanics.

## Character

The Monument appears as a black obsidian object that behaves like liquid—those who touch it are drawn inward as if through a permeable membrane. It radiates a tangible sense of otherworldly power and danger. The structure is described as monolithic and immovable, yet its surface seems to absorb matter and consciousness alike. Those in proximity report a strange, disorienting quality, as if standing before something that bridges the material and spirit realms. ## Known Facts

- Black obsidian monument located in the Eingangshalle of [[Skjall]]
- Behaves like liquid; those who touch it are drawn inward
- Touching the exterior causes maximum HP loss (documented: Leumas lost max HP on contact, Session 061)
- Entering it fully triggers a death/revival cycle; those who enter experience black-water visions and temporal distortion before re-emerging (Barzan, Session 061)
- Contains an inner dimension — the [[Mushroom Realm (Seelenschredder)]] — where souls captured by the Wild Hunt exist as spores
- [[Thautok]] (alias Eradrin) inhabits or appears within this inner realm and has shared knowledge of the Wild Hunt and Wall mechanics with party members
- Connected to the Wall's power system, activation requirements, and the Obsidian crystal in the Thronsaal
- Campaign-critical element tied to the larger arc involving the Wild Hunt, the Wall, and soul mechanics

## First Appearance

- Session 061: Leumas investigates the Monument and touches it, triggering immediate maximum HP loss; Barzan intentionally enters the Monument and survives a death/revival cycle, returning transformed; the event is witnessed by the party and the king
