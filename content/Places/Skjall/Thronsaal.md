## Overview

The Thronsaal is the grand throne room of [[Skjall]], the frozen northern fortress-city. It serves as both the royal residence of the [[König|Torr]] (Maradak Dreamkiller) and the primary assembly hall for military strategy, diplomatic councils, and major civic events. The room is tied to the metaphysical heart of Skjall's power system — the [[Obsidian Crystal]], believed to contain and regulate the energy of the Wall that encases the city. As of Session 070, the Thronsaal has suffered catastrophic structural damage following a massive assault by [[Mason]].

## Character

A cavernous and imposing hall befitting the seat of a warforged-maintained fortress-city. The space is vast enough to accommodate thousands of combatants and spectators simultaneously; it hosted an estimated 30,000–40,000 fighters during the Session 060 battle. The room features royal regalia, a prominent throne, a bar or trestle area (where historical logbooks are kept), and a raised podium from which the king observes state functions. High stone walls and structural elements give it the feel of a war-time stronghold as much as a royal chamber. The atmosphere is cold, utilitarian, and sacred — a place where decisions shape the survival of all Skjall.

## Notable Areas

- **The Veil** — a rune-sealed threshold door within the Thronsaal that opens onto an afterlife/limbo space; accessed by the party in Session 063, leading to an otherworldly throne room with royal armor and inscriptions tied to [[Torr|Vorad]], the first king.
- **Royal Podium** — a raised spectator platform from which the Torr observes ceremonial events and battles (Session 061).
- **Bar/Trestle Area** — contains archived logbooks, including one recovered in Session 064 with a black star / missing-date pattern tied to the forgotten war and references to Agarath and Aeducan.
- **The Monument/Cube** — a throne-monument structure within or beneath the hall, investigated and explored in Session 076; its exact position and relationship to the main chamber is unresolved.

## Key Figures

- **[[König|Maradak Dreamkiller]] (The Torr)** — The king of Skjall; fought at the front during the Session 060 battle and remained the nominal ruler until killed by [[Leumas]] in Session 075. Appeared as a spirit-image and physically throughout the Thronsaal arc.
- **[[Leumas]]** — Party member who isolated [[Mason]] in the throne room during Session 069 and ultimately killed the Torr in Session 075, afterward claiming kingship.

## Known Facts

- The [[Obsidian Crystal]] (believed to store and regulate the Wall's power) is located within or beneath the Thronsaal.
- Served as the site of a massive coordinated battle (~30,000–40,000 fighters) in Session 060, with the Torr and allied forces fighting at the front.
- The [[Obsidian Crystal|Obsidian Crystal]] may require a recharge cycle; Barzan's ritual vision in Session 060 linked the wall's power directly to this room.
- A rune-sealed threshold door called the Veil provides access to an afterlife/limbo space containing royal armor and inscriptions tied to [[Torr|Vorad]] (the first king, founder of the wall).
- A logbook recovered from the bar/trestle area in Session 064 contains a black star / missing-date pattern tied to a forgotten war, with references to Agarath and Aeducan.
- A massive corrupted flesh creature called The Mother was fought and subdued here in Session 062; it was contained in an [[Obsidian Crystal|obsidian]] tube.
- During Session 062, the throne room wall opened to reveal a wall of many eyes — a much larger hidden presence still unresolved.
- An obelisk rose during [[Mason]]'s assault in Session 069, destabilizing the structure.
- The room suffered catastrophic structural collapse in Session 070, with the party falling through a hole in the floor and landing on a mountain of dead bodies.
- As of Session 076, the throne monument/cube chamber beneath the main hall was investigated and confirmed as a distinct sub-space.
- [[Leumas]] claimed the kingship/leadership role of Skjall publicly after the Torr's death (Session 076).

## First Appearance

- Session 060: Site of the major Skjall battle; [[Obsidian Crystal]] confirmed to store the Wall's power; [[Barzan]] receives a lore vision linking the throne room to the wall's activation.
