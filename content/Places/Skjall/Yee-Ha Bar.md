# Yee-Ha Bar

## Overview

The Yee-Ha Bar (full official name: **Yee-Ha Dance Bar**) is a vibrant bar and dance venue within the Orcs' district of [[Skjall]]. Operated by [[Fred]], it is the focal gathering place for Orc culture in the city, featuring energetic line dancing and a reputation as the "Orc's Yee-Ha dancehall." The venue also serves as an unexpected center for civic education — Fred runs a seminar on nonviolent everyday conduct from a side room, bridging the celebratory and contemplative sides of Orc community life.

## Character

The Yee-Ha Bar pulses with Orc energy — loud music, high spirits, and the coordinated stomping of line dancers creating a rhythmic, celebratory atmosphere. The main hall is alive with movement and community; a dedicated side room provides quieter space for Fred's seminars, creating distinct zones within the single venue. The overall feel is welcoming and informal, rooted in Orc traditions of camaraderie and physical expression.
