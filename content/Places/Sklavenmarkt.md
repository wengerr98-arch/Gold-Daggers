# Sklavenmarkt

## Overview
The Sklavenmarkt (Slave Market) is a public venue located on the Zweite Platte (second plateau) in [[Dalior]]. It is the primary slave trading location in the city, specializing in the sale of unusual and rare races — virtually anyone except humans. The location serves as both a marketplace and a seat of official city authority during sales events.

## Character
The Sklavenmarkt is dominated by a raised auction podium where slave sales are conducted. Sessions describe large crowds of onlookers gathering before the podium, with uniformed officials (described as resembling high soldiers) conducting the sales and addressing the crowd. The crowd atmosphere is often tense and emotionally charged; during one observed event, the gathered crowd appeared aggressively disposed and in poor spirits. The location is clearly a place of significant foot traffic and civic activity within Dalior's layered geography.

## Notable Areas
- Auction podium (Sklaven-Verkauf podium) — the raised platform where sales are conducted
- Main gathering area — open space in front of the podium for crowd assembly

## Key Figures
- Official auction conductors / city officials — uniformed staff who oversee slave sales and address the crowd (name(s) unknown)

## Known Facts
- Located on the Zweite Platte (second plateau) within [[Dalior]].
- Specializes in non-human races for sale; humans are rare in Dalior and thus in this market.
- Known by alternative name "Aktionshaus" (auction house).
- Called "Slavenmarkt" in Swiss German colloquial speech; formal German name is "Sklavenmarkt."
- Draws large crowds of prospective buyers and onlookers, suggesting significant commercial and social activity.
- Official city personnel staff the sales, indicating it is a regulated, state-sanctioned operation.

## First Appearance
- Session 005: The Gold Daggers made a purchase at the Slavenmarkt (exact item recorded as "Tats," meaning unclear).
- Session 008: The party observes a crowd gathering before the Aktionshaus with an ongoing slave sale, witnessing city officials conducting the auction.
