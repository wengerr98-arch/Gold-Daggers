# Stahlmaus

## Known Information

Stahlmaus is a pet shop located in [[Ophris]] (Stadt / Shicla). The party visited during Session 035, when [[Eloryn]] wanted to buy fish and [[Koda]] browsed the bear selection but ultimately did not purchase.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 035]]: Party visited — [[Eloryn]] sought fish; [[Koda]] browsed bears (did not purchase).

## Known Facts

- Pet shop in [[Ophris]] Stadt (Shicla district).
- Sells fish and bears among its inventory.
