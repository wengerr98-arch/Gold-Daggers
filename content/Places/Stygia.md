# Stygia

## Known Information

Stygia is the 5th Circle of Hell and the domain of [[Asmodeus]], functioning as a city-state with bureaucratic entry procedures, trade regulations, credit systems, duel protocols, and a visible social hierarchy. The party reached Stygia via the [[River Styx]] in Session 098 and spent several sessions there researching the forgotten war, negotiating with [[Levistus]], and infiltrating the Stygian Ball. [[Levistus]] is imprisoned within Stygia yet rules from his domain, the [[Bastion]].

## Session Appearances

- Session 098: Party enters via the River Styx, receives temporary visas at the city wall. They explore the administration building, gather trade-route intelligence (including an Arcadia portal), attend [[EAZA]] for food and socialising, and visit the library the next morning. Barzan sees a reflection connected to [[Lady Ty]] and recalls the name Verus. A Legend Lore-style vision about [[Levistus]] is experienced. Session ends with the party offered help by a library contact.
- [[Session 099]]: Party continues in the Stygian library — researching forgotten peoples, old gods, planar history, and elven history. A follower ([[Ken Paul]]) with a ball ticket and unregistered Sigil is identified. The party contacts [[Levistus]] via Sending and proceeds to his domain via the [[Bastion]] and lift, navigating the audience queue before a major combat erupts in the throne room area.
- Session 100: [[Levistus]] audience takes place; he briefs the party on planar corruption, titan lore, and provides astral-anchor outfits. Last confirmed scene in Stygia before the party departs to the Material Plane.
- Session 101: Referenced as setting for the Stygian Ball — the Gold Daggers' political infiltration and Leumas impersonating Odyss Trion are a key scene set in or connected to Stygia's infernal court.

## Known Facts

- Located in the 5th Circle of Hell; domain of [[Asmodeus]].
- Entry requires a temporary visa processed at the city wall.
- Local rules include trade regulations, credit systems, duel protocols, and etiquette laws.
- Sub-locations include a city administration building, [[EAZA]] (restaurant/social hub), a library, and [[Bastion]] (Levistus's tower).
- A portal route to Arcadia exists via a split tunnel between Elysium and Gehenna, known to local traders.
- Some residents bear branded sigils visible on their persons.
- [[Levistus]] is imprisoned within Stygia but rules from the [[Bastion]]; his audience is reached via a lift and formal queue of politically significant attendees.
- The Stygian library contains books and notes related to the forgotten war, old gods, and planar history.
