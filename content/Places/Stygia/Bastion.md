# Bastion

<div class="gd-seen"><p><span>Visited in 1 session</span> <span><a href="../../sessions/session-099">Session 99</a></span></p></div>


<p class="session-meta gd-tl-meta"><span class="gd-tl-link" data-to="timeline" data-q="place=bastion" data-label="On the timeline →"></span></p>

## Known Information

The Bastion is [[Levistus]]'s tower and seat of power within [[Stygia]], serving as both an administrative centre and the venue for his formal audience system. It contains a staffed reception area, a lift system that transports visitors between levels, and a formal waiting queue used by politically significant attendees. The party was brought directly to the lift upon arrival in Session 099.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 099]]: Primary setting of the session. Party arrives at the Bastion, passes through reception, and participates in the formal audience queue. Notable attendees present included [[General Meirad]] (with 10 bodyguards), [[Frau Echlin]], [[Marseili]], [[Kathlan]], and the First Torr/Thautok. A major combat erupted in the throne room area. The Stygian Gala appears to be organised from this location.

## Known Facts

- Sub-location of [[Stygia]]; [[Levistus]]'s tower and domain.
- Contains a reception area with receptionists and staff who distribute flyers.
- A lift system connects levels within the Bastion.
- Features a formal audience queue for political and military visitors.
- Named attendees in Session 099: [[General Meirad]] (10 bodyguards), [[Frau Echlin]], [[Marseili]], [[Kathlan]], First Torr/Thautok.
- The Stygian Gala appears to be organised from the Bastion.
