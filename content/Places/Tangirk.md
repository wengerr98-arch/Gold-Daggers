# Tangirk

## Known Information

The Tangirk is a river flowing through all of [[Nolia]], originating in the mountains to the north in the region of Hjarl. The party had to cross it during Session 014.

## Session Appearances

- [[Session 014]]: The party must cross the Tangirk river. Session text confirms the river originates from the northern mountains in Hjarl.
