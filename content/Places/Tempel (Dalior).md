## Overview

The Tempel (also known as the Rakshasa Tempel) is the Gold Daggers' primary bar and base of operations within the [[Entertainment District (Dalior)]]. Named for the near-immortal Rakshasa that serves as its supernatural protector, the temple functions as a gathering hub for adventurers, merchants, and allies. Its protection permits magical use on the premises without triggering the city's alarm systems—a rare privilege in [[Dalior]].

## Character

A vibrant, welcoming bar atmosphere filled with the ambient sounds of conversation and the clinking of glasses. Located at the end of a dead-end alley in the Entertainment District, the bar is marked by a medium-quality painting of a tiger-man (reversed hands) on its exterior wall. Hookah smoke drifts through the common areas; patrons include adventurers, merchants, and various faction members. The space feels simultaneously safe and edgy, a refuge for those who operate outside official channels. The temple has a marked festival-like energy, with [[Silk]] moving fluidly through the crowd—warm and sociable when greeting regulars, firm and physically intimidating when enforcing the district's rules against city guards.

## Notable Areas

- Cellar — underground chamber, formerly infested with large black humanoid rat-furred creatures (cleared by the Gold Daggers in Session 006 for 35 gold); later served as overnight shelter during the party's rescue missions; also used for treating wounded (demon-inflicted wounds in Session 024)
- Teleportation circle — present on the premises; used to transport [[Shelandria Van Meehren]] (Shelly) to the city in Session 014

## Key Figures

- [[Cetzy]] — manager and primary owner; provides protection and maintains order
- [[Silk]] — de facto manager and face of the establishment; enforces the district's rule against city guards (will physically confront guards if necessary); maintains a contact network for [[Amare]]; carries a thick scepter (post-Session 024)
