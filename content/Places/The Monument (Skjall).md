# The Monument (Skjall)

## Overview

The Monument in [[Skjall]] is the failsafe mechanism built by [[Abrahim (Ephraim)|Ephraim]] and others to release [[Anubis]] from [[Bob (Golem)|Bob's]] containment. Also known as the **Seelen-Schredder** — the party has visited it before.

## Function

The Monument serves as a failsafe key for the Anubis containment. To trigger it, the party must **move national borders** — options discussed include:
- Unclaimed land with the Shifters
- Nolia

The mechanism appears linked to the rune-map on Bob, which follows and updates with national borders.

## History

Discovered during Session 108 time travel:
- Party teleports via time anchor (drinking contest → boat → mushroom)
- Arrive hundreds of years in the past, deep in the North
- During the construction of the Wall in Skjall
- [[Thautok]] is present, mid-transition, working on the Monument
- The mushroom Barzan shows is the same one Tautok hands to Ephraim

## Construction

Built by Ephraim and others as part of the Anubis containment failsafe:
- A man was involved with the Behemoth component
- A woman was involved with the Leviathan component

## Known Facts

- Located in Skjall
- Built during the Wall construction era
- Functions as the failsafe for Anubis release
- Triggered by moving national borders
- Party has visited before (as Seelen-Schredder)
