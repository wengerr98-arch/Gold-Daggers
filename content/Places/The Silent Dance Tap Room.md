# The Silent Dance Tap Room

## Overview
The Silent Dance Tap Room is a mobile, dream-manifested tavern that materializes as a physical house when a glass taken from within it is filled with beer. Appearing as an uncanny, empty tavern resembling Skjall's architecture, it houses historical artifacts from the forgotten war and is presided over by "Lottie," a barkeeper-like statue. The venue functions as both a location of strategic importance to factions seeking historical knowledge and a nexus point for otherworldly entities and dangers.

## Character
The tavern manifests as an impossible structure: rooms shift visibility, doors lead unexpectedly between floors, and the exterior appears to be held aloft by many unseen hands and arms beneath it. The atmosphere is ethereal and unsettling—windows and sight lines change without explanation, and visibility between party members is unstable. The space smells of aged wood and stone, with a faint dreamlike quality that suggests the location exists partially outside normal reality. (Derived from Session 064 sidecar descriptions and session logs.)

- [[The Nightmare Overseer]] — boss entity encountered and defeated in Session 064

## Known Facts
- Location type: mobile / dream-manifested — exists partially outside normal space; not a fixed geographic settlement
- Alias in use: "Magic Taverne" (documented in Session 073)
- Activation mechanic: filling a glass physically taken from the tavern with beer causes the full house to manifest as a tangible structure
- Contains: [[Logbook (Silent Dance Tap Room)]] with black-star-marked entries and references to the Aeducan and Agarath families, connecting to forgotten war lore
- Notable feature: barkeeper-like statue nicknamed "Lottie" — crumbled to dust at the end of Session 064 after the Nightmare Overseer's defeat
- Boss encounter: [[The Nightmare Overseer]] was encountered and defeated here; the tavern collapsed after the confrontation
- Distinct from: [[Silent Coin Taproom]] (a separate location, referenced as a possible party base)
- The space appears to be manipulated or held aloft by otherworldly forces, evidenced by multiple hand- and arm-like shapes visible beneath the structure
- The tavern has manifested at least twice: once in Session 064 and again in Session 073 when used as a meeting site ("Magic Taverne")

## First Appearance
- Session 064: Party entered a dream-house manifestation; encountered [[The Nightmare Overseer]]; recovered the [[Logbook (Silent Dance Tap Room)]]; Lottie statue crumbled; tavern collapsed; one character was pulled into the Wildhunt
