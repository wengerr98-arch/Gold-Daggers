# The Unclaimed Lands

## Overview

A region between [[Skjall]], [[Kasta]], and [[Nolia]] with no central authority. The shifters are the oldest inhabitants.

## Known Facts

- No king, no council, no capital — the only thing everyone agrees on: nobody should be in charge
- No common culture — each family, each group lives by their own rules; one valley over, everything can be different
- The shifters are the oldest inhabitants
- Population includes deserters from Kasta, Nolia, and Skjall, war refugees, kobolds, orcs, tieflings
- Anyone who lives there and leaves others alone belongs
- Family-based governance, not tribal or state-based
- Nothing is written down — no contracts, no debt ledgers, no maps; a debt exists as long as both remember it
- Guests may stay three nights without asking; after that, help or move on
- Multiple attempts to claim the land have failed — not against an army, but because there was no one to defeat; fighters disappear overnight into the villages
- Every boundary stone, every flag, every surveyor's stake that appears there vanishes
- Wind and water serve as messengers, traders, and gatherers

## Trade & Outsiders

- Traders from [[Amare|Amarre]] do business there, but never with "the land" — only with individual families
- A trade applies exactly to those who made it; the next valley knows nothing about it
- There are no real trade routes, no roads — too dangerous for traders who don't know the land
- Tesk is a trader in Amarre who deals in northern relics; delivers on time, charges too much, never issues receipts; sells the finest pieces to Kasta, where no one asks questions

## Lore & Legends

- A name that caravans speak before entering the eastern valley — no one knows exactly whether she has any say there; they only know it's quieter if you visit her first
- A story told in taverns: a surveyor from Kasta went north with his stakes and came back without them, unharmed; he said only one name and never spoke of it again

## Geography

- Located between Skjall, Kasta, and Nolia
- Southern biome is heavily altered — volcanic soil, very fertile
- Predates the [[Ophris]] mountains
- Forests not very different from surrounding regions, but the mountains have their own unique flora and fauna
- In the east there is a valley where you must always say a name before crossing
