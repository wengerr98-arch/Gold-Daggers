# Tiger Stallungen

## Overview
Tiger Stallungen (lit. "Tiger Stables") is a stable complex in or near Dalior where the party maintains animals and equipment. The party returns here after transporting freed prisoners and uses it as a waypoint for resupply and magical communication, suggesting it serves as a semi-permanent base of operations or safe haven during their early campaign activities.

## Character
A functional stable facility designed for housing large animals and cargo. The stables likely feature stone or wooden construction with open bays for the tigers the party relies on for transportation. Practical, working atmosphere—the kind of place organized for efficiency rather than comfort. (inferred from function and context)
