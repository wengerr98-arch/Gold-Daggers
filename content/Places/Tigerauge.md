## Overview
Tigerauge is one of the largest jewelry and diamond shops in Dalior, located on the city's main market street. It is operated by the master appraiser [[Meister Bar Kergov]] and serves as a major hub for trading precious stones, gems, and fine jewelry within the city.

## Character
A respectable establishment on Dalior's busy market street, Tigerauge exudes the atmosphere of wealth and fine craftsmanship. The shop displays glittering diamonds, gems, and jewelry pieces across multiple counters. The proprietor conducts business with professional precision, appraising items and negotiating prices with the clientele. The lighting from Dalior's magical Licht-Blumen catches the facets of displayed stones, creating a bright, crystalline ambiance within the shop.
