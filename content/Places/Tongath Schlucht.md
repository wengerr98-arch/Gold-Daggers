# Tongath Schlucht

## Overview
Tongath Schlucht is a large chasm located to the right of [[Helykonos]], the Triton city. It serves as a geographic hazard and ongoing threat to the region, with rumors of monsters that periodically emerge from its depths. The chasm represents a significant environmental danger and potential plot element in the Helikonos arc.

## Character
A vast and yawning gorge with an ominous reputation. The chasm is visible from a distance as the party approaches Helikonos, looming on the landscape as a physical reminder of the perils surrounding the city. Locals speak warily of the creatures that crawl up from its depths, and its presence shapes travel and settlement patterns in the region.
