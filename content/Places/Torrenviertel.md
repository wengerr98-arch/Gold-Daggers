# Torrenviertel

## Overview
Torrenviertel is a commercial and industrial district of [[Aarum]] centred on metalworking and foundry operations. Controlled by the [[Torren Family]], it serves as a stronghold of their economic power within the city. The district's character is defined by heat, commerce, and the omnipresent coat of arms of its ruling family.

## Character
A working-class industrial quarter dominated by furnaces and foundry operations. The air carries the tang of hot metal and the constant heat of active forges. The Torren family coat of arms (a casting kettle / *Giesskessel*) appears on every building, marking the family's complete control over the district. The main street feels alive with merchant activity and commercial transactions.

## Notable Areas
- [[Torren Marktgasse]] – the main commercial street and heart of the district
- [[Giesserei]] – a major foundry operated by [[Dabri]], capable of casting and producing metal goods, tools, and weapons

## Key Figures
- [[Grandir]] – merchant and contact at the Marktgasse; broker of metal trade information
- [[Dabri]] – master founder and operator of the Giesserei; controls metal sourcing and production

## Known Facts
- Located within [[Aarum]], home to a significant portion of the city's metalworking industry
- Controlled and strongly affiliated with the [[Torren Family]]
- The Torren family coat of arms (a casting kettle) is displayed on every building
- Centre of metal trade and manufacturing for the region; deals in raw materials including Ithrium, Mithril, Kaltstahl, copper, bronze, silver, and gold
- The Giesserei can produce foundry work of all types: tools, weapons, and magical item components (raw blanks/rohlinge)
- Merchants in the district are fair negotiators but expect volume discounts on large purchases

## First Appearance
- Session 039: The party arrives in Aarum and visits the Torrenviertel market district. They speak with Grandir about metal pricing and conduct a major commercial negotiation with Dabri at the Giesserei, establishing a trading partnership and receiving a sending stone.
