# Unfreiwillig Glücklich

## Overview
Unfreiwillig Glücklich ("Involuntarily Happy") is a magic shop located in [[Dalior]], run by its eccentric proprietor Felix. The shop's peculiar name derives from Felix's own curse—a permanent enchantment that forces him to experience involuntary happiness. The shop is notable throughout Dalior for one unique property: it operates outside the city's magical surveillance network, allowing spells to be cast within its walls without triggering the Stadtverwaltung's alarm systems.

## Character
A cramped, cluttered magic shop with the air of a personal collection rather than an organized retail establishment. The space is narrow and crowded with magical supplies, reagents, and merchandise. The atmosphere is quirky and oddly welcoming, tinged with the infectious (and slightly unsettling) cheerfulness that emanates from Felix himself. The presence of the cheerful, smiling proprietor creates a surreal mood—he seems perpetually upbeat despite his cursed condition, lending the whole place an eerie forced-joy sensation.
