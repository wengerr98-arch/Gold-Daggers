# Vergessener Tempel

## Overview

The Vergessener Tempel (Forgotten Temple) is an ancient, sacred site located in the [[Ophris]] / [[Kasta]] desert region near a mountain range. Named after and dedicated to the divine child [[Ophris]], who sacrificed themselves and became the landscape itself, the temple serves as a memorial to their sacrifice and contains visions of the Vergessener Krieg (Forgotten War). It is heavily guarded and protected — six prior investigation teams sent to explore it returned nothing.

## Character

A place of profound spiritual weight and mystery. The entrance features a dramatic bridge of black stone inlaid with copper, flanked by towering illusory statues of ten legendary and divine figures (Undaris, Sarlsaquil/Salsa, Felix Calamiten, a Faun/Satyr, Cinder, an unnamed weightlifter figure, [[Nyx]], Verus, Dellio, and Levistus). The bridge culminates in a runic memorial inscription carved in stone, dedicated to the god-child: *"Aria Zima — Ruhe unser Kind. Deine Opfer werden nicht vergessen. Dies ist eine Grabstätte deiner Erinnerung…"* (Peace, our Child. Your sacrifice will not be forgotten. This is a sepulcher of your memory…) The atmosphere is solemn, ancient, and laden with divine mystery.

## Known Facts

- Located in the [[Ophris]] / [[Kasta]] desert region, near a mountain range
- Sacred site dedicated to the divine child [[Ophris]]
- Near [[Kloster der Herbstgöttin]] / [[Ophris]] area
- Entrance features a bridge of black stone with copper inlay
- Bridge flanked by ten illusory statues of legendary figures: Undaris, Sarlsaquil (Salsa), Felix Calamiten, a Faun/Satyr, Cinder, an unnamed weightlifter figure, [[Nyx]], Verus, Dellio, and Levistus
- Runic memorial inscription dedicated to the divine child [[Ophris]]: *"Aria Zima — Ruhe unser Kind. Deine Opfer werden nicht vergessen. Dies ist eine Grabstätte deiner Erinnerung…"*
- Entrance lock accepts [[Barzan Aeducan]]'s Kryptex
- When unlocked, the lock revealed two divine wedding rings inscribed "Aegis" and "Kazan"
- Interior contains visions or memories of the Forgotten War — including scenes of childhood, youth, exile, and return of [[Ophris]]
- Connected to the Vergessener Krieg (Forgotten War)
- Alternate name "Magentempel" noted in historical context
- At least six prior investigation teams were sent to explore the temple; none returned
- Entry requires passing a test set by [[Don Hor]]; patience is the first test (not fleeing)
- A training ground exists associated with the temple, where animated dummies and more complex challenges test entrants
