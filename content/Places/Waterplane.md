# Waterplane

## Overview
The Waterplane is an elemental or cosmological region accessible only through rifts that emerge near major coastal settlements. It appears to function as an alternate plane of water and oceanic reality, populated by aquatic creatures and accessible via portals from the Material Plane. [[Shen]] is actively constructing a rift network to connect the Waterplane to at least four major cities, suggesting it is a location of strategic and planar importance in the campaign.

## Character
A plane of perpetual ocean and water. The Waterplane manifests as vast underwater expanse with visible rifts and portals that shimmer at the edges. Whale-sized ships navigate its depths, and strange titanic fish inhabit its waters. The environment is cool, humid, and alien—a space of foreign light and pressure where marine biology operates on a scale beyond the Material Plane. A tentacled guardian dwells at the entrance rift near [[Helykonos]], suggesting both defense and mysterious ownership. (Atmosphere drawn from session encounters and strategic planar mechanics.)

## Known Facts
- Cosmological classification: elemental plane (campaign-relevant; exact classification unconfirmed).
- Entrance near [[Helykonos]]: a rift at the bottom of a ~10 km ocean trench.
- A tentacled creature of unknown identity dwells at the trench entrance.
- Rift network exists near [[Ophris]], [[Amare]], [[Kasta]], and [[Rhino]] — constructed under [[Shen]]'s direction.
- Chain of dominion: Azatoth → R'lyeh → [[Shen]] → [[Blobbert]].
- The stone shard carried by the party is connected to the Waterplane.
- Familiar spirits (e.g. [[Blobbert]]) return to the Waterplane upon death.
- Whale-ships and other organized forces operate within the Waterplane, suggesting habitation or military organization.
- A Titan-like fish of enormous scale was encountered in the Waterplane; connection to cosmic balance unclear.
