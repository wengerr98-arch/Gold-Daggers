# Wizard Island

## Overview

Wizard Island is a magical, mobile region located in the [[Amarre]] area, also known as the Moving Wizard City. The island rests on the back of Schnuffie, a large sentient creature, which serves as its living foundation and causes the island to move and reappear at different locations over time. The island is the property and seat of power of Wanda, the Queen of Wands, and represents a unique planar crossing point in the campaign world.

## Character

The island is a place of powerful magic and mystery, periodically vanishing and reappearing across the realm. Its physical form and immediate surroundings are tied to Schnuffie's nature and movements; the island's magical atmosphere reflects Wanda's arcane influence and her connection to the larger cosmological forces. The exact sensory qualities of the island itself remain largely unknown to the party, though its role as a nexus of planar and personal significance is clear.
