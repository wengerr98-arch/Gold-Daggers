## Overview

The Zweite Platte (Second Tier) is the second-highest residential and administrative tier of [[Dalior]], home to the city's wealthy elite and nobility. It sits directly below the [[Palast (Dalior)]] and above the Hauptplatte, accessed by permit-controlled central lift or grand staircases. The tier serves as an exclusive administrative and commercial hub for the aristocracy and merchant class, featuring the [[Sklavenmarkt]] (slave market) and various institutional buildings including [[Mason Haus]].

## Character

The Zweite Platte presents a stark contrast to the lower tiers. The atmosphere is refined but exclusive, with snobbish service from local escorts (notably the [[Rosengarde]]). Visitors ascending from the Hauptplatte encounter a horizon view before reaching the tier itself. The lighting is bright and orderly, maintained by the city's magical Licht-Blumen. Unlike the lower plates, the Zweite Platte lacks magical detection systems that trigger on unauthorized magic use, granting residents and permitted visitors greater freedom. The tier is predominantly populated by humans, with rigid social segregation from other races and lower classes.

## Known Facts

- The Zweite Platte is the second-highest tier of [[Dalior]], accessible by permit from the Stadtverwaltung.
- Access is strictly controlled; the party obtained permits in Session 007 to carry out [[Dr. Mason]]'s undead-clearing commission.
- The tier is home exclusively to wealthy humans, nobility, and merchant-class residents; other races and non-permit holders are barred.
- Unlike the Hauptplatte and lower tiers, the Zweite Platte has no magical alarm detection system.
- The [[Sklavenmarkt]] operates on this tier, featuring public auctions with large crowds (sometimes aggressive).
- The tier is well-maintained and clean compared to lower tiers; sweeping and orderly management are evident.
- [[Dr. Mason]] maintains a large mansion and basement laboratory complex on this tier, including necromantic research facilities.
- The Burned Quarter (Heruntergebranntes Viertel) is visible from the approach to the Zweite Platte, appearing cleaner during the party's ascent to this tier in Session 008, though corpse piles remained.
- [[Rosengarde]] escorts visitors on the tier and is known for condescending attitude.
- No recorded day-night cycle; magical light-blooms maintain perpetual illumination.
