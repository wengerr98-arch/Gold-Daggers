The party begins with artifact and curse research in rainy Peoryn, identifying the Meat Cube, the master key, and Dr. Fitz Mason’s work on necromancy and Felix’s curse. They travel to Lerchen, reconnect with Brovin/Bramir/Brentan and Mona, arrange repairs, clothing, and passage toward Helykonos, and share an evening of stories and food with the crew.
