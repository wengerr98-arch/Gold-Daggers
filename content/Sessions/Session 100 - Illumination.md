Er macht nömme so langi wanderige, nömme so langi toure. Er esch meh deheime und bliibt ruhiger. Er merkt langsam dases ziit werd zom goh und er wott no e reis go mache. und er goht höch es gebirge. ## Extracted Summary

Session 100, titled "Illumination," opens with a recap of the party's long journey and then moves into a major audience with Levistus. The group asks about earthbreakers, titans, the Silence, and the current threat behind the events affecting the Material Plane. Levistus explains several layers of the cosmic situation, including the role of a titan-like body, a ritual execution pattern, and the idea that a presence or corruption may be infecting planes. BB's card and identity are explored through an extended vision sequence. Levistus later provides the party with outfits as anchors for travel in the Astral Sea and hints at future work involving a presence that must be located and dealt with. The party then returns to the material world, follows leads in a wealthy district, encounters Odyss Trion / T'Dis Oroen, and is drawn through a portal back to Aarum. A major combat follows against a tarrasque, featuring the full party's spellcasting, healing, control, and damage output, ending with the creature's defeat. The session concludes with notes and open leads about portals, titans, Shen, Stygia contacts, time anchor traders, and unresolved cosmological threats.
