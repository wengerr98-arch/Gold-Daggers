## Extracted Summary

Session 102 centers on the Gold Daggers attending a grand Planar Ball, framed by Levistus-enchanted costumes granting one-time mechanical buffs. After a mixed red-carpet entrance (Eloryn and Leumas shine; Snix and Lorilla stumble), the party navigates the Ball's social arena.

BB experiences a vision of Odyss's backstory: a Tabaxi-like island childhood of hubris and entitlement, escalating into a series of failed glory-hunts — dragon turtle, dragon — before encountering Mason in Nolia, who kills and revives him. Odyss steals a scalpel, escapes, and eventually shifts allegiance from Mason to Jorni, spending years on procurement work while pursuing ever-greater personal power. The vision ends with Odyss hunting Tritons in the south, doing whatever he wants.

At the Ball, Barzan casts Heroes Feast (+19 Max HP modifier). The party outdrinks a Stygian trader named Plasna, placing her under a Gold Daggers Sigil; she reveals Odyss is moving directly toward Asmodeus. Meanwhile BB spots Sani Ambulan (a Leonin in a Jute Sack) in Asmodeus's entourage and alerts the party.

Odyss shapeshifts into Leumas to challenge Asmodeus to a duel. Barzan's Subtle Spell reverses the disguise in front of Asmodeus, who offers "Die Wette des Hadeses" — a board game staking HP pools. The Gold Daggers demand that all "Nothing"-infected people be banished to the 5th plane; Asmodeus demands 650 HP immunity in return. After a dramatic three-round combat sequence involving wild magic, confusion effects, a Jabberwalker, and clutch Dimension Door plays, the party wins. Six infected people vanish from the Ball.

Leumas then faces Odyss (now revealed) in a formal dance battle for social HP, fame, and titles. After 15 intense rounds of contested athletics, acrobatics, illusions, and tricks, Leumas is ultimately ejected from the Gala — charisma depleted. The session ends with a bittersweet tone: the board game is won, but Leumas loses the duel.
