## Extracted Summary

The session opens with Leumas locked out of the Gala, having lost his fame, honor, and title. He disguises himself as a staff member and convinces Levistus to let him back in. The Gold Daggers are added to a group chat with Levistus; Levistus can no longer track Odyss since he has left the plane.

The party approaches Sani Ambulan (her full name revealed as Lervick Ambulan), who is now working as a court smith for the Abadon family. She panics, is restrained by Eloryn with the immovable rod, and admits she works for Odyss.

The party encounters Zariel, who is behaving delusionally (insight 26: paranoid and deluded, fed bad information). Barzan attempts Dispel Magic on her but finds no valid target — she appears to not be magically influenced.

Plasna is freed after sharing intel: Odyss has been asking everyone at the Gala specifically about solar eclipses. Plasna has no authorization for the Material Plane, so no deal can be struck.

Levistus rewards each party member with a magic item: Periapt of Wound Closure (BB), Amulett of the Devout (Barzan), Metallic-Blender-Arm (Eloryn), Steady-Aim (Leumas), tattoo-distraction ability (Snix), Excellerate Amulett (Lorilla).

Sendings are dispatched to Koda, Tautok, and Murin warning about Odyss impersonating Torr and warning of frost giants.

The party infiltrates Odyss's villa. Four generals are captured (including Marius Wehl and Helen Dravik), who were coordinating the takeover of the plane Ophris. Yum Ka'ax the fox is found imprisoned in a mystery room. A massive battle with the Hushes follows across three rounds (plus a Round 5), ending in total victory — both Hushes destroyed in explosive finishes by Lorilla (HOW DO YOU WANT TO DO THIS) and Snix.
