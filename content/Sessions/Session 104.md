## Extracted Summary

The session picks up directly after the Battle of the Hushes. The party investigates the smoke residue and finds two Void Shards — glass-like containers that absorb things placed into them. The Blast Zepter is identified from one shard. A second shard yields a captive: Enara, a middle-aged woman wearing a robe identical to Eloryn's, establishing that people can live inside the shards. Generals attempt to follow.

The Mystery Door leads Barzan into a ritual baptismal chamber, filling with smoke. He temporarily forgets his feet (failed INT check). A WC toilet is found to contain a hidden portal directly to the Third Plate of Nolia.

The party escapes via Stigia's library. There, research divides: Barzan reads a religious book about an unknown deity (cult for the wealthy); Eloryn and Snix research solar eclipses and find multiple books; BB and Leumas sort Odyss's notes.

Key discovery: Seven stacks of notes, six predicting Solar Eclipse tables. The seventh discusses Zariel's massive flying warship. The eclipses are not simultaneous — they follow each other in sequence. The Planar Wheel is figure-eight shaped. Silence grows stronger during solar eclipses. The Material Plane sits at the center/axis of the Planar Wheel. Solar eclipses occur roughly every 400 years on the Material Plane.

Critical intel: The next solar eclipse is approximately 1.5 months away.

Lorilla retrieves a few hairs from Odyss's house. The party learns Odyss is waiting on his ship in the Astral Sea. They race back to Aarum to find a massive rift in the sky — hundreds of times larger than the Kalamitas — Zariel's warship.

Snix's Major Schwert is restored by Dakun and Nasri at the tower. Koda is managing logistics in Torren's absence. Susra and Kafra are handling logistics at Goldie Bräu.

Eloryn contacts Chetzy, who sends Silk — 32 years old, from Amare. Silk is installed as interim leader of Aarum while Torren is absent.

Barzan's Scrying attempt on the Sky Titan fails — the titan is not on their plane.

The next morning, Zariel's warship looms overhead. A "crazy lady" stands at the bow.
