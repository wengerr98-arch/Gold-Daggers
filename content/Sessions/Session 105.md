## Extracted Summary

The session begins with the party crashing into Avernus after their portal is severed, losing the front section of the Calamitas. Aarum's harbor is severely damaged by falling debris; the party spends the rest of the day helping with reconstruction. BB uses Disintegrate to clear large debris; Elo's Wall of Force protects The Tower from a huge chunk. Snix crafts leather gloves for the party.

Loot from the Zariel engagement is distributed: the Blade of Avernus (Legendary Vorpal Sword artifact), multiple rare armor pieces, and weapons including the Baleful Talon dagger (assigned to Snix).

The party teleports to Ophris via their safe-house teleportation circle. At the Palace of Ophris, BB detects scrying; Lorilla accidentally triggers an alarm dispelling it. BB (Nat 20 Persuasion, 31) talks them out of punishment. They meet Aazan and Don Hor, hand over the four captured Generals (Marius Wehl, Helen Dravik + 2); the generals try to detonate their glyph-teeth but the magic prison contains the blasts. Myrolox (magic shop) provides Leumas a Rhythm Maker's Drum +3 and Lorilla a Moon Sickle +3, plus 600,000 gold (100k per person). Crew receives 100g each from Barzan.

In the Astral Sea, the party faces Odyss's space-ship and two Astral Dreadnoughts. Ephraim (Principle of Abjuration) appears on a crane-like bird, driving nails into its neck, then disappears through a portal. The battle against Odyss runs 12 rounds. Highlights: Odyss uses Banishing Smite to exile Leumas (to Dalior) and Elo (to Helykonos) during the fight. Odyss transforms into his Torr (King) form in round 6. Barzan and Lorilla go down multiple times. BB is banished to a demi-plane in round 10. Snix delivers the killing blow in round 12 (HOW DO YOU WANT TO DO THIS). Snix acquires Torr's armor — dragonscale, fire-breathing, Legendary.

Post-combat: Leumas wanders his home village and donates 500 gold. Elo finds Helykonos devastated; Shen shrunken in his throne room (power source removed). She fights off remaining Shen soldiers as a bird (Kormoran). Party reunites at the Werft. Brevin (Snix's older cousin) is introduced; Calamitas is deemed too dangerous to use as a normal ship. Barzan sends 100 gold to each soldier. Party levels up. Wish prepared for BB (Power Word Heal or Gate). Leumas takes Blade of Disaster.

Revivify tested on a soldier (d6 roll: 5 — came back, appears OK, but not guaranteed). Party buys healing potions in bulk.
