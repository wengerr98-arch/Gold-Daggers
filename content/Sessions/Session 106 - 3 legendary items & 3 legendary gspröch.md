## Extracted Summary

The party debriefs in Helikonos after returning to the Calamitas at the shipyard (8 days repair needed, 1.5 months until the Ziz darkens the sun). Barzan performs a Legend Lore ritual targeting the scroll's author, entering a time-spanning vision: he sees Ephraim writing the four Pergament phrases across four separate timelines (as Ephraim, Abrahim, König Saul/Jakob, and a fourth). The most recent phrase (C) was written in Ephraim's own blood in a state of near-breakdown. Barzan is noticed and confronted in the vision — Ephraim recognises him and pulls him physically into the present (the 8th Tower of Kasta).

In person, Ephraim reveals: the Anubis sealing was a mistake, he was manipulated; the Anubis follows the borders as an invisible desert-storm; Ephraim placed the Winter there. The Taverne was made by Ephraim ("drom hend MER die Taverne gmacht"). He warns never to let the Imbalance grow too large, and that infection means all is lost — he has to relearn everything every day. He explains Al Bidaya (the Beginning / Mana), which he is searching for again to create life. He explains the cosmological hierarchy: mortals and all species are the original "Balance Quartet" — gods and Djinn are intermediary and unnecessary; Genasi are a new tier between mortals and the Balance. The Cithrul ritual = end of all existence.

Ephraim takes Barzan to his workshop, draws blood, and transforms Barzan into an Astral Dreadnaught to enter the Astral Sea toward the Ziz. Ephraim tears a feather from the Sky Titan and pours near-Al-Bidaya over it. Barzan is returned with a ritual knife (high quality, non-magical) from Ephraim.

Back at the shipyard, Barzan returns dramatically (200m in the air, caught by Lorilla as Giant Eagle). Party distributes the Loot of Odyss: Spell Guard Shield (very rare) → Eloryn; Nostos Spear +3 (legendary) → Leumas; Obsidian Flint Dragon Plate (legendary, too weak for current party); 9 greater healing potions; 200gp. Ritual Knife: high quality but non-magical.

Party travels north via Lloyd to a mountain peak in a storm. BB finds a crypt, digs it out with Mold Earth — a skeleton emerges wearing the Talisman of Ultimate Evil (6 charges, DC 20 Dex save or fall into a chasm). BB picks it up and her appearance shifts (eyes more evil, hair wilder). Party teleports back to Kasta safehouse (Lloyd sanctuary).

BB uses Wish to create a Simulacrum of Snix. BB then lays Punishment Bad Dream cards (Judgement) targeting Yorni and Leumas — burned into their retinas, activate when they sleep.

Barzan performs True Resurrection on Erkas (Yorni's brother, 25,000gp diamond dust). Erkas reveals: he died of starvation in Dalior underground; family surname is Ambulan; his younger siblings are Yorni and Yaen; he knew the Ambulans from Lervik; a half-orc called Sedal lived with them when Yorni was ~8.

Barzan sends to Yorni — Insight 30 vs Nat 1: Yorni is furious (Erkas lives, Odyss failed, Gold Daggers live). Yorni asks to retrieve Erkas; party gives him back without exchange. Yorni had secretly enchanted one of the party's coins as a tracker. Barzan returns Yorni's gold and warns him it's never too late to change.
