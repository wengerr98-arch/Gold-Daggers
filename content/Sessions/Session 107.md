## Extracted Summary

The session opens with the party in the Lloyd sanctuary, considering the tracker coin situation. Barzan sends to Ephraim asking why Anubis hasn't been freed. Ephraim's face appears in the water and a door manifests — the party steps through and finds themselves in the invisible tower above Kasta.

Ephraim (calendar year 2152) is confused about when they are; he believes they are simultaneous. He explains that Anubis could have negative side-effects if freed; the Idolosphere is a self-contained system that seeks balance. He has previously inserted and removed minor gods. Mana was stolen by Farid. He confirms the Taverne was his creation — always with Lotti — to fight the Nothingness, but something always prevented them acting. After each reset, everything that happened becomes undone. He made everyone including Lotti forget. An island was also lost. The Nothingness is more a force than a physical entity. Ephraim suspects the "other persons from the time-stream" he can't remember are the ones orchestrating things. The 35-minute conversation takes zero time for Ephraim.

He mentions Mason wrote in undefined lands between Ophris and Kasta where Dalior later formed, and was fascinated with eliminating death. Ephraim identified him as a prime manipulation target and deliberately gave him braindamage ~400-500 years ago to prevent him reaching his full magical potential. Ephraim's books are mostly rambling and difficult magical theory. He mentions Genasi may be the counterpart/opposite to objects being returned.

Back in the Sanctuary: Lorilla nat 1 on tree check; BB saves (28) on meadow check; Eloryn 22 water check. Long rest. BB's Punishment Dreams deferred to next session, but BB loses 8 Strength (drops to 4) during the night.

BB reads Ephraim's books for 12 hours (3 consecutive saving throws). She nearly reads herself to death — Eloryn has to splash her awake. Greater Restoration from Barzan restores BB. Barzan reads about sealing magic (history 21 + 18): Ephraim wrote of attempting to seal a minor snow-storm deity as an experiment — the storm never returned, the sea froze, and a land bridge formed. Barzan attempts dispel magic on various numbers (tracker coin?): all fail. Eloryn tries to bond with the face apparition — nothing.

Lorilla reads while Snix peeks into the book — Wisdom save Nat 1. Snix gains a permanent grudge against books. 38 damage from a sneak attack on Lorilla before they separate. Lorilla later reads safely (24 save) and gains two pieces of lore: (1) before the current planar structure there was one planet; (2) geological lists show identical stones across all planes.
