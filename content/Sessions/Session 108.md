## Summary

### Dreams

**Leumas Dream:** Trapped in a fake game-like world on a cliff with a dog, shield, and sword. Everything feels uncanny — villagers praise him, goblins ignore him if he stays on the path. Each attempt to break free (jumping off cliff, stabbing himself, going behind the flat-earth sun) costs Wisdom via D4 rolls. Eventually reduced to Wisdom 2, he succumbs to the game logic: kills goblins, slays a dragon, takes treasure, walks into the horizon.

**BB Dream:** Courtroom scenario shifting through roles — Judge (making life/death decisions with a sword), defendant (arguing about Yorni/Erdbrecher), audience member, lawyer, jury head. Each role shift costs D4 Wisdom. Key insight: when she stops making judgments and lets clear decisions emerge, the voices clarify and she wakes up. Lesson about the weight of judgment.

### Storm Confrontation

Party approaches the storm on Lloyd. Inside: elemental chaos between Water and Earth. Ghosts cling to everyone; a Leonin face pounds on the ship's barrier.

Party splits:
- **Team Wasser** (BB, Barzan, Leumas): see the Titan Krab (giant red claw)
- **Team Sand** (Snix, Elo, Lorilla): see the Sand Turtle/Leviathan with trees on its shell

### Lorilla Reaches the Golem

Lorilla wildshapes into an Albatross with Warding Wind bubble, flies through the storm. Nearly loses her soul when touching/approaching the Golem. Transforms through Albatross → Whale → Bat to maintain control.

**The Golem (Bob):** Hundreds of meters tall. Pyramid on its back. Giant swords, sandals, no face. The earthquakes were its footsteps. Runes on its body = containment sigils for Anubis. Arcana 31 reveals: sigils are nearly indestructible (thousands of HP), need a key to open. Anubis is neither fully contained nor free — "a bug" in the soul mechanics.

### Ephraim Encounter & Failsafe

Ephraim appears at the Golem. Reveals:
- The Golem is named **Bob**
- He built it differently — original used Behemoth and Leviathan bodies
- There's a **failsafe** built with others (a man for Behemoth, a woman for Leviathan)

**Time Travel:** Party teleports via time anchor (drinking contest → boat → mushroom) to Skjall during the Wall construction, hundreds of years in the past.

**Discovery:** Tautok is there, mid-transition, working on the Monument. The mushroom Barzan shows is the same one Tautok hands to Ephraim. **The failsafe is the Monument itself.**

**Solution:** Party must move national borders to trigger the failsafe. Options: unclaimed Shifter lands or Nolia.

**Ephraim's admission:** He was misguided when he performed the second ritual. He thought he was on the right path.

1. **Golem variant:** Ephraim's notes describe a different Golem type with a soul inside and third-party blood (not just animated construct)
