## Extracted Summary

The party, aboard the Aarum, has time-travelled approximately 500 years into the past (to year 323 after the Forgotten War). They gather information about the Unclaimed Lands — a region between Skjall, Kasta, and Nolia with no central authority, family-based governance, and deep-rooted resistance to outside control. The shifters are the oldest inhabitants.

Flying over the region, they observe the second-largest Nolia city (which shouldn't exist yet in this timeline) featuring the Kletterndi Kirche (Climbing Church). They land and are approached by a 5-person patrol: Sterak (Kobold leader), Linsea (Half-Elf), Kelpi (Oger Shifter), Huton (Warforged), and Uossa (Hyena-faced scout).

After initial suspicion, the party builds rapport through conversation. Barzan discusses religion, Leumas eventually reveals they are time travellers trying to save the world (Persuasion 36). The patrol mentions Hobgoblin Jeska as a powerful and aggressive local figure. The party gifts them a +1 armor.

During the night watch, Lorilla (Perception 22) and Leumas (Nat 20) detect 5 uniformed city scouts approaching. Combat begins against these uniformed attackers. Notable Round 1 actions: Barzan's Divine Intervention creates a Hallow zone with radiant vulnerability, Eloryn scores double Nat 20s with Eldritch Blast for a double kill. Session ends mid-combat.
